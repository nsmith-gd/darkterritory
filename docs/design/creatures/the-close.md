# THE CLOSE — the air in a crowded car goes bad

*Creature proposal, Moth (queue #706, ARCHITECTURE §8 note 1000), 23 Oct 2026. **Status: a proposal for the director;
nothing is built until the director approves it.** Numbers are a first pass in the roster's units.*

> **It's close in here. Too close.**

## 1. What it is

Not a beast: a breath. A slow grey haze that seeps in through a shut car's boards when too many people have been shut in
it too long, drawn by their breathing. The lamp gutters blue, everyone's breath fogs, and it starts breathing *with*
them. Then it goes into whoever's breathing hardest.

## 2. Roster entry (GDD §21, INTERIOR)

**THE CLOSE** · *in a shut car with the crew in it*
The lamp burning blue, everyone's breath fogging.
> **RULE: when the lamp gutters, spread out.**

**Sense:** scent (breath; several crew in one shut car). **Zone:** interior. **Want:** Kill. **Cost:** 2.

## 3. Silhouette and look

A thin grey haze along the floor and the ceiling of the car, thickening into slow coils that drift toward faces; inside it,
just visible in a lamp, the suggestion of a mouth, open, breathing. The lamp's flame shrinks and turns blue (the visual
tell, in every car's lamp, which already burn). Clips: `seep`, `gather`, `breathe` (pulsing with the crew's breath),
`enter` (a coil into a mouth), `driven` (scattering out a door).

## 4. The tell (spec A.3/A.4)

- **The guttering** (tier 1): the lamp's flame drops and goes blue; a second in-breath in the car with no one breathing
  it, a smooth rising hiss 6.5–9 kHz, slow (one breath every 3 s). Told from the car fire's crackle (6–9 kHz) by rhythm:
  a slow swell, not pops; and from the Fire Flies (9–12 kHz) by band.
- **Seen:** the blue flame, the breath fogging.

## 5. The rule (App. A.4 format)

```
SEEP      3 or more crew (2 on a crew of 2–3) in one car with its doors shut, for 45 s → it seeps in
          └ TELEGRAPH: the lamp guttering blue and the second breath (8 s)
          ├ a door opened and the car down to fewer than 3 (or 2) → it thins and is gone
          └ else
ENTER     into the crewmate breathing hardest (the one who ran in last, or talked most, by the voice meter) → GRAB:
          they choke, their voice muffled (12 s)
          └ a friend drags them out of the car (a carry, or Shove out a door) → it's out of them
PUNISH    left in the car: dead
```

## 6. Aboard or afoot; what it counters

**Aboard, at speed.** It counters **turtling in one car** (the crew holed up in the lit, shut car where nothing else can
reach them) and **everyone on one job** in one car.

## 7. Compositions

- **The Close + Climbers:** Climbers mount any gap held by fewer than there are of them, so outnumber them at the gaps;
  the Close takes a crowded car. Hold the gaps in numbers, outside.
- **The Close + the Choir:** the Choir says shut every door and hush; the Close says not all of you in one car.
- **The Close + Ribbits / Lurcher:** afoot, be together; aboard, be apart.

## 8. Early versus late

- **Early:** from Frontier; 60 s before it seeps; the hardest-breathing is whoever ran in last.
- **Late:** 30 s; and in every shut car at once over its count.
