# THE WINDFALL — the tree that gets up

*Creature proposal, Sloe (queue #709, ARCHITECTURE §8 note 1004), 23 Oct 2026: one of the forest's heavies (`docs/playtest/2026-10-23.md` line 36: "big, kind of scary, that come out of the trees ... they can kill the player pretty easily if they get grabbed ... They can get into cars and cause problems ... relatively hard to take down. Like, stopping in the wild feels like it should feel scary"), for #711's big forests (Oak, note 1006). **Status: a proposal for the director; nothing is built until the director approves it.** Numbers are a first pass in the roster's units.*

> **Stand still. Someone bang the rail.**

## 1. What it is

Four metres of it, the colour and shape of a fallen spruce, roots and all, until it stands. It walks out of the deep
forest on four long legs of root to a train stood in the woods, and it pulls cars open: tears a side door off its runners
and reaches in. Its front is bark, and nothing hurts it there. Its back is split, and in the split is something wet and
red. It has no eyes: it goes to whatever shakes the ground, a standing train's pump, a running crewmate, a wrench on a rail.

## 2. Roster entry (GDD §21, OUTSIDE: the wild)

**THE WINDFALL** · *out of the deep forest, at a train stood in it*
Trees cracking one after another, coming closer.
> **RULE: stand still; someone bang the rail.**

**Sense:** vibration (the train standing; steps afoot). **Zone:** outside, the wilderness (Rook's `wilderness` stance,
#711's deep forest). **Want:** Kill. **Cost:** 4 (a heavy: one at a time early).

## 3. Silhouette and look

Lying, a windfall across the forest floor. Standing, a trunk on root-legs, its crown of dead branches dragging, the split
down its back glowing faintly red in a lamp. Taller than a car's roof: it looks in the clerestory. Clips: `lie`, `rise`,
`walk` (branches breaking), `tear` (a door off a car), `reach` (an arm of root into a car), `grab`, `crush`,
`turn`, `stagger` (the split hit), `fall` (death: it's a log again).

## 4. The tell (spec A.3/A.4)

- **The cracking** (tier 1): trees snapping in a slow line toward the train, 80–250 Hz cracks with a 1–3 kHz splinter,
  one every 2 s, from 60 m. Told from the Tower Jaw's felled tower (one great fall, a gnawing before it) by being a walk,
  a line of cracks coming closer.
- **Seen:** tops of trees shaking in a line; then it, standing up out of the dark.

## 5. The rule (App. A.4 format)

```
COME      the train stood in the wilderness in deep forest (#711) for 30 s, or anyone afoot more than 30 m into the trees
          → one rises 60 m off and walks to the train at 1.6 m/s
          └ TELEGRAPH: the cracking (35 s of it at that pace: time to go)
          ├ the train moves off over 6 m/s → it can't keep up; it lies down again
          └ else
TEAR      at a car: a door torn off in 3 s (the car is open for the rest of the night: the Climbers' and the Close's
          problem too); then it reaches in
GRAB      the nearest crewmate, in a car or afoot within 4 m → lifted: CRUSH, 10 a second, dead in 10 s
          ├ a blow on its split (from behind) → it drops them; 8 blows on the split kill it (2 gun rounds, if the gun bears)
          └ blows on its bark do nothing
HUNT      it goes to the strongest shaking within 25 m: a runner (over 2 m/s), a blow, a wrench rung on a rail (Use on the
          rail, 1 s, loud), then the train's pump; a crewmate standing still or walking slowly isn't felt
          └ so one rings the rail away from the train to draw it, the others come up on its split, still, then strike
PUNISH    alone, it can't be hurt from the front and can't be drawn off the one you're saving: the train has to go
```

## 6. Aboard or afoot; what it counters

**Afoot and aboard, a stand in the wild.** It foils **stopping in the wild too long** (Rook's `wilderness`) and
**running off from the crew into the trees** (dispersing). Hard to take down (the director): only two, swapping, can.

## 7. Compositions

- **The Windfall + the Hotbox or the Knotter:** they're why the train is stood in the woods; fix it with something walking
  out of the trees.
- **The Windfall + the Bruin:** two heavies, two counters that fight: the Windfall wants you still and quiet, the Bruin wants you round its sides (flanking is moving, and moving is what the Windfall feels).
- **The Windfall + the Close / Climbers:** a car without a door is open for the rest of the night.

## 8. Early versus late

- **Early:** none on Local; one on Frontier, only in deep forest, after 45 s stood.
- **Late:** after 20 s; two from either side; 10 blows on the split.

(the model is the cost), the cracking. Needs #711's forests to stand in.
