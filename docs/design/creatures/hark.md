# THE HARK — the thing in the wires that sings along

*Creature proposal, Sloe (queue #709, ARCHITECTURE §8 note 1004), 23 Oct 2026, for the director's rule "every creature's purpose is to foil some sort of behavior" (`docs/playtest/2026-10-23.md` line 36). **Status: a proposal for the director; nothing is built until the director approves it.** Numbers are a first pass in the roster's units.*

> **When the wires sing with you, hush.**

## 1. What it is

A bat-winged thing the size of a dog that hangs in the telegraph wires along the line and listens. Most of a crew it
ignores. The one who never stops talking, it tunes to: the wires start to hum at the pitch of their voice, following it, and
when it has the tune it drops on them. It hears only how loud and how long, never what's said (no voice is ever kept or
copied, note 907).

## 2. Roster entry (GDD §21, FLANK)

**THE HARK** · *in the telegraph wires*
The wires humming along with your voice.
> **RULE: when the wires sing with you, hush.**

**Sense:** sound (one crewmate's voice, long). **Zone:** flank (the wires; aboard or at a stop by the line). **Want:** Kill.
**Cost:** 1.

## 3. Silhouette and look

A hunched shape upside down on the wire, wings wrapped like a closed umbrella, two huge ears; the wire bowing under it.
Clips: `hang`, `tune` (ears turning to the talker), `drop`, `cling` (on the head and shoulders, wings over the face),
`shot`, `death`.

## 4. The tell (spec A.3/A.4)

- **The wires' song** (tier 1): the wire hum (the line's own bed, 300–600 Hz) rising and falling *with the talker's
  voice level* (the host's level, so everyone hears it follow the same person). Told from the Knotter's rope creak by
  following a voice, not the train's speed.
- **Seen:** a shape on the wires, turning to follow one player.

## 5. The rule (App. A.4 format)

```
TUNE      a crewmate never quiet (voice.loud: over 60% of the last 120 s talking) near the line (aboard, or within 30 m)
          → it tunes to them
          └ TELEGRAPH: the wires' song, following their voice (10 s)
          ├ they hush for 4 s → it loses the tune and has to start again
          ├ a gun round or a thrown thing into it on the wire → it falls and flaps off; 2 kill it
          └ else
DROP      on their head: CLING, wings over their face (they can't see; their voice goes into it, muffled) for 10 s, 5 a second
          └ a friend's blow (2), or the victim holding still and silent for 3 s → it lets go
PUNISH    10 s: dead
```

## 6. Aboard or afoot; what it counters

**Both, along the line.** It foils **never quiet**. The counter is turn-taking: hush, and let someone else call.

## 7. Compositions

- **The Hark + the Witness:** one punishes the loud, the other the quiet; talk in turns.
- **The Hark + the Choir:** the Choir already punishes a loud crew; the Hark picks the one who's loudest.
- **The Hark + the Passenger:** the Passenger wants everyone to speak, the Hark wants the talker to stop.

## 8. Early versus late

- **Early:** from Local's second night; 60% of 120 s; 10 s to tune.
- **Late:** 45%; 6 s; one on each side of the line.

## 10. Build cost

**Small.** The rolling talk share (shared with the Witness), a hanging rig on the line's wires (the poles are built), a
face cling (the Hugger's latch on the head). About 1 agent-day of sim and bots, 1 of art, the song is a filter on the wire
bed driven by one voice level.
