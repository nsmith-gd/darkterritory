    - **What a night earned.** 64 nights (`--as-app`, 4 bots, seeds 1–8 of local:3/:7 on 4 cars, frontier:3/:7 on 8, deadLines:3/:7 on 13, deepTerritory:3/:7 on 18), main 943e86f, net a delivered night: local +868, frontier +1,004, dead lines −450, deep territory −757. On dead lines and in deep territory a night lost money, so a raise alone couldn't help: the more a car-load paid, the more each death cost.
    - **First, the loading faults** (D1's (a)). The mine head's lift lever wasn't replicated, so the bots never wound it (note 638); nobody restarted a dead yard's powerhouse (note 639).     - **Fees by tier, not by the pay** (D1's (c)). A death's crew-loss fee (holdouts.json `crewLossFee`) and a creature's monster brigade fee (run.json `broughtIn.fee`) are halves of the tier's **fee value** (run.json `economy.feeValue`: 450 / 700 / 1,100 / 1,700, the old per-car values), so a death costs what it did. A body brought home refunds 75% of its fee, as before. The recovery fee for a stranded night (0.5 of the tier's pay) still follows the pay: a stranded night earns nothing, so it doesn't change what a delivered night nets, and the tow still costs half a car-load.

Local already nets that at 450, so it stays there. D1 proposed ÷ 1.7 first; at ÷ 1.7 the standard crew needs 63 runs to 20 cars, past F.4's 55–60, so it's ÷ 1.5.
