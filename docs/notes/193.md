193. **The train's, the world's and the places' cues in the game (GameAudio.Train, GameAudio.Outside).** Every checklist cue on the train, the line, the stops, the radio and the Holdouts is played from replicated state (out/audio/hooks-map.md), on its edge or held while its state lasts.
    - **The bed's recordings stand in for its synths, never on top of them.** `GameAudio.Synth` plays a synthesised bed sound (boiler-roar, chuff, wheel-rail, brake, wind, safety-valve, boiler-strain, vent-hiss, boiler-burst) only while none of the cues that replace it is installed, and stops it if one is installed while it plays; the slack's run-in and run-out each fall back to the synth's clunk. The exhaust beats four times a turn of 1.7 m wheels (chuff.json's figure), the rods once a turn, a joint under each axle every 12 m rail. While a recorded roll plays and the joint isn't installed, `rail-joint.json` (the synth roll's own click) keeps the click-clack.
    - **Levels.** As recorded, the wheels and the wind sat 20 dB over the synths they replace and buried every tell in `AudioTests`' benches. Their levels are `tools/audio/install.py`'s `CUE_GAIN_DB` (matched to the synths in the chaos and tells benches), and the wind's and the wheels' takes are lowpassed (`LAYER_EXTRAS`, 1.5 and 2.5 kHz) as the synths were, so the tells' bands stay clear.
    - **A derailment is its wreck (the director's note on state-derail).** Read off note 162's physics, the poses every machine has (note 233): each car's wheels off the rail as it starts to slide sideways, a hit where its velocity jumps (on the ground when the jump's more up and down than along, into another car otherwise), going over past 55°, steel on the rails while it slides on them and grinding through the ballast once it's off, a coupling torn as its cars pull apart past the slack, and settling as each comes to rest. Each is its own cue where it happens (state-derail's climb, impact, collide, tip, rail-scrape, grind, tear, settle); where one isn't installed, the synth crash and grind stand in (wreck-crash, wreck-grind).
    - **A Call Out** is note 179's `Holdout.Calls`, played as the count goes up: a call or a shout in the occupant's voice set, or a bout of banging. The eight sets are dealt by the night's seed and given out by player id, so no two players on a run share one; holdout-shout and holdout-bang are the synth stand-ins under them.
    - **Chosen here, not modelled:** which animals a livestock car carries (by its id), the night's far things, thunder and gusts (at random), and a weak bridge's groan (from cars over its limit, and speed over its crossing speed). Still unhookable for want of state: graded signal on your own set (it's live or dead: static plays while you key it where it's dead; a crewmate breaking up is VoiceChat's).
