266. **The director's pacing by pressure, and a grace picked per night (GDD App. B.1 "Pressure" and "Pacing rules", spec B.10; design decision, 2026-10).** The tuning had a 20 s grace where the GDD said 90 s, and a forced spawn whenever the line had been quiet `paceSeconds` (18 s), cooldown or not: the harness's nights got a send every 40 s or so from the gate to the end, flat (frontier:7's director spawns per five minutes: 8, 9, 7, 9, 3, 6), and the director's own per-second tally was 80% "cooldown". The user's decision: the grace is a range, and spawns are algorithmic, driven by escalating pressures.
    - **The grace:** `graceMinSeconds` 20 to `graceMaxSeconds` 90, drawn from the night's seed on its own PCG stream (the director's draws are untouched), the draw above the minimum × `pressure.graceTierScale` (1 Local, 0.85 Frontier, 0.7 Dead Lines, 0.55 Deep Territory). `Director.Grace`. A generated line's grace stretch now gives way at the night's grace (`lineGraceSeconds` 0: no extra). "Later in a campaign" isn't a separate input: the campaign's tier steps with the consist (`campaign.json` `tiers`), so the tier scale is it; the director isn't told the campaign night.
    - **The pressure** (`Director.Build`, once a second after the grace and outside a ban, the terrain's ceiling or the final approach; starts at 4):
      `rate = tier × conditions × relief × busy × escalation × (base + quiet + loud + cargo)`, banked to at most 18.
      escalation = 1 + 3 × progress (along the line or toward dawn, whichever is further); base 0.03 /s; quiet + 0.1 /s × min(1, seconds since a threat was confronting the crew or sent ÷ 90); loud + 0.1 /s × min(1.5, Choir meter ÷ its threshold); cargo + 0.01 /s per car-load (load × cargo integrity × value: comet 2, livestock 1.5, food and medicine 1.3, ammunition 1.2); tier 0.8/1/1.2/1.4; conditions 1 + 0.15 lamp out + 0.1 × weather cold + 0.05 per deep-cold step + 0.05 rain + 0.05 × wind; relief (alive ÷ crew)² × (1 − 0.25 × share of the living under 35 health); busy 1 ÷ (1 + 0.5 × confronting threats × (1 − progress)).
      At the threshold 10 the director spends on what its weights pick (budget curve, caps, gates, saving up, pairs, variety, wants, the dead's votes within want tags: all unchanged); each spawn takes 3 × its cost off and restarts the quiet. At 16 (`pressAt`) the 25–45 s cooldown gives way and the curve may be overdrawn by `pacedCost` (3), as the paced spawn did; `DirectorSpawn.Paced` now means that. Condition-triggered charges (the Stoker) relieve it too.
    - **Ambiguities, read:** "since the last threat engaged" is `Director.Confronting` (telegraph, commit, grab, punish), not the caps' `Engaged`: a Climber pacing a car nobody walked into held the quiet at zero and the night's pressure flat (the first measured round). The dead's votes stay out of the pressure: D.11 says the vote never changes pacing. The cooldown was 30–60 s; with the relief making the trough it is 25–45 s, and the GDD's text says so.
    - **Observable:** the harness report's `threats.pressure` (beside the per-second `threats.director` tally, which keeps its shape and gains "building"): the night's grace and threshold, the director's own spawns per five minutes out on the line, and a sample every 30 s (time, km, pressure, rate, escalation, quiet, loud, cargo, relief, conditions, what it held for). `dt harness` prints it with the rest of the JSON.
