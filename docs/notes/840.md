840. **No dark smudge round the hands and tools over the map (C1, queue #568; the director's playtest of 16 Oct, line 16).**
The director, playing the 16 Oct morning build: "When I'm looking at the map, there's a sort of like weird shader shadow thing around both my hands and any of the tools that I have" (a screenshot: a dark smudge down the map's middle round the held tool).

**What it is** (reproduced: `dt screenshot --view cabmap --own wrench` against `--own none`)
- It's the screen-space occlusion (`ssao.frag`). Your own hands and the tool in them are a few tenths of a metre from your eye, and the map they're over is the better part of a metre away.
- The occlusion counts anything nearer the eye than a sample as covering it. Its range check (`r / |Δz|`) only fades an occluder that's far in front of the surface: half a metre in front, at the map's distance, still counts about half. So a speckled dark halo grows round anything held up over a surface behind it, the size of the occlusion's radius.

**What changes**
- An occluder further in front of a surface than its radius no longer counts: it fades out between one and two radii (`ssao.frag`). Contact darkening, the dark where a thing meets what it stands on, is all within the radius, so it's as before. A halo round anything well in front of what's behind it is gone: your hands and tools over the map, a crewmate against a far wall.
