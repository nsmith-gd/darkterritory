654. **A most-a-night for every creature without its own (D1, queue #384; for the director's `D1-roster-piles-up`, note 652).**
Under the director's roster every kind is about only on some nights, and a night with few kinds about sends those few
again and again (note 652: 9 Draggers and 10 Whistlers on a Deep Territory night, the train dragged short of the
terminus at dawn). The director was asked whether to keep that, cap every kind at 3, or set their own caps. This builds
the second so that any of the three answers is a number on the Tweaker, and leaves it **off**:

- `director.perNightDefault` (null, no cap): the most a night of any kind `director.perNight` doesn't name.
  `DirectorTuning.CapFor` is a kind's own cap, else this; `Director.Tonight` holds every kind to it as it held the named
  ones (note 635), the house creatures and the Pickers counted as the director's own are (note 644).
- **On the Creature Tweaker**, under "The whole night": "Most a night, for every creature without its own", blank for no
  cap. Each creature's own "Most a night" shows the default as its placeholder (≤ 3), and the page's estimate uses it.
  `tools/tweaker/apply.py` writes it (`settings/night` `want.perNightDefault`; a null turns it off).
- `NightCallsTests.ADefaultCapHoldsEveryKindWithoutItsOwn`: off in the game; on, a kind not named stops at it, a house
  creature counted its own way too, and the Knotter's own 2 still wins over a default of 1.

The page's estimate doesn't show the pile-up itself (on Deep Territory it has 1.7 Draggers a night where 4.0 were
measured): it doesn't follow a night's few kinds sharing its picks. The measured column does, and "Measure my values"
plays the nights.
