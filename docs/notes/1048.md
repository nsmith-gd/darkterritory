   - **The item.** Note 1032's `dt balance` found the director spending well off enemies.json `director.wantShares` (kill 70 % against 40 %, cargo none against 15 %) on two nights, Local and Deep, crew of 4, 6 cars, and the same nights failing the pace checks.    - **Why, measured** (every charged spend and every menu the pressure chose from, traced over the 16 nights):
     1. **The terrain affinity zeroed every kind it didn't name** (a bug). Linegen plan §15.1's affinity (`linegen/tiers.json` `director.affinity`: under `climb` the Cinder Hounds ×1.5, under `near_facility` the Soot Children ×1.5, ...) was read through `GetValueOrDefault`, which gives 0 for a kind the tag doesn't list: under any tag every other kind weighed 0. With the whole menu at 0 the pick falls to its last option, regardless of the want weighting. On a climb that was the Hounds again and again.
     2. **What a night can buy for each want: the composition, not the weighting.** The nightChance roll (note 635) leaves most kinds out of a night (Local seed 1: 18 rolled out, every rule-free kind among them, and every cargo kind but the Fire Flies). In the before sweep, 149 of the pressure's 247 decisions had kill kinds only on the menu (a moving train's rules let in the Hounds and the Ribbits), 42 of them pressed. The √(target/share) weighting, clamped ×0.5–×2, can only move weight between wants that are on the menu; with one want on it, it does nothing. Note 868's floor brings kinds back so a night isn't empty, but by chance alone, whatever their wants.
     3. **The shares' reading** is a target per pick (the weighting above), with the share the running balance of what's been charged (the pressure's spends, its fill, the Stoker's and the insisted specials; not the free spawns or the stops' own, which are beside the budget). That reading is sound; it just had nothing to choose between.
   - **Fix** (director code and its tuning; no creature's numbers changed):
     - `PlanDirector.AffinityOf`: a kind a tag doesn't name keeps ×1 under it (`Director.Spend` uses it). `DirectorAffinityTests`.
     - `director.wantHold` (enemies.json; `WantHoldTuning`), on:
       - **floor** 2: each want with a share has at least two of its kinds about tonight, and (with fill on) one of its rule-less kinds (`curve.kinds`) if it has any, brought back from the night's chances by their chances on the roll's stream, after note 868's floor (whose draws stay as they were; `Director.BringBack` is its old loop). A chance of 0 is still never. `WantHoldTests`.
       - **fill** (from 6 spent): pressed (`curve.fillPressed`), with every kind the rules let in now of a want already over its share, the pressure spends on the curve's rule-less kinds of the wants under theirs instead, charged and paced as the curve's fill. None open: the rules' menu as it was. Cargo has no rule-less kind, so it comes by its rules (the floor makes sure some are about).
