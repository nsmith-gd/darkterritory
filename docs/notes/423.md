423. **The tipple (A1, queue #159; spec D.2: "Clamp the car, rotate it to load. 1 crew. Bad clamp derails the car on the spur"; D.3: "tipple fails through precision — a sloppy clamp costs you").** The last of D.2's modules never built (note 400's conveyor was the other). It's at the mine head (facilities.json `kinds`: `["lift", "tipple", "winch", "crates"]`, D.1's most, four).
    - **Where:** a cradle on the spur a car's pitch (`behindLift` 15.5) behind the steam lift's chute, so a car under the chute and the one behind it in the cradle could load at once. Without a lift it's laid from the buffer stop as the spout is (`back` 84). The ore bin stands `binLateral` (7) out on the site's side, its chute `binHeight` (6) up; the lever is `leverAlong` (3.5) on from the cradle and `leverLateral` (3.6) out. All three clear the crates, the winch and the lift's lever.
    - **How it works** (`Run.Tipple`):
        - A cargo car whose middle is within `tolerance` (1.2) of the cradle is in it. With the car standing and room in it, someone holding Use at the lever for `clampSeconds` (1.5) clamps it. Letting go first starts the clamp over.
        - Within `goodClamp` (0.6) of the cradle's middle the clamp is good; further off, it's bad.
        - Held on, the cradle rolls the car over toward the bin over `rollSeconds` (6); let go, it holds where it is. At the top the chute tips `perRoll` (0.5) of a car-load in, out of `ore` (3). Then it rolls back by itself over `backSeconds` (3) and lets go. So a car takes two rolls.
        - A bad clamp gives `badAt` (0.3) of the way over: the car comes off its rails in the cradle, spills `spill` (0.5) of a car-load and loses `derailDamage` (0.2) of its integrity. The train moving with a car clamped (0.05 m/s, or the car pulled out of the cradle) does the same at once.
        - The roll is machinery, so it's loud (`Run.Machinery`).
    - **A car off its rails** (`Vehicle.OffRails`) is new train state:
        - It holds its whole rake fast: `TrainOnLine.OffRailsDrag` (1000 m/s²) joins the track's drag, more than any engine pulls or any grade drives. The cars behind and the engine can't move until it's back on.
        - Someone holding Use within `rerailReach` (2.5) of it, with the wrench in hand (the repair tool since note 301; the repair kit where train.json `repair.wrench` is off, as at the burst boiler), puts it back on in `rerailSeconds` (20). Two at it take half the time, and the work keeps through a let-go, as at any break.
        - It's in the vehicle record (index 27, before the car's fire) and in `VehicleState`, so a predicting client re-simulates it exactly. Protocol 42; the run record's sites carry the bin's ore, the clamped car and whether the clamp's good, the roll, rolling back, and how far through clamping and re-railing someone is.
    - **Readings:**
        - "Bad clamp" is a car clamped off its mark, the precision D.3 names. The driver has to stand the car true; the lever hand reads how far off it is before clamping (the HUD says), and can call the driver to move it.
        - A bad clamp always derails once rolled; there's no unclamping. The only way out of a clamp is through the roll, so the precision is all before the clamp.
        - The row said "put back on with the repair kit". Note 301 made the wrench the repair tool for every break, so it's the wrench here too, and the kit only where the wrench isn't the tool.
    - **Bots:**
