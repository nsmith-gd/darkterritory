999. **Pressure by state and by player: the director reads whether the train runs or stands, what each crewmate is doing, and sends its threats at someone for a reason (queue #703; the director's playtest of 23 Oct, lines 27–29 and 35; Rook, the Token Budget Manager's worker, nathaniel2).**
   - **The ask,** in the director's words (`docs/playtest/2026-10-23.md` line 35): "There's basically two gameplay states with
     substates attached to them ... stopped ... and presumably players are outside of the train ... running and players are
     presumably on the train. We really should be trying to mount pressure based on whether a player is on or off the train.
     And we should be targeting players specifically. We should be tracking things that they're doing during gameplay so
     that we are understanding how to apply pressure to them as countermeasures to the things that they are doing. And the
     frequency at which that pressure is applied is what builds the difficulty curve." With lines 27–29: "much more numerous
     now, but ... less lethal"; "anytime we stop, monsters should be coming out of the woods, coming out of town"; "more
     threats that can show up when I'm on the train"; "a few monsters per POI" early, "six or seven at a time" later.
   - **What it builds on** (nathaniel1's overhaul, note 874; nothing here rewrites it): #606's rate λ (`EncounterPacing`,
     note 877) and #608's one clock (`EncounterClock`, note 908) say *how often*; #609's selection and placement
     (`EncounterPlacement`, note 895) and the orchestrator's census (`Census`, note 345: each crewmate's `Post`, slack and
     the threats on them; note 753's commitment) say *what and where*; #611's `Concurrency` (note 906) bounds *how many at
     once*; #600/#610's `EncounterLog` and severity (notes 880, 893) *measure* it; #595's stop counts (notes 860, 868) and
     the halt (note 895) are *the stops' own*; #646's dread (note 944, off) is *time afoot*. The gap the director names is
     that none of these knows the two states as states, and the census aims at a *post* (where someone stands), not at what
     they're *doing*.

   **The plan.**

   - **1. The states** (`Enemies/Stance.cs`, pure, from the train and the run, once a second on the host):

     | State | Substate | Read from |
     |---|---|---|
     | **Running** | `atSpeed` | moving, not slowing |
     | | `slowing` | moving under `stance.slowBelow` (m/s) and slower than a second ago: a crew braking for something |
     | | `throughFacility` | moving while the run is `AtFacility` (shunting a yard, creeping a spur) |
     | **Stopped** | `atStop` | stood (`stance.standBelow`) at a facility or a stop (`Director.StopAt` < 1000, or `RunPhase.AtFacility`) |
     | | `halt` | stood on the main line over an abandoned village (`StopAt` ≥ 1000) |
     | | `wilderness` | stood anywhere else: a bend, a block, a breakdown, a spur thrown |

     The forts, the grace, the final stretch and the line's bans stay what they are (held quiets, note 908): a stance never
     opens one.

   - **2. Per player** (`Enemies/Doings.cs`, pure). Where (the census's `Post`: cab, gunner, walker on roof/landing/gap,
     rider inside, ground, left behind; the car is `PlayerState.Parent`) and, new, **what they're doing**, from state the
     Sim already has, first match wins:

     | Doing | Read from |
     |---|---|
     | `driving` | in the cab, the train moving (`orchestrator.drivingAbove`) |
     | `firing` | in the cab with a shovelful (`PlayerFlags.Shovelful`) |
     | `gunning` | seated at a gun (`Seated`) |
     | `repairing` | a timed Use job under way (`ActionProgress` > 0: grease, a coupling, a breach, the wrench) or pumping |
     | `operating` | at a crane or a stop's module (`Operating`, `Site.Works`) |
     | `carrying` | a heavy load, a body, freight (`Heavy`, `Carrying`, `SoloCarry`) |
     | `looting` | afoot inside an open house (`StopWalls.HouseAt`) |
     | `sprinting` | afoot faster than `doings.sprintAbove` (m/s; the run is 5.5) |
     | `hiding` | inside a car with its doors shut (the room, `DoorsOpen` 0) |
     | `walking` | out on the train: a roof, a landing, a gap (`Post.Walker`) |
     | `riding` | inside a car otherwise |
     | `afield` | on the ground otherwise |
     | `idling` | not a doing of its own: the census's slack (seconds with nothing to answer) weighs whatever they're doing up |

     The encounter telemetry already keeps where each encounter came (`engine`/`car`/`roof`/`trackside`/`afield`), whether
     the crew were afoot, and what it did to them; slice 1 adds who it was sent at and why (`Director.Targeted`, by doing).

     **For Moth (#706) and Sloe (#709):** the four gaps in bold, plus "something at speed for someone hiding in a shut car"
     if Moth judges the Passenger too rare to count, and the foils for §2b's behaviours below. Each proposal against §20's
     social test and App. F.1, as G1's creatures are; the director, 23 Oct: "every creature's purpose is to foil some sort
     of behavior", always "with a clear tell and a clear counter". **This table and §2b's are the one source** Moth, Sloe

   - **2b. Behaviours to foil** (the director, 23 Oct, line 36, relayed by the Token Budget Manager: "I'm going looting by
     myself. I'm going into the basement by myself. I'm running away from all of my friends ... sitting on the back gun alone
     for too long ... hanging out on some of the fun cosmetic stuff ... Players who don't particularly communicate on mic too
     much. Some players who never shut up ... griefing too much ... min-maxing too much ... track some things about player
     behavior during the game in a performant way ... build foils for that ... pushing players for adaptability or towards
     teamwork"). Each a signal the host reads from what the Sim already has, once a director second (1 Hz, beside the census,
     never per frame on a client), deterministic (players by id, no draws), host-only, read only by the director's choice of
     which kind and at whom. The cost is per director second, C the living crew (at most 8 to 16):

     | Behaviour | Signal (built from) | Cost / s | Existing foil | Foil wanted (Sloe) |
     |---|---|---|---|---|
     | Looting alone | doing `looting` and `CrewSense.Alone` (nobody within the voice's 8 m) | O(C²), 64 distance checks at 8 | Gaunt, Followers | something that takes the lone looter in a house and needs a second to pull them free |
     | Into the basement alone | the looter's open search is a `ContainerKind.Cellar` (`Run/Search`), alone | O(C) | none director-sent (the house creatures are the world's) | a cellar foil: the tell a sound below, the counter a friend at the hatch |
     | Running from friends, dispersing | each one's distance to the nearest crewmate (the same O(C²)), and the crew's spread; afoot further than `afoot.fromTrainM` | O(C²) | Followers, Soot Children, Moose | a hunter of the straggler, its counter regrouping |
     | Sitting on the back gun alone too long | doing `gunning` held N s (a per-player seconds-in-doing counter) and alone | O(C) | Climbers, Hounds, Gannet (they come at the gun) | something that comes from behind the gunner, the counter a second at their back |
     | Hanging out on the cosmetics (the BBQ deck, the hammock, the hot tub, the band) | `PlayerFlags.Lounging`, `Playing`, and standing on a dress-up's deck, held N s ("if you spend too much time up there, something should come and get you") | O(C) | the Gannet (the roofs) | a deck foil with a long tell: get up and it's gone |
     | Silent on the mic | the intent's `Voice` byte (App. C.7: their microphone's loudness, already sent to the host as intent, already heard by the Gaunt), seconds under a floor | O(1) a tick per player (a byte added) | the Gaunt (it listens for silence) | — |
     | Never stops talking | the same byte, seconds over a ceiling | O(1) a tick | the Whistler, Soot Children (they answer voices) | a mimic that uses the talker's own noise |
     | Griefing | the horseplay's mark (`World.IsMarked`, note 717: the shover marked), shoves given, a crewmate hurt by them | O(C) | the mark's pressure (`horseplay.marked`) | a foil that turns on the griefer, its counter the crew covering them |
     | Min-maxing | the share of the night in one doing (a crewmate who only guns, only drives), stops skipped (`StopsStood` against the line's stops) | O(C) | the census's slack and empty posts (note 345) | foils of the safe routine: the post that's always held gets the threat |

     Voice is intent, not state: the host reads it as it reads keys, and it changes no player or train state, so a client's
     prediction never needs it (the rule on intent holds). A bot's `Voice` is what it sets. Nothing here is per frame: the
     O(C²) is shared with the census's own distances. Slice 2 adds a `Behaviours` reader (one pass, a `SortedDictionary` by
     player) and a `director.encounters.target.behaviours` table (behaviour → pull and kinds), as `answers` is.

   - **4. Targeting** (`Enemies/Targeting.cs`, pure, the host's; no draws). Each second the director scores each living
     crewmate: `pull[doing]` × (1 + slack / `slackScale`) × `afootStopped` if they're afoot while the train stands ×
     `aboardRunning` if they're aboard while it runs; nobody who already has `orchestrator.perPlayer` threats on them.
     The highest is the target, ties to the lowest id (deterministic: players by id, no dictionary order). The reason is
     their doing (and "idle N s" past `slackPress`). The target's answers weigh their kinds ×`answerWeight` and everything
     else ×`offWeight`, in every channel's choice (the pressure's spend, the free channel, the halt, the stops' own), after
     the census's `WhoseNext` and #609's zone spread. Only *which kind*: never when, never how many, never a rule bent. The
     same draw picks as before; with the flag off, every weight is 1. Placement near the target (#609's `Pick` weighted
     toward the target's car) is slice 2.

   - **5. Frequency is the difficulty curve.** λ already rises with progress, crew, cars and tier (note 877). Slice 1 adds a
     factor by state, `stance.rate.<substate>`, on λ in `Director.Pace` (so every channel shares it, note 908), and a
     **stood ramp**: stopped, the factor grows from 1 to its value over `stance.rampSeconds` of standing (so a crew that
     dawdles at a stop or a bend meets more, as the brief §1 A asks; #646's dread does the same afoot). By tier: λ's own
     `tier` factor, and the stops' counts by tier (#595: "a few monsters per POI" early). **By the campaign's progress:**
     the Sim has no campaign night index today (the route's tier is the campaign's step); a `campaign` factor is the
     director's call below.
