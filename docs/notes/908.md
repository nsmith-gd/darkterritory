908. **One pacing model in the director: the spawn director overhaul's #608 (C3) (the director's brief, `docs/design/spawn-director-overhaul.md` §2 issues 1 and 2, §3; the plan, note 874; n1-C2, 20 Oct 2026).**
   - **The model:** `src/DarkTerritory.Sim/Enemies/EncounterClock.cs`, a pure `readonly record struct EncounterClock(Progress, Due,
     Threshold, Debt)` the director keeps and steps once a second after its choice (`Director.Pace`).
     - **Owed:** each second the night isn't held for a quiet it means, `Due` gains λ/60 (#606's `EncounterPacing.Rate` at
       the night's progress, the crew alive, the cars, the tier).
     - **Intended quiet:** the grace, a fort, the final stretch and the line's bans, the aftermath, a facility's lull, the
       terrain at its ceiling, the engaged caps full. It gains nothing, and #607's debt release is held (`Director.Held(why,
       DebtCause)`).
     - **Paid:** every encounter placed, by any channel (the pressure's spend and fill, the stops' own, a free spawn, the
       Stoker and the specials, through #603's `Commit`), pays one off, a pack as one. So the channels share one account, and
       none adds to another's rate.
     - **The free channel:** it sends when `Due` passes a threshold drawn 1 ± `jitter` at each payment (its own stream,
       0xD21E), not on the curve's roll. That roll is still drawn so the curve's dice run as before, but no longer read. Its
       spacing, the caps and each kind's night still hold.
     - **Debt:** `Due` is capped at `dueCap`. What λ would add past it, the director wanting an encounter and finding none it
       could place, goes to #607's debt as `Ineligible` (nothing fitting) or `NoPlacement` (a free attempt that found
       nowhere). The debt comes back into the room under the cap at its release rate, never past the engaged caps' headroom.
       `dueFloor` keeps a stop's several residents from buying minutes of silence.
     - **The pressure's own rate:** with `drive.pressureByLambda`, the crew and the train scale the night once, in λ.
       The pressure, the director's answer to what the crew does, is the same for every crew. That replaces
       `pressure.crewScale/lengthScale` about a four-crew, eight-car train (issue 1: a solo crew's was a third of a four's)
       and avoids scaling twice (issue 2).
     - **Issue 2:** with `drive.on`, the free spawns' crew fade is no longer read. The crew now raises their rate, through λ.
     - **A full cap:** counts as an intended quiet only while something holding it threatens or engages the crew (#604's
       `Threats.Counts`, `Director.CapQuiet`). While it's full of what is dormant or only about, the night still owes, and what
       it can't place goes to the debt.
   - **Tuning:** `director.encounters.drive` (`on`, `dueCap` 1.5, `dueFloor` −1, `jitter` 0.35, `pressureScale`). `on: false` is
     the director as #603 left it, for comparison.
