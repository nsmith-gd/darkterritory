888. **`dt director sweep`: the spawn director overhaul's matrix of nights, and the old director's baseline (the director's brief §8; note 874's #601 A2; n1-A2, nathaniel1).**
   - **The command:** `dt director sweep` runs `dt harness --as-app ... --enemies --upkeep` nights (hosted as the app hosts
     them, as the trends' nights are) side by side, one a cell, and watches each for its encounters.
     - `--quick`: note 874's quick sweep. frontier:7, crews of 1, 2, 4 and 8 on 3, 5, 8 and 12 cars (the brief's §7
       sessions), seeds 1–4: 16 nights, about 10 minutes on 4 cores. Every item that changes the nights runs it.
     - `--routes local,frontier,deadLines,deepTerritory` (a tier alone: the route of the night's own seed, as `dt balance`
       does; `frontier:7`: that route every seed), `--crews 1,2,4,8 --cars 2,4,8,12,18` (every crew on every train) or
       `--cells 1x3,2x5` (those), `--seeds 1-10`, `--seconds 2700`, `--parallel n`, `--group 20`.
     - `--out f.json` writes the report: `groups` (one per route, crew and train, the means) and `nights` (every night's
       row). `--compare base.json` prints its groups against an earlier report's, before → after. The table goes to
       stderr, the JSON to stdout.
   - **What it measures, and how** (`src/DarkTerritory.Dev/Encounters/EncounterWatch.cs`): the watch reads every enemy every
     tick through the harness's `Observe` hook and never writes. A watched night plays exactly as an unwatched one
     (`DirectorSweepTests`). Facts only (§8): an enemy *comes on* when its spine reaches Telegraph, Commit, Grab or Punish.
     That is the sim's own state, and the watch never claims a player saw it.
     - **Encounters** (distinct, not bodies, §7): the first enemy of a kind to come on, plus every other of that kind coming
       on within `--group` seconds (20) of it, is one encounter (a pack, a swarm).
     - **Hazards and incidents** (a car fire, the Drift, Sleepers) are *danger*, never encounters. `longestDangerDrought`
       counts them as §6's sustained trouble; `longestDrought` doesn't.
     - **Droughts:** stretches after the director's grace with no encounter on, the one running at the night's end included.
       Also `droughtsOver90` (§7's 90 s).
     - **Overlap:** encounters on at once, sampled each second: the night's most (`maxOverlap`), and seconds with two or
       more (`overlapSeconds`).
     - **`cappedIdleSeconds`:** seconds the director's engaged cap (`Director.Engaged`) counted something while no
       encounter was on. This is §2 issue 4 measured: bodies holding the cap that threaten nobody.
     - From the harness report: `sent` (the director's sends and those come their own way, by kind), `heldBack` (seconds by
       why the director held back: at the cap, building, grace ...), end, delivered, minutes, km, deaths, `cargoKept` (the
       cargo cars' mean integrity), cars delivered and lost, pack fires, first threat after grace, encounters in each half.
     - **Placement success** isn't measurable on today's main: a failed pressure or free spawn is logged as sent (note 874,
       issue 3). #600's encounter block will carry attempts and failures. Once a harness report has one (`threats.encounters`
       or `encounters`), each row copies it whole into `telemetry`, so the sweep reports both side by side with no change
       here.
