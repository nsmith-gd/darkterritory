1014. **Failing cars holed for real, worse each stage: boards knocked out and roof sheets gone, cut through the car's own body (Sable, queue #717; the Look Review's verdict of 23 Oct on `cap-derail`, "change"; the Art Checklist's `derail`, `failing-stages`, `car-eaten` and `boiler-rupture`).**

The director, on round `r-failing-779` (note 779's stages): "This feels like just textures, I think failing cabs should have
holes and bits of board missing and progressively getting worse to show the failure." Quill's note 1012 (PR #1712) listed
it. Note 779's failing look was all overlays: sprung boards with a black panel drawn behind them, a peeled sheet over a
crate texture, the wrecked state's breaches (black discs), and under it the scar mask's dark blotches. From the lineside at
night every one of them read as a dark patch on the plate.

## What changed

- **Holes cut through the body** (`DamageKit.CarHoles`, `DamageKit.Holes.cs`). A car's holes are gaps in its side walls, a
  board's height each (0.22 m), and roof sheets gone off an eave. `TrainKit.Car` builds its walls and roof without them
  (`DamageKit.Pieces`, `Subtract`, `RoofSpans`, `RoofProfile`), so the car is open there. Through a hole you see the dark
  inside, the load and the far wall, and the broken ends are raw:
    - each gap's end ragged, a long splinter and teeth sticking into the hole (`Splinters`);
    - the split edge of the board above and below, a ragged pale strip;
    - the cut faces of the wall pale split wood, or plate scraped bright on a steel car (`Raw`);
    - round a roof gap, the sheets' torn edges bright, a tab bent up at each end (`RoofEdges`).
- **Worse each stage** (`HoleLevel`): holding 0, failing 1, breaking 2, come apart 3 (`MostHoles`).
    - Twelve wall sites, chosen whatever the level, so they never move. Four open failing (two a side, so either side of
      the line sees them), four more breaking, four more come apart.
    - A site is a ragged hole four to six boards high, or (two in five) a board or two gone along much of a panel.
    - It grows from its middle row out as the car gets worse, each row's gap longer at both ends.
    - Each level holds every bit of the last's gaps: getting worse never closes a hole.
    - Four roof sites, two breaking (one a side) and two come apart, each three sheets, going further in toward the walk.
    - Open area on a cargo car: about 1 to 4 m² failing, rising to about 6 to 20 m² come apart (`OpenArea`).
    - The sites keep off the side doors (whose leaves slide over the wall), the ends (gangways), the hatch, and the roof
      walk (never in past 0.45 m of the centreline).
- **The overlays tied to the holes** (`DamageKit.Failing`):
    - a car's sprung boards now hang off the gaps they left, nailed at one end, with no black behind (the hole's there);
      failing, each site's middle board; breaking, the board two down too;
    - breaking's peeled sheet is hinged on a real hole (`Holes.Peel`, the first breaking site), with no crate panel drawn;
    - with holes cut, `DamageKit.Car`'s wrecked breaches are left out (`open`): no black discs. The flaps and gouges stay.
- **The derail:**
    - a car come apart on the line is at level 3;
    - so are the wreck yard's heaps (`SceneArt.Wreckage`), with their wrecked damage `open`.
- **The eaten car** (`car-eaten`): a car being eaten gets the worse of its failing level and its bite's.
    - The bite's level is look.json's `damage.eatenHoles` (0.15, 0.35, 0.6 of the bite's fraction, `BiteTuning.Fraction`).
    - Its boards are knocked out ahead of the mouth while the shader's frontier (note 109) eats the rest.
    - Only three bodies a car, not one per 12 cm bite.
- **The boiler rupture** (`boiler-rupture`): the hood's plate is cut away round `RuptureSeam` (`DamageKit.HoodHole`, an oval
  of five rows inside the tear's petals), threaded through `TrainKit.Engine` to the cab's `CabWall` boxes.
    - The tear drawn over it (`TrainKit.RuptureTear(open: true)`, piece `rupture-tear-open`) has no black disc; its soot is
      only a ring round the hole.
    - So the steam comes out of an opening into the corridor's dark.
    - An engine without a hood keeps the old black tear on its boiler.
- **The scene** (`SceneArt.Car`):
    - the body piece is keyed by its level and car (`:holes{level}:{seed}`), and the ruptured engine's by `:burst`;
    - `GreyboxScene` passes `ruptured`;
    - all of it is read off the replicated integrity, eaten and rupture, so every machine draws the same holes and nothing's
      sent.
- **Determinism:**
    - the sites come from `new Random(7300 + seed * 29)`, the car's id;
    - the splinters' raggedness comes from a hash of where they are;
    - no clock, and presentation only. The Sim's collision is unchanged: the walls and roof are still whole to the motor.
