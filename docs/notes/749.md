749. **Radios for everyone, on right-click (S1, queue #479; the director, 14 Oct, on the audit).**
"make the radios part of the default kit ... Hold down right click to use the radio since communication is so important"; "A grab should allow players to still use the radio but their voice should be muffle filtered as if the grab is covering their mouth partially". And, asked about inventory and the key clash: "Radio is part of uniform and doesnt take inventory space, swap input conflict there so right click is radio call. Throw is so rarely used."

   - **What it was:** two loose sets (`kit.radios` 2), one on the cab floor and one in the guard van, picked up onto the belt; push-to-talk on T, Throw on the right mouse button; a grab smashed the victim's set 25% of the time (`radioBreakOnGrab` 0.25), undoing App. C.8's broadcast of a GRAB.
   - **Now:**
     - **A radio each, part of the uniform** (train.json `kit.radioEach` true; `KitTuning.RadioEach`, `World.IssueRadios`):
       - Once the train's stocked, the host puts a radio on the belt of every living crewmate without one, in crew order: the night's first tick, a joiner, one back from a Holdout, a bot. No loose sets are placed (`Stock`).
       - It never comes off: Throw with empty hands no longer sets it down, the dead don't drop it (`Bodies.Handle`, `DropOut`). It takes no slot (as before: a worn radio was never a slot or in the hands). The dead's is taken off them, so they come back with a working one.
       - A broken one is mended on the belt by its wearer, wrench in hand (note 301), as before. Nobody else can mend it for them (it can't be handed over); left as is.
       - Not saved in the autosave's things aboard (`World.Aboard`): a resumed crew are given theirs again.
       - Off: the two loose sets, picked up and passed on, as before.
     - **Right mouse is the radio** (`Controls.Defaults`): Radio `MouseRight`, Throw `T` (the radio's old key). The HUD's prompts say Throw as `[T]` and `[RMB]` now names the radio (`Hud.Bound`). The hotbar's radio slot shows its key short (`Controls.ShortKeyLabel`: RMB), beside the slot where it's longer than a letter (in its corner it ran over the picture; over it, into the tool's name). Saved settings keep only what differs from the defaults, so a player who never rebound gets the swap; one who'd bound another control to T now shares T with Throw until they rebind (not migrated).
     - **A grab never breaks the set:** `radioBreakOnGrab` 0.25 → 0. Damage breakage unchanged (`radioBreakPerDamage` 0.006).
     - **A hand over the mouth** (player.json `voices.covered { on: true, gainDb: -6 }`; `CoveredVoiceTuning`; `VoiceChat`):
       - Each listener's client, from the held flag the snapshots already carry (no wire change): a held speaker's voice in the air and on the radio is 6 dB down and through a 1.4 kHz low-pass, the sounds' own (`voice.json`, `voice-radio.json`: a `lowPass` on the new `covered` parameter, parked at 20 kHz otherwise). Muffled, never cut; the GRAB's keyed-open radio (C.8) stays.
       - A creature's own effect wins, alone: a frame the host marks Muffled (Tippy Toesie) or Fading (a Soot Child) keeps its sound, without the hand on top.
       - Off: a held voice as it was.
   - **GDD first:** §12 "Every crewmate wears a radio"; App. C.8 (the grab never breaks the set; "A hand over the mouth"); §23's "Radio breaks" row.
   - **Checked:**
     - `RadioTests` (Sim, 12, 5 new): every living crewmate wears one from the first tick, a joiner too, the dead none, no seconds; it takes no slot and Throw doesn't take it off; the dead lose theirs and come back with a working one; a grab never breaks it; off, the loose sets lie as before. The loose-set tests run with `Tuning.LooseRadios` (radio each off), as do `HazardTests`' (the grab-break test at its old 0.25), `UpgradeTests`' and `LockerTests`'.
     - `VoiceTests.AHeldSpeakerIsMuffledUnderAHandInTheAirAndOnTheRadioNotCut` (Game, `VoiceBench` with `held`): every frame heard; the air 6.8 dB down and its 2–6 kHz 14.5 dB down; the radio 7 dB down and its 1.8–3 kHz 10.6 dB down.
     - Key prompts: `BoundKeysTests`, `HudTests`, `DeadPhaseTests` (the dead card still fits with Throw rebound to the right mouse button), `HoldLatchTests`.
