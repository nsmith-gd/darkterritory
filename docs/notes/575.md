575. **The train departs empty (D1.5, queue #305; the director, 9 Oct 2026, playtest: "It also doesnt make sense that if I skip everything and just drive the train to town, I get scrip and a payout? the train cars should be totally empty and we are the ones who have to fill them"; GDD §1 *The fantasy*, note 577).**
    - **Why a do-nothing night paid.** `run.json` `departureLoad` was 0.5 (notes 28, 36): every cargo car left the fortress half full of the contract's freight, and `Run.Tally` pays `perCar × Σ Load × CargoIntegrity × rate` on whatever is attached at the gates. A frontier 6-car train driven straight home grossed 5 × 0.5 × 700 = 1,750. Nothing else paid without work: there's no base pay, distance pay or per-car fee (spec F.1: "delivered cargo that survives. Nothing else").
    - **Now.** `departureLoad` 0. Every car leaves empty (`Consist.Uniform`, in `NetPlaySession`, `PrototypeSession`, the harness, `FacilityWork`), so the night's gross is only the loads the crew put aboard at the stops (crates, sleds, castings, the chute, skip, conveyor, tipple, herd and hose), the village finds stowed (`Scavenged`), the children brought home and the mail. The costs are unchanged: coal burned, rounds fired, repairs (1 − integrity, attached cars), crew-loss fees, the stranded tow. Driven straight home, the night's net is minus its coal and repairs, out of the scrip (`Campaign.Settle`; scrip can go below nothing).
    - **The spec's tables stay true.** F.1's net-by-consist table is the fully loaded train (the ceiling, as `run.json` always said); `Campaign.NetFor`/`Simulate` model that and never read `departureLoad`. No SpecTableTests number moves.
    - **Contracts.** A contract named "the freight the train leaves the fortress carrying" (note 182: `Consist.Carrying` tagged every loaded car with it), so on an empty train it has nothing to tag. Until the director says what a contract asks of the crew (the Director's Inbox, `D1.5-contracts`), an ordinary contract still names a freight and what a car of it pays, and the freight's modifiers (B.8) apply only to cars a stop has loaded with it (`Run.StepSites` retags a car with what loaded it, as before). **The comet contract is off** (`campaign.json` `contracts.comet` false): no stop loads comet material, so it would be a contract nobody could carry. Its rules stand (`CampaignTests` turns it on to test them).
    - **Stops have the stock.** A stop is stocked for `stops.json` `empties` car-loads × `stock` (1.3–1.6): local 2 → 2.6–3.2, frontier 4 → 5.2–6.4, enough to fill an empty 6-car train's five cargo cars at one stop. Stop generation and its measure (level-design D.1) are unchanged; only the comments say what `empties` means now.
    - **The bots** never assumed a load aboard: the stop crew loads the cars with room (`StopCrew.WithRoom`). Harness nights below show what they bring home.
    - **What else moves.** The director's pressure from cargo aboard (`Director.cs`) starts at nothing and builds as the cars fill; the Gaunt's pick of a car by its worth weighs only what's been loaded. The departure radio's "Freight:" line has nothing to say (the manifest is off anyway, note 267).
