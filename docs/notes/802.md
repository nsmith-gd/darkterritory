802. **A drop off the crane's hook is a gag (K1, queue #529; the director, 15 Oct 2026, on note 726).**
The director, on hoisting a friend (note 726, merged in #1046): "If we're hoisting a player and dropping them lets make it so the player can only lose 10 health at most from a drop and then scale that based on height. It's a silly gag not a big grief."

   - **What it was:** a rider who let go of the hook (Jump), or was shaken off it (the operator's Fire, CREW MISCHIEF on), fell by the world's fall rules (`PlayerMotor.Land`, player.json `landing.fallHurtFrom` 5.5 / `fallLethalFrom` 7.5, note 573).
     - From the top of the foundry's 8 m gantry a rider's feet are 6.35 m up, so a let-go knocked about 48 of 100.
     - A taller gantry killed (`DeathCause.Dropped`, the operator's death in the report).
   - **GDD §18 first** ("A friend on the hook": the drop is a gag, not a grief), and friendslop.md's #460 row.
   - **Now** (`PlayerFlags.OffTheHook`, `PlayerMotor.Land`, `World.LetGo`, player.json `landing.offTheHook`):
     - **The knock.** A landing on the ground after coming off the hook (let go or shaken off, lifted or not) knocks by the height fallen alone, measured by the speed it lands at as note 573's fall is:
       - nothing from `from` (1.0 m) or less, a step down off a low hook;
       - rising evenly to `maxKnock` (10) at `fullAt` (6.0 m), so the foundry's top (6.35 m) is the full 10;
       - never more, from any gantry or at any speed (the hook's own run isn't counted);
       - never a death, and never below 1 health (a rider on 5 lands on 1).
       - Note 573's fall table and spec B.3's speed table don't apply to it. A landing on a car's roof is still no knock (the motor's rule for any drop onto the train).
     - **Predicted.** The landing is the motor's, which a client re-simulates, so the motor has to know the player came off a hook. `PlayerFlags` was full (`Hoisted` took the ushort's last bit), so it's widened to a `uint` (main's train upgrades, protocol 74, did the same for `Lounging` and `Playing`, bits 16 and 17), and the new flag `OffTheHook` (262144) is its bit 18.
       - The host sets it in `World.LetGo`, a host move that already bumps `Placed`, so a client adopts it with the rest.
       - The motor clears it on any footing (with `OffTheGround` and `Shoved`), and `Land` clears it as it lands them.
       - It's cleared where `Shoved` is, when someone is hauled up (`Rescue`'s hand-up), carried, or on a hook again.
     - **The wire.** `WorldRecords` writes the flags as a `long` (`(long)s.Flags`) and reads them back with `(PlayerFlags)f[15]`, as signed varints, so nothing truncates at 16 bits. The Game's audio keeps a `PlayerFlags` copy (no cast). A recording (`.dtrec`, note 515) holds what the host's transport heard and a digest of what it sent, never a flags field of its own. Nothing hashes the flags apart from the records.
     - **The report.** A hurt landing after a lift is still the lifter's faint line ("Dropped from the crane by Dave, 6 m up."; "Shaken off the hook by Dave, 6 m up."). `World.StepHoist` no longer turns a `Fell` into `Dropped`. `DeathCause.Dropped` is kept, as it's on the wire, with the HUD's and the report's text for it, but nothing sets it now.
     - `facilities.json crane.hoist`'s comment points at `landing.offTheHook`. Its numbers are as they were.
