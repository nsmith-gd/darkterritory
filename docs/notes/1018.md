1018. **Ladders taken by jumping or walking into them (Juniper, nathaniel2; queue #721; the director's playtest of 24 Oct: "ladders feel hard to use, if I jump into a ladder I should automatically grab it, if I walk into a ladder with that intention I should automatically grab it").** *In review.*
   - **Why they were hard** (`PlayerMotor`, after `UpdateSupport`): without the ladder key or Use, a ladder was taken only by walking forward into its foot, **on the ground**, from within 0.45 m (`WalkInReach`, a const in code; `ladder.grabRange` is 0.7). In the air nothing took one: a jump into a ladder hit the car and fell.
   - **Built** (Sim only, all intent; deterministic, `DMath` for the angles; no wire or state change). One rule, `PlayerMotor.Meant`, for every ladder: the train's and the world's (`WorldLadder`: the towns' and the walls'). The bots go the same way.
     - **The walk-in**: facing the ladder and pushing at it (the stick's push, strafe and forward, turned by the facing), both within `ladder.walkInDegrees` (45) of straight up its rungs, from `ladder.walkInReach` (0.6 m, out of code into `player.json`). On the ground or off it. Walking past one alongside, you face along it and push along it, so it's never taken.
     - **The jump-in** (`ladder.airGrab`): in the air, moving at the ladder (relative to it, so a moving car's) at `ladder.airTowards` (0.5 m/s) or more, up against it (`ladder.airReach` 0.45 m: a body's radius and the ladder's 0.1 m off the car), under `grabMaxRelativeSpeed` as ever, takes hold of it at the height you meet it. Letting go pushes you off at 1.5 m/s, away, so a jump off is never taken back.
     - **Not taken**: in its top half metre (a gap's jump lands on the roof); from its top (walking off a roof's end, eyes down at the plate, steps you down onto the plate as note 717 has it; the end ladder there goes past the plate to the ballast, and taking it caught the crate hands going down to shut a door: `StopCrewTests`). The ladder key still takes a ladder from its top. Nor in the air over the train's own footing (off a roof's end onto the plate, falling past the next car's end ladder). An indoor hatch ladder still only looking up it (note 704), walked or jumped into.
   - **Not changed**: the ladder key and Use + forward (`grabRange`); letting go (jump, or Use and back).
   - Tests: `PlayerMotorTests` (`JumpingIntoALadderTakesHoldOfItWhereYouMeetIt`, `WalkingIntoALadderTakesHoldOfItFromAFairReach`, `WalkingPastALadderAlongsideItDoesNotTakeIt`, `WalkingOffARoofsEndOntoThePlateDoesNotTakeTheEndLadder`, `RunningAlongTheRoofToJumpTheGapDoesNotTakeTheEndLadders`). `WalkingAlongTheRoofPastALaddersTopDoesNotGrabIt` now stops short of the roof's end (walked off it there, beside the plate, you can fall into car 3's end ladder, which takes you: falling into a ladder).
