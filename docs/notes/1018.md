1018. **Ladders taken by jumping or walking into them (Juniper, nathaniel2; queue #721; the director's playtest of 24 Oct: "ladders feel hard to use, if I jump into a ladder I should automatically grab it, if I walk into a ladder with that intention I should automatically grab it").** *In review.*
   - **Why they were hard** (`PlayerMotor`, after `UpdateSupport`): without the ladder key or Use, a ladder was taken only by walking forward into its foot, **on the ground**, from within 0.45 m (`WalkInReach`, a const in code; `ladder.grabRange` is 0.7). In the air nothing took one: a jump into a ladder hit the car and fell. From a roof, nothing but the ladder key took the end ladder down.
   - **Built** (Sim only, all intent; deterministic, `DMath` for the angles; no wire change: one new `PlayerFlags` bit in the state that's already sent):
     - **The walk-in** (`Meant`): facing the ladder and pushing at it (the stick's push, strafe and forward, turned by the facing), both within `ladder.walkInDegrees` (45) of straight up its rungs, from `ladder.walkInReach` (0.6 m, out of code into `player.json`). On the ground or off it. Walking past one alongside, you face along it and push along it, so it's never taken.
     - **The jump-in** (`ladder.airGrab`): in the air, moving at the ladder (relative to it, so a moving car's) at `ladder.airTowards` (0.5 m/s) or more, within `grabRange` and under `grabMaxRelativeSpeed` as ever, takes hold of it at the height you meet it. Not in its top half metre: there you land on what it serves (a roof gap's jump still lands on the roof). Letting go pushes you off it at 1.5 m/s, away, so a jump off is never taken back. Walking off a roof's end and falling against the next car's end ladder takes that ladder: falling into a ladder.
     - **From the top**: on your feet on what it serves (a roof's end ladder, a hatch from the roof, a world ladder's deck), walking out over it, facing out and eyes `ladder.topLookDownDegrees` (30) or more down it, takes it. Then held forward goes on **down** (`PlayerFlags.DownLadder`) until forward is let go; after that it's up, as on any ladder. Eyes ahead (running the roof to jump the gap) never takes it.
     - Note 704's look-up rule holds for indoor hatch ladders, walked or jumped into. The train's ladders and the world's (`WorldLadder`: the towns' and the walls') go through the same `Meant`. The bots use the same path; none of their tests moved.
   - **Not changed**: the ladder key and Use + forward reach (`grabRange`); letting go (jump, or Use and back).
   - Tests: `PlayerMotorTests` (`JumpingIntoALadderTakesHoldOfItWhereYouMeetIt`, `WalkingIntoALadderTakesHoldOfItFromAFairReach`, `WalkingPastALadderAlongsideItDoesNotTakeIt`, `WalkingOutOverAnEndLadderLookingDownItTakesItDown`, `RunningAlongTheRoofToJumpTheGapEyesAheadDoesNotTakeTheEndLadder`). `WalkingAlongTheRoofPastALaddersTopDoesNotGrabIt` now stops short of the roof's end: walked off it, the fall now catches car 3's ladder.
