The other four are here, each a `dt` command printing JSON and exiting 1 on a finding.
    - **Combination fairness (§34 "every pair and triple in the roster against every hazard set. Flag unwinnable or trivially solved"; `dt balance --pairs|--triples`, `balance.json combinations`):**

      - **Verdicts** (`Combinations.Judge`): *unwinnable*, lost every seed (derailed, stranded, wiped out, or half the crew dead); *trivial*, everything came on and nothing landed in any seed (no grab, punish or death, under 2 % of the cargo gone); *fair*. Not judged: *unplaced* (a spawn that found nowhere) and *dormant* (put there, never came on).
      - **Reading:** "every hazard set" by default is each combination against one set, the sets in turn, so every combination runs and every set is met in minutes. `--every-hazard` is the whole cross (the nightly's). `--triples` runs a seeded `tripleSample` (136) of the 680, `--sample 0` all of them.

A stop's night costs several times a line's, so that's the nightly's.
    - **Cascade audit (§34 "no failure chain is unrecoverable"; `dt audit cascades`, `CascadeAudit`, `balance.json cascades`):** §23's table, each chain scripted into a night (`HarnessOptions.Script`, before the host steps, ended early by `Until`), and the crew left to answer it: six bots over the loopback, through intent like any player.

    - **Degraded comms (§34 "agents on lossy, laggy, restricted voice — simulating people shouting over each other"; `dt harness --comms poor|awful`, `balance.json comms`):** `CrewCalls` (the bots' shared board of who's where with which part) can go over a `CrewVoice`. Each call or repeat is lost with chance `loss`, heard `latencySeconds` (± `jitterSeconds`) late, and a new call is talked over with chance `talkOver` for each other crewmate who started one in the last `talkOverSeconds`. Older news arriving after newer is dropped as stale.
      - It's seeded from the night's seed and drawn in the bots' fixed order, so it's deterministic. The report's `voice` says what got through.
      - **Reading:** claims (a door to shut, a Holdout to breach) stay instant: they settle who goes where, as much by sight as by word, and they answer on the spot. A lost call is lost to the speaker too: the board is everyone's ears.
