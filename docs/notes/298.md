298. **The yard's fun: emotes and outfits (F1, UI/UX 3; queue #36).** GDD §9, the director's decision of 6 Oct: until the gate the yard is a safe space where "the crew wait for friends, hang out, dance, try on outfits". Nobody could do either: there were no emotes, and a crewmate's look (cap or helmet, scarf, colour) was fixed by their player id.
    - **Emotes.** Hold EMOTE (J, rebindable) and the wheel is round the crosshair in the prompts' fine print: DANCE up, WAVE left, POINT right (`EmoteWheel`). While it's held the mouse leans toward one instead of looking; let go, and that one is sent. A tap without a lean sends the last again. It's intent like any key: two bits on the intent's hotbar byte, sent once. The host turns it into an `EmoteEvent` (who, which, when) and sends it as a `RecordKind.Emote` record, kept for its length (player.json `emotes`: the dance 8 s, the wave and the point 2.5 s). A new one replaces the last, but not within the 0.5 s cooldown. The crew draw it while the emoter stands with nothing else to do (`CrewActs.EmoteOf`); walking ends it. Nothing in the sim reads an emote, so it moves no player or train state, and works in a night as well as the yard (`EmoteAndOutfitTests.AnEmoteMovesNothing`).
    - **The poses are placeholders.** crew_clips has no dance, wave or point, and the clips are art's. Until they come (`CrewPose.Dance`/`Wave`/`Point` play clips of those names the moment crew_clips has them), `CreatureArt.EmoteArms` poses the arms by IK over a clip: the dance steps on the spot (the walk) with the hips swaying and both fists pumped in turn, the wave is a hand up high rocking side to side, and the point is an arm straight out ahead. `dt screenshot --view crew --act dance,wave,point`.
    - **Outfits.** OUTFIT in the settings picks one of the crew's eight looks (look.json `crewColours`, named in `crewColourNames`, each with the cap or helmet and scarf that go with it), or THE CREW'S PICK (your id's, as before). It goes in your Hello. The host keeps everyone's in `World.Outfits` and sends them to the crew (`MessageType.Outfits`) as it does names. In the yard the in-night menu (note 292) has OUTFIT too: changed there, it's tried on at once (`MessageType.Wear`), and the crew see it. Past the gate the host keeps what you wore out; a change then is saved for the next night. A Passenger wearing your face wears your outfit.
    - **Also fixed on the way:** the settings' PLAYER NAME only reached the Hello from the second night on (it was set after the first was joined). The name and the outfit are now set before a night starts.
    - **Protocol 33** (31 is #201's, 32 #202's): the intent's emote bits, `RecordKind.Emote`, the Hello's outfit byte, `Outfits` and `Wear`.
    - **Not yet:** no mirror in the yard to see yourself (the crew see you); bots don't emote; the dance's clip is art's (asked on C1's and E1's PRs).
