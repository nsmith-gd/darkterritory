578. **A bend taken too fast throws the train to its outside (D1.4, queue #310; the director, 9 Oct 2026, playtest: "The derailment physics didn't follow where the inertia would have thrown the train on the bend, we took a bend too fast and it derailed inwards").**
    - **The cause.** `World.Derail` handed `Wreck.Begin` the side the first car off is thrown (note 330's kick, +1 to its right) as `k > 0 ? -1 : 1`. A positive curvature curves the line left (`TrackSegment.Radius`), whose outside is its right: every overspeed derail threw the engine off the inside of its bend, and the train, tangent momentum and all, ended up there (WreckTests: the engine 5 m inside the rails it left, on a 300 m bend at 22 m/s, either hand). The curvature was also read on the main line at the engine's front, whatever path the train was on and even with the engine already past the bend onto the straight.
    - **Now** `World.OffTheBend`: the sharpest bend under any car of the train, on the train's own path; its outside (the line's right for a left-hand curve) taken into the first car's own frame. Off any bend (a washout, a bridge, a dead line's end) it's 0 and the wreck picks a side as before.
    - **Verified:** `WreckTests.ABendTakenTooFastThrowsTheTrainToItsOutside` (left and right bends: the engine more than 3 m outside the curve after 3 s, the train outside on average, and on the outside of where it came off); fails both ways without it. The film (App. E) starts from the wreck as it began, so it shows the same.
