738. **Small finds lying on the floors of the abandoned houses (B4, queue #472; `docs/playtest/2026-10-14.md` lines 15 and 21, the director on frontier:7, 14 Oct; after note 326).**
   - The director's words: line 15, "I feel like I never find anything in some of these abandoned buildings. We're going to want to reward players a little bit more for that type of exploration. Not too much more, but smaller bits of loot"; line 21, "I feel like I'm always looking for cupboards. I don't actually ever find loot like on the floor or anything. It's not really rewarding me for being attentive ... Cupboards that definitely need a redesign, by the way".
   - **What lies on the floors.** A new kind of place a find is kept, `ContainerKind.Loose`: a small thing (a candle, a twist of treats, a tin, a can of lamp oil: loot.json `kinds.loose`) lying on a house's floor in a corner of a room. It isn't searched (loot.json `search` has no `loose`), so it's seen as soon as the room is lit and picked up at once, like a shut house's find on its step.
   - **Where.** Every open village house, plain or composite (an L, a cross, a pair, a long house's two rooms), at stops.json `village.loose.chance` (0.5) one thing; a village with an open house has one at least (`StopGenerator.PlaceLoose`). In a plain house, the corners away from the door first (`StopWalls.InsideLocal`, `LooseCorners`); in a composite, near the end of a wall run with no door, close in to the wall (`KeptComposite`). The art's candle stub takes a corner clear of them, as of every find.
   - **From their own seeds.** The containers are laid from a seed of their own (`StopSeed.Loose`), before the yard's power and crate stacks (which stay the last containers laid, `YardLootTests`), and their worth comes from a stream of its own: every other container, find, toy and kit is where and what it was. The note 593 spread of kept finds (`AGoodShareOfAVillagesHousesHaveSomethingInThem`) counts the kept ones only.
   - **Worth.** Each a share of what a find kept in that village is (loot.json `looseShare`, 0.2 to 0.4 of the village budget over its kept finds), on top of the budget, never more than a kept find: smaller bits of loot. Measured: frontier:7's two villages, 14 of 35 houses with something lying (+6% to the villages' pay); frontier:1 9 of 12 (+13%); deadLines:7 16 of 29 (+21%); local:3 8 of 10 (+11%).
   - **Tests.** `OpenHouseTests.SmallThingsLieOnTheFloorsAndAreTakenAtOnce` (frontier:7, deadLines:7): every village has something lying, inside its house's walls and a metre and more from every way in, not searched for, worth no more than a kept find; `StopGeneratorTests.FindsHoldOnlyWhatTheirKindAllowsAndShareTheBudget` holds the kept finds to the budget and the floors' to their share.
