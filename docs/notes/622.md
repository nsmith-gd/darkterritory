622. **`dt trends` hosts its nights the app's way (D1.5, queue #352, at D1's ask after note 616: "the trend lines measure what the director plays").** The trends (note 523) measured `dt harness` nights. Those started 400 m short of the gate in old commits and now at the gate, with the crew walked aboard or put at their posts, on a lossy link (90 ms, ±20, 3%), the director on the harness's seed, and no finds at the stops. The app hosts a night otherwise (note 616's `HostedAsApp`): the train at the gate, the crew boarded cab first, the perfect lock-step link, the loadout's tunings, the upkeep and the finds.
    - **`dt harness --as-app`** (needs `--route`). The night's options come from `HostedAsApp.For`, a `SessionSetup` of the route, the cars and `--enemies`. The upkeep and the finds are on as in the app (`--no-upkeep`, `--no-loot` to leave them out, as `dt campaign play` has them). `--trace`, `--no-combat` and `--no-boiler` work as before. HostedAsApp itself is unchanged.
    - **The link** (D1.4, on the seam: "the app hosts over UDP loopback, not the perfect lock-step link"). `--as-app` keeps HostedAsApp's perfect link: a trends night has to play the same each time it's measured, and the bisect compares commits by it, while the sockets' timing is the machine's. `--udp` puts the night on localhost sockets, as the app hosts it. What the perfect link can't show is what only a lagging view does (D1.4's lamp key relighting a lamp, note 619).
    - **The seed.** The app seeds the director by the route (one night a route). A trends line is eight nights on one route, so `--seed` seeds the director as well as the bots (as `dt campaign play --as-harness seed` does): eight seeds are eight nights.
    - **The trends' nights** (`TrendNights`) add `--as-app`. The line's nights key changes with it, so the series starts again: `trends show` compares only lines measured on the same nights, and the bands wait for their history as on a new route. `tools/trends/bisect.sh` refuses a good end whose harness has no `--as-app` (an older harness ignores an unknown flag and would quietly measure the harness's own night), and skips any commit in the range without it.
    - **What the switch moves** (frontier:7, 4 bots, 10 cars, 2,700 s, seeds 1–4, main 2e7ca51): km 8.39 → 8.40, deaths a night 0.75 → 0, cars lost 0.5 → 0, crew home 3.5 → 4, cargo 1.40 → 1.50, net +747 → +1,015, the Fire Flies fires 1 → 0, max correction 0.25 → 0.02 m. Most likely the link: on the perfect one the bots' prediction barely parts from the host's (the corrections), and the deaths and the fire were all on the lossy one.
