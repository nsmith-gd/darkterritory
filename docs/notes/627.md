627. **A guttering lamp nobody reaches (D1.4, queue #357, note 626's find, at D1's ask: "a dark car invites the Climbers, so it's worth a walker's minute").** In note 626's sixteen nights, 3 of 16 guttering lamps went out with no hand in the car (seeds 4, 5 and 15). A guttering lamp is now an errand the nearest free walker takes within its 45 s (`upkeep.json` `outAfter`). It comes behind the hot box and the pins, and ahead of idle patrol.
    - **Why no hand went (traced):** none of the three was the errand order inside `GutterCar`. Each time, the hand who knew about the lamp was held by something else.
        - **The wrong hand.** Each walker chose for itself by position whether it was the one to go. On seeds 4 and 5 that picked a gunner who was nearer but busy on the gun, so nobody went.
        - **A stand holding the walkers.** Seeds 4 and 5 both guttered with the train standing for a Knotter. The walkers held their stand posts: `Heed.Knotter`, and the Brakeman's idle wait, `HeedBrakeman`, which ran with the Brakeman still dormant. That intent drowned the errand.
        - **No way in.** With the train moving and car 1 last, `WarmUp.Plan` finds no way into it. Car 1 has no rear door of its own, and nothing is behind it to come through.
    - **The fix (the bots):**
        - **`CrewCalls.ClaimGutter`, one hand per lamp.** Each walker that could go puts in its distance. The nearest one holding the claim keeps it, and a claim not renewed for a second lapses. This is `ClaimPin`'s pattern.
        - **Who can go** (`RoofWalkerBot.LampRun`, also on `GunnerBot`):
            - alive, not held, not too hurt (`TooHurt`) and not in the cab;
            - and the lamp is the walker's errand in `Look`: no trouble of its own, no pin and no car to mend. Trouble a crewmate already covers doesn't count.
        - **What the errand overrides:**
            - **A stand's posts.** On the lamp run, `Heed.Mourners`, `Beetle`, `TowerJaw` and `Knotter` are skipped. So is the Brakeman's watch, unless the Brakeman is awake.
            - **A stop part riding a car.** With the train moving, the hand stays aboard and walks to the lamp (the warm-up's way, through the cars).
            - **A standing train.** The hand goes in by the car's side door from the ground (`StopHand.IntoTrouble`), and stays put once inside.
            - The shunter keeps his post.
        - The hot box, a coupling's pins, the six creatures, a rescue and the Fire Flies still come first.
