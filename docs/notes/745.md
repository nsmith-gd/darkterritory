745. **The whimsies, third round: the yard cat, the dog and the tennis-ball launcher (U1, queue #447; the director, 14 Oct 2026: "keep going with fun batches and whimsies"; docs/design/upgrades.md §4, §7.4, §8.2). Notes 715, 744.**
   - **Pets as bodies** (`BodyKind.Cat`, `.Dog`): stocked aboard (the cat in the cab, the dog in the guard van) and walked by the host (`World.StepPets`, after the bodies) in their car's frame (`Bodies.PlaceIn`; `Bodies.Reparent` leaves them alone). What each is doing (`PetMood`: idle, walk, sleep, warn, fetch) rides the body record's payload as the caboose's `Tipped` does, so every client draws and hears the same. Their wander is hashed from the tick and the body's id: the same on every run of the night.
   - **The yard cat** (`dress:yardCat`): wanders the car it's in at `catSpeed`, stopping `wander` s (4–10) between. While the fire's lit it goes forward car by car to the cab and sleeps on the backhead. A creature aboard its car or within `catWarnCars` (1) of it and it stops, faces it and hisses (`PetMood.Warn`): a tell. It can be picked up (THE CAT). The clerk: "Cat: aboard."
   - **The dog** (`dress:dog`): wanders the guard van at `dogSpeed`. It barks at a creature aboard anywhere on the train, or in the open within `dogHears` (40) m and coming (Telegraph or Commit): the tell for what's behind. A toy lying loose in its car with a crewmate there, it fetches it (within `fetch` m it's in its jaws) to whoever's nearest and drops it within `drop` m of them. It can't be picked up.
   - **The tennis-ball launcher** (`dress:tennisBalls`; `CarShape.WithLauncher`, `InteractableKind.Launcher`): on the guard van's rear platform. A Use press there (FIRE A BALL) fires a `BodyKind.TennisBall` off the back and up the line behind at `launchSpeed` (14) m/s, at most every `launchEvery` (2) s. A Cinder Hound running the train down (Telegraph or Commit, not a runner, not ahead or on the flank) sees a ball on the ground within `ballSeen` (70) m and goes for it instead (`CinderHound.Fetch`), while the train runs on. Within `ballTake` m it's in its jaws; it stands worrying it `ballWorry` (4) s while the train runs on, and then comes on after the train again. One that catches the train up drops it on the platform before it leaps: they bring it back. A hound shot with the ball drops it where it was.
   - **Seen and heard:** the cat (a ginger tom: sat up, walking, curled asleep, arched and hissing, its green eyes catching the light) and the dog (a black-and-white sheepdog: sat, walking, lying down, braced and barking) in `WhimsyKit`; the launcher (a sprung tube on a post clamped to the platform's rail, pointed up and back over the line, a bucket of balls beside it). The cat's hiss and meow and the dog's bark are made in `BandSynth` (`GameAudio.Pets`), at the body, while it warns.
