475. **Interior lighting that works (B4, queue #211; the director, 8 Oct: "I feel like interior lighting can be improved a lot, it feels almost impossible to see anything. We don't need it to be super bright but we do need interior lighting to be functional."; GDD §28 "inside = warm, human, temporary safety", §31).** Inside a Room (a car, an open house, an open barn or shed, a yard's shed: notes 326, 462, 465) the shader kept the moon out and filled with the night's ambient (0.1) times a warm tint, about 0.055, so any corner a lamp didn't reach was black. A house's candle reached 5.5 m with a steep falloff, the hand lamp 7, and the barns and yard sheds had no light. A frame inside an open house averaged about 5 out of 255.
    - **A room's own fill** (`look.json` `atmosphere.interiors.fill`, `FrameLighting.IndoorFill`, `FrameData.Indoor`, `scene.frag`): the indoor term is its own colour now, not the moon's ambient, at [0.16, 0.12, 0.08]. Unset, it's the old ambient times the tint (the greybox's, the tests' defaults).
    - **The house lights and the hand lamp** (`interiors.candle`, `candleRange`, `lamp`, `lampRange`, `handLamp`, `handLampRange`): a candle 2.0 over 9 m, an oil lamp 2.0 over 10, the hand lamp 2.2 over 10 (were 0.9 over 5.5, 0.8 over 6.5, 1.8 over 7). The Gaunt's house still has none: its dark is the tell (and its barn or shed, since note 488).
    - **A lantern in the barns and sheds** (`interiors.shedLantern` 1.1, `shedLanternRange` 10, `shedLanternHeight` 3.2, `shedLanternSpacing` 10): a hurricane lantern turned low hangs in each open barn or shed and along each roofed length of a yard shed, one each 10 m, 3.2 m over the floor (under the eaves at most), with a small halo. Note 462's "no light of its own" is overturned by the director's ask.
    - **The house floors were moonlit** (`HouseInteriors`): a house's Room began at its boards, and the shader fades a room out over its last 15 cm, so the floor counted as outside and took the moon's blue. Every Room now starts 0.3 m under its floor.
