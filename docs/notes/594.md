594. **Loot carried Lethal Company's way: four shared slots (D1.5, queue #326; the director, 9 Oct, playtest: "more akin to like the lethal company style inventory where it's not about holding weapons ... when I go out there and I find loot, I'm able to kind of hold on to it so I can grab a few pieces of loot at a time"; the director, the inbox, 12 Oct, `D1.5-loot-slots`: "Lethal Company's four shared slots").** The design, put on the claim PR before the build; the HUD's seam agreed there with B3 and F1.
    - **Now.** Two systems that shut each other out. Tools are an 8-slot hotbar (`Kit`, `PlayerState.Kit`/`HeldSlot`). Everything else picked up (a find, a toy, a lamp, a crate, a body) is one physics body in the hands (`Body.Carrier`, `Bodies.Handle`), and while it's held the hotbar is dimmed and no tool is used. One find at a time: a village house with three finds is three walks back to the train.
    - **The four slots** (`Kit.Slots`, 4: the number keys and the wire are built round it, so it's the game's shape, not a tuning number). A slot holds a tool or a hand-sized thing; the one selected is in hand, the others are on you (pockets, belt, satchel: not drawn).
        - Hand-sized is what a crew locker holds (train.json `kit.lockers.holds`: lamp, radio, toy, loot, extinguisher, repair kit): what fits a locker fits a pocket. The radio keeps its own belt and takes no slot (note 173's rule stays).
        - Use on a hand-sized thing in reach puts it in the slot in hand if that's empty, else the first empty slot, and takes that slot in hand (Lethal Company's). All four full: it isn't taken ("HANDS FULL"), and it stays where it lies. With a find in hand off the train, Use on another takes it into a free slot and puts the one in hand away (gathering in a village); aboard, Use puts the one in hand down, as it always has (that's where finds are unloaded, and the one put down a moment before is still lying in reach: a take there would pick it straight up again).
        - A tool takes a slot like anything else: the crowbar the night starts with (T108), a wrench or shovel from its rack, a dead crewmate's tools (`Bodies.TakeTools`) go into empty slots, and none is taken with all four full. Everyone starts with the crowbar and the wrench (player.json `kit`, note 301), so two slots for finds; carrying the shovel too leaves one: that's the choice the director's "not about holding weapons" asks for.
        - Number keys 1–4 and the wheel select as now (`Kit.Select`, `Cycle`); a slot's find comes out in the hand, the one put away goes back on you.
    - **What slows you is what's in your hands** (GDD v1.4 App. C.4, note 181: a find in hand, as a crate, is 2.8 m/s and no ladders). One put away in a slot doesn't: Lethal Company's "grab a few pieces of loot at a time". The director's call if finds in slots should weigh too.
    - **Two-handed things stay as now.** A crate, freight, a Heavy (two lifters), a body, a child: held in both arms, the slots locked (no select, no tool) until it's put down; picked up as now, a find in hand put away into its slot first. Nothing two-handed goes in a slot.
    - **Searching** a hiding spot (note 326) is with no find in hand, as it was empty-handed: a number key for a tool or an empty slot first (Use with a find in hand would take what's in reach, or put it down).
    - **Putting down.** Use with a find in hand and nothing to take in reach puts it down where you face (as now, `Release`); Throw throws it (as now). At a locker, a tap stows what's in hand into it and, empty-handed, takes its thing into the slot in hand (as now, note 173).
    - **Paying.** Unchanged: a find pays when it lies still in a car or a locker aboard (loot.json `settleSeconds`, `RunLoot.StepLoot`). A find in a slot is not home: it has to be put down aboard, and it pays nothing if its carrier dies with it. The Gaunt and the loot-seekers rank a crewmate carrying finds by what they carry (GDD App. D's "valued at its refund", B.14).
    - **Dying.** Everything in the slots falls where the crewmate dies, finds and tools alike, as the body in arms does now; the ragdoll keeps the tools (`Body.Tools`) as now and the finds lie beside it.
    - **The sim and the wire.** A find in a slot stays a body (`Carrier` its holder, a new `Pocket` = its slot; hidden from physics and the world while it isn't the one in hand), so lockers, payout, death and the loot-seekers keep working on bodies. `Kit` keeps the tools (slots 4, a byte each). A slot is empty when neither has it. The body record carries `Pocket` (protocol bump). Deterministic: no new state outside `PlayerState` and the bodies.
    - **Bots.** A village hand (`StopHand.Village`) fills its free slots from the houses before it walks back, and puts each down in a car (its crowbar keeps one slot). A crate hand puts its finds away first (it's two-handed).
    - **The HUD's seam (B3, F1).** The Sim gives `Kit.Slot(state, bodies, i)`: the tool, or the body kind and its find's name. The hotbar (`Hud.Hands.Hotbar`) draws four slots and a find's picture and name in one; "HANDS FULL" is a prompt in note 285's form. Built at the minimum (D1, 12 Oct: "don't wait on them for the merge"): the hotbar always draws its four slots, a thing in one as a sack and its name over the bar on a change of hands, and "TAKE …" / "HANDS FULL" prompts. The look is B3's and F1's to restyle. `dt screenshot --hud --slots` draws it.
    - **Verified.** `SlotTests` (finds into the empty slots, the number keys, full slots, aboard a find put down, a death dropping them, a tool taken up past a find); `StopCrewTests.AHandOutInTheVillageFillsItsSlotsBeforeItWalksBack` (a hand carries two finds at once and none is left in a slot as the train goes); the whole suite.
    - **Overlaps.** D1.4's `Bodies.Handle` (#313) is the base; the lockers (note 173); the Gaunt's loot ranking (`GauntCarry`); VR hands (`VrHandsTests`: a find in hand is the same body in the hand); the replay format (`RecordReplayTests`).
