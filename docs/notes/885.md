885. **The spawn transaction: a spawn the director chose commits nothing until it's placed; failures kept with their reason; a pack is one encounter (the spawn director overhaul's B1, queue #603; n1-B1, nathaniel1).**
   - The brief: [docs/design/spawn-director-overhaul.md](../design/spawn-director-overhaul.md) §2 issue 3; the plan, note 874 (its shared `SpawnAttempt` contract).
   - **Verified on main (7a828ad):** `World` called `d.Placed(rule.Spawn(...))` after `Director.Decide`. `Decide` had already charged the pressure's spend (`Charge`: budget, pressure relief, the quiet's counters, the first threat, pairs, the want shares, the log line), drawn and started its cooldown, and, for a free spawn, written its log line and reset the spacing. `Placed(false)` took back only a stop's own (note 868). A pressure spend or free spawn that placed nothing was charged, logged, counted toward the kind's cap (`SentTonight` reads the log) and cooled down. 20 of the 22 rules have a way to place nothing: no lit car, no gap, no crew on the ground, no crane, no freight, no warren near.
   - **The contract** (`Enemies/SpawnAttempt.cs`):
     - `SpawnRule.Spawn` returns a `SpawnOutcome`: placed, with the ids of every member (`SpawnContext.Add` records them; a rule ends `c.Placed()`), or failed, with a `SpawnFailure` (`NoRule`, `Gated`, `NoTarget`, `NoSpot`, `NoGap`, `NoSite`; each `return false` mapped by what it tested).
     - `Director.Choose` returns a `SpawnAttempt` (kind, `SpawnChannel` Pressure / Free / Stop / Special, tick, distance, the engaged counts as they stood, and `Free`, `Paced`, `Cooldown`, `Stop`). It accounts nothing. Every draw the choice needs is drawn then, the pressure's cooldown included, so the night's dice run in the same order placed or not.
     - `Director.Commit(world, attempt, outcome, active)` is the one commit point. Placed: the pressure's and a special's charge (`Charge`), the pressure's cooldown, the free spawn's and the stop's quiet reset and log line, the stop's slot. Failed: none of it. `Director.Attempts` keeps every attempt as it went (`SpawnAttemptResult`: tick, kind, channel, failure, members, distance) for #600's `EncounterLog`; `HeldBecause` reads "failed (NoSpot)".
     - The world: `Choose` → the kind's rule → `Commit`. The Stoker and an insisted kind are `Special` attempts. The hound run and the truss Draggers place directly and can't fail; unchanged. `Director.Decide` stays for tests and tools (choose, then commit as placed: `SpawnOutcome.Assumed`). `Director.Placed` is gone: note 868's take-back is the transaction's.
     - A pack is one encounter: one `DirectorSpawn` with `Members` = N, one toward `SentTonight`.
   - **Retrying** (`director.encounters.attempts`; `EncounterTuning` is a partial record so #606's and #607's blocks go in files of their own):
     - The pressure keeps what it would have spent and its cooldown isn't started, so it chooses again the next second.
     - The kind that failed sits out `failHoldSeconds` (10) of the pressure's, the curve's and the stops' choices: something else is chosen rather than the same kind into the same wall every second.
     - A free spawn that fired and failed is owed for `freeRetrySeconds` (20): the next seconds fire on it without a new roll. The roll is still made each second, so the curve's stream doesn't move.
     - A stop's own that failed keeps its slot (note 868's behaviour).
     - Debt across longer gaps is #607's; this only stops a failure from eating the opportunity.
   - **Tests:** `SpawnTransactionTests` (a failed pressure spend takes no pressure, budget, cooldown or log line, and the kind sits out; a pack is one line of N; a failed free spawn stays owed; a failed stop keeps its slot; the dice run the same placed or failed). `StopsOwnTests`' take-back test goes through `Choose`/`Commit`. The other tests only gained `.Placed` on `rule.Spawn(...)`; nothing weakened.
   - **Measured** (`dt harness --route frontier:7 --enemies --seconds 1200 --seed 1|2`, main 7a828ad against this branch; 1 bot on 3 cars, 2 on 5, 4 on 8, 8 on 12):
     - 6 of the 8 nights are the same tick for tick (sent, spawned, kinds, beats, quiets, km, deaths): no attempt failed on them, and the choice draws as it did.
     - 2 bots seed 2: main's director reported "sent" for 26 seconds and spawned 17 creatures. Most of those seconds were a stop's Ribbits chosen again every second, with no warren near the crew, and taken back. Now: 3 seconds "failed (NoSite)", 8 sent, 16 spawned.
     - 8 bots seed 2: main 147 seconds "sent", 55 spawned, 1 death. Now 12 seconds "failed (NoSite)", 55 sent, **63 spawned**, 167 beats against 143, the longest quiet 107 s against 111, 0 deaths. With the failed kind sitting out, the stop and the pressure send something that places.
     - Not seen in these 8 nights: a pressure or free spawn failing (most rules' gates are checked again by their placement). The tests pin those paths.
