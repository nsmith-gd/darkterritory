1027. **The buyer in the square: finds sold in the town itself (n1-S2, nathaniel1; queue #657; GDD §3.1 "Not yet": "turning loot into scrip in the town itself"; the audit, note 949, rank 10 and question 3).**
   - **Before.** Buying and upgrading happened at the town's blacksmith (note 697), but a find turned into scrip only in the night's tally (stowed aboard or on a locker's shelf as the train came home, notes 649, 659, 718), and a joiner had no way to trade at all.
   - **The director's call is open** (the Director's Inbox, `nathaniel3-gdd-town-buyer`: a buyer in the square, or keep the night's tally). Under the standing orders ("keep working on the rest of the GDD that's missing") this builds the buyer behind a flag, towns.json `buyer.enabled`, on by default.
   - **Built** (the sale is the host's and deterministic; players act through their Use intent; no protocol bump):
     - **The buyer** (`TownGenerator`, role `buyer`; world/towns.json `roles.buyer`, its bynames in world/townsfolk.json, its lean in tuning/townsfolk.json): a townsperson in front of a stall beside the forge, along the train as it stands in the yard (towns.json `buyer.fromGate` -26, `out` 8.5, `stand` 2.2), facing the line. Every town has one, the departure's and the arrival's alike (the same plan). They come after everyone else, and say their lines after everyone else, and aren't named in anyone else's lines, so nobody else's name, place, number or lines changed (`BuyerTests.EverybodyElseIsWhoTheyWereBeforeTheBuyerCameAndOffThereIsNone`). Their stall comes after the forge, last of the fixtures. They never leave it on the rounds (`TownRounds`).
     - **The stall** (`SquareKit.BuyerStall`, fixture `buyerstall`): an awning stall as the night market's are, brass scales on its counter, a dark board on two irons across its front lettered **BUYER** over **FINDS BOUGHT** in lit white, the lantern over it (`WorldArt.Towns`). Its board read close to: world/towns.json `buyerStall`. `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --town buyer` (from the line) and `--town buyerfront`.
     - **Selling** (`World.SellAct`, `Run.Sell`, `World.BuyerOffer`): a find in hand (a `Loot` body the run knows as a find), its holder alive within `buyer.reach` (2.5 m) of the buyer, a press of Use sells it, instead of putting it down. The find leaves the world; the buyer pays `buyer.pays` (0.8) of what the tally pays for it (its worth, knocks and all, note 719), rounded to fives, never under five (`BuyerTuning.Price`). Under 1, so the tally stays the better deal for a find brought home aboard.
     - **The standing** (note 1024): in a town cold to the crew, (1 - `buyer.colder` (0.5) x how cold) of that: at its coldest, half. The buyer opens on a line saying so (world/towns.json `broughtIn.buyer`, drawn after everyone else's on the standing's stream). With `standing.mode` off, every town pays the full 80%.
     - **No double payment with the tally.** Sold before the tally, a find's paid by the buyer and not stowed (it's out of the world): the tally carries it as `RunReport.Sold`, in the gross and the net. At the tally (delivered), every find it paid for (banked aboard, or on a locker's shelf in a car still on the engine) is marked (`Run.Tallied`), and the buyer never buys one of those: one taken back out of the car and carried to them stays in the hand. One still in a hand as the train came home wasn't paid for, and sells.
     - **After the tally** the scrip is `Run.SoldAfter`; the host's app takes it into the campaign's wallet as it's paid, and saves (`Program.Autosave`, `Run.TakeSoldAfter`). The night's report isn't changed after it's been sent.
     - **The joiner.** A joiner's Use press at the buyer crosses as intent like any other, and the host's sim sells their find into the crew's shared wallet (GDD §11), the same as the host's (`BuyerTests.AJoinersFindIsSoldTheSameWayThroughTheirIntent`). Buying at the forge is still the host's (note 697: an order from a joiner would be a protocol bump); a joiner's view of the forge's shelves shows the wallet as the night's setup had it.
     - **What the player sees** (`TownTalk`, `Hud`, the app): at the buyer with a find in hand the prompt is their name and "BUYER   SELL : [E]" (never a price before the sale: GDD §19, the tally's how a crew learns what a find's worth); without one, "... BUYER   TALK : [E]", and their card's foot says "SELL : [E] WITH A FIND IN HAND". The press goes on to the host; when the find's gone from the hands the buyer's card says what they paid ("90 for the pocket watch. On the counter. Count it if you like."), and if it's still there a second later, that the tally paid for it (world/towns.json `buyerSays`).
