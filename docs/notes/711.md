711. **The band, second round (U1, queue #443; the director, 14 Oct 2026: "let's get the band calming the gaunt and stopping the moose. Let's get recorded instruments in place instead of synthesized ones ... source those. We do want bots to participate"). docs/design/upgrades.md §8.4; note 700.**
   - **The Gaunt** (`Gaunt.Talked`, enemies.json `gaunt.bandCalms`): a crewmate playing within its `listenRadius` is talking to it, whoever it's after (the gramophone's rule): with `talkingCalms` it calms a step every `silenceSeconds`, stops attacking under `attackAt`, and in the end is talked down and leaves empty-handed, as by a voice.
   - **The Moose** (`Moose.Music`, `Moose.Listen`; `moose.bandHears` 30 m, `bandCalmPerSecond` 30): music within its hearing stops it whatever it was doing short of a pin (a charge included): it forgets its target, stands in its listening pose (Alert, `MooseMode.Graze`) with its head turned to the music, and its temper falls; the music over, it settles and grazes. While it listens, nothing riles it.
   - **Recorded instruments** (`tools/audio/band_intake.py`, `content/audio/samples/band/`: 59 takes, 1 MB, Opus 48 kHz as every take; `manifest.json`, `provenance.json`; index.json and the credits): all CC0 1.0, from five libraries on GitHub, their licence files hashed at pinned commits (VSCO-2-CE, VCSL, Karoryfer's Shinyguitar, the Ganjo, Body Percussion; the Commons and Freesound are blocked here). The fiddle a solo violin bowed with vibrato; the guitar an archtop, plucked; the spoons real spoons; standing in where no CC0 recording was to be had: a baroque soprano recorder for the tin whistle, a chromatic harmonica doubled 12 cents sharp (the musette's beat) for the accordion, a picked six-string banjo for the tenor banjo, frame drums for the bodhrán (down: the big drum, tip: the small), and a stomp with a tambourine's jingles for the ugly stick's boot and caps. Every melodic take measured and resampled onto its semitone.
   - **Played** (`BandSamples`): a note from its instrument's nearest recorded note, resampled to its pitch; the bowed and blown held for the note and let go over 90 ms; the plucked let ring 0.3 s past it and damped; a stroke a take of its own by where it falls in the bar (three a stroke). An instrument with no recordings falls back on the synth (note 700). Each instrument's level is band.json's `levelDb` (balanced on the bench: the tune's three about −16 dB round a listener among them, the drums and guitar under, all over the wind). `tools/audio/install.py` keeps `band/` with the clerk's voice.
   - **The bots** (`Heed.Band`, last of `BotCrew.Think`'s heeds but the floor's, for the walkers and the gunner): with the band playing and nothing else to do (no buttons, the stick under 0.2), a bot with an instrument in its hands plays it (let go of the press that took it up, then a fresh one held); with empty hands it takes up one within reach, or walks to one lying on its own car's floor within 8 m. When the band stops, or its job wants it moving or its hands, it puts the instrument down where it is. Never the driver.
