729. **The handcar (K1, queue #463; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`, GDD App. F.6).**
The audit's Hackers and Friends goals: "a two-person pump trolley on a flatcar, for a branch, a friend left behind, or a race". Approved with the rest of friendslop.md §3 ("The rest of docs/design/friendslop.md §3 goes ahead").

   - **What it was:** nothing. A crewmate left behind was walked back to, or set back for; a branch was scouted with the whole train.
   - **GDD §24.1 first** (new): the handcar, under Decoupling's "Go back for a player or keep moving?".
   - **Now** (`Train/Handcar.cs`, `TrainOnLine`'s handcar, `World.Handcar.cs`; train.json `handcar`, campaign.json's `handcar` upgrade):
     - **What it is.** A vehicle of its own kind (`VehicleKind.Handcar`) in a rake of its own, made with the train when the night has one (`TrainTuning.HasHandcar`: `composition.handcar`, which the upgrade fits, or `handcar.always` for the tests and the views). Its id is after the train's own vehicles (`OwnVehicles` doesn't count it) and before a yard's standing cars, so every machine numbers it alike. 3 m long, 1.7 m wide, its deck 0.55 m up with a step each side at 0.28 m (walked onto: player.json `stepUp` is 0.35), half a tonne.
     - **Where it rides.** Hung on its end behind the guard van's rear platform (`Handcar.Hung`, laid out from the van's frame; the last guard car, else the engine's rake's tail: `HandcarHost`). Hung, it has no solids (it's out past the platform's rail where nobody walks, and the platform's door, ladder and melee stay as they were), takes no part in contacts or points, and its rake's kept where the van's is.
     - **Down and back.** Held Use beside it (`handcar.liftReach` 2.5 m across the ground, not stood on it, hands free) lifts it: two in `liftSeconds` (4), one alone in `soloLiftScale` (3) times that. Only with the train standing (`standBelow` 0.3 m/s) and room on the rails `setBehind` (1.5 m) behind the platform; back on the hooks from within `stowReach` (6 m) of the van's back, both standing and nobody aboard. A lift that can't go makes no headway. The lift is drawn part-way (`GreyboxScene.Lifted`, from the replicated progress); the sim moves it at the end.
     - **On the rails.** It runs as any rake does (`TakeSwitches`, `Readdress`), so it takes the points as they're set and backs out through them; it couples to nothing and dents nothing (a contact with it shares momentum and stops there, no damage, no jolt), so it can't pass the train. `TrainOnLine.Standing` counts it, so it's never the train's rakes, cars lost, or a yard's cars.
     - **Pumping** (`Handcar.Effort`, `DriveFrom`, `Step`). The handle's a see-saw geared to the wheels: its swing (`Handcar.Phase`) goes round once each `strokeMetres` (5 m) run. A press of Use at an end of it (`handleReach` 1 m; the seat's press, as at the crane, so the app sends `PlayerActions.Seat`) takes it (`PlayerFlags.Pumping`: feet still, stood at the end facing the handle) and the next press or Jump lets go. W pushes your end down, S pulls it up: in time (pushing your end as it comes down, pulling it as it goes up), it drives the car on the way it's going, as hard as the lever has purchase (least, `floor` 0.3, at the top and bottom of the swing); against it, it brakes or fights the other end. Each pumper pushes `pumpAccel` (0.9 m/s²) at a walk, falling off with speed as their power (`pumpPower` 1.8 m²/s³) gives out; `rolling` 0.04, `air` 0.005, the grade's pull, `maxSpeed` 14. Measured: two in time 8 m/s on the level, one 6, a quarter of a swing out of step well under two in time, held W nothing; a 2% climb about 6.5, down it a run away past 9. Standing, a push starts it one way and a pull the other: that's reverse.
     - **The brake.** The brake key (`PlayerButtons.Brake`), anyone aboard: `brakeDecel` 3 m/s², it holds and can't send it back. With nobody aboard it's latched on (`parked`), so one left standing on a bank stays put.
     - **The host's alone, replicated.** The strokes, the brake and the lifts are gathered on the host as each crewmate acts (`World.HandcarAct`) and spent before the train's step (`StepHandcar`); every machine runs the trolley on the drive and brake the host left (`RecordKind.Handcar`). A client predicts its own pumper standing at the handle; the car's world position comes with each snapshot.
     - **The open line.** `CrewSense.Afoot`: on the ground or riding it. The lineside creatures' targets (the spawns' ground crew, the Moose, the outside packs, the Soot Children), the director's hunts for the left behind and its afoot pressure, and the Wakers' pickers take its riders as on foot. The Gannet keys on the train's speed and its roofs: it doesn't come for the handcar. The driver bot sets back for a crewmate out on it as for one on the ballast (`ForTheLeftBehind`). The wreck leaves it out (on the rails it's its own; hung, it goes with the van's body).
     - **Bots** (`Heed.Handcar`): never on their own. A friend at one end of the handle with the other free, the nearest bot on the ground within 15 m at a stand walks aboard by a side step and takes the other end; pumping, it keeps time the way the car's going (it doesn't start it or fight it, and lets the brake be), and lets go when its mate does.
     - **Drawn** (`GreyboxScene.Handcar.cs`): boxes in the look's wood and iron (plank deck, sills, steps, four wheels, the post and the handle swinging with the sim's swing, a lantern on the front end lit on the rails); pumpers posed at the handle with their hands on its grip bar (`CrewActs.AtTheHandle`). The HUD: at the handle PUSH [W] / PULL [S] as the swing has it; THE HANDLE : [E]; LIFT THE HANDCAR DOWN / PUT THE HANDCAR BACK : HOLD [E], and LIFTING (n%).
   - **Protocol 66** (renumbered at merge): `RecordKind.Handcar` (26) (hung, the swing, the drive, the brake, the lift, how many at the handle), `VehicleKind.Handcar`, `PlayerFlags.Pumping` in the player record's flags.
