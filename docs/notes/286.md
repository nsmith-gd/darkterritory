    - **Slow on a tight bend** (`Enemies/Boarding.cs`): `Boarding.OnTightBend` reads the line's curvature under the train and `bendAheadM` ahead of it the way it's going, from the train and the line alone, so every machine agrees. A frontier line's tightest bend (`minRadius` 300 m) is boarded at 14 m/s, under the Climbers' grip, so a driver who reads the boards opens the door there; on frontier:7, whose sharpest bend is 707 m (note 265), the slow places are the stops and the facilities until B1's bends (queue #13) land. Climbers keep their `minSpeed` (5 m/s): they board a moving train, never a standing one.
    - **The Switchman** (the director's decision of 7 Oct 2026; enemies.json `switchman.throwsUnderTrain`, false). The reading that keeps the spec's own tables true: linegen plan §6.3 already says "a switch the Switchman threw costs the clock, never the train", and C.9's Switchman row stays (it names the forward cannon). So no Switchman throws points under the train any more (the derailing kind and its `derailChance` draw stay behind the flag for a mod; the director's dice are still drawn, so the rest of the night runs as it did). Every Switchman throws the dead line's points ahead, the train goes down a line that leads nowhere, and the crew stop, back out and set the points back by hand (the bots already do: `StopCrew`'s `OffDeadLine`, `SetBack`).
        - **A dead line's end** (`Train/DeadEnds.cs`; train.json `overspeed.deadEndDerailAbove`, 6.9 m/s, 25 km/h). Its buffers hit over that, and the train goes through them and off the end: "ran off the end of the dead line the Switchman threw it down, at 36 km/h (over 25 km/h it goes through the buffers)". It's the bend warning's rule (note 265): the cab's warning goes up when the buffers are within the distance to stop under the limit (the same `WarnDistance`: 4 s at the speed now, a service stop at 0.6 of the rated brake, 30 m), the host counts its seconds (`World.DeadEndWarnSeconds`), and the derail commits only after a full lead (4 s). A hit short of that is the buffer stop's damage alone, counted in `World.DeadEndsSpared`. Only dead lines (`BranchKind.DeadLine`): facility spurs keep their buffers' damage. C.9: down a line a Switchman threw (`World.SwitchmanThrew`), the forward cannon, as the row says; any other dead line, the throttle.
        - **Told in the cab** (`Hud.DeadEndLines`): down a dead line, "DOWN A DEAD LINE / THE LINE ENDS IN 2992 M. STOP, BACK UP, SET THE POINTS BACK BY HAND"; within the warning, flashing, "BUFFERS AHEAD: THE LINE ENDS / END OF THE LINE IN 96 M, OFF IT OVER 25 KM/H. YOU'RE AT 36: BRAKE", and the bend warning's bell (`GameAudio.BendWarning`). From the line and the train on each machine: nothing new on the wire.
        - **Killed before the points, its lever falls back** (`switchman.killedSetsBack`): "kill the Switchman before the switch" now means what it says. A cannon round or three blows while it's at the lever and the train's short of the points sets them back for the main line.
        - **A bug found and fixed:** the routing Switchman threw its points every tick it waited, and `World.SetSwitch` returns false for a switch already set, so it went the tick after it threw: the lamp read wrong with nobody at the lever to see or shoot (the baseline night's Switchman was such a one). It throws once now and waits there. Not a switch-mechanics bug (A1.11's #26): the Switchman's own.
