718. **The night's prizes (K1, queue #452; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`).**
"I don't want to have a price shown on every single thing that we find. I want players to eventually understand that certain objects are more valuable than others, and that as they play the game more often, they learn what those objects are. I do agree that Comet Cargo needs to be at actual stops and needs to be extra valuable loot that players get excited for when they see. Team killable creatures can sometimes leave trophies." The audit's Opportunists (grab what's in front of you, greed against risk) were underserved: finds had no rarity, comet cargo was loaded nowhere, one trophy existed.

   - **What it was:**
     - A stop's finds shared a budget (loot.json `villageBudget`, a share of the tier's car-load) split by each container's weight: a box of candles and a pocket watch from the same cupboard were worth the same, and either was worth something else at the next stop.
     - The comet's freight had a cargo rate, director weights and art, and no stop or facility had any.
     - The only trophy was the Gannet's head (note 340), every kill.
   - **GDD §19 first** ("The night's prizes"), §9 Arrival ("The night's best finds, by name"), App. F.1's line on killing together.
   - **Now:**
     - **Value you learn** (loot.json `worth`, `findsByTier`, `outlierDraws`; `StopLoot.Village`, `LootTuning.WorthOf`):
       - each kind of find is worth its kind's share of the tier's car-load, rounded to fives, always (frontier: candles 20, treats 30, tools 55, pocket watch 110, morphine 125). Rarer kinds are worth more.
       - The deep tiers' village finds are worth 1.3 and 1.75 times as much (`findsByTier`), so a night's finds come to about what the old budgets dealt on every tier (about 3 car-loads a local or frontier night).
       - An outlier house (level-design P11) draws its find twice and keeps the worthier (`outlierDraws` 2, on a stream of its own). The old budget's draw is kept on the stream, so every other find a seed deals is the one it always dealt.
       - Nothing shows a price: the HUD names a find, never its worth (as before).
     - **The tally** (`Radio.Prizes`, `RunReport.Prizes`, `Finder`, `FinderWorth`; `Hud.PrizeLine`): after "Salvage", the clerk names the night's best kinds of find, the worthiest first (loot.json `tally.best` 3): "Find: comet fragment, 1080." "Finds: pocket watch, 2 at 110." The run-end screen has the same line over the money. The clerk's existing lines are unchanged.
     - **The finder's line** (`tally.finder`): the host notes who last had each find in their hands (`Run.FinderOf`), and a find stowed is theirs (`Run.Finders`, kept in step with `Stowed`, and in `RunTakings.Finders` for a resumed night). Finds on a locker's shelf count too. "Light fingers: Dave, 1300." for whoever brought in the most worth (ties to the lower player id). The wallet stays shared (GDD §11).
     - **Comet fragments at the stops** (loot.json `comet`; `StopLoot.Comets`, `Run.CometsAt`, `Run.IsComet`, `Run.CometAboard`):
       - each stop's chance by tier (local 0.06, frontier 0.1, dead lines 0.15, deep 0.22; one or two), from the run's seed on a stream of its own. Over 30 nights: local 5 nights in 30, frontier 12, dead lines 11, deep 25.
       - Beside a container, out in the open with no building between it and the main line: a yard's own where it stands, or outside a shed or a house on the side nearest the line (never in a hiding spot).
       - A find's body (`LootOwner(stop, 0xF00 + i)`, so a client names it from its owner like any find), one person's to carry, worth `worth.cometFragment` 1.2 car-loads (1080 on a frontier night): more than a car-load of goods, two-thirds of one of the comet's own freight (`cargoRates.comet` 1.8) in one person's arms, and more than five times any find.
       - While one is in a car of the engine's rake or in the hands of someone aboard, `Director.Aboard` counts the comet's freight aboard: enemies.json `cargoWeights.comet` (×1.4 everything) and `cometRelaxesGates` apply, as for a loaded car of it.
       - Its look (`FindKit` "cometFragment", `SceneArt.Body`): a lump of black slag split by glowing green crystal and shards, the comet's green (the same as a comet-loaded car's seams), a 10 m point light breathing slowly, a halo and a wide faint haze in the fog over it. Presentation only.
     - **Trophies, sometimes** (loot.json `trophies.*.chance`; `Enemy.LeaveTrophy`, `Run.TrophyFor`):
       - every creature the crew kill together (`Enemy.Slay`) leaves its trophy at its chance, rolled from the run's seed and its id: the Grumbler's jaw 0.4 car-loads, the Gaunt's hand 0.6, a Climber's claw 0.15 (chance 0.25), the Passenger's cap 0.5, the Brakeman's wheel 0.5, a Knotter's claw 0.25, a Hotbox plate 0.25, the Beetle's shovel 0.35, Tower Jaw's tooth 0.4 (chance 0.35 each).
       - Where it fell: on what it stood on in a car (its roof or the floor), or on the ground under it.
       - The Choir's ghosts leave nothing (no body).
       - **The Gannet's head stays every time** (chance unset, 1): one bird, rare, only at 18 m/s, and the director's "a dead Gannet can be worth a good deal". "Sometimes" reads right for the creatures a night can hold several of; the Gannet's kill is already the rarest thing in the game. It still goes through its own path (`Gannet.Trophy`), unchanged.
       - Each trophy's simple model in `FindKit` (art reel's `find-*`).
     - **Views:** `comet` (the night's first stop with a fragment, the driver leaning out of the cab coming up on it; `--comet m` the engine's front past it, −22; `--close` stood over it) and `trophies` (every trophy laid out on the ground off the line's left, from beside the engine).
     - **The wire:** unchanged. The fragments and trophies are loot bodies with owners, as the Gannet's head was; the report's new fields travel in its JSON. `Messages.Version` stays 58.
