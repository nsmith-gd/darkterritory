941. **Small things: the floating statue, the village doors, the crane's let-go, the rain (Ezra, queue #644; the director's playtest of 20 Oct evening, lines 3, 5, 16 and 22).**
    - **The crane's let-go is a hold** (line 16: "I accidentally clicked and just let it go").
      - `Crane.LetsGo`: a casting on the hook is let go of only once the operator has held Fire for
        `crane.releaseHold` (0.5 s, `content/tuning/facilities.json`). A click lets go of nothing. Kept held, it lets go
        of one casting and not the next one hooked.
      - With nothing hooked and a friend riding the hook, Fire is still a press for the shake-off (note 726). The rest
        of the controls are unchanged.
      - The HUD says `LET GO : HOLD [LMB]`. The bot operator (`StopCrew.Operate`) holds Fire once the casting is set down.
      - The hold is host state only (the Crane record doesn't carry it), so there's no wire change.
      - `CraneTests.AStrayClickLetsGoOfNothingAHoldLetsGoOfOne` pins it. The tests that let go now hold Fire for the hold;
        their assertions are unchanged.
    - **The rain's squeak** (line 22: "it sounds squeaky almost").
      - Found: both rain loops are modelled in `tools/audio/recipes/world_out.py` with `world_kit.rain`.
        - On tin, every drop rang one of only four fixed panel bodies of a dozen modes each. Struck about 3000 times a
          second, those modes stood as steady whistles from 0.9 to 3 kHz: horizontal lines on the spectrogram, up to
          16 dB over the hiss beside them. This was the roof loop (heard inside a car), and at 900 drops a second also
          the cars' tin in the outdoor loop (its 2.25 kHz line).
        - Puddle bubbles came at six pitches, each rising by a third.
      - Now:
        - Tin rings 24 dense, short sheets (`TIN_PANELS`).
        - Water has 32 bubble pitches, rising a little (`WATER_PITCHES`).
        - The runoff's bubbles rise less.
        - The roof's drum is at q 4, weighted to its low modes, so its 310 Hz band no longer hums.
      - The worst peak over its neighbours is now about 7 dB, and the spectrograms show broadband patter with no lines.
      - The two takes are reinstalled (`content/audio/samples/world-rain/*`, their shas in `index.json`).
      - `dt audio render --scenario sound:world-rain.rain-roof --speed 0` was looked at; AudioTests are green.
    - **The village doors drawn** (line 5: "there's no texture for the abandoned village doors, so it looks like I'm
      picking nothing").
      - `GreyboxScene.HouseDoors` drew each door as flat vertex-coloured boxes: near black, and a 5 cm slab with no
        material. Now it appends `TownKit.HouseDoor`, a cooked piece in the houses' library textures.
      - **A village's door:** five boards of grey deal, grain up them, ledged and braced on the room's side, with
        rusted strap hinges and a ring latch outside.
      - **The jackpot house's door (note 719):** stiles, rails and raised fields painted oxblood, with a brass knob,
        knocker and lock plate whose keyhole is black. While the door is locked (`StopWalls.Locked`), a barrel bolt with
        a padlock hung through its staple sits beside the knob: the lock the wrench picks.
      - A door turned the other way about is built mirrored, never drawn inside out. The greybox (no Look) keeps the
        old boxes.
      - To look at them: `dt screenshot --view jackpothouse --doors-shut --close`, and with `--plain` the nearest
        village door.
    - **The statue on her bier** (line 3: "a statue on a table, but it's like floating above the table").
      - It's `wake_room`'s Zenobia (`tools/models/recipes/wake_room.py`), the prop every village stop puts by a house
        near the line (`WorldArt.OpenRooms`). She was set down on her one lowest vertex: her feet, where the plinth was
        cut, touched the planks, and the rest of her hung over them, her head end 0.3 to 0.39 m up.
      - The recipe now turns her (about the bier's width) onto the edge of her underside's lower hull that lies under
        her middle, as a stone body would settle, so her hips and heels bear on the planks. The arch of her back is at
        most 9 cm up, and her bowed head is on a folded wool blanket.
      - Rebuilt with Blender 4.0 from apt (`python3 tools/models/fetch.py gk-interior gk-hosmer && tools/models/build.sh
        wake_room`). The room's bake re-rendered a few of its texture pages (`wake_room_15` to `_20`).
