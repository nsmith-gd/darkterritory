998. **Bridges as tunnels for the roof, the gates open to leave, trees clear of the walls, the statue's coins (Owen, queue #698).**
   - The item: COORDINATION.md row #698; the director's playtest of 23 Oct (`docs/playtest/2026-10-23.md`), lines 18, 19, 21 and 22.
   - **Line 18, "We went under a bridge, and I clipped right through the bridge top. That should be treated like ... a tunnel."**
     - **What was wrong:** the bridge the train goes *under* is a generated line's iron through truss (`StructureKit.TrussSpan`). Its portals' plates and knee braces hang 5.6–6.4 m over the rail, under a standing roof rider's head. The Sim only knew a tunnel's mouth as a low clearance (`Lineside`'s `SignKind.LowClearance` boards), so the truss was walked through.
     - **The change:** a truss on the main line is flagged on its route feature (`RouteFeature.Through`, set in `PlanRoute` for `StructureType.Truss`). `Lineside.Boards` gives it a low-clearance board like a tunnel's, `Bridge = true`, with its clearance from data (`Sign.Clearance` = sight.json `trussClearance`, 5.6 m). The same warning goes up a full `leadSeconds` ahead (note 260), with the same sound and the same HUD headline ("LOW CLEARANCE · TRUSS BRIDGE IN 6S: GET OFF THE ROOF", and under it "UNDER THE BRIDGE: STAY DOWN"). The roof-walker bots get down for it as they do for a tunnel.
     - **The strike:** the same blow as a tunnel's mouth (`StruckDamage`, `DeathCause.Struck`), so the "tunnel-bonk" cue (`crew-mishaps.tunnel-bonk`) plays, but only for a head stood over the clearance. That is the rider's feet plus player.json `height`; unknown (no movement tuning), they're struck, as at a mouth (`Lineside.Strikes`). A crouch goes under, and a crouched rider is kept down under the truss until it's past (`UnderLowClearance`, now given the standing height).
     - **Deterministic:** the boards come from the route alone, and the strike from the rider's state, the same on every machine. No wire change (`Messages.Version` stays 87).
     - **Not changed:** the death card still reads "STRUCK BY THE TUNNEL MOUTH" (`Hud`, `IncidentLog`), since `DeathCause` doesn't say which. Note 435's truss Dragger is unchanged: crouched under a truss, it can still take you.
     - **Check:** `BridgeClearanceTests`. A truss is boarded low with its clearance from data. A standing rider is warned and struck, a crouched one passes under, and with the clearance raised over a head nobody's struck. Every main-line truss on five generated nights is boarded like a tunnel.
   - **Line 19, "The gate isn't open when we're leaving ... we just clip right through it ... it closes behind us at dawn. And if we're not in by dawn ... it stays open expecting us until the waker ... gets too close."**
     - **What was wrong:** the departure's gate is open from the first (note 869), and `dt screenshot --town wayout` shows it so. The gate that's clipped is the arrival's way in. By note 847's rule it opened only as the engine came within `gates.openAheadM` (40 m), and its leaves take `swingSeconds` (4 s) to swing. A train coming in at speed (15 m/s covers 40 m in 2.7 s; the director came in five minutes before dawn) met leaves still swinging, and the scene draws them across the engine as it goes through.
     - **The change, the director's rule:** the terminus's gate stands open for the train all night, expecting it, and shuts behind it once its last car is in (`shutBehindM`). If the train isn't in by dawn, it stays open until the lead Waker is `wakerShutM` (300 m) from it, or has the train; then it shuts (`Fortresses.Expecting`, run.json `gates.expect`/`wakerShutM`). `World.StandGates` hands `StopWalls.OpenGates` the held gate as well as the train's front and rear. It reads the lead Waker's place on the line, which every client has (`Waker` is `Far`, as `Wakers.SecondsInHand` reads it). A train at a gate still has it open as it goes through (the old rule stays), so it's never clipped. The departure's gate is unchanged: open as the train leaves, shut behind it.
     - **Check:** `GateTests`. The arrival's way in is open from before the first tick and stays open as the train comes up, open while it goes in, and shut once it's home. `TheArrivalsGateIsHeldOpenForTheTrainUntilItsInOrAWakersAtIt`: held open with a Waker far off, shut with him 299 m from it, never held for a train a Waker has or with `expect` off, and open for a train going through even then. `AShutGateStopsAWalkerButItsWicketLetsThemIn` stands the gate shut by the old rule to test the leaves.
     - **Not changed:** the gates' sounds (AU1's `GameAudio.PlaceGates`) still cue "gate-open" as the engine comes within 40 m, so at the arrival they now play while the gate is already open. AU1's call; the shut cue is still right.
   - **Line 21, "I see a tree clipping through one of the walls."**
     - **What was wrong:** the woods beside the line were kept out of a fort, its square and a walled town by their trunk's foot alone: `LinesideProps.InsideAFort`, 1.7–3 m of room. A tree is 15–20 m tall with a crown of `crownShare` (0.2) of its height (3–4 m), so one stood just outside leaned in over the wall.
     - **The change:** `InsideAFort` takes a `room` added to each pad, and `LinesideProps.InAFort(forts, prop)` gives a tree its crown (`SightRadius`, the boards' own measure) as that room. What the Sim stands (`LinesideProps.Walls`), what the art draws (`PlanArt`) and `dt linegen`'s count all go by it.
     - **Check:** `TownWallTreesTests`, on frontier:7, local:509 (the director's), deadLines:3 and frontier:3, each with both its towns as the app builds them. Under the old rule some crowns overlapped a fort or town wall (asserted, so the check is looking where it should). Under the new one none does. `LinesidePropsTests` go by `InAFort`.
   - **Line 22, "Coins at its feet. I don't see coins at its feet."**
     - **What was wrong:** the Child (towns.json civic, "the Child": "Coins at its feet") was drawn on a bare plinth.
     - **The change:** `CivicKit.Coins`, for the Child's statue only (variant 2). There are 26 coins fanned on the plinth top in front of its feet, three small stacks by its toes, and nine on the footing's step, tossed short. It's a fixed scatter, so every town's Child has the same. They're brightened brass, not metal-dark, so they catch the lamp.
     - **Seen:** `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --town statue` (frontier:7's statue is the Child): bright specks round the feet, zoomed in on the PNG. Before, a bare plinth.
