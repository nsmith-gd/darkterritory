950. **The joiner leaves the wait by hand (Quinn, queue #648; found by Wynn's audit, note 949).** The GDD v1.6 "Not yet".
   - **Before.** A joiner whose host went on from the town (note 606) waited on the town's card, "THE HOST IS PICKING THE
     NIGHT AHEAD", while `NetPlaySession.Follow` dialled the host every rejoin.retrySeconds. Nothing let them out: only
     rejoin.followSeconds (600 s) running out took them back to the menu, as a failure. The playtest of 20 Oct (line 27,
     note 937) had the same feeling of being stuck.
   - **Now:**
     - **The card** says "BACK TO THE MENU : [ESC]" under it (`TownBackdrop.LeaveWait`, drawn by
       `Onboarding.DrawLoading`'s new `hint`).
     - **Escape** on it leaves the wait. It goes to the title with nothing said, since it isn't a failure, the way a night's
       LEAVE goes (note 937's `AfterNight`).
     - **`Follow` and `Connect` take `leaving`**, asked each time round with `whileWaiting`. When it says yes, any dial
       under way is hung up (its transport disposed) and `OperationCanceledException` is thrown. Nothing is joined: a next
       night already answering the dial gets nobody of theirs aboard.
     - **The window closed mid-wait** stops the dialling too.
   - **Not the wire:** the joiner hangs up; the host sees a link that never said Hello, or one that went. No protocol change.
   - **Not in a headset:** the loading card reads only the keyboard (as before; the app's loading loop has no headset menu).
   - **Looked at:** `dt screenshot --hud --route frontier:7 --throttle 0 --loading following`: the card over the
     arrival town's square, the way out under its line.
   - **Tests** (`LeaveGameTests`, Game.Tests):
     - a follower leaves before the host's next night is up: it's out at once, not after followSeconds;
     - a follower leaves while the next night answers its dial: it's hung up, and the host has nobody of theirs aboard.
