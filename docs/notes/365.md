365. **The Knotter: a living rope that holds two cars apart (G1, queue #102; the director's brief of 8 Oct 2026; design `docs/design/creatures/knotter.md`; GDD §21, A.7, B.7; spec B.17).**
    - **The gap is the train's:** `Vehicle.Knot`, metres past the coupling's own gap behind a car, replicated with the vehicle (protocol 44), read wherever the gap is (`Consist.OffsetOf`/`LengthMetres`, `TrainOnLine.Layout`, `AverageGrade`, `Uncouple`'s shift). The car's shape behind a knot has the Knotter's back in place of the plate (`CarShape.Knotted`, a rope's width, cached by tenths of a metre), so it can be walked.
    - **The slip is a grab** (App. A.1: only a grab kills): whoever's on its back (`Surface.Coupler` past the car's end) over `slipAbove` slips at `slipPerSecond` (a hash of the tick, the player and its id: the same on every machine); the coil is `coilSeconds`, a friend's Use (`PullsFree`) or blow frees them; else `DeathCause.PulledUnder` (named before, now set).
    - **Killed at a stand** it leaves the cars uncoupled (`TrainOnLine.Uncouple` at its joint, the knot's metres kept as the rakes' spacing, the rear rake's brakes on as any cut at a stand): the crew backs the engine and couples up as at any cut. A coupling cut while it creeps shakes it off. **Overlaps:** D1's loose coupling (#93, note 356) is the same gap; the two don't interact (a loose pin isn't taken; a knotted one isn't worked loose: its own state).
    - **Verified:** `KnotterTests` (5).
