304. **The voice booth (AU1, queue #42; the director, 7 Oct 2026: "Make an artifact for this where I can put my recordings for lines and then listen to them after you've done work modulating and editing them so they don't sound like me").**
    - **The page** (the Dark Territory Voice Booth, claude.ai/artifact/3VvuCkMwP3hJ6iDb1PZaoe; its source `tools/audio/booth/voice-booth.html`): the script as lines by group (the Holdout prisoners, the Soot Children's call, the yard clerk, the crew's breath, creature sources), each with what to say and how. The director adds a take per line from a file (a phone voice memo is the expected form); the page keeps it in the artifact's asset store and its `takes` collection. The disguised versions come back under the take, each played on the page and marked Keep or Redo.
    - **Why files, not a record button:** an artifact can't open the microphone, so takes come from the director's phone or desk. The asset store takes only images, video, PDF, fonts and text: a voice memo (.m4a, MPEG-4 audio) goes in as video/mp4, and anything else is wrapped in a JSON envelope (base64) that `booth.py` unwraps.
    - **The disguise** (`tools/audio/booth.py make LINE TAKE --out DIR`): each version moves the voice's size (its formants) as well as its pitch, so it's another throat and not a sped-up recording of the director. A listener knows a voice by its formants and how it moves, which pitch alone doesn't hide.
        - The prisoners: deep, old, thin, a woman, and behind a wall.
        - The Soot Children: through `recipes/children.py`'s child (a girl, a boy, a small one).
        - The clerk: flat and deep, plus a preview through the radio as the game plays it.
        - The crew: within a semitone, so a breath stays a breath.
        - Creatures: per cue.

      Tails are cut at −50 dB. Each version is a .wav for the game and an .mp4 preview for the page.
    - **What's kept** becomes a candidate on the audio checklist, like any recipe's, and goes into the game through `install.py`.
