980. **A pin working loose heard the length of the train: `state-coupling-loose.warn` (AU1, queue #680; note 865's ask).**
- Note 865 (#592, a jumped pin that takes two on the run; the director: "hella telegraphing") warns of a pin working loose by its knock quickening from 3 to 10 a second (`GameAudio.Faults.LooseSounds`), and asked for a long-carrying sound of its own: till now the ordinary knock stood in, played from no further than 45 m off the ear toward the gap (`Carried`), the knock's own reach being 60 m.
- **The sound** (`tools/audio/recipes/coupling_warn.py`, two candidates, the real one installed): the same pin in the same knuckle as the knock, struck harder and ringing out. Over a few hundred metres of wind and wheels a dull clank is lost and the ring carries, so each take keeps the knock's body (the packs' heavy slam round 360 Hz, choked: the casting's loaded) and lets the coupler head clang through, real metal rings tuned to 1.15 and 1.75 kHz (the pin's, as the knock's ring) dying over a tenth of a second; lifted through 0.7-2 kHz, where the bed is thinnest (63% of it there against the knock's 32%); nothing under 150 Hz. Beside it, the knuckle's casting modelled, struck hard and ringing longer. Never a creature's tell (spec A.4): `tellcheck.features` puts it 0.42-0.52 apart in spectrum from the Knotter's rope and the Brakeman's iron-shod limp, the nearest.
- **In the game:** the hook was there (#592); installed, it plays at the pin, tier 3 (an alarm, as the train's are), out to 400 m with rolloff 0.45: at 300 m (a twenty-car rake's far end) about 4 dB over the stand-in knock pulled in to 45 m, at the gap about as loud as the knock. Without it (a mod that takes it out), the stand-in stays. Caption: IRON CLANGING AT A COUPLING, FASTER.
- **Tests:** `WorldCueSoundTests.APinWorkingLooseIsHeardFromTheCabByItsOwnWarningAtThePinOrTheKnockPulledTowardTheEar` (both ways: the warn at the pin 60 m off, or the knock 45 m off the ear; about three a second at its start), `ThePinsOwnWarningIsInstalledToCarryTheLengthOfTheTrain` (the installed definition's tier, reach and level at 300 m against the stand-in's).
