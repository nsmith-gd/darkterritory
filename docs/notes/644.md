644. **The Pickers and the house creatures under the night's rules and on the Creature Tweaker (D1, queue #374; after G1's #721, note 635).** G1's Pickers (note 592) and house creatures (the Lodger, the Householder, the Hollow House, the Hanger; notes 583–586) come their own way: a group up out of a yard's drains when the train stands there with crates about, a creature put in a stop's open house as the train comes up to it. Neither is the director's spend, so `director.perNight` and `nightChance` (note 635) didn't reach them, and the Tweaker had no rows for them.
   - **The night's rules** (`Director.Tonight`, `Director.Counted`): a house creature is put in its house only if it's about tonight and under its cap (else the house stands empty); a group of Pickers comes up only so (each group counts once), and a group already up finishes as it would. Both are counted against the cap as the director's own sends are, and both keep the minute before dawn (none come then). `dt harness` reports them with the director's spawns (`threats.spawned`, note 643's measure).
   - **At dawn** they go as the night things do (note 635), but the Hollow House: a house can't (G1's `Waker.NightThing`, #721). Their going is G1's #852 (queue #375, note 645): the Pickers down into the ground, the Lodger and the Householder walking off, the Hanger drawn up into its roof; and it drops whatever a gone creature held, which note 635's dismissal left hanging (a Picker's crate, the Householder's thing, the Mourners' dead).
   - **On the Tweaker**: five rows, "comes its own way" for cost; "How often" for the house creatures is their share of the open houses (`dwellings.weights`), for the Pickers nothing; "Leaves at dawn" for all but the Hollow House ("a house: it stays"). The estimate keeps them out of the director's pressure (they move nobody else) and shares the houses filled by their weights (more Lodgers, fewer of the other three). `apply.py` writes their values (`dwellings.weights`, `director.perNight`, `nightChance`, `wakers.staysAtDawn`); `calibrate.py` their counts (`own`, `ownBase`).
   - **Measured** (`dt harness --as-app`, 4 bots, seeds 1–4): a night on frontier:7 has 1 Pickers group, 3 Householders, 3 Hollow Houses and 5 Hangers, deadLines:7 1, 3, 2 and 2; no Lodger on either. The house creatures are the same every seed (the houses are the route's, not the night's dice).
   - **Verified:** `HouseCreatureTests.OnANightTheyreOutOrAtTheirCapTheHousesStandEmpty` (out tonight: none; a cap of 1: one); `apply.py --self-test` with a house creature's how often and cap, and the Pickers' chance.
