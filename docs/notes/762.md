- **The budget** (`Ballast.Render.LightBudget.Fit`, called by `GreyboxRenderer` in place of its sort-and-cut):
    - The nearest by claim are still kept.
    - The last `Fade` (8) kept are dimmed by how near their claim is to the first one cut, with a smoothstep from full at the eighth-last to nothing at the cut.
    - A light's strength is then a continuous function of where the lights are: the one at the edge is already dark when it trades places, and one coming in comes up from nothing. No state is kept from frame to frame, so nothing lags or ghosts.
    - Under the budget, every light is left alone.
- **The shadowing lamp** (`GreyboxScene.HandLamp`): the lamp that cast the shadows last frame keeps them until another is 0.75 m nearer (`ShadowHoldM`, not a design number).
