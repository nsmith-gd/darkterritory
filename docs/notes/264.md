264. **The director's notes on build 1121 (6 Oct 2026): the menus take the mouse, fields don't type until entered, a lighter cab, the whistle cord, the lockers, found toys, no manifest at the gate, and one supplies view.** (Notes 262–266 are left for work in flight.)
    - **Mouse in the menus.** `FrontEnd.Draw` records where each row (and each value's `<` `>` arrows) was drawn; `HitTest`, `Hover`, `Click` and `Scroll` work against that. `MenuInput` (Game) is the one place keys, typing and the pointer meet the front end, so `FrontEndTests` click it headless. The window gained `CaptureOnClick` (off in the menus: a click is a click, not the window taking the mouse), a free pointer position (`InputState.MouseX/Y`, 0..1) and the wheel uncaptured; the app maps them to the 480x270 overlay. Lists scroll by the wheel, or follow the selection when the keys move it. VR keeps `VrMenuInput`.
    - **Text fields.** `TextField` (lobby name, join address, and a new PLAYER NAME in the settings): choosing one doesn't take typing; Enter or a click starts it, Enter, Esc, a click elsewhere or moving off ends it. `WantsText` is only while editing, so WASD and Space navigate everywhere else.
    - **Cab HUD.** In the cab the engine panel is the speed and the levers (`BRAKE ON  [X] FWD`); the steam speed, pressure bar, fire and coal are the backhead's gauges' (GDD §32). The route's name and the next place left the bottom-left block (the top strip names the next place now). The driving prompt is the brake, the vent and the lamp. Before/after: `out/before/hud-cab.png`, `out/after/hud-cab.png`.
    - **Vent.** One key, held anywhere in the cab (`Control.Vent`, default Left Ctrl). The wire's `PlayerActions` byte is full, so it shares `Uncouple`'s bit (that's only on a coupler plate, never in the cab), as Bookmark and Skip share one; `CrewActions.VentHeld`, predicted like the rest. Use at the valve still works. While it's open the prompt says `VENTING STEAM: PRESSURE n, FALLING`; the red-line alerts name the key.
    - **Picking by look** (train.json `pick`). `CrewActions.NearestInteractable` takes the candidate in reach nearest the view's centre within `lookDegrees` (cosines, no Acos: it's deterministic), from `eyeHeight`; none looked at, the nearest as before. `Interactable.Aim` is each thing's height over its footing. Neighbouring lockers, and the firebox, vent, rack and cord, no longer fight over one press; two players work neighbouring lockers at once.
    - **Whistle cord.** `InteractableKind.Whistle` in the driver's front corner over the brake valve (1.26 m from the firebox, outside its 1.1 m reach), a signal-red T-handle where the driver's view has it; Use held on it, looked at (never by proximity), blows the whistle as H does, in the puller's name. `WhistleBy` now rides the World record (appended; old readers ignore it), and the HUD says "THE WHISTLE: <NAME> ON THE CORD". The Whistler's whistle has no name, which keeps App. A.4's tell ("no hand on the cord") readable.
    - **"The whistle went off by itself"** (item 8). Only two things blow it: a player's whistle action (H, or now the cord) and the Whistler (`Flank.cs`: dormant in a gap, it blows the train's whistle `whistleAfterStop` 4 s into any stop, then waits). Bots never whistle, nothing else is bound to it, and the cord had no use volume before this note. A whistle at a stop with nobody on H was the Whistler's tell working as designed; nothing said so. Now a crew whistle names its puller, so an unnamed one is the Whistler.
    - **Lockers.** A tap at a shut locker opens it, and what's in your hands goes in on the same tap (it used to do nothing); empty-handed the tap only opens it. `kit.lockers.stock` (not in the GDD, which names only the fitter's kit; the director's call): DRIVER a lamp, FIREMAN an extinguisher, GUARD a lamp, BRAKESMAN an extinguisher, LAMPMAN two lamps; the rest empty for finds. A shut locker with something in it has a stores tag on its handle (`LockerKit.Tag`), and the prompt at its door names what's in it ("THE FITTER'S LOCKER: THE REPAIR KIT   [E] OPEN"). What's carried in both hands shows lit at the hotbar's left ("IN HANDS: THE REPAIR KIT").
    - **Toys** (item 6). train.json `kit.toys` is 0 and `toyNoises` empty; loot.json `toys` puts them in village cupboards, cellars, haylofts and under floors (chance 0.4 each, about two a village), each noise drawn from two quiet, a squeaker, a music box and a drummer, on their own seed stream (`StopSeed.Toy`), so the finds and kits dealt are unchanged. The Track Doll's appeasement now wants a toy found first.
