   - **The change summary** (the brief's deliverable 8; each change's own note gives its why):
     - **What changed, and why:**
       - **Measured first** (#600–#602; notes 880, 888, 887):
         - `EncounterLog` records each encounter in the Sim.
         - `dt director sweep` runs the §8 matrix, with the old director's baseline at 160 nights.
         - Guard tests locked today's behaviour before anything changed, so every later change had to show its effect on numbers rather than claim it.
       - **One transaction a spawn** (#603; note 885): Choose → Spawn → Commit. A failed placement commits nothing (no cooldown, debt or budget spent), is held 10 s, and is logged with why it failed. The dice are drawn at choice time, so a night replays the same whether a placement lands or not.
       - **Dormant threats off the cap** (#604; note 881): each enemy has an engagement state. Only engaged threats count against the cap, so a sleeping nest no longer starves the night.
       - **One pacing model** (#606–#608; notes 877, 876, 908): an encounter rate from time × crew × cars × tier, bounded debt for what the night owes, and `EncounterClock`. Together they replace the old director's stacked scalings, which double-counted crew and train length (§2 issues 1–2). Intended quiets (the grace, the terminus approach, the aftermath) hold the clock rather than run up debt.
       - **Selection and placement for the players** (#609; note 895): what spawns, and where, follows who is where and what they can see.
       - **Severity and droughts** (#610; note 893): encounters are classed 1–3 from world state, and the four kinds of quiet are told apart, so a drought is measured rather than guessed.
       - **Concurrency by crew and cars** (#611; note 906): caps and pairs from living crew and cars. `concurrency.on` is off by default, for the director.
       - **Eligibility and diversity** (#605, #612; notes 875, 915): the audit of which creatures are rare, gated or underrepresented, with the tuning it called for.
       - **Protection gaps** (#629; note 912): no Runs or Drops in the home stretch, nor at a stop that starts there. Specials are allowed in the aftermath.
       - **The yardstick** (#613; this note): `dt director report` scores every sweep against §7, with the late half judged per open minute.
     - **What it bought:**
       - The first threat is in band on every session on every tier measured. It was the old director's widest miss, at 52–224 s.
       - Eight crew on 12 cars is no longer flooded, and two crew on 5 cars is no longer starved.
       - The late half is busier per open minute for crews of two, four and eight.
       - In band, per open minute / raw, new against old: Frontier 15/12 against 10/9; Local 12/10 against 11/9; Dead Lines 15/14 against 9/7.
     - **Remaining limitations:**
       - **Solo encounters:** 3.75–4.8 per 20 min against 5–8 (the old director 2.1–3.2). `soloCap` 2 fills the gap but loses one night in seven. That trade is the director's.
       - **Droughts over 90 s:** still over 2 a night at two crew on all three tiers, and at four and eight on Local.
       - **Delivered:** below 0.75 for solo and two crew. The cause is the stuck hand (#662), not the director; on today's main the Frontier's solo is .67 and two crew's .8 (in band); the other tiers' figures are pre-662.
       - **The raw late half:** still short of the early half for most sessions, because §1 A's terminus approach takes a third of the late half. `drive.finalSurge` raises it (2p to level, 4p and 8p ahead), at a cost of 8p deaths 0.8 → 2.7; it ships off (#1512), the director's option.
       - **Afoot pressure** (`f1-dread`) can't be measured with bots that stay aboard: the bots never go afoot.
       - **The old baseline** has 10 seeds a group against our 30, so its scores are the noisier of the two.
