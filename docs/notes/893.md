893. **Encounter severity (levels 1–3, from world state) and drought detection that tells recovery from a true drought (queue #610, the spawn director overhaul's D2; the director's brief §4, §6, note 874; n1-A1, nathaniel1).**
    - **What it is:** a pure classifier, `Enemies/EncounterSeverity.cs`, read by #600's `EncounterLog` (note 880) once a
      second. It reads #604's `ThreatState` (note 881) for each member. Like the log, it only watches: no draws, no wire
      state, and nothing in the Sim reads it back yet.
    - **The levels** (§4), each encounter's highest kept as `Encounter.Severity`, its seconds at each in `SecondsAt`:
      - **1, ambient presence:** a member is about (not resolved) within `presenceMetres` (60) of the train or a living
        crewmate. This rests on a proximity proxy, near enough to be noticed, never "noticed" (§8).
      - **2, active harassment:** a member is threatening or engaging (note 881: alert, within its `nearMetres` of someone,
        working on a train system with nobody near, or at someone).
      - **3, compound crisis:** at level 2 while another encounter is at level 2 at least `compoundApartMetres` (15, about a
        car) away. Two problems apart mean the crew has to split up or choose.
      - **Meaningful** is now level 2 or more (it was "confronted someone" in note 880), so `byQuarter`, `firstSeconds` and
        the old droughts count harassment.
    - **The quiet** (§6): each second out on the run is one of four `QuietKind`s:
      - **sustained:** something at level 2+ (an active danger outranks the director's own rest);
      - **recovery:** the director holds for one of `recoveryHolds` (prefixes of `Director.HeldBecause`: grace, aftermath,
        facility lull, in a fort, banned, final stretch, terrain at its ceiling, derailed);
      - **presence:** something at level 1;
      - **drought:** none of these. `EncounterLog.Now` and `DroughtNow` (the true drought running now, seconds) are there
        for the director to answer (#608/#611 wire it); `TrueDroughts` holds every unbroken run of at least `droughtSeconds`.
    - **The `encounters` block** gains:
      - `quiet`: seconds of each kind;
      - `trueDroughts` and `longestTrueDrought`;
      - `firstSeconds`: now the first encounter, the first harassment, and the first compound crisis;
      - `severity`: encounters by the highest level reached;
      - each line's `harassedAt`, `compoundAt` and `secondsAt`.
    - **Tuning:** `enemies.json` `director.telemetry` gains `presenceMetres`, `compoundApartMetres` and `recoveryHolds`.
    - **Pinned by** `EncounterSeverityTests`:
      - the levels from states;
      - compound only when apart;
      - the quiet's order;
      - presence isn't an encounter met;
      - one at the crew is harassment, two apart a crisis;
      - a true drought, with the grace not part of it.
