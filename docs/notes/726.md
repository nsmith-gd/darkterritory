726. **Hoist a friend on the crane's hook (K1, queue #460; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`, GDD App. F.6).**
The audit's Griefers goal, cheap credited mischief. The director: "The griefer ideas are funny." It was approved with the rest of friendslop.md §3.

   - **What it was:** the crane's hook took castings and nothing else. A crewmate under it was only ever in the way of a dropped load.
   - **GDD §18 first** (new paragraph, "A friend on the hook").
   - **Now** (`World.Hoist.cs`, `Crane`'s riders, facilities.json `crane.hoist`):
     - **Taking hold.** The hook has to be empty, with no casting on it and fewer than `riders` (2) aboard, and within `reach` (1.2 m) across the ground. It has to be between `height` [−0.4, 1.9] m over the crewmate's feet, and with no casting there to rig (rigging comes first). The crewmate needs empty hands and steady feet (`World.CanTakeTheHook`), on the ground or on a car's roof. They hold Use for `grabSeconds` (0.6) and they're on (HUD: **HOLD ON : HOLD [E]**). While they hold it, Use reaches nothing else. A carried friend can't take hold, and bots never do.
     - **Riding.** A rider gets the new player flag `Hoisted`. The motor leaves them be, as for `Carried`. `Rescue.CarriedIntent` strips their keys to look, talk and Jump. Once everyone has moved, `World.StepHoist` (HostSession, after the rescues) stands each rider on the hook's lip, `feetAbove` (0.05 m) over the hook and `spread` (0.45 m) apart across the bridge. They are moved in the world's frame, at the hook's own velocity. A rider who took hold from a car's roof is reparented out of the car's frame. The crane moves in the run's step, after the hoist, so a rider trails the hook by one tick (2.7 cm at hoist speed).
     - **A load.** `Crane.Riders` (replicated: the Crane record's last field) and `Crane.Loaded` let every machine count riders as the hook's load:
       - the hook's work lamp hangs over their heads;
       - the operator's eye follows their middle down;
       - the load-swing sound plays;
       - the operator's corner says "THE CRANE: A FRIEND ON THE HOOK";
       - no casting can be rigged under their boots.
     - **Headroom.** With someone on it, the hook stops `headroom` (1.7 m) short of `crane.height`, at 6.3 m on the foundry's 8 m gantry, so the rider's head stays under the bridge and the trolley.
     - **Letting go.** Jump lets go: they're in the air on the hook's motion, and the fall is the world's (player.json `landing`, note 573).
       - **The call made:** the spec wanted "from 7.5 m death". A rider stood on an 8 m gantry's hook can't get their feet that high without their head going through the bridge. So a let-go from the top of the foundry's gantry is a hard knock, about 48 of 100 health, not a death. A taller gantry (or more `headroom` taken off) kills, and the code path is tested on an 11 m one.
       - **Superseded by note 802** (the director, 15 Oct 2026: "It's a silly gag not a big grief"): a drop off the hook is no longer the world's fall. It costs at most 10 health, by its height (player.json `landing.offTheHook`), and never kills, so `Dropped` is no longer set and the 11 m test is now a 10-health knock.
       - Landing on a car's roof is never a knock (the motor's rule for any drop onto the train), so a rider set down on a roof and let go just stands there.
     - **Who's to blame.** After a let-go, whoever last lifted the rider (`Crane.LastDriver`, kept past the operator's per-tick reset) is held against the landing:
       - If it kills, the host turns `Fell` into the new death `Dropped` ("Dropped from the crane"; death screen "DROPPED FROM THE CRANE. YOU DID ASK"), and the report reads "Dropped from the crane by {actor}, 9 m up.".
       - If it only hurts, the report gets a faint line (`IncidentKind.Bonked`) with the same words.
       - A let-go from low, or one with no lift, is nobody's.
       - There's no shover's mark (note 717): the rider let go themselves.
     - **Shaking them off** (mischief): with `shakeOff` and the CREW MISCHIEF switch on (note 724), the operator's Fire with nothing hooked drops everyone on the hook. It's the operator's ("Shaken off the hook by Dave, 6 m up."). The operator's corner offers SHAKE THEM OFF : [LMB] only then. With the switch off, Fire does nothing to a rider.
     - Dead, grabbed by something, the crane brought down, or the night moved on from the stop: a rider comes off where they are.
   - **Protocol 71** (renumbered at merge): the Crane record's riders, the `Hoisted` flag (the last bit of `PlayerFlags`' ushort), and `DeathCause.Dropped`.
   - **Checked:**
