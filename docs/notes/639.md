639. **The bots restart a yard's powerhouse (D1.5, queue #369; found under #366, the tiers' pay).** Deep territory's foundry (deepTerritory:7) loaded no casting on 8 nights of 8. Its crane stood still, its operator at the controls and its rigger calling the hook down, till the loading gave up.
    - **Why.** A yard's power is its tier's (stops.json `tiers.*.power`): frontier live or low, dead lines half low and half dead, deep territory dead four times in five. Dead, the crane and the conveyor don't run; low, at half speed (facilities.json `power`). Level-design D.1's answer is "the cranes wait on a walk to the powerhouse and a restart": Use held at its door, or an open one's switchboard (note 509), for `restartSeconds`. No bot ever did it.
    - **The rule** (`StopHand.PowerUp`).
        - The yard's power down (dead or low), the crane's operator walks to the powerhouse before the crane and holds the restart till it's live. It's the operator because it's the crane that waits on the power.
        - The conveyor's shunter does the same before the belt.
        - Back from the powerhouse, the operator goes to the crane's controls by the foot path from further off (`OnFoot`, note 406), not the track-side walk. From 40 m out past the powerhouse's walls, that walk stuck against them for a minute, and the Gaunt had the operator there (seeds 1 and 2).
    - **Verified.**
        - `StopCrewTests.WithTheYardsPowerDeadTheOperatorRestartsItAndTheCastingsGoOn`: a foundry yard made dead, the operator is seen restarting the power, the power's live, both castings are loaded and nobody dies. Without the rule nobody restarts it.
        - 64 nights (`--as-app`, 4 bots, seeds 1–8 of local:3/:7 on 4 cars, frontier:3/:7 on 8, deadLines:3/:7 on 13, deepTerritory:3/:7 on 18, 5,400 s), main 943e86f against main with the rule. 55 of 64 nights are the same tick for tick; the foundries are not:
            - deepTerritory:7 (dead power): delivered 7 → 8 of 8, car-loads 0.44 → 0.86 a night, net −233 → +1,245, deaths 6 → 0.
            - frontier:7 (low power): net +874 → +878.
    - **Not this.** deadLines:3's mine head loads by the steam lift (note 638). Its own and deadLines:7's and deepTerritory:3's wreck yards (fires at the stand, D1.4's #364) load little for other reasons.
