820. **The bot driver holds its speed without pulsing the full brake (B3, queue #532; AU1's audio audit, note 801's night).**
The brake's a held button, all or nothing. With steam driving (T97) the engine pulls at full effort the moment it's off:
the pressure's kept over what the cruise needs (fired for a metre a second over, and the fire kept off its low mark makes
more: 80 psi, the steam for 17.6 m/s, at a 14 m/s cruise). So the bots' brake was always fighting the steam. Let off as soon
as the train was under the speed it wanted, the brake was back on a tick or two later. On frontier:7 with 4 bots and
enemies (`dt harness --as-app`, 8 minutes): 1,471 applications, 345 a minute with the train moving, each held 0.19 s on
average. Every one is heard as the shoes and the slack running in.
    - **Where:** 1,389 of them were the stop driver's (`StopDriver.Toward`: held at 0.2 over the speed it wanted, let off at
      once). Of those, 1,247 were shunts holding 6 m/s on their open stretch (the spur-in, set-backs, backing out), 106 the
      run in to a stop at the cruise, and 26 a crawl. The other 81 were the driver's cruise (`ConductorBot.Drive`: on at the
      cruise + 0.5, off at the cruise).
    - **On a line plan only.** Every night the game runs is a route with a line plan. The hand-built test lines' nights (no
      plan) were found on the driver as it was, and they keep it.
      - Their stops sit on margins any change in the driver's timing reshuffles. `StopCrewTests`' Choir loading passes on
        main only because a cold walker shuts car 2's side door to warm up just before the train leaves.
      - That door was left open by a crate hand idle in a full car (below).
    - **The stop driver's brake is held** (`StopDriver.Toward`), from the controls' own brake (`World.Controls`: last tick's,
      the same on every client).
      - Out on a move's open stretch, with the curve to its mark well above, it's held about the move's top: on 1 over, off
        1 under.
        - A shunt's 6 m/s runs 5 to 7 (`ShuntBand`), so it runs at its top on the whole, as T110's playtest wanted ("3 m/s
          was agonizingly slow"). Held under it, the spur-in ran 7 s longer and frontier:1's winch missed its sled.
        - The run in to a stop at the open line's cruise runs 13 to 15 (the driver's band, `StopDriver.CruiseBand`).
      - On the curve to the mark, a brake that's on is held till the train's well under the speed wanted
        (`LetOffUnder`: a third of it, 0.3 at a crawl to 2). The 0.2 over the curve is as it was.
      - At a stand it isn't held: a train standing on its brake is let off as before.
      - A crawl keeps its 0.3, so coupling up is as gentle as it was (`KnockAbove` 1.2).
      - Chasing a cut rolling away (`away`), it's let off at once, as it always was. Any slower and the cut's away down the
        grade (`RollBackTests`: main catches it at 300 m, the test's limit).
    - **The open line's cruise is held about the cruise** (`ConductorBot.OnTheOpenLine`, `OpenBand`).
      - On a line plan the driver held to 0.5 over any cruise and let off at the cruise, so on the level it braked against
        its own steam every second.
      - At the open line's own lit cruise, with the line and its hazards allowing more, it's 13 to 15 at 14 now. On the
        whole the train makes the 14 it made before.
      - Under a board, the line's authority, the dark's cruise (10.5, under the Sleepers' 11.1), a hazard's speed, or a
        curve to stand at something (a Holdout, the Doll, the gate), it's held as before.
      - Fire Flies' pull-away cruise (over 14) is the open line's too, and is held 1 either side of it.
    - **The same night now:** 64 applications, 15.8 a minute moving, held 5.2 s on average, 2 let off inside a second.
      - The cruise on the level: one every 4 to 5 s, held 1.5 s or so. It was one a second, held 0.4 s.
      - A 6 m/s set-back: one every 3.1 s, held 1.3 s, from 7 to 5. It was on and off every tick.
      - The run in to a stop: two or three applications, then it stands. It was on and off every tick or two.
    - **Measured from now on.**
      - The harness reports the brake (`HarnessReport.Driving`: applications, a minute moving, taps under a second, the
        mean held).
      - The trends carry it (`driving.brakesPerMinute`, `driving.brakeTaps`; bands in `tools/trends/metrics.json`).
    - **Found on the way, for the bots' owner (D1):** in `StopCrewTests`' Choir loading, with the cars full, a crate hand
      carries its last crate into car 2, puts it down and stands idle there for good. It never shuts car 2's side door,
      and the stop waits out its 300 s give-up with every hand aboard. On main a cold walker happens to shut the door, so
      the test passes. That night is on a hand-built line, so this change leaves it as it was.
    - **Not changed:** the brake itself (`TrainDynamics`, the spec's numbers). A brake that builds and releases, or a
      graduated one, is the director's call. Nor the fireman's firing: the surplus steam is App. A.5's low fire kept off.
    - **Verified:** `DriverBrakeTests` (a bot night on frontier:7: its applications a minute, its taps, how long each is
      held); the Sim, Game and Dev suites.
