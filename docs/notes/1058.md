1058. **Bots load from a siding they've cleared (B4, queue #757, picked up from nathaniel1; level-design I.4; note 1050 rank 8).**
   - **Claimed** 26 Oct 15:10 UTC. Done on `b4-siding-load-757`.
   - **Before:** since #731 (note 1028) a bot crew clears a blocked siding the night has time for: in for its derelicts, out past the points, put away on a free road further up the yard. Then it never used the siding it cleared. Its stop crew loads only the facility's own track (its crane, its modules, its crates). The castings stacked under a siding's own gantry (`Site.YardCranes`, one over every craned loading face but the facility's own, P5, P18) were never loaded by a bot unless someone pinged the crane (note 739).
   - **Built** (all through the intent path; host-side bot state, deterministic):
     - **A siding at its own gantry is a stop of its own** (`StopPlan.YardCrane`, `Siding`, its key `SidingKeys + track`).
       - Its crane is the stop's `Crane`. `Has(module)` answers for the facility's modules only at its own track, so at a siding the pair work that gantry and nothing else of the site's: no winch, crates or spout there.
       - Everything that read `Site.Crane` or `Site.Has` for a stop's loading now reads the stop's: the pair's work and its done, the power walk first (note 639), the driver's allowance (125 s to the loading, as any craned stop's), the record.
       - `Run.YardCraneOn` finds a track's gantry, matched to the layout's track by toe and side, as `BlockedTracks` matches them; `Site.YardCraneTracks` says which track each gantry is over.
     - **Which sidings** (facilities.json `crew`):
       - `loadClearedSidings` (true): one the crew cleared (its clearance made, its derelicts gone), castings still stacked, nothing standing on it, the pair to work it.
       - `loadCranedSidings` (false): any siding with a gantry, not blocked. More cargo and more time in the yard: the director's call.
       - Either way, only with a car that goes in that has room: the cars that go in are the train's first.
     - **Back down the main to it.** A cleared siding's points are behind the free road its derelicts went up the yard to, so `StopPlan.Ahead` takes it up to `SidingBack` (300 m) behind the engine.
       - The driver's approach sets back at a shunt's pace (`SetBackTop`) when it's more than 20 m past a hold; the old crawl at 1 m/s was for an overrun.
       - The driver says the stop it's setting back to (`CrewCalls.SettingBackTo`). A hand still on another stop, with the train whole on the main, has done with it: before, a stop was only over once the train went away up the main from it, and a set-back left every hand on the put-away.
     - **Sidings first** (`crew.sidingsFirst`, false): such a siding is cleared and loaded before the facility's own track, the crew setting back to that after (`Ahead`'s second pass, `Pick(... skipOwn)`).
   - **Why it's rare with the defaults (the geometry):**
     - The cars that go into any track are the train's first: the engine leads in, and with stub sidings and points facing up the line there's no running round to swap empties forward (I.4's "Trailing points and loops").
     - A yard's own track is laid first at its throat and is its longest. So its loading comes first and fills the very cars that would go into the siding.
     - Of 718 blocked sidings on `RouteGenerator`'s frontier, dead lines and deep territory seeds 1–119, 436 have a gantry. None is longer than its yard's own track, and none has its points before the own track's.
     - So with the own track first, the cleared siding's gantry gets loaded only when the own track's loading left room: given up (deepTerritory:6's was held 240 s and never went in), cut short by the loading's give-up, or a small own loading.
     - With sidings first, the gantry's castings get the cars and the own track what room is left. Which the cars should go to is the director's: the facility's machinery or a siding's castings.
   - **Measured:**
     - The drill (`StopCrewTests.ABotCrewLoadsASidingItClearedAtItsGantry`: a `RouteGenerator` yard with a blocked siding under a gantry and a free road, 8 cars, a crew of six, sidings first, the loot dealt so the gantries stand): cleared, put away, set back down the main to the cleared siding and its castings loaded at its gantry, then set back to the facility's own track; all alive.
     - Before the hands' set-back fix: held at the cleared siding 240 s and given up, nobody at the points.
   - **Full nights** (`dt harness --route R --bots 4 --seconds 2700 --enemies`, the routes whose yards have a blocked siding under a gantry with a road up the yard by the layout: frontier:21, deepTerritory:7 and :8; defaults against `sidingsFirst` on, from a copy of `content/` with the one flag flipped):
     - frontier:21 and deepTerritory:7 identical both ways (the Slaughterhouse 422 s, cargo 1.55, net 1,038; the Foundry 632 s, cargo 0.5, net 11,304; 0 deaths).
     - deepTerritory:8 with `sidingsFirst`: the ChemicalWorks 586 s, cargo 1.5, 0 deaths. The defaults' run ran out its 30 minutes.
     - None of them cleared its gantry siding. frontier:21's is at its third yard, which that night's driver didn't make. At deepTerritory:7's Foundry there's no free road for the derelicts when the crew come to it (`FreeRoad`: none clear, long enough and past the blocked siding's points).
     - Earlier, deepTerritory:6 cleared a siding with no gantry, and deadLines:6, :7 and deepTerritory:5 cleared nothing: the same as main.
     - So on the nights the game runs, the defaults change nothing measured. The siding's gantry is loaded where the drill sets it up.
