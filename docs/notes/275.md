275. **Tools matter: which tool, how hard, at what; the fireman's shovel; breach with a tool (GDD §12, §13, App. C.2, D.7; WP19, renumbered from its old branch's note 191).** WP19's branch (`wp19-melee`) was written before notes 197–274 and merged with main here; main is the source of truth wherever both touched a thing. Every tool dealt one blow, the shovel was nobody's, coal went on by hand, and a Holdout's lock could be smashed with empty hands.
    - **Superseded by main, dropped:** the branch's own swing record (`MeleeSwing`, with the tool in hand) and its drawing of a crewmate's swing (`CrewActs.Swinging`, a `Crewmate.Swing` field, the host's tick passed from `NetPlaySession`). Note 197's `SwingEvent` (`RecordKind.Swing`) already puts every swing on the wire, landed or not, and `GreyboxScene.Swung` draws and `GameAudio.Crew` sounds it; the tool in hand is already on the replicated player record. The branch's swing-replication tests went with it (`HitConfirmTests` covers it).
    - **Kept from WP19:** a swing is played once from its start (`CreatureArt`: `CrewPose.Swing` is one of the `fromStart` poses). Without it, main's swing was put off by each crewmate's idle beat (variant × 0.41 s, up to 2.9 s into a 0.8 s clip), so most of the crew were drawn at the end of their swing. `dt art clip crew swing --tool` passes the time unshifted for it too. `dt screenshot --act swing,swing,swing` stages each swing further into its blow (`Crewmate.Phase`) with the shovel, the crowbar and the wrench in turn.
    - **Damage by tool** (enemies.json `melee`, where melee lives; App. C.2 gives no numbers): shovel 1.5 blows, crowbar 1, wrench 0.75, empty hands 0.25 (T108). Enemy health stays in crowbar blows, everyone's tool from the start, so no creature's numbers moved; a 3-health creature takes two of the shovel's. Only what a blow does to a creature changed; nothing here touches player damage (note 272's). **Reading:** "the boiler player's shovel doubling as the crew's best club is intended tension" is about the damage, and the tension needs there to be one shovel. **Flag:** the director's damage-model decision (GDD App. F.1, 6 Oct 2026: "Creatures mostly don't take damage; they're driven off by their rules"; note 272, in flight) may take creature health away altogether, and with it what a tool's blow is worth. The tool-by-tool numbers are one table (`MeleeTuning.Blow`), so they can become a knock-back or drive-off strength, or go, without touching the rest of this note.
    - **The shovel is the boiler's** (boiler.json `shovelInHand`, a tuning flag; §8 reading). There's one, stood by the cab's tool rack (`Boiler.ShovelOut`, bit 64 of the boiler record). Use at the firebox (the keyboard's hold or T29's stroke) puts it in hand first (`CrewActions.TakeShovel`): out of your own kit, else off the rack into the first free slot. Out with someone else, nothing goes on (the HUD says the shovel's out). Use at the rack with it in hand hangs it back. A body keeps it and `Bodies.TakeTools` always takes it, like the wrench. A shovel that's nowhere (no crewmate's kit, no body: its carrier dropped out, or its body went with a car) is back on the rack (host, `World.Step`), so the train can't be left with no way to fire. `player.json` kit stays `["crowbar"]`: §12 gives nobody a starting tool, App. C.2 gives the shovel to the boiler role, and roles aren't assigned (§12 Fluidity), so it's a fitting on the engine and not part of anyone's kit. Predicted like the wrench: the take and the hang-back change the player's kit and the boiler's flag the same way on host and client. Main's Stoker rules are unchanged by it (notes 263, 271): it's reachable only per its own rules (`Enemy.Reachable`: only while it's boarding at the tender; in the firebox no tool does anything, and a blow at the shut door only rings), and opening the door on it burns whoever's nearest, whatever's in their hand. The bots' `FightStoker` clubs it while it's boarding with whatever tool is in hand, and never shovels once it's in. The App. F.1 note "the coal shovel is fun" is about firing; that's unchanged except that the shovel comes into hand as you start.
    - **Breach needs a tool (D.7: "any melee tool: shovel, wrench, crowbar").** `Holdouts.CrewAct` takes `tool` (a tool in hand). Without one, Use at the door does nothing unless it's the repair kit at a lock, so the kit's silent breach is unchanged. A barricade still wants a tool.
    - **Bots.** They use the same intents. A breacher with empty hands presses the first tool's number (`Kit.ToolToHand`, `StopHand.Breach`). A bot fires only with the shovel to hand (`CrewActions.HasShovel` in `ConductorBot.Work`), so a driver doesn't stand at the firebox pressing Use while the fireman has it.
    - **Not yet:** bots never hang the shovel back, so one who takes it out of the cab (a stop's job) leaves the fire to wait until they're back. No lag compensation beyond the reach.
