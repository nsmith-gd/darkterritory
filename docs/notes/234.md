234. **The endings' own sounds: a powder blast, and the Stranded outro (GDD v1.4 §18-19, App. E.9).** Two of the ways a night goes wrong were silent or borrowed.
    - **A powder blast** (`powder-blast`, tier 3): a keg or a powder car going up is `World.Blast`, an impact with no shooter. It played a cannonball's; now it's a slow, enormous whump, the powder's roar burning down from bright to dark, planks and iron coming apart, and wreckage raining for seconds. A ball's impact is unchanged. Synthesised, a stand-in for the audio checklist's place-depot line.
    - **The Stranded outro** (E.9: "wind and the boiler ticking as it cools. No music"): `GameAudio.Stranded`, called by the app each frame with the outro's seconds, holds `boiler-tick` at the firebox (the dead iron contracting: jittered ticks through the shell's narrow resonances, spaced out by its "cool" as the outro goes on) and plays `lamp-out` (a flame's flutter, a pop, the hot glass ticking) at each car as its lamp goes, last car first and the engine last, on the picture's own count (`Views.StrandedLampsOut`). The listener is the camera, so they pull away with the shot. The wind and the bed carry on as they are; the drone is already off (note 192).
    - Tests: `EndingSoundTests`. `dt audio render --sound powder-blast | boiler-tick --param cool=0.5 | lamp-out`.
