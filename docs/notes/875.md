875. **The monster eligibility audit (queue #605, the spawn director overhaul's B3; the director's brief `docs/design/spawn-director-overhaul.md` §2 issue 5, deliverable 5; the plan, note 874; nathaniel1's n1-B3, 20 Oct 2026).** Every kind the director sends (the 22 of `Spawns.Rules`) is measured through ordinary nights: each of its gates, how often it holds, which kinds almost never qualify, and what to change. Report only: no tuning moved here. The changes are for #612 (E2), and the ones that are the director's call are listed in **Questions**.
   - **The tool** (PRs [#1337](https://github.com/nsmith-gd/darkterritory/pull/1337) and this note's PR):
     - `Eligibility.Sample(World)` (`src/DarkTerritory.Sim/Enemies/Eligibility.cs`). For every kind it reads:
       - the night's gates: roster, tonight's roll and cap, slain, and room among what's holding the crew (`Director.RoomFor`);
       - its rule's own gates, one by one and named for the number each reads (`Eligibility.Gates`, in the rule's order, from the same tuning);
       - rule met, and free gate met (`SpawnRule.Free` with a `curve.kinds` weight).
     - It draws no dice. `EligibilityTests` holds three things:
       - a sampled night plays exactly as an unsampled one;
       - a met rule has every named gate holding, so a rule changed in `Spawns` without its gates here fails;
       - every kind has its gates named.
     - `dt eligibility [--routes] [--crews 1:3,2:5,4:8] [--seeds] [--seconds 2700] [--markdown f]` plays the nights `dt trends` plays (`dt harness --as-app --enemies --upkeep`). It reads every kind once a second and reports:
       - each kind's share of the night's **open seconds**: past the grace, short of the final stretch, outside the forts and the line's bans, the night not derailed;
       - each gate's share, and the gate that fails first;
       - what came, and by which channel;
       - the speed bands;
       - what-ifs for the two speed gates.
     - `dt eligibility rolls [--seeds 1-2000]` gives each kind's nightChance with the night's floor (note 868), by tier and crew, from the director's own constructor, with no night played.

   - **The rolls** (`dt eligibility rolls`, 2,000 seeds):
     - Each kind is about on its `nightChance`, ±2 points.
     - The night's floor brings back 1–2 points more for the stops' residents at a crew of eight.
     - Tier and crew don't change it otherwise.
     - So a kind at 0.1–0.22 (the Passenger 10%, the Grumbler 11%, the Moose 19%, the Gaunt 21%, the Track Doll 22%) is missing on four nights in five, whatever its gates. That rarity is the Creature Tweaker's (note 643), not its rule's.

   - **Findings: the kinds that almost never qualify**
     1. **The Gannet never came: 0 of 24 nights.**
        - Its rule wants the train at 18 m/s or more for 30 s (spec B.13, `arriveAbove` 18, pinned by `SpecTableTests.TheGannetMatchesB13`).
        - No ordinary night reached 18 m/s for a single second. The bots hold the spec's 14 m/s cruise (B.3, "target efficiency"); 14–19% of the open seconds are at 14 or over, never 18.
        - The line speeds are under it too: Frontier 18, Dead Lines 16, Deep Territory 14 (`tiers.json` `lineSpeed`, note 376). So on those tiers only a crew running over the boards ever meets it.
        - At **13 m/s** (just under cruise) its 30 s held on **every** night, crew and tier alike, 8–16% of the open seconds. At 14 it held on none: the driver sits just under 14 and is over it only in short bursts.
        - Its identity is a seabird that rides a train that's *running* (out of a tunnel, by its biome, peeling off under `stallBelow` 12). None of that needs 18.
     2. **The hound run never came: 0 of 24 nights.**
        - `director.run` (note 328) counts metres at `fromSpeed` 19 or more, and starts again whenever the train goes under 2 m/s. A run comes at 2,400 m.
        - Two things stop it:
          - nothing reaches 19 (Local's line speed is 20, every other tier's is under 19);
          - the facility slots are about 1.1 km apart on the 7 km night (note 554), so a crew that takes its stops never runs 2.4 km between stands.
        - Even at 11 m/s only the solo nights (whose bot takes fewer stops) met it: once a night at 1p, 0.3 at 4p, **never at 2p**.
        - The run was made as the answer to the top-speed strategy (notes 328, 376; orchestrator.md §8 question 4). As it stands, it is that and nothing else.
        - Ordinary Cinder Hounds are no rarer for it: situational 37% of the open seconds (speed ≥ 8), sent 65 times in 24 nights (2.7 a night).
        - The brief's §9 warns against more hound packs (car fires, #1285's ~9 a night).
     3. **The Car Hugger qualified 1.4% of the open seconds** (Dead Lines 2–4%, Frontier under 0.1%).
        - Its spot (`CarHugger.Spot`) is only a `Marsh` or `Bridge` feature whose middle is 250–800 m ahead and level.
        - Its rule (App. B.3) says "marsh, water crossings, *low ground*": the low ground isn't laid. frontier:7 has almost no marsh or bridge.
        - Everything else of its gates held 99%.
     4. **The Fire Flies qualified 3.4%.**
        - They need a lit car with a way in (19%), the train stood (42%), and not at a facility.
        - That is the director's own "rare, and only while the car is stopped" (note 269). **Intentionally rare: keep.**
     5. **The Tippy Toesie qualified 4.7%, and never solo.**
        - It needs a crew of 2, the train stood, and someone idle 5 s and alone.
        - Its identity is the one left alone, so a crew of one is out by design. **Keep;** the solo note is for #611.
     6. **The stop-structure kinds are rare where they live, by design.** The stops' own channel (note 860) sends them when they're there.
        - The Tower Jaw: 11%, needs a coaling tower or gantry at the stop (12%).
        - The Mouse Moose: 7.2%, needs a facility with castings at a crane.
        - The Freight Beetle: 7.7%, needs loose freight. Sent 12 times, 8 of them by the stops.
        - The Grumbler: 25%, needs a stop with a crane and castings stacked.
     7. **The crew-gated kinds are never solo, by design.**
        - The Brakeman: crew ≥ 2 and cars ≥ 4. Never at 1p/3c; 31–41% otherwise.
        - The Passenger: Dead Lines and on, crew ≥ 3. 39% at deadLines 4p, 0 elsewhere.
        - The Tippy Toesie (above).
     8. **The ground kinds depend on a crewmate off the train at a stop.**
        - These are the Ribbits, Gaunt, Soot Children, Moose and Followers. Someone was afoot 6% of the open seconds on solo Dead Lines nights, against 51–58% at 4p.
        - So their solo situational share is 3–6% on Dead Lines and about 20% on Frontier, against 35–58% at 4p.
        - Partly the solo bot: it stops at fewer yards (the deadLines 1p nights stood at a stop 9% of the time on seeds 1–3). A human solo night may well see more.
        - The free gates already carry them off the stops: the Followers' free gate held 32% against their rule's 7%.
     9. **Well in reach:**
        - Cinder Hounds 37%, Knotter 32%, Hotbox 30% (speed ≥ 10), Whistler 30% (stood), Track Doll 46%, Draggers 81%, Climbers 99% (their free gate: any gap, any speed).
        - The Climbers are rare by their roll alone: 35%, and out on all four seeds here.

   - **Proposals** (for #612 unless said; each a tuning number, none a rule removed):
     - **The Gannet:** `arriveAbove` 18 → **13** (just under the cruise every crew holds); `stallBelow` 12 → **10**, so it doesn't peel off at once; `arriveSeconds` 30 kept.
       - Measured: its arrival then holds on every ordinary night.
       - Once a night, its biome weights, its tunnel rule and its roof prey all stay.
       - It's the spec's B.13 table and the director's decision of 7 Oct (note 340), so it's question 1. Until they answer, #612 should hold it behind a tuning flag at 13.
     - **The hound run:** leave it as the answer to running flat out, and **say so in the spec**: it comes only to a crew running over the boards.
       - If the director wants it in ordinary nights (question 2), the change that works is this: count `afterMetres` from the last stop's departure, not from the last stand under 2 m/s, and lower `fromSpeed` to the tier's line speed − 4 (Frontier 14, Dead Lines 12).
       - Lowering `fromSpeed` alone does nothing at 2p.
       - Measure car fires with it (§9, #1285).
     - **The Car Hugger:** lay its "low ground": the line plan's marsh, dykeland and contaminated-marsh biome stretches, and the lakes' shores (`linegen water`), as spots beside the marsh and bridge features. Widen `lurkAheadMax` 800 → **1,500**. Its level-ground rule and its 2 cars stay.
       - Identity kept (App. B.3's own words). It's #612's code, measured with this tool.
     - **The rare rolls:** the Grumbler (11%), Passenger (10%), Moose (19%), Gaunt (21%) and Track Doll (22%) are rare by their `nightChance`, not by their gates. Their gates hold 25%, 6%, 34%, 28% and 46% of an ordinary night.
       - Raising them is the Creature Tweaker's, the director's own (note 643; question 3).
       - The fresh kinds of #590 ([#1285](https://github.com/nsmith-gd/darkterritory/pull/1285)) answer the diversity without touching them.
     - **Kept rare, with the reason:**
       - the Fire Flies (note 269);
       - the Tippy Toesie solo (its identity);
       - the Brakeman and Passenger by crew (App. B.8: "never with a crew of one", "a crowd to hide in");
       - the Tower Jaw, Mouse Moose, Freight Beetle and Grumbler by the stops that have their structures;
       - the Passenger by tier.
     - **For #613 and the bots (A1's):** the solo bot takes fewer stops and leaves the train less (afoot 6% on Dead Lines), which undercounts the ground kinds on solo nights. A solo balance read should note it.
