767. **The house creatures heard (AU1, queue #497; G1's #316-#319, notes 583-586; their design docs' §4).** The Lodger,
the Householder, the Hollow House and the Hanger were on main with no sound of their own (AU1's survey, 14 Oct). The
Lodger's shriek, its telegraph, was silent, and its door broken in played the crew's latch. The Hollow House's doors shut
with the crew's door. The Householder's stove, clock and kettle, its chair and tread, and the Hanger's rafters and the
strands' hum went unheard. Each is now heard off its record (`GameAudio.Houses`: its mode in `Height`, `Extra2` its house
among the stop walls' open houses), through the house's walls as the house's own (`OccludedInHouse`): clear to an ear
inside, dulled from the street.
- **The Lodger:** its slow, wet breathing held while it's folded in its corner (`Hidden`); the shriek each time it enters
  `Shriek` (before every lunge); its bare feet as it lunges; a shut door in its way splintering, a blow about every half
  second of its break. The door it breaks in is heard as its last blow, not a crewmate's latch (`HouseDoorBy`).
- **The Householder:** its house's stove, clock and kettle held while it sits or watches. Watching, its chair and neck
  creak every 2.5-4.5 s. Rising, the chair is scraped back and the cutlery rattles. Hunting, it takes a step each 1.3 m it
  covers (never quicker than 0.45 s: it never hurries) and its dry breath is held. Then the clutch on whoever it grabs,
  and home again the thing set down and the chair drawn in.
- **The Hollow House:** the groan underfoot held while it rumbles; each door it shuts heard as its slam; its timbers
  screaming as it sinks, louder the deeper (`HoldLevel` 0.6-1.0 of its sink depth); the collapse. The voices of those
  inside cut off with them, by the death's own hard cut (`VoiceChat.HardCut`), the director's "mic cut moment".
- **The Hanger:** the rafters' creak and the wet clicking overhead, held only to an ear in its house (`_space`). The
  strands' hum held while anyone's in its house (its telegraph, A1's note 683 makes the strands quiver then), swelling as
  the nearest of them comes within a metre of a strand (`Hanger.Strands`). Then the snap of its grab, the haul held, and
  its hiss and scuttle as it lets go.

The first build's footfalls and blows were squishy (the director, 8 Oct): 75-83% of the Householder's tread and the

- **Footfalls:** high-passed. The shoes are cut at 170 Hz, now 5-17%, level with the crew's own steps on wood (11-16%).
- **The Lodger's splitting door:** cut at 120 Hz.
- **The modelled slam:** its leaf lowered under its planks and cut at 150 Hz.
- **The crash:** the dropped thunder's whump cut at 80 Hz.
