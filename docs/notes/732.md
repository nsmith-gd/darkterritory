732. **Sound cues in the world: what waits in a house, what's about to go, what's been hit (K1, queue #466; the director's calls of 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`; the 14 Oct playtest's line 12).**
Cutting the dead's haunt, the director said: "I don't want dead people seeing things that other people don't, and then helping them play the game. I do like the idea of using sound-only cues in the design of the environment." And of the loose coupling, at the second playtest of 14 Oct (`docs/playtest/2026-10-14.md` line 12): "We still have this coupling is loose for no reason issue. The reason's not obvious". The evening before (line 18): "There seems to be some audio of clicking going on near the loose coupling ... I feel like it loosened from nothing."

   - **What it was:**
     - Since note 640 a pin works loose only from a cause: a run-in (the whole brake held from speed) or a knock (coupling up too hard). The clerk named the cause in the report, but only after the cars had gone. In play, the pin just started knocking. The run-in's slack was heard down the train as any brake's, so nothing tied the knock to the slam 90 s before it parted.
     - The house creatures waited in silence until they struck. AU1's queue #497 (note 767, merged into this branch from main) gave them their own sounds: the Lodger's breathing and shriek, the Householder's house and its chair creaking as it watches, the Hanger's rafters and humming strands. Nothing in the world said, before you went in, that something was waiting.
     - Nothing told the crew that something of theirs had been lost somewhere they weren't.
   - **GDD first:** §32 "Sound cues in the world" (new), App. A.1's fairness contract (the world sounds before it bites, too), the summary at the top (the coupling's causes), and F.6 points at §32.
   - **Now:**
     - **A pin jumps from something named, and is heard as it** (`Couplings`, `LooseCause`, `Vehicle.LooseCause`; upkeep.json `coupling.bendSeconds`):
       - A third cause: a bend taken too fast. The engine's rake lurches over a posted board by sight.json `lurchOver` (the cargo thrown about, `Lineside.Hazards`, now flagging `TrainOnLine.Lurching` on the host). Held for `bendSeconds` (2 s), it jumps one gap's pin, once a bend. The clerk says: "A bend taken at 62 km/h worked the coupling's pin loose, and it parted."
       - What jumped it rides on the car's record (bits 2 and up of the wound/seized field). So every client hears the moment at the gap as what did it (`GameAudio.PinJumped`):
         - The cause's own cue where installed: `state-coupling-loose.jump-brake`, `.jump-coupled`, `.jump-bend`.
         - Until then, the train's own sounds stand in. A run-in is `bed-slack.run-in`, the slack slamming in, at the gap. A bend is `bed-groan.creak`, the frame. Each adds `crew-coupling.pin-lift`, the pin lifting in its knuckle. A knock's clash is the coupling up's own sound.
       - The knock follows 0.6 s after the moment (its first used to wait out a whole beat unheard), and starts louder: 0.7 of full (was 0.55), rising to full by the drop. It runs the whole `partAfter` (90 s) of running, so it plainly starts with the slam, the clash or the lurch, well before the pin parts.
       - Cleared with the pin: tight, or parted, the cause goes from the record.
       - **The Knotter** works a coupling but never a pin (note 365: the two don't interact). It already has its own sound, distinct from the pin's knock: AU1's wet creak at the gap (`tell-knotter.creep`, note 388), from the moment it creeps in until it forces the cars apart. "A different sound when a creature caused it" is that creak. I've given the Knotter no pin of its own: that's a change to G1's creature, not a sound.
     - **The sound cues in the world** (`Sim.Run.SoundCues`, `WorldCue`, `World.Cues`, `RecordKind.Cue`; enemies.json `soundCues`). Made on the host after the creatures' step, kept on the wire `keepSeconds` (1 s) so a dropped snapshot doesn't lose one, heard once by each client by id (`GameAudio.WorldCueSounds`). Nothing in them changes a player or the train.
       - **What waits in a house** (`creak`): boards creak where it waits.
         - The Lodger: at its corner while it's hidden, measured from the nearest of the living to it.
         - The Hanger: up in the roof while it's hidden, measured from the nearest of the living to any strand.
         - Timing: the first creak comes as soon as anyone's within `within` (10 m), then one every `everyFar` (2.4 s), quickening to `everyNear` (0.7 s) by `near` (2 m). A hash of the creature and the tick evens them out by ±15%. Its strength (how near) is on the record, and sets the volume (0.45 to 1).
         - On frontier:7's first village, walking in at 1.5 m/s: the Lodger's first creak comes 1.5 s or more before its shriek, and quickens. The Hanger's comes 1.5 s or more before a strand's grab.
         - Heard as `tell-house.creak` (its own, to come), or until then `crew-footsteps.walk.wood` (a board taking a weight), through the house's walls as AU1's house sounds are (`OccludedInHouse`).
       - **The Householder's chair** (`Shift`): one of its things lifted with nothing left on its table to pay for it, and it shifts in its chair, once a lift (`tell-householder.shift` to come; until then AU1's `cs-householder.creak`). Its chair creaks the same way (`cs-householder.creak`) as the carrier nears the door, quickening with the door's nearness. It still rises only out of the door. Paid for, or a guest's empty hands, and it's silent.
       - **What's been hit** (`bell`): a far bell, `height` (6 m) up where a stop's bell would hang (`world-bell.far`, to come; no stand-in):
         - `carsTaken`: a car of the crew's taken (the Car Hugger, the Passenger, the corrupted) with nobody living within `unseenBeyond` (60 m) of it.
         - `cutsLost`: a cut of theirs (cars that ran in the engine's rake tonight, not a yard's or a derelict) every living crewmate is further from than run.json `stranded.kitLostBeyond` (§23.2's 400 m: the night has it). Once, until someone's back by it.
         - `holdoutsLeft`: a Holdout released with a friend still in it (App. D.5, `World.StepHoldouts`).
       - **Something at the lamps' edge** (`bark`): a dog barking far off (`world-dog.bark`, to come; no stand-in).
         - It barks at any creature on the ground or the line moving faster than `movingAbove` (0.8 m/s), between `edgeFrom` (30 m) and `edgeTo` (55 m) of the engine's rake and the living.
         - Not at a hazard, nor at Dave or Jacob, nor at anything aboard.
         - The bark comes from `beyond` (120 m) further out, past what it barks at. Not at the same creature for `everyCreature` (30 s), nor at anything for `atMost` (8 s).
     - **The spec's reading.** "Something's taken a crew's parked cars or a Holdout": on main, no creature takes a parked cut or a Holdout. Before #459, the 400 m rule was the only thing that took one: "until a cut is more than 400 m from every living crewmate, nothing is lost". So the bell rings for what that rule takes, for a Holdout left with someone in it, and for cars a creature takes out of sight. Each is a flag in `soundCues.bell`.
     - **Protocol 62:** the `Cue` record kind, and the cause in the vehicle record (`Messages.Version`; `RejoinTests`, `CrewCapTests` and `SessionsTests` pinned).
