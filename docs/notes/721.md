The friendslop audit's Friends goal (note 733: "It's extremely important that friends exceeds our target meaningfully") has a pillar, a crew remembers: in-jokes need a place to live, and a campaign the Wakers end should be a story to keep, not a reset.

   - **What it was:**
     - A night left nothing a crew kept together. The bookmark album (note 203) kept one machine's stills of the deaths; the profile (note 180) kept the commendations a player was given, by count.
     - A campaign taken at dawn (note 632) was a closed save on the host's disk, and nothing on anyone else's.
   - **Now** (memory.json, `CrewPhoto`, `CrewMemory`, `LogbookReader`, `Sim/Campaign/Logbook.cs`, `Hud.Memory.cs`, `GreyboxScene.Memory.cs`; GDD §9 Arrival, §11 "What a crew keeps", §3.1):
     - **The crew photo at the gate** (`photo`): on a delivered night, `delaySeconds` (1.2) after this machine has the report, the app stages the shot (`CrewPhoto.Stage`) beside the engine on the yard's open side, away from the town's square (`CrewPhoto.YardSide`: the square's side has the terminus platform's canopy posts in front of any lens). The living stand in a line `out` (3 m) from the track, `spacing` (0.95 m) apart, ordered by id, each in the emote they're holding (caught `emoteAt` 0.8 s in) and sooty if sooted (note 717); bots stand in like anyone. The dead are their caps on a crate `crateGap` (1 m) past the line's right end, each in its crewmate's colours. The slate on an easel `slateOut` in front and `slateAside` left of the line has the tally chalked on it ("FRONTIER 7 / NIGHT 3 / NET 1450"). The lens is `cameraOut` (5.6 m) out, `cameraAhead` toward the engine's front, `fov` 46. The frame is drawn off screen like a bookmark's still, lit by a point light at the lens (`flashRange`, `flashStrength`), the screen flashes white over `flashSeconds`, and the bookmark cue clicks (`UiCue.Bookmark`). Who's in it comes only from the replicated world (`CrewPhoto.Sitters`: `CrewStates`, names, emotes, outfits, the soot flag), so every machine's photo is the same photo.

     - **U1's yard photographer** (docs/design/upgrades.md §4) is one call away: `CrewPhoto.Stage` on the departure's engine and `KeepPhoto(still, sidecar, name)` under a name of its own.
     - **The logbook** (`logbook`): a ledger on a small shelf `height` (1.25 m) up the wall across the aisle from the kit locker in car one (`LogbookReader.Shelf`; the lockers' tops are up under the roof). `place` is the hook for the big cab's back wall (queue #450); every value but "lockers" is the lockers' for now. "THE LOGBOOK   READ : [E]" within `reach` (1.9 m) and `lookDegrees` (22°); Use opens it at the last night's page, the walking keys turn pages (and walk nobody while it's open), Use shuts it, dying shuts it. Each machine writes the night's page into its own profile (`PlayerProfile.Data.Crews`, a `CrewLog` per crew) as the report comes in, again with the photo, and once more as the night's left with all its commendations (`Logbook.With` replaces a night's page in its place, keeping a photo).
