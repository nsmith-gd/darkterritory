978. **Speaking tubes between neighbouring cars (T2, queue #678; #410's leftover, upgrades.md §6: "Voice carries between neighbouring cars as in one"; note 678's handoff, item 3).**

**What it does**
- A small upgrade from Dead lines (campaign.json `speakingTubes`, effect `speakingTubes` → `KitTuning.SpeakingTubes`). Fitted, every car between the cab and the guard van that has a room gets a speaking tube's horn on its front left wall (`SpeakingTube.CarMouth`: the right front is where a car's crates stack, and they hid the horn there in the first shot), turned in to the room.
- `SpeakingTube.Ends`: the cab's (0) and the van's (1) as ever, then the cars' (2..) in the train's order. `Tubes`: the cab's pipe to the van (note 720) and, fitted, each end to the next along the train (cab, cars, van). Said into a horn, it comes out of every end joined to it (`HeardAll`); the host gives each listener the nearest of those they're within `voices.tube.heardWithin` of, and the frame's tube end is that one's, so the client plays it from that horn as before. No wire change.
- The horns are drawn where the ends are (`SceneArt.Voices` reads `Ends`), so they appear with the upgrade and nothing else draws them. Looked at: `dt screenshot --view inside --upgrades speakingTubes` with the camera turned to the car's front (the view itself unchanged).
- `VoicesTests.FittedTheTubesCarryAVoiceToTheNeighbouringCarsEitherWay`; `UpgradeTests` (the effect, off as built).

**Decisions:** "neighbouring cars as in one" read as GDD App. C.8's "a later upgrade can add tubes between neighbouring cars": it's the tube's voice (tinny, heard near the horn), not the cars' walls taken away. One horn a car, joined both ways, rather than one at each end of a car: a car's two neighbours hear it alike. A van cut off still breaks the whole system (no van, no ends), as note 720 had it.

**Not yet:** the car-to-car horns sound as the cab's tube does (AU1 may give them their own pipe note); the clerk's tally doesn't count tube talk.
