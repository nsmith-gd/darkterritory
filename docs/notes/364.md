364. **The Brakeman: a dead railwayman winding the brakes on (G1, queue #101; the director's brief of 8 Oct 2026; design `docs/design/creatures/brakeman.md`; GDD §21, A.8, B.8; spec B.16).**
    - **A corrupted human** (zone `Corrupted`: one at a time with the Passenger, the Switchman and the Grumbler).
    - **A wound car** (`Vehicle.Wound`, replicated with the vehicle; protocol 44): in the engine's rake its own handbrake's drag (`couplings.handbrakeDecel` of its mass, `TrainOnLine.CreatureDrag`) on the train, which holds a standing train like any brake and stalls a climb. Unwound at its wheel (the handbrake interactable, Use held `handbrakeSeconds`); a wheel on a car that isn't wound turns the rake's handbrakes as before.
    - **He moves in the train's own length** (`Extra`: metres back from the first car's front), so the train moves under him; placed on the car there each tick. Chased (a roof crewmate within `spook`) he runs at `flee` and ducks blows; clear by `lose` or at the train's end he hides `hide` s (a hash of his id and count of hides) and comes up at the far end. Crewmates within `cornerSpan` on both sides corner him: blows land, and he lashes every `lashEvery` (the spine's telegraph, never a kill).
    - **"Never spawns solo"** read as never with fewer than `minCrew` (2) alive: the brief's kill needs two. A tuning number.
