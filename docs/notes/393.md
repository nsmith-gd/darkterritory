393. **The slaughterhouse modelled (queue #130, C1; GDD §18's set pieces, the art checklist's `slaughterhouse`: "Animals are loud, and something already lives here. Oversized, partially abandoned, barely operable").** After #118's elevator, the slaughterhouse's killing hall was the plainest building at any demo facility. It was StructureKit's long windowless works box, 14 × 44 × 9 m of brick with a gable. Now it's modelled and baked (`tools/models/recipes/facility_pieces.py` `slaughterhouse`, 814 triangles against a budget of 3000) at the same footprint, 22 m off the line:
    - **The hall:** soot-black brick on a stone plinth to a corbelled cornice, with a pier at each corner and every 4.4 m between. Its windows are small and high between the piers, barred, with rust and soot run down the brick from their sills; some are broken dark, and one is lit (something already lives here). A slate roof, brick gables, and a clerestory of dark louvres along the ridge.
    - **The front, toward the line:** a cattle door each side, at ±10.5 m along, so whichever side the hall stands on the decorative pens are before one of them. Their timber leaves are hung open. Between them is the high door the dressing rail comes out of, under an iron canopy, with a dark-red run down the brick below it. The rail is an iron beam 4.5 m out over the yard on an A-frame, with its hooks hanging on their chains, 11 m off the line and clear of the herd's pen and the ramp.
    - **Behind:** the boiler house, its flue into the hall and its 27 m brick chimney, and a water tank on a 13 m tower. Both show over the roof from the line.
    - **One layer at 1024** (#118's `cook.finish(size=)`): about 2,000 m² of brick, slate and iron.
    - **StructureKit's `Slaughterhouse` case** sets it where the kit's hall stood (`Piece`), keeping the works box as the fallback; the pens and the ramp are unchanged.
    - **Verified:** `dt art check` (814 triangles against the large prop's 8,000; the whole facility is 2,794 against 90,000), `PropArtTests`, `StopArtTests`, `LookTests`, `StopShellArtTests`. Looked at: the turntable, and frontier:3's slaughterhouse from 50 m down the spur at dawn and at night, and from over its pens, before and after.
