838. **What the crane loaded rides on, drawn with the train (A1, queue #566; the director, 16 Oct, `docs/playtest/2026-10-16.md` line 21).**
"Where are the crates that I loaded up? In car one, they're no longer here. What happened? ... I don't know why they're gone. That feels really bad."

   - **What it was:** the director had used the Foundry's crane to load a casting into car 1.
     - A casting isn't a body. It's a record on its stop's crane (`Crane.Casting`, its state and car), and the sim kept it `Loaded` in car 1 all night. It was still paid at the gates and still sent to clients: every stop's crane record is written, not only the current stop's.
     - The scene drew a stop's crane only while the crane's hook was within `DrawDistance` (400 m) of the eye. Its castings came with it, including any already loaded into a car (`GreyboxScene`, the sites loop). So 400 m out from the Foundry, the casting in car 1 was gone from sight.
   - **Now:** a crane out of draw distance still has its loaded castings drawn, in their car, wherever the train is.
     - `SceneArt.Castings(..., loadedOnly)` is the art pass's casting drawing, taken out of `SceneArt.Crane`. `GreyboxScene.Castings(..., loadedOnly)` is the greybox's.
     - Near the crane, everything draws as before.
   - **Checked:**
     - Crate bodies were not the cause. Six crates lying in car 1, with all its doors open, stay put through a 180 m bend each way at 16 m/s and a 2.5 m/s² stop (`CratesStayAboardTests`).
     - Nothing in the sim takes a loaded casting back. The Mouse Moose (note 790) takes castings only from the stack.
     - `CastingsRideOnTests` puts a casting in car 1 with the train 2 km past the Foundry's crane, then counts what's drawn there, with the art pass and in greybox. Without the fix nothing is drawn; with it, the casting is.
     - `LookTests` still pass.
   - **Not done:** a lit screenshot of the casting riding in car 1. The test counts its triangles where it lies. The geometry and its placement in the car are the same as at the stop.
