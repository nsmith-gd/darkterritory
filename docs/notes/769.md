769. **A car plainly under a grain head is under it (A1, queue #499; the director, 14 Oct, `docs/playtest/2026-10-14.md` line 17).**
"I'm getting a note that says the car, the belt is running, but no car is under its head. But I clearly have car one positioned underneath. Maybe it needs to be positioned more, like in the center".

   - **Why:** the conveyor's head (note 400), the grain elevator's spout and the steam lift's chute (note 368) each took a cargo car only with its middle within `tolerance` (1.5 m) of them. A car stood under a head but a few metres off its middle was "no car": the belt ran and the HUD said NO CAR UNDER ITS HEAD. It loaded once the director had walked it nearer the middle.
   - **The fix:** a car is under a head when the head hangs over its open top (`Run.CarUnder`): along its own body at least `endMargin` (1 m, `facilities.json` `spout`, `lift`, `conveyor`) in from either end, and within its width. Of two, the one whose middle is nearer. What comes down a head falls where it is, so a car takes it wherever its top is.
   - **Unchanged:**
     - over a car's end wall or the gap between two cars, it's on the ballast, as before;
     - the tipple's cradle (note 423) still wants a car's middle within its own `tolerance` (1.2 m): it tips the whole car;
     - the bots still walk a car's middle under a head (StopCrew).
   - **Checked:** `ConveyorTests.ACarStoodUnderTheHeadOffItsMiddleIsStillUnderIt`: a car stood 3 m either way off its middle, or to within 0.2 m of its end margin, is under the head; stood 3 m off, it fills at the belt's rate; over its end wall it isn't. The conveyor's, spout's, lift's and tipple's tests (the "between cars" checks, 7 m off) are unchanged.
