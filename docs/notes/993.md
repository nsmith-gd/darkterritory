**The faces (Tamsin, the Token Budget Manager's worker on nathaniel3, 25 Oct; the director, 24 Oct, on a hooded
townsperson close up: "this can't seriously look like a human face").**
- **Why it looked like that:** Lee Perry-Smith's map (Map-COL.jpg) had been laid on the scan **upside down** on every bare
  head crewfigure baked (the townsfolk, the survivors, the husk, Dave): its texture coordinates run down the map (made for
  three.js, whose loader flips the image). So the stubble went round the eyes, the lips onto the chin, the closed eyes' red
  flecks onto the cheeks. And the game mesh's 2000 triangles spread over the whole skull left the face a few millimetres
  deep (a plate); the head baked from the scan alone, so the face came out evenly lit to its edge against the hat or hood;
  the face had ~110 px of the atlas.
- **Fix, in the build recipe for all ten** (`crewfigure.Style(face=True)`, set by `townfolk.recipe`; tools/models/faces.py):
  the map turned the right way up (crewfigure, for every bare head it bakes from now on: the survivors, the husk and Dave
  keep the upside-down face until they're rebaked); the scan made symmetric and subdivided once; the brow ridge,
  cheekbones and the hollows under them, the nose's bridge, the jaw's angle and the chin pushed into the mesh; the game
  mesh collapsed face-dense (the skull under the hat let go first; the same 2000 triangles, the ears kept); the head's
  unwrap stretched over the face and the face's boost 3.5 -> 4 (about twice the texels: ~280 px across); the hat, hood,
  hair and collar's occlusion over the face baked as a mask (`contact`) and the face's own AO taken harder; the soot
  smudged by place on the head, mirrored; both eyes painted open on the shut lids from each texel's baked position in a
  box round the eyes (`eyes_at`, `paint_eyes`): an almond of white, the iris under the lid's edge, the pupil, a glint,
  the lashes' line and the crease, the same either side; the lips' studio red calmed, the cut neck's flaps under the
  jaw cleaned. (Carving the lids open over modelled eyeballs was tried: the bake's rays missed the balls through the
  folded lids, grey holes in the eyes.) The teacher's spectacles are rims only (`LookbookKit.Spectacles`: the lit grey
  lens discs hid her eyes). The wall and fort folk (#1694) are drawn as these figures, so they take the same faces.
- **Rebaked:** all ten, bpy 5.2.3: trapper 9794 triangles, watchman 9870, market 9691, lamplighter 10198, linesman 9242,
