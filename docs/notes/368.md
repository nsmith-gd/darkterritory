368. **The steam lift (A1, queue #105; spec D.2: "requires the locomotive coupled nearby and venting pressure to power it. 2 crew. Ties loading directly to the boiler; you're at zero pressure while it runs"; D.3: "steam lift fails through resource conflict: the boiler can't do two jobs").** The one module in the Wiki's Loading table that was never built (spec D.2's conveyor line and tipple, which the Wiki doesn't list, are still to come). The mine head has it now, beside its winch and crates (facilities.json `kinds`: `["lift", "winch", "crates"]`): two 2-person modules at one stop, D.3's "someone is alone somewhere".
    - **How it works:** a headframe over the shaft out on the site's side, where the mine head's buildings already put theirs (StructureKit's, 25 m back from the layout point and 16 m out, so the sim's and the art's agree), its chute out over the spur. Someone holds the lever (`Run.LiftLeverInReach`); the engine stands with its middle within `lift.steamReach` (56 m) of the chute (`EngineInReach`: the first three cars behind it come under the chute in reach; the rest are the winch's and the crates'); and the cab's vent is held open. Then the vented steam winds the skip instead of going into the air: each point of pressure vented is 1/`steamPerSkip` (20) of a wind, and a wound skip tips `perSkip` (0.25) of a car-load of ore down the chute into the car under it (within `tolerance`), out of `ore` (3) car-loads. No car there, it's on the ballast; a full one overflows and strains, as the spout's does. The winding engine is machinery, loud (`Run.Machinery`).
    - **"Zero pressure while it runs":** `Boiler.VentedSteam` is what the vent actually let go each step: its full rate (boiler.json `ventRate`, 12 a second) while there's pressure, and with the gauge run down only what the fire's making after the engine's own heating and auxiliaries. So a full boiler winds about four skips (a car-load) at once, then the gauge sits at nothing and the lift goes at the fire's pace (a standing full firebox about 1.8 a second, about 1 a second to the lift after heating: a skip in about 20 s), and the engine pulls nothing until the vent's shut and the gauge is back over `powerFloor`. Walking the next car under the chute waits on the fire.
    - **Readings:** "venting pressure to power it" is the cab's vent, held by someone in the cab (the driver), so the 2 crew are the lever and the vent: one on the ground, one in the cab. "The locomotive coupled nearby" is the engine standing in reach of the headframe's steam line, with no hose to couple (a distance, `steamReach`); the train is walked under the chute a car at a time as under the spout. The mine head keeps its winch and crates: added, not swapped.
    - **Bots:** `StopPlan.LiftTarget` (the spout's, limited to cars that come under the chute with the engine in reach) and a `Lifting` leg after the loading: the driver walks each car under the chute and vents while the shunter's on the lever (`Site.LeverHeld`, last tick's, host only: the "steam!" a driver hears), and holds the train still while it winds; the shunter's `Lift` stands at the lever and holds it while a car with room stands under the chute. At an ordinary mine head the winch and crates fill the spur's four empties first, so a bot crew rarely needs the lift; it works it when there's room left.
