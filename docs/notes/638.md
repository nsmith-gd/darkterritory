638. **The steam lift's lever reaches the clients: a bot driver vents for it (D1.5, queue #368; found under #366, the tiers' pay).** Measuring the tiers the app's way (`--as-app`, each tier on its own train), every dead lines night with a mine head brought home nothing. deadLines:3 has the only mine head in those nights: 16 nights, 0 car-loads, net −695 a night. Its stop stood 523 s, and the lift was given up after 240 s.
    - **Why.** The lift (note 368) winds only while someone holds its lever and the cab's vent is open. The bot driver opens the vent at the chute when it sees the lever held (`StopDriver`'s Lifting leg, `site.LeverHeld`). The driver is a client's bot, and `LeverHeld` wasn't in the snapshot (`WorldRecords`; `Winding` was), so on a client it read false forever. The driver never vented and the skip never wound: the walker stood holding a lever that turned nothing, pressure at 95 the whole time. The tests that pinned the lift (`SteamLiftTests`) step the host's world, which has it.
    - **The fix.** `LeverHeld` is sent: bit 1024 of a site's flags, `SiteState.LeverHeld`, mirrored as the rest are.
    - **Verified.**
        - `SteamLiftTests.AClientSeesTheLeverHeldBeforeAnythingWinds`: held and not yet winding, a client sees it held; let go, it sees it let go. `AClientSeesTheLift` checks it too. Without the fix both fail.
        - deadLines:3, 8 nights (4 bots, 13 cars, `--as-app`, 5,400 s), main 47b9138 against main with the fix:
            - Car-loads home 0 → 1.88 a night; net −695 → +1,556.
            - Delivered 8 and 8; arrival 1,422 → 1,352 s; deaths 9 and 9.
            - The lift takes 78 s on seed 1, not a 240 s give-up.
    - **Not this.** The wreck yards (deadLines:7, deepTerritory:3) load as little for another reason: a car the Fire Flies lit at its lamp is burning as the train pulls in, so the winch's second hand is at the fire and its two handles are never held together (D1.4's #364).
