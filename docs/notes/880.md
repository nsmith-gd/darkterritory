880. **Encounter telemetry: an `EncounterLog` in the Sim, reported as the `encounters` block of `dt harness` and `dt playthrough` (queue #600, the spawn director overhaul's A1; the director's brief §8, note 874; n1-A1, nathaniel1).**
    - **What it is:** `Enemies/EncounterLog.cs`, host-side beside the director (`World.Encounters`, made with `World.Director`).
      It only watches; nothing in the Sim reads it back. It takes no draws and adds no wire state, so a night is the same with
      it off (`World.Encounters = null`). `EncounterLogTests.TheLogOnlyWatchesAndTheSameNightReportsTheSame` pins that, and
      that the same night twice gives the same block.
    - **How it hears:**
      - `World` tells it each spawn attempt (`Attempt`): the director's `Decide` → `rule.Spawn`, and the insisted kinds.
      - It tells it what each spawn call put in (`Came`, from an id mark): the Stoker, the hound runs, the drops and the hunts
        for the left behind are `special`; the house creatures, the Pickers, the Mourners, the lineside Moose and Dave are `world`.
      - Each tick's spine events give the grabs and punishes (`Heard`).
      - A sweep a second after the enemies step (`Sweep`) claims anything unclaimed as `world`, then reads each open
        encounter's members, the concurrency, the director's held reason and the drought clock.
    - **The records:**
      - `SpawnAttemptRecord`: tick, run seconds, progress, channel (`pressure`, `free`, `fill`, `stop`, `special`, `world`),
        kind, placed, failure reason, charged, members, crew, cars, debt, the encounter it made.
      - `Encounter`, one per spawn call and kind, so a pack is one. It holds:
        - channel, kind, members, zone;
        - where: `engine`, `car`, `roof`, `trackside` or `afield`; the vehicle; the train's third (`front`, `middle`, `rear`);
        - afoot (every living crewmate off the train);
        - seconds, progress, crew, cars;
        - overlap: the other encounters engaged when it came.
      - **Measured** (each `Encounter` keeps these apart from the inferred):
        - first engaged (`Director.Engaged`), first confronted (`Director.Confronting`: telegraph, commit, grab, punish), last engaged;
        - engaged and dormant seconds, grabs, punishes;
        - the end: `killed` (every member's health gone), `gone`, or `about`.
      - **Inferred:** `NearAt` and `NearestMetres` are a perception proxy: a crewmate within `nearMetres`. They mean "near",
        never "seen" (§8).
      - `Severity` is 0 until #610 writes it, and `Debt` 0 until #607/#608 set `EncounterLog.Debt` each second.
      - Hazards (the marsh, Dave, Jacob, a Wakers' line) and car fires are not encounters.
    - **The `encounters` block** (`EncounterReport.Of`):
      - attempts by channel (tried, placed, `chargedUnplaced`: the brief's issue 3), and failures by reason;
      - count, meaningful (a member confronted someone), bodies, kinds; by kind, channel, where, and train part;
      - `byQuarter`: encounters, and meaningful ones, in each quarter of the run's progress;
      - `firstSeconds`: the first encounter, and the first meaningful one;
      - ends: killed, gone, about, engaged and dormant seconds, grabs, punishes;
      - droughts, and the longest;
      - `concurrent`: seconds with 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4+ encounters engaged; peak active, peak bodies;
      - `suppressed`: seconds out by `Director.HeldBecause`;
      - severity counts, the debt peak, the inferred block, and the per-encounter `list`.
    - **Progress** is the larger of the run's seconds over the route's `DawnSeconds` and the train's distance over its
      `RunLength`, never falling. #606's `EncounterPacing.Progress` replaces it when it lands.
    - **"Meaningful", until #610:** a member confronted someone. A drought is at least `droughtSeconds` out on the run (the
      yard and the arrival not counted) with nothing confronting. #604's `ThreatState` and #610's severities are to refine this:
      a creature working on a train system counts as danger with nobody near it.
    - **Tuning:** `enemies.json` `director.telemetry` (`nearMetres` 25, `droughtSeconds` 90 from §7, `tracksideMetres` 30). It
      shapes the report only. It sits beside `director.encounters`, not under it, so it can't collide with #606/#607 adding that
      block.
    - **A first look** (frontier:7, 2 bots, 600 s, `dt harness --route frontier:7 --bots 2 --enemies --seconds 600`):
      - 9 encounters (6 meaningful, 5 kinds): 3 pressure, 3 fill, 2 stop, 1 world (a Householder asleep afield all night).
      - No failed attempts. The first meaningful came at 135 s, the night's one drought (0–135 s, held mostly "building").
      - Out of 589 s out, the director held 191 s "building", 104 "cooldown", 98 "nothing fits", 90 "at the cap", and sent 8.
      - Engaged 662 s against dormant 706 s, summed over encounters.
