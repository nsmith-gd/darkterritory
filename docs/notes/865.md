865. **Two on the run: a jumped pin that takes two (Otis's call 5 in note 861, "something for two on the run"; the director, 19 Oct: "Yes but not on solo nights") (Bram, queue #592).**
   - Claimed 2026-10-19 17:40 UTC by the Token Budget Manager (nathaniel3) for Bram.
   - **What it is** (GDD §12 "One can, two do it faster", the "Two on the run" bullet; systems spec B.20; upkeep.json `coupling.jump`): now and then, running at 12 m/s or more with two or more of the crew alive, a rail joint jumps a gap's pin clear (`LooseCause.Jumped`, a loose pin of its own kind: the same yard, fort, pack, stand and one-per-crewmate rules as #466's three causes, note 732). It's told as it jumps (note 854's line): "PIN JUMPED BEHIND CAR 3 · A RAIL JOINT AT 64 KM/H · TWO TO HOLD IT", heard as the slack's snatch and the pin lifting (AU1's own cue `state-coupling-loose.jump-joint` to come), and the gap's amber callout shows where.
   - **Reworked (the director, 19 Oct, having played it: "I want a pin to jump almost never and when it does it should have hella telegraphing to the players"; the PR, #1306, set to draft for it).** Below is the mechanic as first built; this is what changed:
     - **Almost never:** `perMinute` 0.25 → 0.03 and `maxPerNight` 1 (`Couplings.Jumped` counts them). Measured nights have 39-375 s a pin could jump in, about 230 on average (16 nights' `jumpSeconds`, frontier 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 2 and 4 bots); over 4000 nights drawn on their own streams against those, about one in nine jump (`TwoOnTheRunTests.ComesAboutOneNightInNine`, within 1/12 to 1/7). Never twice (`NeverTwiceANight`, with a jump due every tick for an hour).
     - **A warning first:** the jump now starts as `LooseCause.Working` (8, on the car's record: still protocol 80) for `warnSeconds` (10 s), and only then becomes `Jumped` (`Couplings.Step`; the train stood, the warning waits; two on it, it waits for them). Told at its start, "PIN WORKING LOOSE BEHIND CAR 3 · A RAIL JOINT AT 64 KM/H · GET TWO TO IT" (an instruction this once: the director's "hella telegraphing"), then at the jump "PIN JUMPED BEHIND CAR 3 · TWO TO HOLD IT". Heard: the knock quickening from 3 to 10 a second, full loud, the pin rattling between (`GameAudio.LooseSounds`), played from no further than 45 m off the ear toward the gap so the cab hears it (the knock's own sound stops at 60 m; AU1's own long-carrying `state-coupling-loose.warn` takes over when installed: a line for AU1's checklist); the jump, the slack's snatch and the pin flung up twice. Seen: the gap's callout `Urgent` (`BreakCallout.Urgent`, `Effects.Repairs`), beating 3.3 times a second against 1.1, half as big again, a ring swelling twice as far, the struck shower of sparks. Felt: `PinShake`, the eye of whoever's on either car of the gap shaking, 4 mm rising to 20 mm through the warning, a 35 mm jolt for 0.6 s at the jump, a 5 mm judder after (the settings' CAMERA SHAKE scales it).
     - **Caught in the warning:** two on it drive it home as on a jumped pin (`Couplings.TakesTwo` covers both causes everywhere: the hands, the HUD, the bots, the driver's ease), and letting go then parts nothing (`LetsGo`): the warning's the time to get there. `Couplings.Caught` (the harness's `jumpCaught`) counts those.
     - **Checked:** `TwoOnTheRunTests` 17/17 (new: the warning told and held, then the jump told; two catching it in the warning, a let-go then parting nothing; bot crews of 2 and 4 catching it in the warning; never twice; one night in nine); `dt audio render --scenario pin --listener 0|2` (the knock from the cab -31.6 dB beside the wheels' -31, from car 2's roof -28: the spectrograms show the knock's ring quickening to the jump at 10.5 s); `dt screenshot --view roof --hud --news working` and `--view gap --hud --news jumped`, looked at.
   - **It takes two** (`Couplings.Hands`, host): one in the gap holding it down (Use within the pin's reach; bare hands, no wrench) and a second taking the slack off it, either car's brake wheel over the gap held from its roof (or a headset's reach), or the throttle shut (`LastControls.Throttle` 0) with that crewmate in the cab; the train stood does it on its own (so a crew down to one can stop and pin it). Both on it, the car's `Vehicle.Pinned` is set (on the vehicle record, bit 10 of field 28) and the pinner's Use drives it home in `driveSeconds` (4 s, `CrewActions`, predicted on the pinner's client from the host's flag). Alone on it, the hands are on it and nothing moves: the prompt says "HOLD THE PIN : HOLD [E]   TAKES TWO: BRAKE WHEEL OR THROTTLE SHUT"; the wheel's prompt over such a gap is "TAKE THE SLACK OFF THE PIN"; both on it, "DRIVE THE PIN HOME (n%)".
   - **If either lets go, it parts** (`Couplings.LetsGo`): once both have had hold of it, either off it for `letGo` (0.3 s, a dropped packet's grace) parts it then and there, and what's behind rolls free. The line and the report say who and what: "CARS 3-6 LOST · DAVE LET GO OF THE BRAKE WHEEL", "· PRIYA LET GO OF THE PIN", "· DAVE OPENED THE THROTTLE"; the report "Two had hold of the jumped pin, Dave let go of the brake wheel, and it parted." (`Attribution.LetGo`, `LooseCause.LetGoPin/Brake/Throttle`, only ever the report's and the news's, never a car's). Nobody on it, it parts after `partAfter` (60 s, a worked-loose pin's 90): "THE PIN THE RAIL JOINT JUMPED".
   - **Never solo:** `minCrew` 2 alive (bots count: they're crew), from the host's own actors; `TwoOnTheRunTests.ItNeverJumpsOnANightOfOneAndDoesWithTwo` with a jump every tick.
   - **Bots:** a walker (or the gunner of a crew of two) goes to it as to any loose pin, wrench or none, and holds Use (`RoofWalkerBot.LoosePin`, `Grease`, `Heed.Coupling`); the bot driver shuts the throttle while any crewmate is within reach of a jumped pin (`ConductorBot.Ease`), so a bot crew drives it home and a person in the gap gets a bot driver's help. With a person driving, the person has to shut it (the cab doesn't do it for them). Not built: a bot going up to the brake wheel for a person on the pin (the driver's ease covers it with a bot driving).
   - **Its own draws** (`Couplings._jumps`, a Pcg32 stream of its own), so the night's other pins come where they did; but a night with a jump plays differently from then on. **Found:** `Pcg32` is a mutable struct, and a `readonly` field of it draws from a fresh copy each call, the same first number forever: `_jumps` was readonly at first, and no night ever jumped (an 8-night sweep, 0 jumps in 260-375 eligible seconds each). It isn't now (`TwoOnTheRunTests.ItComesAsOftenAsItsRateSaysAtGapsOfItsOwnPicking`). **The same bug is on main, untouched here:** `HotBoxes._rng`, `Gutters._rng`, `Couplings._rng` (the gap a hard brake or a bend picks) and `Wreck._rng` are all `readonly Pcg32`: every hot box and lamp comes at the same spacing and the same share along the train, every run-in or bend loosens the same share of the tight gaps, the wreck's jostles repeat. Fixing it moves every night's upkeep; it wants an item of its own.
   - **Otis's `shared` watch (#1292, note 861):** it counts a `pairJob` for two crewmates the census reads as committed within 6 m. A pinner and a hand at the brake wheel over the gap are a few metres apart; whether the census reads both as committed wasn't checked here (its `IsCommitted` is S1's, note 753). A pinner and the driver easing are 20 m and more apart and aren't counted. Once #1292 is on main, a line in `SharedWatch.Watch` counting `Couplings.Hands`' pair (pinner, second) as a `pairJob` would count every one, wherever the second is. Left for after #1292 merges, to keep out of Otis's file.
