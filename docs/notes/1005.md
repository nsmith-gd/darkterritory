1005. **Houses with floors and dark basements (Larch, the Token Budget Manager's worker, nathaniel2; queue #710; the director's 23 Oct review, `docs/playtest/2026-10-23.md` line 36; note 949's question 4, upstairs).** *In review.*
   - The director: "The houses are super basic that we generate for the villages. I think we maybe want more complex houses
     that have multiple levels, including basements. Those basements should get pretty dark. They should have windows that
     you can escape from, especially. Maybe a couple doors. And we should spawn fewer of them. It's going to help us hide
     loot. It's going to make looking in a house feel a bit more scary. Especially people going in that basement should feel
     like high risk, high reward sometimes."
   - **The plan, as built.**
     - **Forms** (stops.json `village.forms`, `HouseForm` on `StopBuilding.Form`): an open plain house (a rectangle or a
       square, `StopGenerator.Storeyed`) is built as `upstairsBasement` 0.4, `basement` 0.3, `upstairs` 0.15 or `plain`
       0.15 (one floor, as before), one draw a house after the village is laid; at least `leastBasements` (1) a village
       with a plain house, the plain houses furthest from the line given one first. L, cross and paired houses stay one floor.
     - **Fewer houses** (stops.json `village.keep` 0.65): a lot the village's form would build on is left empty at that
       share (counted as built, so the form doesn't fill it again). With the finds below, a village keeps about as many
       finds as before, more of them a house (StopGeneratorTests' share: 0.9–2.2 a house).
     - **The shell** (`StopWalls.Storeys.cs`, `StopWalls.Shell`, one list the sim stands and the art draws): one room on the
       ground floor (the floors are its rooms: no back room), a front door and a back door in the end away from it (an
       open doorway, its lintel over), the side walls and ends up to run.json's walls.topM with lintels over every opening.
       - **Upstairs:** a straight flight of 10 rises (0.28 m each, under player.json's stepUp 0.35, both ways) and 0.4 m
         treads (deeper than a crewmate is round, 0.3 m, or the tread after next is in their way before they're on the
         next: the first build's 0.28 m treads stuck everyone at the first), 1 m wide, along the side wall away from the door,
         from 0.9 m in from the front end up toward the back; the upper floor 2.8 m up, a slab open over the flight, a rail
         along the well's open side and across its end.
       - **Basement:** the house raised 0.9 m on a stone plinth, three steps up to each door; the ground floor a slab; a
         0.9 m hatch let into it in the back corner away from the stair, a ladder down its room side to the basement's floor
         2.5 m under the boards (a world ladder, as note 973's houseboats' are: the ladder key or Use takes hold of it from
         the boards); two cellar windows high in the side walls toward the front (0.9 m wide, 5 to 65 cm over the ground
         outside), each with three crates stacked under it, a world ladder whose top is the sill: up it and out over the
         sill onto the ground outside (and in again from outside). The hatch and the windows are the ways out; the deep end,
         away from the ladder, is where the chests are.
       - **Under the boards:** in a basement's footprint the earth isn't what anything stands on (`StopWalls.Under`): the
         basement's floor is, for a crewmate (`PlayerMotor.UpdateSupport`) and what's thrown or dropped (`Bodies`).
     - **The finds** (`StopContainer.Level`, `.Slot`): a house with a basement keeps a cellar's find down there (a chest
       against the deep end's wall), one with an upstairs its cabinet's up there (against the back wall); besides its
       others, a basement find at `forms.basementFind` (0.6) and an upstairs one at `upstairsFind` (0.35). On the ground
       floor each kind has its own places clear of the flight, the hatch and the doors (`KeptStoreyed`), each find its slot
       (its rank among its kind's on its floor). Loot.json `levelDraws`: a basement find is drawn 3 times and the worthiest
       kept, an upstairs one 2 (on a stream of its own, `StopSeed.Deeper`, so every other find a seed deals is as it was).
       The search reaches only a spot on the searcher's own floor (loot.json search's 2 m).
     - **The dark:** a house's basement is a dark room (`Room.Dark`): its fill is look.json `interiors.cellarFill` (0.12)
       of a room's, and no candle's lit there. A hand lamp is what you see by.
     - **Bots** keep to the ground floor (their path, `FootPath`, takes a house with floors' floors, steps and lintels as
       no obstacle; they don't choose a find on another floor). **The house creatures** (the Lodger, the Householder, the
       Hollow House, the Hanger) and **the Gaunt's roost** keep to one-floor houses, and a stop's jackpot house (note 719) is
       never one with floors (its back door stands open).
   - **Decisions (the director's to change):** a basement by a hatch and a ladder, not a flight: committing, and slower to
     get out of than in (the windows are the other way); the upstairs a dead end with no window to climb out of (a 4 m drop
     is spec B.3's knock, and nothing would have it); the back door an open doorway, no leaf to shut.
