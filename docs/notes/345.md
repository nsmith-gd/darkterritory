345. **The orchestrator's census of posts and slack (D1.2 for D1, queue #82; [orchestrator.md](design/orchestrator.md) §3.1, §3.2 items 2–4; GDD App. F.3).** Note 336 gave the director the live crew and its caps; this gives it who's where and who's idle. enemies.json `director.orchestrator`; `Enemies/Census.cs`; `Director.Count`, `Director.Posts`, `Director.Next`, `Director.WhoseNext`.
    - **The census** (once a second, host only, players and threats by id): each crewmate alive and their `Post`:
        - cab: in it, or on the engine; the driver and the fireman share it;
        - gunner: seated at a gun;
        - walker: the roofs, a ladder, a gap or a landing;
        - rider: a car's floor inside its walls;
        - ground: off the train within `abandoned.behindM`;
        - left behind: further off; their own hunt answers them (note 273).

      Which engaged threats are on whom: the one it holds; else the nearest within `onRadius` (20 m); else the nearest crewmate at a post that `answers` it (a pack running behind is the gunner's).

      Their slack: seconds since they last had something to answer. Something to answer is a threat on them; an upkeep job on their car (an open hot box, note 331; a guttering lamp, D1's note 346, merged meanwhile); a hot box or a coupling working loose (D1's note 356) anywhere for a walker (greased from the landing, made fast in the gap; a loose coupling also for anyone on either car of its gap), or a guttering lamp anywhere for a rider (trimmed inside); or, in the cab, the train moving (the line and the fire are the driver's).
    - **Slack counts only on the run between stops:** the train moving faster than `drivingAbove` (0.5 m/s), past the grace, out of the forts and facilities, short of the final approach. A generated stop keeps the run `Underway` while the train stands to be worked. Counted then, the first crew-4 nights showed the driver at 530 s of slack with nothing wrong.
    - **It steers** (`census`, with the crew it counted at `minCrew`, 2, or more; a crew of one is App. B.1's as it was):
        - who's next: the free crewmate with the most slack, `minSlack` (30 s) or more. A kind answered at their post weighs ×`slackWeight` (2); a kind answered only at posts nobody holds, ×`emptyPostWeight` (0.5), except the Track Doll, whose rule is the empty cab.
        - slack presses: + `slackPerSecond` (0.05) a second for each crewmate at `slackPress` (150 s) or more.
        - one threat on any one player (`perTarget`): a kind whose every answering crewmate already has one on them weighs nothing; if that leaves nothing, it holds ("one on each"). It works on the pick, not as a hold on the crew: with the cap at ceil(0.75 × active), the whole crew is never taken before the cap holds.
    - **Two fixes found on the way:**

        - The "one on each" hold also caught lists already weighing nothing for other reasons, which App. B.1 sends from anyway (its last pick). That changed solo nights. It now holds only when the census zeroed them.
