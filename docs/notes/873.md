873. **A crate hand sent to shut a car's door stood still at the foot of its steps (queue #553; B3's find on #532, note 820).**
The row said a hand idles inside a full car. What the trace showed (`StopCrewTests`' Choir-loading night on a hand-built line,
from 680 m instead of 700) was the same outcome from another cause.
    - **The fault:** `StopHand.ShutUp` takes a hand on the ground up the steps with `Head` only while it's within a step's width
      (`outward < StepWidth - 0.1` = 0.5 m); otherwise `WalkTo` goes to the foot of the steps. `WalkTo` counts it arrived within
      0.25 m of a point 0.3 m out, so it stops at up to 0.55 m: past the window and arrived, it returns no step, for good. Car 2's
      door stayed open for the stop's 300 s give-up (the train left at 751 s, not 419 s). The hand's `Doing` stayed "shutting up".
    - **Fixed:** the window runs out to the step's whole width (`outward < StepWidth`, 0.6 m). `Head` then walks it onto the tread.
    - **Pinned by** `StopCrewTests.ACrateHandStoppedAtTheFootOfTheStepsStillShutsItsDoor` (fails at 751 s on main, passes at 419 s).
      The Sim is no different on the wire; the intents are the bots' own, so `NetcodeTests` are unaffected.
    - **Not this:** from 600 m the same night stalls to 747 s on a heavy crate nobody can lift together ("to lend a hand" for 150 s+),
      the door open all along: that's note 843's follow-up, not changed here.
