185. **Facility set pieces: the grain elevator's spout, the slaughterhouse's herd, the chemical works' hose, the depot's powder (GDD §18, §19; spec D.2; WP15).**
    - **Built on what was there:** every facility already had its spur, its stop layout, its art, its cargo (note 182) and spec D.2's crates, winch and crane. Three more D.2 modules join `ModuleKind` (`Spout`, `Ramp`, `Hose`, on the end), laid out by `Site` from the route like the rest, and run on the host in `Run.StepSetPieces` (`Run/SetPieces.cs`). They work whether or not the engine counts as "stopped" (a train walked a car's length under the spout is moving). `facilities.json` `kinds`: the grain elevator is its spout; the slaughterhouse the ramp and crates; the chemical works the hose and crates; the depot keeps crates and winch, its crates being its powder.
    - **Grain elevator (§18 "one spout, one car at a time. Endless repositioning and switch calls"):** a bin on legs astride the spur, its spout as far back from the buffer stop as the standing track allows (`spout.back` 80 m), so the car nearest the engine comes under it with the engine well short of the buffer stop and the cars behind it as the engine runs on. Grain comes down only while someone holds the lever on the ground (`pourPerSecond` 0.05 a second from a `bin` of 3 car-loads), into the cargo car whose middle is within `tolerance` (1.5 m) of the spout: nothing there, it's on the ballast; a full car there overflows, and the overflow strains the car (D.2's chute: "overfill damages car and spills"). It's machinery: loud while it pours.
      - **Bots:** once the stop's other loading is done the driver takes a `Spouting` leg. It puts each car with room under the spout in turn, nearest the buffer stop first so the train only ever backs, the way it backs out anyway (`StopPlan.SpoutTarget`). It holds while grain's coming down and while anyone but the shunter is still climbing aboard. The shunter stands at the lever and holds it while a car with room stands under the spout, letting go the tick it's full.
    - **Slaughterhouse (§18 "animals are loud, and something already lives here"; D.2 livestock ramp):** a pen of `ramp.head` (4–6, by the seed) beyond a ramp up to a car's doorway. With `herders` (2) or more in the pen holding Use and a car with room at the ramp's top, one goes up every `secondsPerHead` (6 s), a fifth of a car-load of livestock each. Every herder past two quickens it by a quarter. From the first one aboard until the last, the herd is stirred: the Choir's floor is `ramp.choirFloor` (D.2 "raises Choir floor while active"), and driving them is machinery-loud. "Something already lives here" is enemies.json `director.residents`: while stopped at a slaughterhouse the Gaunt comes on ×4 and Followers ×1.5. **Bots:** the winch pair drive the herd, then carry crates.
    - **Chemical works (§18 "Leaks. Do not fire indoors"; D.2 fluid gantry):** hold Use at the stand for `connectSeconds` (2.5 s) to put the hose on the cargo car with room by it, and again to take it off. On, it fills the car (`flowPerSecond` 0.04). Its pressure climbs `pressureRise` (0.05) a second unless someone stands at the stand, which bleeds it at 0.15. At 1 it leaks: everyone within `leakRadius` (6 m) takes `leakDamagePerSecond` (12, a point at a time), death cause `Leak`, attributed to whoever put the hose on; and the car's load spoils at `spoilPerSecond`. Moving the car off with the hose on tears it: it sprays for `tornSeconds` (20 s) and the load spoils by `tornSpoil` (0.25) at once. **Bots:** Winch0 puts it on, stands by it while the car fills, takes it off once it's full; the driver doesn't leave with it on.
    - **"Do not fire indoors":** a cannon fired from a car within `hose.indoorsM` (60 m) of the works' stand gasses as one fired beside a loaded chemicals car does (note 182, `World.Fumes`): the gunner and everyone within 8 m of the gun's car, death cause `Poisoned`, attributed to the gunner.
    - **Military depot (§18 "best payout, worst cargo"; §19 "explodes"):** its crates are powder kegs (cargo ammunition). A keg brought up short by more than `kegs.blowSpeed` (6 m/s) goes up: that's a throw, or a fall from a roof. Set down by hand it falls a hand's height, about 3.7 m/s, and nothing happens. The change is measured on its world velocity tick to tick, so frames don't matter, and never on the tick it leaves someone's hands. Everyone within `killRadius` (3 m) takes 150, less out to 9 m, death cause `Keg` (attributed to whoever last carried it). Cars within reach lose 0.3 integrity, every keg within `chain` (3 m) goes up the tick after, and every client sees and hears it as a blast (`World.Blast`). A powder car at full blaze already went up (note 182). **Bots:** the crate hands already set crates down inside the car, and never throw them.
    - **The harm lands next tick:** a blast or a leak found in `Run.Step` (after the world's damage has been applied for the tick) is handed out from the next tick's crew step (`Run.Harm`), as the crane's crush already was (`Crushes`).
    - **Headless:** `dt facility drill <kind>` has a bot crew work a facility of that kind (a hand-tuned frontier night's first spur facility made that kind, or `--route tier:seed`'s own) from 600 m short of its spur until the train leaves, all through intent (`Bots/FacilityWork`), and reports legs, what each hand did, each car's load and cargo, and the set piece's state. All four leave with every car down the spur full of the facility's cargo, the crew alive and the switch back. `dt screenshot --route r --site --facility <kind> [--leak]` shows the set piece working.
    - **Readings:**
      - "Endless repositioning and switch calls" is the spout's one car at a time with the train walked under it. The cars are already in the spur and the spout reaches every one of them, so no extra switch moves are invented.
      - "Do not fire indoors" is a cannon fired anywhere in among the works (60 m of its stand takes in every car down the spur). There is no indoors for a train's guns, and personal weapons don't fire.
      - D.2's livestock ramp "raises Choir floor while active" is from the first head aboard until the last (or while being driven). A herd still standing quiet in its pen doesn't count.
      - The grain elevator offers its spout alone, not D.1's 2–4 modules: with crates too, the crates filled the cars before the spout got a turn.
