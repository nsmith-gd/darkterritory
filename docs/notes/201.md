201. **Hazards, the rest: wind on a roof's footing, the cold step on the HUD, mending a broken radio (GDD §22, §23; WP14, note 183's "not yet").**
    - **Wind on footing (§22; spec B.2 "roof run: wind and balance penalty"):** up on a roof, `PlayerMotor.WindPush` adds a sideways push across the car to the motor's velocity: `ITrackConditions.Wind` where you are (the night's, ×1.5 on exposed track) × `player.json wind.drift` (0.3 m/s), from `wind.still` (0.4) of that with the train stopped up to all of it at `train.json maxSpeed`, in gusts. ×`wind.walking` (0.5) walking or standing; a roof run takes it all. The roof handrails take it to `composition.rails.wind` (0.4) of that; a gun's seat, a grabbed player and anyone off a roof get none. At the worst (exposed, flat out, running) about 0.45 m/s: walking against it holds you, but stood at the edge in a gust it walks you off, and the fall is the landing rules' (never a kill of its own).
    - **Reading: the gusts.** The weather has a wind but no direction, so the push comes in gusts from either side: a hash of each `wind.gustMetres` (120 m) of line where you are (`PlayerMotor.Gust`, −1 to +1, eased from one to the next). No trig and no dice, and the conditions are built from the night's seed on every machine, so a predicting client pushes exactly as the host does.
    - **Reading: no crouch, no camera roll.** The sim has no crouch to resist it with (a headset player crouches for real, unseen), so walking rather than running is the balance, and the rails are the upgrade. The drift is what's seen; a camera roll would be felt in a headset's stomach.
    - **The cold step on the HUD (§22):** under the radio line, top right, while the line where you are has a cold step: "DEEP COLD" (1), "BITTER COLD" (2), "KILLING COLD" (3+), then what it does ("OUTSIDE, IT COMES ON 1.5X FASTER", from `cold.perColdStep`). Amber out in it, dim in the warm. A client knows it as the host does (`PlayerMotor.ColdStep`, the route's conditions built from the seed). `dt screenshot --hud --hazards cold` stages a hazard set over the line.
    - **Mending a broken radio (§23 "radio breaks"; §12 "the engineer is whoever has the kit"):** the repair kit in hand, at nothing Use works, and a broken radio on your own belt or lying in reach (a crewmate takes theirs off with [RMB] to hand it over): Use held, standing, for `train.json kit.radioMendSeconds` (8 s, a third of the boiler's 25) mends it. Moving or letting go starts it over. Like a locker's tap and hold, the hands wait for the release, so a tap still puts the kit down. Host-decided in `Bodies.Handle` from intent, and mirrored on the body record as the broken flag is. The HUD prompts "[E] HOLD: MEND YOUR RADIO WITH THE KIT (8S)", then "MENDING ... (n%)" with its bar, and "RADIO BROKEN" now adds ": THE REPAIR KIT MENDS IT".
    - **Reading:** the kit, not the lockers. §12 has the kit mend "a ruptured boiler, and nothing else can"; that says nothing else mends a boiler, not that the kit mends nothing else. A spare radio from the stores is still the quicker fix.
    - `kit.radioBreakPerDamage` and `radioBreakOnGrab` are written into train.json now (they were code defaults).
    - Protocol 23: a broken radio's body record carries how far the mending's got (0 whole, 1 + ticks broken). Tests: `HazardTests` (the push and its sum over a second, a run, the rails and the gun seat, the gusts both ways and smooth, a cold-and-windy harness night predicted exactly, the cold step, mending held, a tap putting the kit down, walking not mending, a loose radio mended), `HudTests` (the cold line, the mend prompt and its progress).
