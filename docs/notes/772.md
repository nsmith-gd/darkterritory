772. **The Wakers' dawn heard (AU1, queue #502; note 588, docs/design/creatures/wakers.md §2-§5, §8.4: "the sounds to
AU1").** What gets up at dawn makes no sound: the stir's thuds are felt (the tremor, the lamps, the coal) but not heard,
its calls are unheard, and the rise, the chase, the catch and the eating are silent.
- **The stir:** its thuds are heard on `DawnStir.Thud`'s own beat, so each lands with the tremor and the lamps' swing. The
  app hands `GameAudio` its stir clock (`StirClock`); headless, the audio's own. Long low calls come from behind the
  hills, more often by dawn, below the night creatures' range.
- **At dawn:** the land getting up (a Waker's first tick is its rise). Its footfalls follow `WakerGait` as it runs the
  train down, with a roar now and then, and it roars stood at the walls.
- **The catch:** the last car seized; the train dragged while it still rolls; each car after the last torn up as
  `Waker.Lifted` counts it; every car eaten as `EatenCars` counts it.
- **Those who ran (note 710):** one rises beside them and strides over on its walk, then a grip and a swallow at its mouth.
- **Range:** hill-sized, so far beyond the areas' (install.py `CUE_DEF`): a rise at 1.2 km about -10 dB, a footfall
  there about -19 dB, full by 50 m.
