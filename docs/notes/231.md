231. **The Deep sweep: steam against the brake, Sleepers in the grease, a step off the roof beside the plate (T81; note 131's "next").** Note 131 ended with deepTerritory:1 delivering at a crew of 2 and deepTerritory:2 standing at 24.5 km; note 188 found the stand gone. Since then nobody had run the Deep sweep.
    - **Measured** (`dt balance --tiers deepTerritory --seeds 4 --crews 2,8`, 10 cars, the sweep's 5,400 s window, about 20 min on 4 cores). On main, 1 night of 8 delivered (deepTerritory:2 at 8). Five derailed, four of them on the Sleepers at 42–44 km/h, at the same place for both crew sizes (deepTerritory:1 at 17.48 km, :4 at 9.08 km).
    - **Steam against a faded brake.** With steam driving (T97) the engine pulls at full effort against the brake while its steam would make more than the train's speed. On deepTerritory:4 the train came off a 1.3 % descent at 12 m/s with the brake faded to 0.45 (spec B.5's fade on descents) and steam for 16 m/s (the gauge at 75). The lamp found the Sleepers 120 m out, and the driver braked all the way: 10 cars brake at 1.01 m/s² fresh and pull at 0.42, so at 0.45 the brake was a match for the steam and no more, and the train lost 0.3 m/s. The vent is the counter (T97: "the vent, or the brake, for less"; T106 pairs them for a board), but the fireman vents only for what he thinks the line allows, and that never included the Sleepers the driver was braking for. A driver alone (a crew under the fireman's six) never vents at all.
      - The Sleepers in the lamp are now a limit in their own right (`ConductorBot.HazardAllow`): under their derailing speed (enemies.json `sleepers.derailAbove`) by `UnderSleepers`, 1.6 m/s. The driver's cruise takes it, and so does the fireman's (`MindingCruise`). So the fireman vents for them, and stops firing for 14 m/s.
      - A driver alone vents for them himself (the cab's vent is a step across, T109), with the brake held.
    - **Sleepers in the grease, down a grade.** Once the first ones were passed, deepTerritory:4 at 19.56 km and :1 at 17.48 km derailed on Sleepers lying in greased rail on a 2–2.5 % descent. The lamp first finds them once the engine is 10 m into the grease, where the brakes barely bite (App. A.2) and the grade gains on them. Now greased rail seen ahead on a fall of over 0.5 % (`Lineside.GreaseAhead`, the lamp's 150 m) is entered under the same speed, not braked for once on it.
    - **The brake fading on a long descent.** With those fixed, deepTerritory:1 at crew 8 ran 7 km further and took a 45 km/h bend at 57. Down the grade, the driver held 14 m/s on the brake against steam for 17.7 m/s. The brake faded to its 0.25 floor, and the train ran away at 16 m/s. The fireman's vent never came because the train never read over what was allowed: the brake held it there until it was gone. Now a brake faded under `FadedBrake` (0.7) is vented, by the fireman or a driver alone, down to steam that doesn't pull at the speed held (`driveSpeedBand` short of it). The fire is kept to that as well, so it isn't fired back up against the vent.
    - **A step off the roof beside the plate.** On deepTerritory:3 the gunner of a crew of two, going in from the guard van's roof to the car ahead, stepped off its front end 7 cm right of the roof's middle. Since #94 the plates lie on the end doors' line (`PlateX`, 0.45 m left of centre), so a plate's right edge is 5 cm right of the middle, and walkers still aimed at the middle. He went down past the plate's edge to the ballast at 14 m/s and was left on 1 hp. Then he was dragged off a roof, and the driver alone lost the train to the Stoker.
      - `WarmUp` now steers along the roof to the plate's line, and at the end doesn't step off until it's over the plate (`PlateMargin` inside its edge).
      - The stop crew's walk to a cut does the same (`StopHand.AlongRoofs`).
