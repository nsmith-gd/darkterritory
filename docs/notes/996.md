996. **The Tower Jaw's felled tower never ends a run, and its fall is a real threat (n1-B3, nathaniel1; queue #696; the director's playtest of 23 Oct, `docs/playtest/2026-10-23.md` lines 1 and 26).**
   - Claimed 2026-10-23 17:05 UTC by n1-B3 (nathaniel1).

   - **What happened** (line 1, a campaign night on local:509, twice): "a beaver" (the Tower Jaw, docs/design/creatures/tower-jaw.md) gnawed a stop's coaling tower down across the main line. The train ran into its wreck: "Engine just breaks down immediately, and now we're perma-stuck ... the player has no way of dealing with it".
   - **Why it locked the night** (read, then pinned by test):
     - The wreck stops a train run into it and hurts the car that hits it by (speed − safeSpeed)² × damagePerSpeed (0.06). At 12 m/s that's 7.3, against an engine's whole 1.
     - An engine at no integrity is Broken (`Failing.Stage`): no power. Stood, the night ends stranded (`Run`, GDD §23.2).
     - Clearing the wreck (Use held at it, 30 crew-seconds; the bots do it, `Heed.Six`) couldn't help a dead engine.
     - Nothing on the screen said the wreck can be cleared.
     - `TowerJawTests.ATrainRunIntoTheWreckIsStoppedThereAndHurtByItsSpeed` ran in at 12 m/s and checked only that the engine was hurt: it passed on a dead engine.
   - **Why it did nothing elsewhere** (line 26): the fall crushes crew within crushRadius (3.5 m) and nothing else; a car under it was untouched.
   - **The fix** (`content/tuning/enemies.json` `towerJaw`, deterministic, the host's):
     - `wreckLeaves` 0.3: the wreck's blow, and its fall, never take a car or the engine below 0.3 of itself (`TowerJaw.Dent`). A dent the wrench mends (`Repairs`), never a breakdown. The engine still pulls (`Failing.Power` at 0.3: the failing stage's reduced pull).
     - `crushCar` 0.35: a car within crushRadius of its sides or ends where the tower comes down loses 0.35, floored the same.
     - The crosshair at the wreck (within clearReach, 4 m): `CLEAR THE WRECK`, held, the crew's seconds so far (`Hud.WreckPrompt`).
     - The wreck still closes the line until it's cleared: that's the creature's punish (its brief: "get it off before it falls"), and now it costs time and a dent, not the night.
   - **Tests:**
     - `TowerJawTests.NoFallLeavesTheLineImpassable(20, 6)`: run into the wreck at 20 m/s (the fastest tier's line speed) and at 6. The engine is left at wreckLeaves and still pulls; one crewmate clears it in clearCrewSeconds; the train runs on past where it lay. On the old numbers, 20 m/s took 21.7 of the engine: no power, stranded.
     - `ATrainRunIntoTheWreck…` now also checks the engine isn't Broken.
     - `ItsFallDentsACarStoodUnderItNeverBreaksIt`: the train stood at the tower, its cars under the fall lose crushCar, the others nothing.
     - `Game.Tests.WreckPromptTests`: the prompt at the wreck, none out of reach.
   - **Not reproduced as a whole night:** `dt harness --as-app --route local:509 --bots 1 --enemies --upkeep --insist towerJaw --seed 509` sent no Tower Jaw (its rule wants a coaling tower at a stop the bots stand at); the mechanism is pinned in the tests above instead.
