381. **The grain elevator modelled (queue #118, C1; GDD §18's set pieces, the art checklist's `grain-elevator`).** The grain elevator was still the kit's: three plain cylinders 26 m high with a box on them. It was the weakest of the demo's facilities beside the modelled headframe, chemical tank and watchtower (facility_pieces). Now it's built as a prairie concrete elevator is (`tools/models/recipes/facility_pieces.py` `grain_elevator`, 612 triangles against a budget of 3000):
    - **The silos:** four slip-formed concrete bins in a row along the track, 8.8 m across and 26 m high, touching, with interstice walls closing the gaps between them. The slip form's pour lines every 1.5 m and the rain streaks off the gallery's eaves down the line side are baked onto the shell. Each is 28-sided in the game mesh, so it reads round from the track.
    - **Over them:** the bin floor's gallery in rusted corrugated iron, its windows dark but one lit; and the leg house over the far end, another 12 m up under a pyramid roof (where the bucket elevator's legs run), one window lit. A caged ladder runs up the near silo. At the foot, the receiving shed stands on the line side, its doorway dark.
    - **The spout:** swung down from the gallery to 6 m over the track and 2.5 m out from its centre (a car's side is 1.5 m out), banded, its canvas sock hanging, held up by two stays from the leg house. It's dressing: the sim still pours from the site's bin on its legs, 80 m along (`spout.back`).
    - **One layer at 1024:** some 4,500 m² of concrete and iron would get about 13 cm a texel at the props' 512. `cook.finish` takes a `size` now (unset, it's 512 as before), and the elevator's layer is a hero layer: GreyboxRenderer keeps those in arrays of their own (up to 64; the creatures and crew use about 20).
    - **Where:** StructureKit's `GrainElevator` case sets it where the kit's silos stood, 15 m out on the site's side, and keeps the kit's silos as the fallback when the model isn't there. At frontier:7's elevator that's 19 m off the spur and 25 m back from the site's layout point.
    - **`dt screenshot --site --facility <kind> --building [m]`:** a facility's buildings rather than its machinery. The camera stands 50 m along its track and 5 m out at a crewman's eye, looking up at what stands `m` out (19 by default, the elevator's silos). The site's other set-piece cameras are unchanged.
    - **Verified:** `dt art check` (612 triangles against the large prop's 8,000 and the facility's 90,000), `PropArtTests`, `StopArtTests`, `LookTests`. Looked at: the turntable, and frontier:7's elevator at night and at dawn, before and after.
