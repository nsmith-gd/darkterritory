274. **The fortresses are solid, and the guns hit them (T124; build 1121's notes, GDD App. F: "Fort buildings have no collision, and gun shots hit nothing").** T114's `StopWalls` already stood a village's houses, barns and outbuildings as boxes that `PlayerMotor.Collide` pushes a crewmate out of and that `Guns.Solid` stops a ball at (`ImpactSurface.Structure`, the impact told to everyone as a `CannonImpact`). The fortresses were never in it: their walls, gun towers, gatehouse and houses were laid out only by the art pass (`WorldFeatures.Fortress`, from the line), so the sim had nothing there to collide with or hit.
    - **The layout moved into the sim.** `Sim.Run.Fortresses` holds the rules the art used: `Of(route, line, yardLength, terminusZone)` gives the home fortress's yard (0 to the gate at `YardLength`, its platform, lived in) and the terminus's (the plan's `Terminus.GateM`, or `TerminusZone` + 200 m short of the end on a hand-laid route, to the end of the line; dark and empty when `Terminus.Silent`). `InTheVillage`, `OnThePlatform` and `HouseAt` are the village's spacing and gaps; `LivedSize`/`House(variant)` a house's size from its seed (`HouseSeed` 7001, the draws `TownKit.LivedHouse` makes first). `WorldFeatures.Fortress`, `FortVillage`, `FortFolk`, `TownKit.LivedHouse` and `GreyboxScene` now read those, so a solid is wherever its piece is drawn, and nothing drawn moved (`dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --at 420 --view roof` looked at).
    - **The solids** (`Fortresses.Solids`, added to `StopWalls.Of` by `World.EnableRun`, alike on the host and every client): each 10 m wall bay a box 1.6 m thick, 14.8 m out, up to the crenellations' tops (8.9 m); each gun tower 5.4 m square, 14.4 m high; the gatehouse's two towers (3.5–8.5 m out, 7 m deep, 14.9 m) and the arch over the line from 7.8 to 13 m, above anyone on a roof; each lived-in house its footprint up to its ridge. Every box stands on the rail's height where it is and runs 3 m into the ground, as the pieces are set. These are the art's own sizes, not design numbers, so they stay in code beside the rules (as `StopWalls`' 9 m does).
    - **Bodies too.** `Bodies.Contact` now takes every `StopWalls` box as well as the cars and the ground: a crate, lamp or ragdoll thrown at a house or a fort wall comes to rest against it (it had passed through every building, villages included). Its contact is the world's (`touchedCar` −2), as the ground's is.
    - **Not solid (the director's call if they should be):** the yards' sheds and hero buildings (unchanged from T114: their crates and crane bays are worked at and in them), a Holdout's building, the home fortress's platform (a low floor with thin posts), the facilities' buildings (`StructureKit.Facility`, still placed by the art alone) and the dead towns, churches and windmills far out on the lineside (`WorldArt` settlements, `PlanArt`: placed with the art's own `System.Random`, beyond where a crew walks). Creatures don't read `StopWalls` (keeping them out of a fortress is T128).
