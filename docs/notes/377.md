377. **Bots bring the powder (queue #114, D1.3 for D1; [orchestrator.md](design/orchestrator.md) §5.1 U4, §6.2 item 2: "it makes the guns a two-person job in a wave"; note 374's "a walker bringing it while the gunner keeps firing is the outline's crew job; bots don't split it yet").**
    - **What it does.** One walker brings a manned gun (a crewmate in its seat) its powder. It takes a charge from the guard van's locker and fills the rack from beside the gun (`Guns.MannedGun`'s 1 m reach, 0.7 m to the side of the pivot), so the gunner keeps the seat and keeps firing.
    - **When it goes** (`PowderCarry.Wanting`), from `combat.json` `guns`:
      - A gun in action (a shot in the last `feedWhileFiring`, 20 s) wants a charge whenever its rack is a round short. The walker then stands by at the gun with it and fills the rack after the next shot. A ten-car train's walk to the guard van is about a minute; a wave isn't that long.
      - Otherwise a gun wants one when its rack is low: `feedAt` (2) for the guard van's own gun, `feedAtFar` (5) for one on another car (the engine's).
      - `feedAt` -1 turns it all off, and the gunner fetches its own (note 374).
      - A bot sees a shot by its own tick (`ShotWatch`): a gun's `LastShotTick` is the host's world tick, which a client's world doesn't keep, so the bot notes when it changes.
    - **Who goes** (`PowderCarry.Carrier`) is worked out alike by every bot, with nobody calling it:
      - Whoever already has a charge in hand.
      - Failing that, the lowest player id among the walkers who say, each tick, that they're free for it (`CrewCalls.CanFeed`). A walker is free unless it's in for the cold, a tunnel or the Choir, or working trouble in a car. One waiting in a car for a mail bag counts as free and leaves the bag: the gun comes first.
      - The claim is instant, like `CanBreach`, because "busy" can't be read off a crewmate from outside: a walker waiting for a mail bag looks the same as one warming up. A walker already on a run stays free while its warm-up's way out of a car takes it out.
      - The gunner's own legs never say it (`RoofWalkerBot.Feeds`), nor does a person playing. Without the calls (tests), it falls back to who's visibly free: out on the roofs or jumping between them, or already at the locker.
    - **The run** (`PowderRun`) is the gunner's run from note 374, moved out of `GunnerBot` so both use it: along the roofs to the van (the legs' walk), the hatch, the locker, back up.
      - Where it stands at the gun differs: behind it, in the seat, for a gunner; beside it for a walker.
      - At the locker it taps every `KitRun.TapEvery` ticks and remembers a charge it has just taken. The client hears it's in hand a snapshot late, and going back for another with it in hand would put it down.
      - The engine's gun is new ground for a walker. The legs' walk turns round short of the engine (it's the cab's), so from car 1's roof the walker makes its own running jump onto the hood (note 338: level with the cab roof). It comes back the same way: round the stack, which stands up through the hood near its back, then a jump down onto car 1. Both jumps are the relief driver's (`ReliefDriver.OntoTheEngine`, `OffTheEngine`, note 399). The relief only sends back off the engine a walker that came forward for a relief, not one up there with the gun's powder.
      - At a gun whose rack is full, a walker keeps its charge and is free for anything else until the next shot.
    - **The gunner waits for it.** A gunner whose rack runs dry keeps its seat while a walker is bringing a charge, for up to `gunnerWaits` (30 s); after that it goes for its own. Up out of the seat, the gun is unmanned and the walker stands down. It never goes while someone else has a charge in hand (`PowderCarry.Brought`).
    - **Verified.**
      - `GunPowderTests` (10, 4 new): a 12-round string from the guard van's gun (a shot every 6 s, with bare bots and as `BotCrew.Make` makes them) and from the engine's (every 12 s), with a walker feeding and the gunner never leaving its seat; who goes (the lowest free walker, or whoever has a charge); a dry gunner waiting for a charge on its way.
      - The harness's `upkeep` (#337's `UpkeepReport`) also gets `rounds`, `filledBy` (who carried each charge up, by bot) and `drySeconds` (seconds a gun that has fired stood dry with powder below). Measured with `dt harness --route frontier:7 --bots 4 --enemies --upkeep --insist CinderHound --seconds 900`, seeds 1–3 (a ten-car train):
        - Walkers feeding: the gun stood dry 14.1, 9.8 and 15.4 s. Walkers filled every rack (7, 5, 7), and the gunner never left its seat. 21, 19 and 22 rounds.
        - `feedAt` -1 (the gunner fetching its own, note 374): dry 49.8, 49.9 and 86.5 s. The gunner filled all 3 racks itself, up out of its seat each time. 23, 23 and 24 rounds.
        - Both: no deaths, 5.4–5.5 km, 2 cars lost.
