957. **A stop's time against the dawn: a dead yard's belt started from the powerhouse, and the stand for someone left behind yields to the dawn (queue #662, under #613; n1-A2's finding; n1-A1, nathaniel1).**
   - **The finding** (n1-A2, for #613's survival drop): part of the drop is time at stops, not the spawn director. On frontier:4 2×5
     seed 4 the crew spent about 14 minutes at the first stop, and the dawn caught the train 6.6 km in:
     - 214–754 s at the loading (the driver in `Conveying`);
     - 815–986 s at the switch, the gunner "at the switch", then "boarding";
     - 986–1106 s with the driver at Cruise, standing for the gunner on the ground.
     - The old director delivered that night only because its gunner was devoured at the yard and the driver left at once.
   - **The cause wasn't the driver's budget.** The stop is frontier:4's grain elevator (about 2390 m) on a dead yard's power.
     Its powerhouse is out by the switch, 320 m back down the spur from the loading.
     - The gunner, the crew's only hand, walked to the powerhouse and restarted it (live at 287 s). Then it walked to the belt's
       drive house by `WalkTo`, the track-side walk along the spur, which from out by the switch went nowhere.
     - It stood by the powerhouse from 305 s until the train left without it at 1106 s, whatever its label said.
     - So the belt never ran. The driver waited out the belt's give-up (`ConveyGiveUp`, 420 s) and the aboard wait (120 s), then
       the switch's aboard wait, then `ForTheLeftBehind`'s 120 s for someone on the ground. All three waited for the same hand,
       who couldn't move.
     - The driver's dawn reckoning (`Late`, `Onward`, note 609/617/621) was never what kept it there.
   - **Fixed:**
     - **`StopHand.Convey` walks by `OnFoot`** (the foot path from further off, round the walls), as `PowerUp` has since note
       639. On the same night the gunner starts the belt at about 340 s, minds it and is back at its gun by 540 s. The train
       backs out at 423 s and is home, delivered, at 1136 s with 2.52 car-loads and both crew.
     - **The stand for someone left behind yields to the dawn.** `ConductorBot.ForTheLeftBehind` (T96) neither sets back nor
       stands for a crewmate on the ground once `StopDriver.LateHere` says it's late. That's the same reckoning that calls a
       stop's loading done and leaves anyone still on the ground there (note 935's `LateAt`), from where the train is on the
       main line.
       - #1451's "never leaves with someone on the ground" is note 935's `Waiting`/`MayLeave` at a stop's departure, and it
         already yields to `LateAt`. The stand on the open line (T96) didn't.
