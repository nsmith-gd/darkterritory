176. **Bookmarks (GDD v1.4 App. D.10, D.12, D.13, E.5, E.9; WP8).** D.12: "A bookmark is made with a button while dead. It stores a timestamp, the followed player's name, and a still capture of the followed view. There are no replays." And the automatic ones: a still at every GRAB start and every PUNISH, "from the nearest living crew member with line of sight to the victim, or from the victim's own view if nobody can see it"; one per crew member from a derailment's cinematic; the Stranded outro's last frame; shown beside the cause-of-death line they belong to, capped per run (D.13: 12, range 8-20; derailment > PUNISH > GRAB).
    - **The host records descriptors, not pixels** (`World.Bookmarks`, `Run/Bookmarks.cs`): kind, run seconds, victim, viewer, and the camera, an eye and a look direction in the frame of whoever's eyes it is (a car's, or the world's). Clients rebuild the camera on their own copy of that car, so a still taken a few ticks after the host made the bookmark still has the eye on the right car. The GRAB hook sits beside App. A.9's attribution record in `Enemy.Grab`; the PUNISH hook is in `Enemy.Enter`, the one way into the phase, so it sees whom the enemy holds before a punish that ends in `Gone` lets go. The night's end (`Run.Finish`) writes the derailment's and the outro's, with the crew ids from the last `StepBodies` (`World.LastCrew`; `StepRun` only has states).
    - **Line of sight** is `Guns.TrainRaycast` against the train's solids, from a crewmate's eye (feet + `eyeHeight`, 1.65, the game's `Eyes.Height`) to the victim's chest (`chestHeight` 1.1), skipping 0.15 m at the eye and stopping 0.4 m short of the target, so an eye by a wall or a victim standing on a roof doesn't block itself. It doesn't see terrain, stops or structures (the sim has no ray test for those): a crewmate behind a station building counts as a witness. The witness looks at the victim; the victim's own view is where they were looking.
    - **Readings.**
        - *"Nearest ... with line of sight"* has no range in the GDD. It's night, and a still from 200 m back is black, so a crewmate over `witnessRange` (60 m) isn't a witness. Nearest by eye-to-chest distance, then by id.
        - *A PUNISH with nobody held* (a car alight, the Sleepers under the engine, the marsh before it has anyone) is of the enemy: the nearest crewmate who can see it, or no bookmark if nobody can (there's no victim whose eyes to use). A marsh that loses its man and finds him again is one punish: one bookmark per enemy per victim.
        - *Which line a still belongs to:* a GRAB's or PUNISH's sits beside the victim's death line if they died within `lineWindowSeconds` (30 s) of it; a GRAB nobody died of gets a line of its own in its place in the night, "Grabbed by the Dragger on the roof of car 3 at km 4. Got away." A victimless PUNISH gets a line the same way ("Punished by the Car Fire ..."). A derailment's stills all sit beside the derailment's line, and the outro's beside the stranding's.
        - *The cap* (`autoCap` 12) keeps by `priority` (derail, stranded, punish, grab: D.13's order, with the outro's single frame ranked with the derailment as the other end of the night), then a still beside a death line before one that isn't, then the earliest. It's applied when the report is written, not as bookmarks are made: a late fatal GRAB shouldn't lose its picture to twelve early ones somebody got away from. To bound what every machine renders and keeps, the host stops taking GRABs and PUNISHes at `recordLimit` (48); the night's end is always taken.
        - *E.5's "each player's peak frame"*: the build's derailment has no per-player shots yet (it's first person, the chase replay, the orbit: note 170), so each crew member's still is their first-person beat, from their eye in the car they rode, `wreck.json bookmarkSeconds` (2 s) in, carried and rolled with the wreck (`DerailSequence.Riding`, which the first beat now uses too). The crew it took, that is: anyone alive or killed in it, not the dead already watching. (Now the stand-in until the film is in: note 203.)
        - *Manual bookmarks* aren't capped in the GDD. `manualPerPlayer` (3) and `manualPerRun` (8) keep the dead's row to one line of the report. One needs someone to follow: a dead player with nobody alive to watch has nothing to bookmark.
    - **The button is intent.** `PlayerActions.Bookmark` (the second action byte's last free bit; `P` by default, "BOOKMARK (DEAD)" in the controls). The host takes it on the press (a held or resent intent is one bookmark), only from the dead, only while the run's under way, of whoever `EarsOf` says they watch (their intent's `Watch`). The dead card shows "[P] BOOKMARK THIS (2 LEFT)", then "BOOKMARKED AT 18:24" for 3 s.
    - **Replication.** Each bookmark goes to every client as it's made (`MessageType.Bookmark`, reliable, its JSON), so each machine takes the still from its own world at the moment, and the report (`RunReport.Bookmarks`, `ReportLine.Marks`) carries the ones shown, so a joiner's run-end screen has the lines even without the pictures. `Double3Json` writes a camera as `[x, y, z]` (a `Double3` serialised as is recurses through `Normalized`). **Protocol 10.**
    - **The stills** (`BookmarkStills`, Game): a GRAB's, PUNISH's or manual one is due on the frame it arrives; a derailment's `bookmarkSeconds` after this machine heard of it; the outro's once the outro's over, from `Views.Stranded` at its last second. The app draws the due frame offscreen through the same `renderer.Render` the `--capture` path uses, with everyone in view but whoever's eyes it is (this machine's own figure included, which the app otherwise never draws), crops it to 16:9 and keeps it at 320x180. It's a stall of one frame per bookmark, which is rare. Interest management means a client sees enemies only near its own player (or whom it watches), so a still of something far from you can miss the creature; it's 520 m (`enemies.json interestRadius`), far past the witness range, so it rarely does.
    - **On the report.** The overlay has no textures, so `Overlay.Image` draws a picture as cells of flat colour, one quad a run of equal colour in a row (16 levels a channel). Each still sits at the right of its line, 32x18 on the 480x270 canvas at half-pixel cells; the dead's own have a row of their own under the lines, 48x27, each with its time and whom it followed. A bookmark this machine has no still for (it joined after) is a dark plate. A full screen of thumbnails is around 20k quads.
    - **Not yet:** the short clips D.15's question 5 wonders about. E.5's per-player peak still and keeping the stills past the run-end screen are note 203's.
    - `BookmarkTests` covers the witness (nearest with a clear line, not the nearer one behind the car), the victim's own view, a Dragger's GRAB and PUNISH (one each, beside the death), a GRAB got away from, the derailment's crew and the outro, the cap and its priority, punish de-duplication and the record limit, a dead player's press over loopback (one per press, of whom they watch, to every client; none from the living; the per-player cap) and the wire. `SpecTableTests.AutoBookmarksMatchGddD13` pins D.13's rows. `BookmarkStillsTests` covers when each still is due and from what camera, the crop, and the report's thumbnails. `dt screenshot --hud --route frontier:7 --report [derailed]` renders the staged report with its stills drawn from their cameras (`--stills dir` writes each on its own).
