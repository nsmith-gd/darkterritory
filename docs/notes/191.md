191. **Recorded sounds (the audio checklist's pass, merged with main in note 233).** The mixer could only synthesise; the audio pass brings hundreds of recorded one-shots, several takes each, and loops. A layer can be `"source": "sample"`:
    - **Takes** are Ogg Opus (mono, 48 kHz, `ffmpeg -c:a libopus`) under `content/audio/samples`, the sounds folder's sibling (`SoundBank.Samples`). `"sample"` names a folder (its `*.opus` are the takes; each instance picks one by its seed) or one file. `OggOpus` is a small page reader over Concentus that honours pre-skip and the last granule position, so a take is exactly what was encoded and a loop's seam is where it was cut. Takes decode on first play (about 50x real time) and are kept as 16 bit; a long one decodes on a worker (note 192), and `SoundBank.Preload` decodes a sound's takes up front.
    - **On the layer:** `rate` (value or curve; 2 is an octave up in half the time; the pitch envelope and vibrato scale it), `pitchJitter` (semitones) and `gainJitter` (dB), each picked once per instance from its seed. Filters, tremolo, envelope, delay and gain curves work as on any layer. A loop loops its take; a one-shot plays it once.
    - **When a one-shot ends.** Synth layers have no end of their own, so `duration` (now optional, 1 s unset) is still their length. A sample layer ends with its take, at its rate, after its delay. An all-sample one-shot ends with its takes and ignores `duration` (a 0.2 s step doesn't hold a voice through 0.8 s of silence; a 3 s crash isn't cut at 1 s); a mixed one at the later of its takes' end and a given `duration` (`SoundInstance.Finished`). A virtual voice's takes keep time, so it ends too.
    - **A missing sample is silence, not a crash:** the bank's `LastError` names it as the sound loads (so `EverySoundFileLoads` catches a typo), or a take that won't decode when first played. A name can't reach outside the samples root.
    - **One sample source, two recordings.** A sample layer that names a take plays it; one that names none plays its instance's own clip (`SoundInstance.Clip`, from `ClipSeconds` on: note 174's opera), and a sound playing a clip is over when the clip is.
    - `dt audio render --sound <name> [--param k=v]` plays one sound alone to a WAV and spectrogram, with its length, takes and level. `SampleTests` pin the decode (length, pitch, phase), packets across pages, the end rule, rate, a seamless loop, and takes and jitter by seed.
