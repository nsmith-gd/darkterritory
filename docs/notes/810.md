810. **A Holdout's banging heard as one frantic bout (AU1, queue #538; AU1's weak-sound sweep).** A prisoner's call (GDD App. D.7, the host's Call Out on the Holdout's record) is a shout from their voice set or a bout of banging on the Holdout from inside (`voice-callout.bang`, three calls in ten). Two faults, both from AU1's sound package (#169, 5 Oct):
- **Bouts piled up.** The hook played two to four of the bang takes 0.3 s apart, as if each were a single blow, but each take is a whole frantic bout (five to eight blows in clumps of two to four, ragged pauses between: `world_voice.frantic`). A banging call was twelve to thirty-two blows on top of each other, and the clumps that make it read as someone desperate were lost. Now one call is one bout, as the synthesised stand-in (`holdout-bang`, note 179) always was.
- **Too far through the wall.** The takes were built "heard outside a shut building" with the top cut at 1.6 kHz, and the game then occludes them (0.35: a lowpass at about 6.8 kHz and −4 dB). The fists on planks were a dull thud (centre 145 Hz, 58-93% of the energy below 150 Hz). A new candidate, `door`, has the same bouts heard at the door: the door itself sounds, so each blow's crack carries (to 4.5 kHz) over the boards' boom, the palms a plank's slap where `walls` has the packs' soft thump. All four takes are under the squish rule (11-44% below 150 Hz). `door` is installed; `walls` stays a candidate, unchanged to the bit.
- **Found by** a sweep of every installed sample folder (775): clipping (one run in the kept `cs-tippy.grab`), duplicate takes (the kept `cs-passenger.step`, the crew's own steps by design, and `ui-menus.move`, a menu tick), and energy below 150 Hz, A-weighted and by centre. The kept sets are the director's and stay; of the first picks, this was the one a call depends on.
