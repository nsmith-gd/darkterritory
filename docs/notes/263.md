263. **Build 1121's playtest: the safe yard, the runaway nobody drove, the kit "the train has none", the Stoker redesigned, car fires (the director's decisions of 6 Oct 2026).** The director hosted frontier:7 alone (4 cars, no bots) and reported six bugs. Each was reproduced headless and root-caused before it was fixed.
    - **The train left on its own.** The cause was not the throttle, the gun seat's controls, the run start or a bot driver. The host spawns in the cab with the brake held (`HostSession`: with steam driving, T97, a standing engine off its brake pulls away). The director went up to the roof gun and sat there. Nobody fired the boiler, and in the yard the pressure fell under 40. After 45 s at that, App. B.5's Stoker came down the stack, and its FEED `Tamper` ramped the applied brake to 0. The train moved off with nobody in the cab, through the gate (so the run began), and up to 22 m/s. The Stoker held the safety valve shut, so the pressure reached 100 and 20 s later the boiler ruptured. Reproduced by `SafeYardTests.ALoneHostAtTheGunAndTheTrainNeverLeavesWithoutThem`: the app's own `NetPlaySession.HostGame`, the host put at the roof gun and seated, ten minutes. Before the fix, the Stoker arrived at 335 s and the train moved at 365 s. It never slowed because the Stoker overrode the brake each tick. With nobody in the cab, `HostSession` never clears or re-applies the held brake, so nothing put it back. Two fixes:
      - `enemies.json` `tamperReleasesStandingBrake` (false): whatever is at the controls (the Stoker's runaway, the Track Doll's tampering), a train standing on its held brake stays on it (`World.Step`; host and clients alike). Under way, they still take the brake off.
      - The safe yard (below): nothing spawns there at all.
    - **The fortress is a safe space** (the director's decision; GDD §9 "The fortress is a safe space"; `run.json` `yardIsSafe`). `World.SafeYard` holds while the run is in `RunPhase.Yard`, and is read from the replicated run phase, so clients predict the same. While it holds:
      - the director doesn't think: no spawns, no Stoker, no marsh;
      - the Choir's meter doesn't step;
      - `TrainOnLine.HeldInYard` skips `Boiler.Step`, so there is no pressure gain or loss, no coal burned and no rupture;
      - `PlayerMotor.StepCold` returns early.
      The director's grace counts from the run's start, not from the world's first tick, so half an hour at the gate doesn't spend it. The train moves only when someone in the cab lets the brake off (`CabControls.ReleasesBrake`) and drives through the gate. Verified by `SafeYardTests.HalfAnHourAfkInTheYardAndNothingHappens`: host and joiner AFK (one in the cab, one on a roof) for 30 minutes. Nothing moves, the gauge, fire and tender are unchanged, there are no spawns, health and cold are unchanged, and nothing is logged.
    - **"Boiler ruptured: the repair kit mends it, and the train has none."** The check itself was right. Its input was wrong: `Hud.RepairKitWhere` read the kit from this machine's bodies, and the host sends only bodies within `interestRadius` (520 m). The director was left behind by a runaway train, so the kit was out of range and the HUD said the train had none. The same applied to a kit in any locker, in a crewmate's hands or on a roof. The host's `Run.Kit` (`EngineeringKit.Where`: place, car, loss) was never on the wire, so a client's `Kit.Lost` was always false and "STRANDED WHEN SHE STOPS" never showed on any machine but the host's. Now the run record carries it (`RunHead` 6 → 9; **protocol 29**; the pinned version in `RejoinTests` and `CrewCapTests` updated), and the HUD falls back to it when no kit body is in sight. `KitCarry` (the bots' fetch from car 2 and back) and the run report already read the host's bodies: they were right, and are unchanged. Verified by `SafeYardTests.LeftBehindTheTrainYouAreStillToldWhereTheKitIs`: the run under way, the host a kilometre back, ruptured. No kit body is on the client, `Run.Kit` says Lying, and the HUD says "IT'S IN CAR 1".
    - **The Stoker clubbed through a shut door.** `World.Swing` struck anything in reach. App. A.5's KILL is "open the firebox and bludgeon it". New `Enemy.Reachable(World)`: a Stoker in the fire is reachable only with `Boiler.FireDoorOpen` (a shovelful opens it). A blow at the shut door gets the metal ring (`GameAudio.Crew`). The bots' `FightStoker` shovels to open the door, then swings. Verified by `Build1121Tests.TheStokerIsClubbedOnlyThroughTheOpenFireboxDoor`.
    - **The Stoker, redesigned** (the director's decision, GDD App. A.5 and B.5 rewritten; `enemies.json` `stoker`). This replaces App. B.5's low fire and open door. "It came straight back" had the same root as the yard bug: the low-pressure clock kept running while one was in, so killing it under a low gauge brought the next one within a second.
      - **Drawn by heat.** A firebox at `heatFirebox` (4.5 of 6) or more for `heatSeconds` (20) draws it. A low fire never does.
      - **Boards at the tender.** `Stoker.AtTender`: on the coal, then it crosses the footplate to the fire door over `boardSeconds` (8, its telegraph). `Extra2` = boarding, replicated. It is drawn on the coal with its sick glow (`CreatureArt`) and the hiss loops from where it is.
      - **Caught on the way in.** A blow while it's boarding counts `tenderBlowScale` (4) times over and doesn't burn: one blow.
      - **Worse once in.** It eats `eatPerSecond` (0.2) of the fire. Its heat swings, `feedRate` 4 ± `swing` 3 over 3 s, with the valve held shut, so the gauge climbs in surges. The swing stays under the feed, so the gauge never falls back and resets the rupture clock. The brake ramps off over `runawayRampSeconds` (20). From 75 the boiler goes in about 26 s.
      - **A break.** None comes for `breakSeconds` (150) after one's gone (`World.StokerBreakSeconds`; the heat clock doesn't run meanwhile). However it went, the boiler's external heat and the jammed valve are cleared, including when the director's linger rule dismissed it and skipped its `Leave`.
      - `Stoker.InFirebox` stays, for tests, audits and the director's insist.
      Verified by `Build1121Tests`:
      - `ALowOrWarmFireNeverDrawsTheStokerAndAHotOneDoes`;
      - `ItBoardsAtTheTenderAndTelegraphsBeforeItGetsIn` (fair: Telegraph ≥ `boardSeconds` before Commit);
      - `TheFiremanCatchesItAtTheTenderAndItStaysGoneForTheBreak` (no burn; the next one comes at break + heat ±2 s);
      - `OnceInItEatsTheFireAndSwingsTheGaugeToARupture` (20–45 s);
      - `AStokerFeedingAStandingTrainDoesNotTakeItOffItsBrake`.
      `RosterTests` uses a hot fire now.
    - **"One extinguisher douses the whole train."** Spraying was already per car. The cause was T114's linger rule (`World.Unmet`): any engaged threat with nobody within 12 m for 120 s was dismissed, car fires included. While the crew fought one fire, the others went out by themselves. Car fires are now exempt. Verified by `Build1121Tests.AFireWithNobodyNearKeepsBurning`. **Time to put one out:** neither App. C.5 nor the spec gives a number, so there's nothing to be over. With one extinguisher it takes about 7 s caught as it takes hold (0.35), about 12 s at 0.6, and at full blaze one charge (15 s) isn't enough, by design ("a big one takes several extinguishers"). **For the director:** spraying ignores aim. It counts within `sprayReach` (2.5 m) of the fire's anchor on the cargo face, but the flames are drawn further along the car than that. Aiming at the flames 3 m away does nothing, which may be "nothing is really going out".
    - **"Touching fire kills instantly."** A fire burned `10 × intensity` every 2 s, on a timer per fire rather than per player. Walking in just before a pulse cost 10 at once, and that was all the fire could do. The likelier cause is that the director's health was already gone. A clubbed Stoker burns 12 a blow, 48 for one with health 4, and they fought two. There is no regeneration, so they had about 4 health left, and any burn killed them. The new burn is by the second (the director's decision; `enemies.json` `carFire.burnPerSecond` 10, `burnBrushShare` 0.5, `burnRampSeconds` 3; `CarFire.Burn`, a dose per player). A brush costs a few points. A second in a full blaze costs about 6, and standing in it kills in about 11 s. Verified by `Build1121Tests.AFireBurnsByTheSecondABrushHurtsAndStandingInItKills`.
    - **Needs the director's call:**
      - Should the Track Doll's tampering also leave a standing train on its brake? It does now (`tamperReleasesStandingBrake` covers both).
      - Spray aim against reach (above).
      - Whether a clubbed Stoker should cost less health. Two fights take nearly all of it.
      - The heat threshold against what the bots fire to. The driver bot keeps a full box at a stand, so bot crews now meet the Stoker more (not swept).
