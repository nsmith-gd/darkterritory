1053. **The houses' windows shut and can be escaped through, the Hollow House's too (B4, queue #751, picked up from nathaniel1; GDD F.5 Decided, 13 Oct; `creatures/hollow-house.md` §7.2; note 1050 rank 2).**
   - **Claimed** 26 Oct 12:45 UTC. Done on `b4-house-windows-751`.
   - **What it was:** only the basements' windows (#710, note 1005) could be climbed out of. A house's own windows were boards drawn over solid wall, so the Hollow House trapped by its doors alone. GDD App. F.5 (the director, 13 Oct): "The village's houses have real doors and windows, which open and shut. In the Hollow House, a door slamming shut is what's coming, and a crewmate can still get out through a door or a window while they slam."
   - **Which windows (`StopWalls.WindowsOf`, `GroundWindows`):**
     - The art's windows, now the sim's: one or two along each outside run with no door, as before.
     - The ground floor's are working windows. That covers every open house: one-floor, composite (L, cross, pair), a house of parts (note 1022) and a storeyed house (note 1005), but not a storeyed house's back end, where its back door is.
     - Upstairs windows stay boarded art.
     - Sill 0.8 m and head 1.9 m over the floor's boards, 0.88 m wide (`WindowHalf`). These are the boarded windows' own sizes (TownKit's 0.95 m was the art frame's, 0.15 m under the ground).
   - **The opening (`StopWalls.Cut`):**
     - The wall is cut round each window: whole either side, under the sill, over the head.
     - It is one cut for the sim and the art alike. `Shell` (storeyed) now uses it for its back door and cellar windows too: identical pieces where no openings overlap, and correct where a raised house's ground-floor window stands over a cellar window.
     - The piece under a window is a `Wall` (never `Level`), so the bots' way never goes through a window.
   - **The shutter:**
     - Each window has a wall in its opening with `Door` = the window's key (`WindowKey`, negative, never a door's).
     - `Near` and `Sight` already stand a door's wall only while shut, so a window is a door to them: shut, it's solid and blocks sight; open, sight and a ball go through.
     - Windows start open, as the doors do, as the ransack left them.
     - `_shut`, `Shut`, `SetShut`, `ShutDoors` and `MirrorShut` (the doors' record on the wire) carry window keys as they are. A client that doesn't know a key lets it be.
   - **Working one (`World.DoorAct`, unchanged):**
     - `World.DoorInReach` gives the door in reach and, with none, a window within `walls.houseDoorReachM`. Use held `houseDoorSeconds` shuts or opens it, as at a door.
     - HUD: "SHUT THE WINDOW : HOLD [E]   CLIMB THROUGH : WALK INTO IT" / "OPEN THE WINDOW : HOLD [E]".
     - Doors win where both are in reach, so the bots' door errands and the jackpot lock are untouched.
   - **Climbing through:**
     - An open window has a world ladder each side, as the cellar windows' crates do. Inside it runs from the floor; outside from the ground, which is the plinth higher for a raised house. Each tops out at the sill and steps 0.75 m over and past the wall (`WindowOnto`).
     - It is taken by walking into it (note 1018's walk-in rule) or with the ladder key. That is the intent path: bots and players alike.
     - A shut window has no ladders (`LaddersNear` filters by `_shut`), so whoever was on one falls back as it slams.
     - A window with the house's own furniture against it inside (a cupboard, a ransack's heavy piece, a storeyed house's flight) has no ladders: climbed in, you'd be set down in it. Its shutter's worked as any.
     - The window ladders are kept by cell, as there are around a thousand down a line. The motor asks `LaddersNear(at)`. `Ladders` is still the full list for the tests.
   - **The Hollow House:** in `Shut`, its doors slam one every `shutEvery` as before, then its windows in key order. `Sink`, `Collapse` and `Settled` hold all of them shut.
     - **The call (no director's call needed; the GDD says it):** windows slam after the doors on the same cadence. A house with more windows takes longer to shut: a one-door house with three windows is shut 1.05 s after the rumble ends instead of at once.
     - That time is the GDD's "can still get out ... while they slam". A crewmate standing at the last window when the rumble ends climbs out and lives (`HouseWindowTests`).
     - `HouseCreatureTests`' kill waits the extra slams.
   - **The jackpot house (note 719):** `Lock` shuts its windows too, and `Barred` keeps them shut while its door's locked, so the wrench is still the way in. The HUD says "WINDOW BARRED : THE DOOR'S LOCKED". Once the door's open, they're windows like any other.
   - **The Choir (GDD §21, "not behind a closed door"):** a house is still shut up by its doors alone (`Seal`). An open window doesn't let the Choir in: windows are a way out, not a new way to die. If the director wants open windows to count, it's one line in `Seal`.
   - **Art:**
     - The walls are cut where the sim cuts them, and the working windows keep their white trim with no pane or planks in them.
     - `TownKit.Shutter` is the boarded window as a plank shutter: dark boards upright, three raw planks across ragged, strap hinges.
     - `GreyboxScene.HouseWindows` hangs it on one side of the opening at the outside face. Shut, it's the boarded window the houses always had; open, it's swung out 165° against the wall beside it. It slams as a door does (`DoorSwing`) and moves and comes down with a house a creature moves.
   - **Views:**
     - `dt screenshot --view window` shows the staged house from outside, one window open and one shut.
     - `--view windowin` looks out through an open window from inside.
     - The hollowhouse views shut the windows as well as the doors.
   - **Tests (`HouseWindowTests`):**
     - Each window is an opening with wall under and over it, its shutter standing only while shut, the same on every machine.
     - Most windows can be climbed through.
     - A storeyed house's windows go through its shell over its cellar windows, and never in its back end.
     - Walking into an open window climbs out and back in, for a one-floor house and a raised one; a shut one doesn't let you through.
     - Use held shuts a window and opens it again.
     - The Hollow House slams its windows after its door, and the last is a way out.
     - The client mirrors the host's windows on the doors' record.
     - A jackpot house's windows are barred while it's locked.
   - **Not yet:**
     - The fortress towns' houses (#725's `TownHouses`) and the dead towns' derelicts have no working windows. They are shut buildings, or their own kits.
     - No sound of a window being worked; the doors' sounds are the doors' only.
   - **How to check:**
     - At a village, walk to a house's window and hold E: its shutter swings shut, and holding E again opens it. Walk into the open window: you climb up over the sill and out.
     - In a Hollow House, stand at a window as the ground rumbles. The door slams, then the windows one by one; get out through one before it slams.
     - `dt screenshot --view window` and `--view windowin`.
