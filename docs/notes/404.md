404. **TEXT BACKING (F1, UI/UX 3; queue #140; GDD §32 "Accessibility"; after note 349's captions).** The HUD's print in play sits on the picture with a one-pixel shadow (note 285: "no frames in play"). That reads in the cab and the dark, but cream fine print washes out over a car fire's glare or the fog's brightest, and a caption a player depends on can go unread. The accessibility guidelines' answer is a subtitle background.
    - **The setting.** ACCESSIBILITY's TEXT BACKING, ON or OFF. It's off by default, so the HUD is as note 285 drew it unless a player asks. With it on, every line of print the HUD writes on the picture has a black band behind it, a line at a time: the captions, the corner's keys and its head, the headline and its fine print, a place's name and the cold, the first nights' card, the lobby, the dead's card. Its darkness is hud.json's `textBacking` (0.6): enough that the print reads over the brightest the night gets, light enough that the night shows through.
    - **How.** `Overlay.Backing` is a colour the HUD sets for its own build and clears after it, so the in-night menu and the front end drawn over it are untouched. `Overlay.Text` puts the band behind shadowed text only: unshadowed text is on something of its own (a keycap, a plate). `UiStyle.Keyed` bands a keyed line once, keycaps and all, rather than piece by piece. What already has its own ground sets it aside with `UiStyle.OnPlate`: the radio's card, a town's card, the ballot, the run's report, the prompt's strip, the film's cards, the roster, the supplies and the hotbar's slots. Nothing is said differently: only drawn behind.
    - **Verified.** `TextBackingTests`: one band a line, drawn under the print and round it and its shadow; a keyed line one band; a plate sets it aside and puts it back; the HUD with captions at every TEXT SIZE gains only bands (the print unchanged), one a caption at the foot of the frame, all inside it, and the build leaves no backing for what's drawn after; no band in the hotbar's slots or on the roster; off until turned on, and saved. `dt screenshot --hud --captions --text-backing` (with `--roof-warning`, `--first-night --route frontier:7 --throttle 0`), looked at in the cab, the yard and on a roof in the fog.
