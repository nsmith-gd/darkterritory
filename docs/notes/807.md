- **The high five and the fist bump**, each in two halves:
    - **Offered** (`high_five`, `fist_bump`; 3 s, the offer's `emotes.highFive`/`fistBump`, played from the offer). The palm goes up to whoever's coming, with a beckon of the fingers (or the fist out at the chest). Left hanging from 1.2 s, it sags, the shoulders go, the head tips, and they look round for someone.
    - **Met** (`high_five_met`, `fist_bump_met`; 0.8 s, `emotes.met`, played from the meet tick). It slaps in from cocked back over the shoulder, landing at 0.1 s and held a moment. The hand bounces back up off it with a point at them on the way down ("yes!"). The fist bump's fist is blown open with a wiggle of the fingers.
    - **The hands meet** (`CreatureArt.Meet`). Either side of the landing, the right hand goes from the clip's path onto the one point between the two:
        - the middle of their feet, worked out from the sim's own positions (`EmoteDetail.Gap` and the new `Across`: how much of the gap is to their right, which is the same for both);
        - the palm short of it by its thickness, so the wrists are 6 cm apart palm to palm; the fist bump's knuckles touch, the wrists 20 cm apart;
        - a high five up over the head when they're close, coming down to the head's height as the gap widens to `emotes.reach`'s 1.5 m;
        - leant in from the waist only as far as the arm needs.
- **The dance-off** (`dance_off`, three 2 s moves; `dance_off_cheer`, 2 s):
    - Each turn's lead (`EmoteRules.DanceOff`'s, every `danceOffBeat`) dances one move from the turn's start. The turn and how far into it come from the host's tick, as the lead does (`EmoteDetail.Round`, `OnBeat`), so every machine draws the same move on the same beat.
    - The moves, each ended pointing both hands at whoever's next ("your go"):
        - the windmill: the right arm round over the top, the left fist pumping, stomping on the beat;
        - the sprinkler: a hand behind the head, the other arm ratcheted round and swept back;
        - the knees: hands on the knees knocking them in and out on the way down, and up with the arms in a V.
    - A lead never dances the move whoever went just before them danced, nor the one they danced last time it came round (`DanceOffMoveOf`, for any number in the ring).
    - The rest cheer on the same beat: clapping, a "whoa!" leant back with the hands up at the lead's best bit, then the hands rubbed together, ready, as the lead points it over.
- **Sat on the edge** (`sit_edge`, 6 s, loop):
    - The seat is on the edge's top, the backs of the knees just over it. `CrewActs.SatOn` now puts the root 0.3 m in from the edge (`SitBack`, was 0.14), where the clip's seat is.
    - The shins hang and swing a foot at a time, only forward of plumb, so the heels never go back into the side under it.
    - The hands grip the edge beside the thighs. They lean back on their hands with a look up the train, come forward to look down between the knees at the drop, then out again.
    - Roof edges and a door landing's alike.
- **Leant out** (`lean_out`, 4 s, loop; `lean_out_left`, the same mirrored, for the outside on the left):
    - Side on in the doorway, the hand nearer the car round the jamb ahead at the shoulder's height, the weight hung off it. The body is tipped out from the feet past the car's side.
    - The other arm points up the line, then hangs loose out in the air, swaying with the car. The head is still turned on to where they look.
    - `CrewActs.LeantOut` draws them along the sill so the jamb is where the hand is (`JambFrom`, `JambAhead` 0.45 m): it looks out through the doorway at the knee and the chest, a step at a time ahead, until the car's side is in the way. In a doorway narrower than that, they stay a hand's breadth inside its other jamb. The sim leaves them where they stood, as it does a sitter.
