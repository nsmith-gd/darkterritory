975. **The flare pistol heard: the shot, the flare burning, an empty click (AU1, queue #675; the Audio Checklist's `up-flare` line).**
- The flare pistol (n1-w3's note 871, #598) is in play and drawn (note 964, #1545); it made no sound. Now it does (`GameAudio.Flare`, off the pistol's and the flare's own bodies, which every machine has):
    - **The shot** (`up-flare.shot`): the shooter hears it the moment they press Fire with a flare left, as the sim fires it (alive, not seated, the pistol the thing in hand, not again within `flarePistol.every`), at their hand, so a client doesn't wait a round trip for the host's flare. Everyone else hears it where the flare first flies. The shooter's own flare, when its body comes (the same tick on the host, a few later on a client), isn't heard again: a flare seen within 1.5 s and 40 m of this machine's own shot is that shot's. Last tick's flares decide shot or click, because the host has already taken this one off by the time the sound looks.
    - **The burn** (`up-flare.burn`, a loop): held on the flare while its burn lasts (its `Charge`, 0..1), coming in from 0.9 s after it's first seen over 0.9 s (as the shot's own hiss of it flying off dies), and dying away over the last 5% of its minute (3 s), so it ends with the flare rather than stopping. Seen already burning (joining, or in range late), it's full at once. Heard out to 300 m, rolloff 0.8: across a village, as its light is seen across one.
    - **The click** (`up-flare.click`): Fire with none left, the hammer falling dry, in the shooter's hand.
- **The sounds** (`tools/audio/recipes/flare.py`, two candidates a cue, the real ones first installed): a signal pistol, not a gun: a hollow "thoonk" (the packs' soft heavy blow and a book's snap through a 23 cm barrel's quarter-wave, 370 Hz and its odd harmonics), a puff of gas, and the flare's hiss tearing away up its arc (the packs' real air release, a lowpass closing on it); nothing under 140 Hz. The burn: a steady fierce roar with a fizz in it (the real air release paulstretched over a jet's roar), dry spits and the odd pop, fluttering a little; held under 5 kHz (6-9 kHz 0.1% of it), so it's never the car-fire tell's crackle, and steady where the Stoker's hiss comes and goes (`tellcheck.features`: 0.34-0.41 apart from the Stoker in spectrum, 0.6 in rhythm; nothing flagged). The click: the packs' metal click pitched down, the trigger's stop after it.
- **Captions:** A POP AND A HISS, A FLARE GOING UP; A FLARE HISSING; A DRY CLICK.
