1007. **The Bruin: the first of the forest's heavies, a rotten bear that comes out of the trees at a train stood in the wild, climbs into the cargo car and mauls whoever faces it (queue #712; the director's playtest of 23 Oct, line 36; Sloe's design, note 1004; Rook, the Token Budget Manager's worker, nathaniel2).**
   - **The ask:** the director, 23 Oct, line 36: "if you stop in the wild to deal with something, you should just have, like,
     some wild creatures that ... feel kind of big, kind of scary, that come out of the trees ... they can kill the player
     pretty easily if they get grabbed ... They can get into cars and cause problems. They should also be relatively hard
     to take down. Like, stopping in the wild feels like it should feel scary." And "every creature's purpose is to foil some
     sort of behavior", always "with a clear tell and a clear counter". Sloe ranked the Bruin first
     (`docs/design/creatures/bruin.md`). It foils stopping in the wild with the train left unwatched (note 999 §3's gap
     "nothing comes out of the woods at a standing train") and the lone fighter.
   - **Off by default** (`enemies.json` `bruin.on`): the director hasn't answered `nathaniel2-sloe-forest-heavies` yet. Line 36
     reads as wanting it, but the creature isn't approved, so it waits on that answer. The `r712-bruin` edition turns it on
     for measuring.

   **What's built.**
   - **Sim** (`Enemies/Bruin.cs`, `EnemyKind.Bruin` = 48, protocol 88):
     - **Comes:** its spawn rule (`Spawns`) holds when the director's stance (note 999) is `wilderness`, stood
       `standSeconds` (45). The train must be out of the forts and carry a car of cargo with a room (`Bruin.CarFor`: the
       most cargo left, then nearest the engine). One at a time, `tierWeights` by tier.
     - **The approach:** out of the trees `comeFromM` (40 m) off that car's side, on the director's draw. It walks to the
       car's door at `come` (4 m/s), huffing. The telegraph lasts at least `huffSeconds` (10) before it climbs in.
     - **Never at speed:** a train moving off past `giveUpAbove` (2 m/s) before it's in sends it back to the trees.
     - **Feeds:** in the car (attached, on its floor), `feedPerSecond` of the car's `CargoIntegrity` a second. Aboard, it
       rides along feeding.
     - **Leaves:** after `leaveAfter` s fed by tier (40, 40, 90, never), or with the cargo gone.
     - **Turns and charges:** it turns (`turnDegreesPerSecond` 90) to face the nearest crewmate within `reach` (6 m), its
       last hitter first. Whoever stays within `frontDegrees` (50°) of its front for `windupSeconds` (3), it rises over and
       charges. Caught within `chargeReach` (3 m), the maul: a grab, dead in `maulSeconds` (8), `DeathCause.Mauled`. Then
       it goes back to feeding.
     - **The flank counter** (`Bruin.Struck`):
       - From the front, a blow does ×`frontFactor` (0.2) and it keeps hold.
       - From the side or behind, it's staggered `staggerSeconds` (1.2), drops whoever it's mauling (a rescue) and turns on
         the hitter.
       - So one alone is always in front of it. Two or three round its sides keep resetting its wind-up, and it never lands
         a maul.
     - **Driven off or killed:** `driveOffBlows` (8) blows in its car drive it out to the trees (gone there, or `leaveFarM`
       from the engine). `health` (20) kills it: the crew's kill (`Slay`, a trophy's chance), out for the night.
     - Deterministic and the host's: its one draw is the side it comes from (the director's stream), and the rest reads
       state. `Working` (note 881) is true in its car, feeding: a threat with nobody near. `StaysAboard`: never dismissed
       for want of company.
   - **A kind that's off is out tonight** (`SpawnRule.Enabled`; `Director`'s `off`). The night's floor (note 868) counts
     every kind the director can send as "about tonight" unless `nightChance` names it. An off Bruin would have counted, and
     the floor would have brought back one kind fewer every night. Now it's out, as if rolled out at chance 0.
     `NightCurveTests` showed this: its "only the free" nights now also turn the rare kinds' trial off (note 915), which had
     let a Track Doll in once the rolls moved.
   - **The director's tables:** `costs.bruin` 4. `target.answers.afield` + `bruin` (note 999 §3). Eligibility's gates
     (`dt eligibility`) and `HitConfirmTests`' roster. Its hit body: `bodies.bruin`.
   - **The tell** (synth stand-ins, `content/audio/sounds`; `GameAudio.Bruin.cs`):
     - `tell-bruin.huff` (tier 1): two chuffs, their body 150–400 Hz (kept above the 150 Hz squish), with the opened
       chest's whistle as a clean line at ~820 Hz. That whistle tells it from the Moose's bellow. Fired at an uneven pace
       while it comes (about 2.2 s) and while it feeds (half as often).
     - `cs-bruin.rock`: the car rocking on its springs, held while it's in its car.
     - `cs-bruin.roar`: as it rises to charge.
     - Checked with `dt audio render --sound tell-bruin.huff` (its spectrogram). `AudioTests` green.
