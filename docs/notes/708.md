708. **An unrigged load falls (A1, queue #440; the director, 13 Oct evening, `docs/playtest/2026-10-13-evening.md` line 17).**
"It did unhook, but it sort of just stays there in the air, doesn't fall" (a casting unrigged before it was on the floor; it stayed in the car, floating). The director gave A1 this line on 14 Oct.

   - **The cause** (`Crane.Hatch`):
     - A casting going in through an open hatch comes to rest on the highest thing under its footprint.
     - That was read with `CarShape.TopAt`, which counts a cargo car's heap (`PartKind.Cargo`, down the car's load side) at its full built height, 2.4 m.
     - The heap only exists as high as what's been poured in (`Solid.Present`, `BoxIn`; note 659), so in an empty car the casting's corner caught a heap that wasn't there.
     - The hook stopped 1.3 m over the floor; unhooked or let go, the casting was loaded there, in the air.
   - **The fix:**
     - `CarShape.TopAt(x, z, vehicle, maxY)` reads the car as it is now: what's there, at its present height.
     - The hatch's landing uses it, so the hook lowers the casting to the floor (or onto the load or the castings already in), and a casting let go lands there.
   - **GDD §18 first:** "A load let go comes down".
   - **Checked:**
     - `CraneTests.LetGoInAnEmptyCarsOpenHatchItComesDownOnTheFloor`: from 0.3 m and 2.5 m over the roof, it's loaded at the floor; the hook goes down to it.
     - CraneTests, HatchTests and StopCrewTests: 77, all green.
   - **Not done:** the fall is still instant. A casting let go from high lands where it lands that tick (note 659's "as if set down there"), not drawn falling. A visible fall is a presentation change for later if it's wanted.
