267. **Fire is a grid (the director's decision of 6 Oct 2026, GDD App. F.1: "Each car's surfaces (floor, walls, roof; never mid-air) are cut into large cells of 1–2 m. Fire spreads cell to cell, the extinguisher puts out the cell you aim at, and burnt cells char the textures").** A car fire was one number (`Extra`, 0–1) at one spot on the floor: it grew, it was knocked back by anyone holding Fire with an extinguisher within 2.5 m of that spot whichever way they faced, and it burned whoever was within 4 m of it along the car.
    - **The grid** (`Enemies/FireGrid.cs`). The cells are laid out from the car's interior box alone: floor and roof along × across, side walls along × up, at enemies.json `carFire.cellSize` 1.5 m. That's 72 cells in a cargo car. The host and every client have the same cells in the same order, so only heat and char go over the wire. Each cell's neighbours are those sharing an edge with it, on its own surface or round a corner onto the next. The end walls aren't cells: a fire leaves a car through its ends. Floor cells sit on the boards, `FloorPad` (0.1 m) above the interior box's floor. CarFrame pads the box below the boards, and TrainKit draws them there. `Hit` is the spray's ray against the room's floor, roof and side walls.
    - **The fire** (`CarFire`, Incidents.cs). Each cell has heat and fuel, and the car's `Vehicle.Char` is the fuel that's gone. A lit cell grows. Floor cells grow at the cargo's rate, since it's the load burning there; walls and roof are the car's boards. A cell burns `charPerSecond` × its heat of its own fuel and dies down once that's gone. A cell at `catchFrom` heats its neighbours, `climb` × as fast upward, so the walls and roof catch before the far end of the floor. Embers below `outBelow` don't grow on their own, and go out unless a neighbour is heating them: a sprayed cell doesn't creep back from a spark. This was found in a probe: one bot sprayed a 40 s fire down to a single 0.01 cell it wasn't aiming at, which regrew and jumped the coupling. Every tick reads the cells as they were at its start, in cell order, so it's deterministic. `Extra` is now the mean heat. It still drives the explosion (`explodeAt` 1 → 0.6: more than half the powder car well alight), cargo and car damage, the sound, the bots' "too far gone" and the HUD. It's alight (the commit) once any cell reaches `burnFrom`. `Local.Z` follows the heat-weighted middle, for the sound, the smoke and anything that still wants one spot. A fire that burns a car out goes out: the car is charred through.
    - **The extinguisher puts out the cell you aim at.** Each sprayer's look (`Bookmarks.Forward`, from `pick.eyeHeight`) is cast against the cells within `sprayReach` (2.5 → 3.5 m, since the old 2.5 m from the eye didn't reach the floor at a natural downward look). The cell hit cools by `sprayPerSecond` (0.06 on the whole fire → 0.35 on the cell), the cells round it by `sprayShare` of that, and the cell is wet for `dampSeconds`, catching from nothing round it. Aimed at no cell, the charge is wasted. `CarFire.Sprayed` lists each sprayer's cell this tick.
    - **Burns** come from the cells: the hottest within `burnReach` (4 → 1.6 m) of a burning cell's patch, measured from feet to `bodyHeight`, falling off with distance; the roof's at `ceilingBurnShare`. The by-the-second brush/ramp of note 263 is unchanged. Standing in it still kills in 8–15 s (`Build1121Tests`), and you're clear of it outside the burning cells, not 4 m from one spot.
    - **Jumping the coupling** is from a cell against an end wall at `spreadFrom` for `spreadSeconds` (30 → 15, now the fire has to walk to the end first). The cargo multipliers are unchanged. The fire takes the next car at its near end, once from each end.
    - **The wire** (protocol 30). A fire's record carries its cells' heat, and a car's record its cells' char, four bits a cell (`CarFire.Pack`, rounded up so a cell alight shows alight): 5 fields for 72 cells. Neither touches player or train motion, so prediction is unaffected. Char rides in `VehicleState` so a rejoin sees it.
    - **The bots** (`Fight` in Bots.cs) stand in the aisle 1.2 m short of the nearest burning cell, turn to look at it (yaw and pitch from the eye), and spray. They used to stand by the fire's one spot facing along the car.
    - **The art.** `GreyboxScene.FireGrids` draws each burning cell where it is (`Effects.FireCell`): tongues standing off the floor, licking up the walls from the lower part of each patch, rolling flat under the roof. A cell short of alight is a red glow in its boards. `Effects.CarFire` keeps the smoke, glow, cinders and light at the heart of it (`CellFlames`). Char is solid geometry (`Effects.Char`): a ragged patch per burnt cell, scorched brown to black, a little bigger than the cell so burnt cells run together. It isn't drawn as an effect because the blended effects only ever add light; a black effect quad drew nothing (checked against a red one). `Staging.Cells` gives `dt screenshot --threats --view fire` a fire that's been burning a while, with its char; `--smoulder` shows the char under a fire knocked back to one cell.
    - **Measured** (headless probe, not kept; frontier test train, 5 cars):
        - **Goods car, left alone:** alight at 25 s, the roof caught by 56 s, half the car by 70 s, the next car taken at 86 s, burnt out (72/72 cells charred) inside 400 s.
        - **Coal:** 18 / 45 / 55 / 71 s. **Chemicals:** 19 / 47 / 58 / 76 s.
        - **One walker with the car's extinguisher:** a fire found at 20 s is out in 4 s, using 6% of the charge; at 40 s, out in 7 s (19%). At 60 s (about 60% of the car alight) one extinguisher can't put it out: it takes more hands, or cutting the car loose.
        - `dt audit cascades --only car-fire` passes on seeds 1–3: out in 12–13 s, the car whole.
