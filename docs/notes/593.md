593. **Loot in the abandoned villages (D1.5, queue #325; the director, 9 Oct 2026, playtest, via D1: "I'm not really finding a lot of loot. I feel like not every house certainly should have loot in it, but a good chunk of them should have something here, something there, things that we can stash in our lockers").**
    - **What it was.** A village's open house held anything only on the tier's `find` (stops.json tiers: 0.45, 0.38, 0.32, 0.26), with a second find at `secondFindChance` 0.35. By `dt site sweep --seeds 40`'s new `houses` census, per tier, Village kind: 49%, 44%, 35% and 29% of the open houses had something in them, 0.65, 0.59, 0.48 and 0.37 finds a house. About eight finds in a frontier village of thirteen houses.
    - **Now.** `find` 0.9, 0.8, 0.72, 0.65; `secondFindChance` 0.45 and a new `thirdFindChance` 0.15 (`StopGenerator.Village`). The census: 93%, 83%, 75% and 67% of the houses hold something, 1.36, 1.25, 1.15 and 1.0 finds a house, up to three in one. A frontier village is about sixteen finds. Barns, sheds and the goods shed are as they were.
    - **What each find is worth** stays what it was: the stop's budget (loot.json `villageBudget`) is split over its finds, so it doubled with them, [0.3, 0.6] to [0.6, 1.2] of the tier's per-car value. With the train leaving empty (note 575) the villages are more of a night's pay.
    - **Healing stays rare** (note 272, `DamageModelTests.HealingFindsAreRare`): with twice the finds a cabinet heals one time in eight (6 of its 48 entries, bandages the likeliest), not one in four: still about one healing find a frontier night.
    - **The stop's measure** (level-design D.1, P15) counts the odds of a find (`score.findOdds / find`), so more finds lowered villages under their tiers' bands. `findOdds` doubled with them (3 to 6) and local's `find` set to 0.9 so its term is where it was; the sweep's in-band share is 1 on frontier, dead lines and deep territory, 0.975 (village) and 0.95 (dead town) on local against 1 before (the extra houses to search add to its walk). `StopGeneratorTests.EveryStopPassesEveryInvariant` allows one in thirty outside the band; the bands themselves are B1/B4's and untouched.
    - **Verified:** `StopGeneratorTests.AGoodShareOfAVillagesHousesHaveSomethingInThem` (every tier: 60–97% of the open houses with a find, some with none, some with three, 0.9–1.6 a house); the generator, healing, locker and search tests; `dt site sweep --seeds 40` before and after (the `houses` census).
