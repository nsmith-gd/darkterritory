**What changes**
- `boiler.json`: the steam a unit of coal makes (`steamPerUnit` 15 → 17.55) and the steam the train draws (`auxiliaryDrain` 0.05 → 0.0585, `heatingPerCar` 0.0675 → 0.078975, `fullThrottleDemand` 0.5 → 0.585) all go up 17% together. The gauge moves 17% faster either way.

More steam a unit alone would have made coal last 17% longer and the fireman's post lighter, which wasn't asked; at a standstill at twenty cars it would have built nearly twice as fast, not 17%.
