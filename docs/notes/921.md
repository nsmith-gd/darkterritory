921. **Bandwidth at 8 crew back under the budget (n1-N1, nathaniel1; queue #631; ARCHITECTURE §6's ≤ 64 kbit/s down per client typical, 128 peak; protocol 83).** On main of 20 Oct, a crew of eight on a route's night was sent **80.8 kbit/s** down each (frontier:7) and 73.4 (deadLines:3), past the typical budget. `dt harness --wire` (new) says where it went: a third of it, **24.9 kbit/s**, was the run's record. Now a record of any length goes as a delta, a mask word for each 64 fields (protocol 83).
