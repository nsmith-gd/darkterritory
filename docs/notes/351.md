351. **The polish pass in the real app window (F1, UI/UX 3; queue #88; the director, 8 Oct 2026).** Every menu screen, the yard, the in-night menu, the route card and the supplies walked in the app itself (xvfb, the real window path, 1280x720), at 100% and 150% TEXT SIZE and in the colourblind palette, beside the headless renders. What was off, fixed:
    - **TEXT SIZE shrank the fine print on a small window.** `Hud.PromptScaleAt` picked the half step wherever a font pixel got two of the screen's, but 150%'s smaller canvas gives a 720p window four screen pixels a canvas pixel, so it took the half step: the prompts, the corner and the panels came out smaller at 150% than at 100% (2 screen pixels a font pixel against 2.67). It now asks TEXT SIZE's scale more of the screen (`PromptScaleAt(pixels, textScale)`, `Keys.TextScale` by default), so the fine print only grows as the text does: 720p is 2.67, 3.33 and 4; 1080p 2, 2.5 and 3. `TextSizeTests.TheFinePrintNeverShrinksAsTheTextGrows` (540p to 1440p), and the HUD's fit test now runs at a 540p, 720p, 1080p and 1440p window (it had only 1080p's scale).
    - **The first nights' card ran off a small frame** (150% on a 540p window) and showed through the supplies panel. Its rows break between controls where one is wider than the frame, and it gives way to the roster, the supplies, the route card and the map.
    - **The supplies panel** steps down to the half step when the whole step won't fit the frame.
    - **A night from the command line** drew its first frame on the default canvas (no menu frame before it to take TEXT SIZE): the display settings are applied as the night starts.
    - **A long menu's "..."** on its first or last row read as part of that row's label ("TEXT SIZE: 100%  ..."): a small arrow on the plate's top or bottom edge says there's more.
    - **Not changed:** the HUD's "REPAIR KIT" against the wiki's "engineering kit" (moot: #299, note 301's second slice, took the kit and its HUD lines out); the in-night menu's heading and RESUME both say the night goes on (`NightMenuTests` holds RESUME's); paths on the profile screen print in capitals, the pixel font having no lower case.
    - **The app headless, for menus too:** `--screen name` opens a menu screen (`fortress` is slot 1's) and `--select n` moves down it, and `--capture` with no night writes the menu as last drawn.
