826. **The Hope Tower's Flame of Hope heard (AU1, queue #554; B2's #1091, note 819).** A harbour town's light is a coal fire in an iron basket on an 80 m stone tower, "Flames of Hope for the world to find in the darkness" (the director, 12 Oct, the World Building board's Cultures tab). It burnt in silence.
- **The Flame** (`place-town.flame`, a loop): a great coal fire burning in the open, the wind off the sea at it. Its roar is its weight: a deep breathing roar that swells as the gusts fan it, the white-hot bed hissing, cinders cracking off and going up, the basket's iron ticking now and then.
- **Where it's heard:** `GameAudio.Town` holds it on the tower's top, 2.5 m over the basket's brim (`TownFixtures`' `lighthouse` height), from anywhere within 800 m: across the town and out to the train in the yard. Its reach (min 20 m, rolloff 0.7) puts it a few dB down at the tower's foot, 85 m below it, and still there across the town, as a big source's sound falls off slower than a near thing's. From inside a house, through its walls, as the square's fires are.
- **Never a car alight:** both candidates are lowpassed at 4.5 kHz (it's high up and far off, and the car-fire tell is 6-9 kHz, spec A.4). Tier 6, the town's: under every tell. No caption: it's the town's bed, not a moment.
- **Candidates** (`tools/audio/recipes/hope_tower.py`, on the Audio Checklist's `place-town` row): `basket` (the bed and its weight, installed) and `tongues` (the flames flapping like canvas in the wind). Both under the squish rule (25% and 16% below 150 Hz).
- **Test:** `WorldSoundTests.AHarbourTownsFlameOfHopeIsHeardAtTheTopOfItsTowerAcrossTheTown` (at its door, held at the top; at the train in the yard, still held; 3 km down the line, gone).
