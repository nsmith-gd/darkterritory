741. **The driver dies and no bot takes the cab (A1, queue #475; the director, 14 Oct, `docs/playtest/2026-10-14-second.md` line 5).**
"I was driving the train and then I died. And the bots did not take over driving the train. They just stood there. They didn't know what to do".

   - **Why** (note 399's relief driver, note 655's gunner rule):
     - **Relief only heard a dead bot driver.** It went forward only when the calls lost the driver bot's `StopJob.Driver`, and someone playing at the controls is never on the calls.
     - **While the driver bot lived**, it was out working as a walker (handed over, note 574), and its own way back to the cab was the only fallback.
     - **With the driver bot already dead**, the train's only other bot in a crew of three was a gunner, and it never went:
       - note 655 left the relief to "a walker", and counted the crewmate playing as one;
       - seated at its gun, the gunner gave its legs no turn to relieve in at all.
     - A crew of the director and two bots (a driver and a gunner) is exactly that case once the driver bot dies out on its errands.
   - **The fix** (`RoofWalkerBot.DriverGone`, `AWalkerFor`, `ReliefWanted`; `GunnerBot.Decide`):
     - Nobody's driving when no driver bot is alive on the calls and nobody playing holds the controls (`World.ControlsHolder`, note 574). While someone playing holds them, no relief goes; the moment they let go (dead, or out of the cab), it does.
     - Only a bot counts as the walker a gunner leaves it to.
     - A gunner checks for a relief on every turn, before its gun's own work, and leaves the gun for the cab when there's nobody else to go.
   - **GDD §12 first:** "Nobody at the controls, a bot takes them".
   - **Checked:** `HumanDriverDiesTests` (new): a host, a driver bot and a gunner bot, and someone playing who takes the controls, drives off and dies at them. With the driver bot alive, it comes back to the cab and drives on; with it dead first (the case that failed: the gunner sat at its gun on car 4), the gunner goes forward, takes the cab and drives on.
