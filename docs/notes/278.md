278. **Bends worth braking for: every night carries its tier's hard bends (queue #13, B1; note 265's open call; the director, 6 Oct 2026: derailing on a bend is the core fear, and always "a mistake by the driver, someone not paying attention to the map").** With the Sleepers gone, nothing guaranteed a night anywhere the train could come off. frontier:7's sharpest bend was 707 m (it holds √707 = 26.6 m/s, over the engine's 22), and `dt linegen sweep --cars 6 --seeds 30` found a bend that derails under 22 m/s on only 8/30 Local nights, 11/30 Frontier, 4/30 Dead Lines and 12/30 Deep. The only pieces that laid one (the Drop, the Blind Throat, the Ledge, a curved tunnel) were random picks from whatever budget the quotas left, and on frontier:7 the quotas took it all.
    - **The count is the tier's** (tiers.json columns `bends` [min, max] and `bendDerail` [m/s]; level-design.md Part B has the rules): Local 1–2 at 20–21 m/s, Frontier 3–4 at 16–19, Dead Lines 3–4 at 15–18, Deep 4–5 at 13.5–17, Deep max 5–6 at 12.5–16 (one fewer for Dead Lines and Deep than first set: note 270 made every night 24 km, and the deep tiers' 29–40 km counts didn't fit). The radius is v² / a_derail (plan §8.5's 1.0), never under the tier's `minRadius`: Local's stay at 400–450 m, so its bends teach the board (it derails at 72–76 km/h, boarded 58–61), and a Frontier one is 256–361 m (58–68 km/h, boarded 47–54).
    - **The piece** (setpieces.json `hardBend`, kind `bend`, `LineBuilder.BendShape`): one curve of 40–90° at that radius with 120–260 m of straight either side, so it's seen coming; where room is short, shorter straights, then a smaller turn, and none under 60% of the least. Weight 0: never a budget pick. Not a crunch: like brass it's a demand, and the lethal spacing check (§7.3) keeps it a braking distance from the next lethal check, so a driver who brakes for one can brake for the next.
    - **Where** (`AssignBends`, `CarveBends`): after the quotas, hazards and signatures are handed out, one in each equal share of the line between the threshold and the home straight, to the stretch with room nearest the share's middle. That first try came up short on Dead Lines and Deep (a stretch's musts each reserve a whole recovery they seldom use, so the room estimate says "full" where the filled line has slack): 75 of 30×3 attempts failed the count. So a share still without its bend once the stretches are filled gets one where the line already is: cut into its longest plain connector (a run-up and a run-out of straight either side), or laid on a climb, descent, summit or roller as that piece's own turn (`Params["hardBend"]`), the line going round a hill on its grade. Dead settlements every 4–8 km no longer take room a stretch still owes its queued pieces (`Settlement(…, end − s − Owed(queue))`).
    - **The land says why** (`IntentsFor`): `LedgeUp` 8–20 m on the bend's inside (positive curvature turns left: forward is −Z, right +X), which also hides the way out of it, the reason for the board; `Embankment` 2–6 m down on the outside, what a train would go off into. The geometric sightline round a 283 m bend at the shoulder's 5 m is about √(8 R m) ≈ 106 m, so most aren't blind enough (under `blindSightM` 70) for a restricted zone of its own.
