384. **The Gannet heard (AU1, queue #121; G1's note 340 and its four lines on the audio checklist; design `docs/design/creatures/gannet.md` §4).** The Gannet landed with no sound. It now has its tells and its acts, read off its record as the art reads it.
    - **What plays.** `GameAudio.GannetSounds` goes by its `GannetMode` (in Height), the spine's phase and time, and its Health:
        - **Soaring or climbing** over the train: its call or a great wingbeat every 2.5–6 s (`tell-gannet-calls`).
        - **Hanging over a walker:** nothing. The calls stop, and the silence is the tell.
        - **The fold:** the wings' crack, and the whistle of air for its 1.6 s (`tell-gannet-fold`). The whistle is a one-shot that follows the bird down its line (`Creature.Moving`), so it's heard dropping from above.
        - **The strike:** a stab is heard only if a crewmate was hurt under the beak as it climbs out of the dive. A miss is the thunk into the planks, the thrashing held while it's stuck, and the tear free as the art's `tearFree` starts (`stuckSeconds` less `CreatureArt.GannetTearFreeSeconds`).
        - **The bank:** the scream as it starts, and the wingbeats held, coming in with it (`tell-gannet-bank`).
        - **The pin:** its weight landing, then each peck's wind-up and blow where `CreatureArt.GannetClip` draws them, on the spine's grab clock. Driven off with its victim alive, a screech.
        - **A blow or a ball** that doesn't kill it: a hurt.
        - **Killed:** the crash across the roof. Shot out of the air, it plays when it has fallen to the roof (dead weight from its height, as `GreyboxScene.Falling` draws it), where the car will be by then.
    - **The candidates** (`tools/audio/recipes/gannet_tells.py`, `gannet_acts.py`; 32, two a cue). The CC0 packs have no bird or feather recordings, so its voice is synthesised (a two-sided syrinx through a long neck and the bill's resonances) or bent from a big cat's roar, and its wings are cloth and modelled air.
        - Its call is a gannet's pulsed rasp, 2–4 syllables broken at 25–33 Hz, held in spec A.4's 1–3 kHz. So it can't be taken for the Choir's chorus, the Moose's tonal bellow or the hounds' howl.
        - The fold's whistle is noise through a resonance climbing 2 to 4.8 kHz, swelling about 26 dB to the strike. The train's whistle (the Whistler's tell) is 200–800 Hz and steady.
        - The scream climbs from the call's rasp to about 820 Hz, with its brightest part rising 1 to 2.4 kHz.
        - Every act is high-passed at 100 Hz.
        - The first of each is installed (the hurt's squawk over the croak, whose voice is a tenth of a second: `FIRST_CHOICE`). All four lines are at L1.
    - **Pinned:**
        - `CreatureSoundTests.TheGannetCallsOverheadFallsSilentOverAWalkerWhistlesDownBanksScreamingAndPecksOnItsBeat`: soaring calls; silence over a walker; the fold's two sounds once; a miss's thunk, thrash and tear; a stab only with someone hurt; the bank's scream and wingbeats; the land and four wind-ups and pecks on the beat; driven off; hurt; killed in the air, its crash only once it has fallen.
        - `TheGannetsFoldWhistlesUpItsOwnBandNotTheTrainWhistlesAndItsCallsSitInTheirs`: the installed whistle is over 20 dB above the train whistle's band, rising and swelling to the strike; the calls sit in 1–3 kHz.
    - **Not yet:** the director's Keep or Redo, and real seabird calls and wings (Sonniss, when the network allows) for the voice and feathers.
