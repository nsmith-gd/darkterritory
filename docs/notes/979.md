979. **A note out along the line is left only at a leaving that's there (queue #679, B1; found under note 965's notes at the line's leavings, 21 Oct).**
    - **What was wrong:** #665 (note 965) leaves a note at the railway's leavings, the ones where somebody stopped: a lineman's hut, a camp gone cold, a grave, a wreck. It took them from `LinesideProps.Laid`, which lays every leaving the night deals. But not every one of those is there to see:
        - Inside the departure and arrival towns, nothing of the lineside is drawn or stands. The art passes over it and so do the walls (`LinesideProps.InsideAFort`, note 371). The departure town and its yard run to 1,180 m on a frontier night.
        - Out on the line, a leaving in a board's sight line isn't there either (`Hidden`, note 560), and neither is one crowded out by a building (`InABuilding`). `LinesideProps.Props`, what the art draws and the walls stand, leaves those out.
    - **How much:** on 48 nights (local, frontier, deadLines and deepTerritory, seeds 1-12), 75 of the 183 notes between the stops lay alone, with no hut, camp, grave or wreck beside them. That's two in five:
        - 40 lay in a town: on the departure town's yard by the line (the notes a crew would find first), under its platform by the line (four, out of sight, with the PICK UP prompt over nothing), or in a house of the arrival town (one).
        - 35 more lay at leavings kept out of a board's sight or crowded out by a building, and 5 at leavings only partly drawn.
        - The comet fragments (0 of 32) and the village doorstep notes were clear of everything solid.
    - **The fix:**
        - `LinesideProps.Whole(yaw, pieces, forts)`: a leaving `Laid` lays is there whole when none of its pieces is in a fort's or a town's ground, kept out of a board's sight, or crowded out by a building. These are `Props`' own tests, on the same pieces (`LeavingPiece`, which `LineLeavings` now builds them with).
        - `Oddities.Of(..., forts)` leaves a lineside note only at a whole leaving, and never with its spot in a fort. Each stretch's draw is made first, so the line's other notes stay where they were.
        - `World.EnableOddities` passes the world's `Forts`. `EnableTown` deals the odd things again once the towns' squares and walls are on the forts, before anything's been done to them, as it builds the walls again. On the 48 nights the towns add nothing over the plain fortresses, but the rule is the art's.
        - `dt screenshot --odd ... ` deals them with the night's fortresses too. `--odd note --lineside` now stands the train just past the note, so its leaving is built in the scene, and frames it from down the line over the verge's grass.
    - **The rate:** run.json `oddities.note.lineside` goes from 0.2 to 0.3, so note 965's "three or four a frontier night" holds with every note real. Measured on the same 48 nights, a night's notes between the stops:

      | | local | frontier | deadLines | deepTerritory |
      |---|---|---|---|---|
      | 0.2, whole only | 1.9 | 2.3 | 2.3 | 2.1 |
      | 0.3, whole only | 2.7 | 3.2 | 3.5 | 2.6 |

      Before the fix, 0.2 gave about 3.8 a night, two in five of them alone.
