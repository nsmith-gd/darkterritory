805. **A running jump heard as a jump off the train (AU1, queue #533; AU1's audio audit).** A crewmate's landing (`GameAudio.Crew`, the feet) was the hit of a jump off a moving train (spec B.3: `crew-jump-off.impact`, and on the ground a tumble and `crew-mishaps.pocket-scatter`) whenever it came down faster than `landing.rollAbove` (1.5 m/s) across what it landed on. That's every running jump: from roof to roof (the run's 5.5 m/s over the car), and along the ground. The sim's own knock (`PlayerMotor`'s Land) is only for a landing on the ground, and takes off a run's speed where they left the ground on foot (`PlayerFlags.OffTheGround`, note 573) and a shove's (note 717); a landing on a car takes none.
- **Found by** a bot night heard whole (4 bots, enemies, frontier:7, 8 minutes, the ear on the middle roof, every voice counted): 32 jumps on the roofs, no landing on one, 34 `crew-jump-off.impact.grate`.
- **The fix** (`GameAudio.Knock`): the landing's knock reckoned as the sim reckons it: 0 on a car, less a run's speed off the ground on foot, less a shove's. Over `rollAbove` it's the hit off the train (on the ground, the only place it can be now); under it, a landing (`crew-footsteps.land` on what's underfoot, or the gap's plate).
