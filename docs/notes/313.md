313. **Fog that fills the low ground first (B4, queue #52; linegen plan §14 "Fog density ×1.3 in `low_ground` and `marsh`, ×0.8 on crests"; maritime-rules §4 "fog filling the coves first").** The plan has carried a fog factor per stretch since §14 landed (`PlanExposure.Fog`), and nothing read it: the renderer's fog was the route's one density for the night, the same in a marsh hollow as on a summit.
    - **The factor reaches the eye.** `ITrackConditions.Fog(path, distance)` (1 by default; `PlanConditions` reads the plan) is the stretch's factor averaged over `weather.fogBlendM` (150 m) either side (the runs' exact mean; it was nine samples until note 278 found two run edges 300 m apart stepping it twice over), so a hollow's fog comes up round the train over a few hundred metres and drains away again; at cruise that's about 20 s, never a step. `Views.FogDensity(route, train)` is the route's density times the factor where the engine is, and the app, `dt screenshot`, `dt film`, `dt trailer` and `dt playthrough` all set the frame's fog with it. The drifting fog cards (`Effects.Fog`) each take the factor where they lie, so the low ground ahead is seen filling before the train's in it.
    - **Water is foggy like low ground.** A stretch along a shore (`LinePlan.Shores`: sea, Fundy, dyke, river), or within `weather.fogWaterM` (40 m) of a lake's edge or across one, takes `weather.fogWater` (×1.3); in low ground too it's the larger of the two, not both (`why` "water"). frontier:7's Fundy shore (km 15.6–18.7) and dyke (km 22–24.3) are its foggiest stretches now.
    - **For the eye only.** Nothing the sim decides reads it: §14's fairness plans sightlines at the night's minimum fog distance and leaves that alone, boards stand where they stood, and the lamp still reveals along the track (§22.6). The caveat: at ×1.3 the 20 % contrast distance (density is 1.6 over the fog distance) falls to about 0.77 of the night's mean fog distance, so in a hollow the eye sees a little less than authority planned for; the boards already stand well beyond the fog (§9.5's worked example: 251 m), and restricted speed carries the rest. `weather.fogAlongLine` false keeps the night's one density everywhere; a plan saved before this note reads as false.
    - **Verified:** `FogTests.TheFogThickensInTheLowGroundAndByWaterAndNeverSteps` (frontier:7, deadLines:3: deep in any stretch longer than the blend the fog is that stretch's own; every shore's middle at ×1.3 or more; along the whole line, 10 m at a time, no change bigger than one sample's share of crest-to-hollow) and `FogIsTheSameOnEveryMachineAndOffThePlanItsTheNights`. Seen in `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --at 17000 --view ahead` against main's: on the Fundy shore the buildings across the flats go into the fog and the near ground still reads.
