325. **More set dressing, the first slice: what the railway left beside its line (queue #64, E1; the director's notes of 7 Oct, GDD App. F.3: "Right now I'm seeing the same like three things over and over again, as set dressing, and it's getting kind of boring").** Between the stops, a generated line's lineside was its trees, tufts, rocks and poles, with a shrine now and then; nothing a railway leaves.
    - **Now, the railway's leavings** (`WorldArt.Leavings`, on generated and hand-laid lines alike): one thing to a stretch of 85 m (`LeavingsEvery`), about four in five stretches (`LeavingsChance`), 3 to 14 m out from the rails. Fifteen kinds, the most a scatter has:
      - new in `JunkKit`, each its own piece: a stack of old ties, rails racked on bearers (one bent), oil drums (one on its side, a tar stain), a cable reel, a milepost, a platelayers' hut (brick or board, its roof half off) with ties stacked by it, a wheelset off a wreck, a heap of spilt coal, a camp gone cold, a platelayers' trolley tipped on its side, a grave or two (a tie cross, a cap on one);
      - and props already made but never scattered: stores crates dumped in a heap, fallen freight (timber, sacks, carboys), a sandbagged post with drums behind, a dead signal (on a generated line for the first time).
    - **No kind comes round again soon.** The kinds are dealt from a deck shuffled per 15 stretches (about 1.3 km): within a deck no kind twice, and across a join a deck's first four cards are none of the last four before it (swapped only with its middle, so the deck before's tail is known without dealing every deck back to the start). No kind within four stretches (340 m) of itself, and nearly every kind in 5 km (`LinesideArtTests.TheLeavingsBesideTheLineAreDealtSoNoKindComesRoundAgainSoon`).
    - **Cheap.** 26 to 308 triangles a piece; every one is in `ArtCatalog` (`junk-*`, small props, the hut a medium one), so `dt art check` budgets them and `dt art show junk-hut-0` shows them. The stops, branches, tunnels and bridges clear them as they clear the trees.
    - **Not a mail crane:** a mail crane beside the line is the sim's (note 149), so one dressed in would read as a stop that isn't.
    - **The second slice, a stop's yard** (`WorldArt.YardPiles`, drawn by `YardLeavings` with each building): the yards had buildings and nothing small round them. Now the leavings that belong by a building (ties, rails, drums, a cable reel, a wheelset, spilt coal, stores, fallen freight, sandbags) lie about every works building (a shed, the hero, a goods shed, the powerhouse, a barn, an outbuilding, a station, a signal box, a lamp room, a water tower):
      - Spots at both corners of each gable end and every 7 m along both long walls (`YardSpacing`), 1.3 m out (`YardStandOff`); seven in ten have a pile (`YardChance`).
      - **Never on a track.** A spot within 3.2 m (`YardTrackClear`) of the main line or any of the yard's branches is left out, so the doors' side, where the served track runs, is mostly bare, and so is a shed's back wall when the next track runs by it (P5). A shed with a crane's runway through it has none (the castings lie there).
      - The kinds are dealt from one shuffled deck a stop, so the buildings of one yard don't wear the same pile.
      - The houses are the villages' (B4's and B2's), the wells and the Holdouts their own.
      - **Verified:** `StopArtTests.AYardsPilesLieAboutItsBuildingsAndOffItsTracks` (both nights: every pile 3.1 m or more from any track, the main line's 600 m about it and every branch whole, and close by its building; at least six piles a night); `PerfBudgetTests` green; shots from frontier:7's third stop (the gable of a goods shed by the main line, the powerhouse).
    - **Not yet (the next slices):** more kinds for the villages; the hand-laid line's pines and the generated line's biomes still repeat their own few trees.
