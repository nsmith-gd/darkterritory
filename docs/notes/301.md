301. **Wrenches are the repair tool, Sea of Thieves style (queue #39, D1.3 for D1 at the director's call, the number C1's; GDD App. F.3, the director, 7 Oct 2026: "When it comes to damages on the train, people running into things, anything like that, the wrench doesn't seem to be repairing things in the Sea of Thieves style ... I want to see that as soon as possible").** The first slice, behind `train.json` `repair.wrench` (on):
    - **Everyone carries a wrench** (`player.json` `kit`: crowbar, wrench; slot 2). The cab's rack wrench stays, for anyone who's lost theirs.
    - **The wrench in hand, Use at the break, mends it** (`Repairs`, `CrewActions.Apply`). The breaks: the burst boiler at its fire door (`boiler.json` `repairSeconds`, 25 s, the campaign's faster repair upgrade still divides it; the repair kit no longer mends it); a breach at its hole, from inside (`breach.boardSeconds`; bare hands no longer board it up, and Use at the hole without the wrench does nothing, not even the end door beside it); and, new, a car battered by what it ran into or what ran into it (couplings, hazards, the bend's strain), mended at its dent on the left wall three quarters back (`Repairs.DentAt`, clear of the end doors, the side doors, the crew lockers and the cargo), `integrityPerSecond` (0.04) back while it's worked, never past what a Car Hugger ate (`1 − Eaten`).
    - **A few presses do it as well as a hold.** A mend half done is kept with Use let go while you're still at the break with the wrench in hand (`Repairs.Keeps`; it's the player's own `ActionProgress`, so nothing new is replicated and a client predicts it as before: `NetcodeTests.AClientBoardingUpABreachIsPredictedExactly`, `...MendingADentIsPredictedExactly`). Walk off or put the wrench away, and it's started over. A dent's progress is the car's own `Integrity`, kept anyway.
    - **Every break is called out** (`RepairCallouts.Of`, drawn by `Effects.Repairs`): one language for all of them, an amber glow beating about once a second at the break, a halo and a ring swelling out of it, and torn metal spitting sparks; a shower off each strike while someone's wrench is at it (`GreyboxScene.Mending`); a small hot light so the wall round it reads. A dent is called out below `dentedBelow` (0.9) of what the car can come back to; above it, it's still mended at the dent, but nothing calls you to it. `dt screenshot --view mend --integrity 1,0.5 --breached 2` (`--mending` for the strikes), and `--view firebox --ruptured`.
    - **The HUD** (`Hud.RepairPrompt`): at a break with the wrench, `BOARD IT UP` / `MEND THE BOILER` / `MEND THE CAR : HOLD [E] (n%)`; without it, the break and the key that puts the wrench in hand (`BOILER RUPTURED   WRENCH : [2]`).
    - **The bots mend with it.** The fireman (or the driver alone in the cab) takes its wrench into hand and works the fire door; nobody goes back down the train for the kit (`KitCarry` stands down). A walker boards a breach with its wrench.
    - **Stranded (§23.2) is the kit's rule,** so with the wrench a lost kit strands nobody (`Run`: `StrandedTests.WhereTheWrenchMendsTheBoilerALostKitStrandsNobody`). Whether a night can still be stranded (a rupture with no wrench left aboard?) is the director's call for the next slice. **Answered (the director, 8 Oct 2026):** "If every crew member drops their wrench off the train then leaves them behind and then the train breaks down they could be stranded." So the next slice strands a ruptured night with no wrench left: every wrench (the crew's and the cab rack's) gone the ways §23.2 loses a kit, or dropped off the train and left behind (GDD §23.2).
