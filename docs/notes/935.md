935. **The driver bot vents, waits for its crew, and stops at the stop (Nora, queue #638; the director's playtest of 20 Oct evening).**
   - The lines and the item: `docs/playtest/2026-10-20-evening.md` lines 1, 14 and 18, COORDINATION.md row #638. The director
     played with one bot (Dunmore) driving.
   - **Where the change is** (A1's area, bot behaviour): only in the routines that do it. `ConductorBot.Relieve` (Bots.cs, the
     driver's and the fireman's vent), `StopDriver`'s departure and run-in (StopCrew.cs), and one tuning number
     (facilities.json `crew.approachEase`). Nothing in the boiler, the safety valve, #623's surge (note 903), the ping's
     routines (CrewCalls.Pings.cs, Finn's #639) or the spawn director's files. No wire change.
   - **Line 1, the vent.** The driver vented only for a board, the Sleepers or a fading brake (`Vent`, T106, note 231). With
     the bed full and nothing drawing on it (stood at a stop, or easing off), the gauge climbed past the working band into
     the red and sat on the safety valve, which sheds only 0.6 psi/s. Two bots stood at frontier:7's first stop with the gauge at 100,
     a few seconds short of `ruptureHoldSeconds`.
     - Now `ConductorBot.Relieve` holds the vent key (note 264: anywhere in the cab, whatever else it's doing) while the gauge
       is over the top of the working band (boiler.json `workingBandMax`, 90). If the speed it's driving to wants more (an
       express's 21 m/s wants 92), it uses that, never above `redline` − 1. It lets go 4 under (`ReliefBand`).
     - Steam over the band is steam the valve would blow off, and the hounds come to a hot boiler (enemies.json
       `houndsHotBoilerWeight`), so nothing the driving needs is lost.
     - Someone playing at the controls keeps the gauge (note 597's rule stands: the fireman then vents only at the red).
   - **Line 14, the run in.** The harness never showed the train standing past a stop's hold (the most was 0.1 m). What the
     director saw was the run in: on the brake's own curve (`BrakeRate`, about 0.66 m/s² on frontier:7) the driver ran at the
     cruise until 160 m short and came down hard. From half a kilometre out it looked like it wasn't stopping.
     - Now the approach to a stop's hold, and to a coaling tower's, is held under a stop at `crew.approachEase` (0.25 m/s²)
       from where it is (`StopDriver.Eased`, never under 1 m/s, the last metres on `Toward`'s own curve). The stop is taken
       up far enough out for that (`EaseDistance`). From 14 m/s it's seen easing down about 400 m out. It costs about 20 s
       a stop.
   - **Line 18, waiting for the crew.** `CrewCalls.Riding` and `AllAboard` count the bots that say a part. A person playing
     never says one, so with one bot and one person "everyone aboard" was always true. The driver backed out the moment it
     called the loading done: the crane counted done with no pair for it (`Loaded`, `!pairs`), or the crates idle for
     `crew.peopleWait`, with the director at the crane.
     - Now `StopDriver.People` (set each tick from `ConductorBot.Players`) is the people playing. A stop's departure
       (`Riding`, `AllAboard` in the Loading, Clear, Coaling, SetBack, Spouting, Conveying, Tippling and Lifting legs) waits
       for anyone playing on the ground. The give-ups ("long enough waited") no longer leave them: only being late for the
       dawn does (`MayLeave`, `LateAt`), the same rule that calls the loading done.
     - The handcar counts as aboard. A dead person isn't waited for. Bot-only crews are as they were (`Players` is empty).
     - **The pinged fire** (line 18's "he didn't put out the fire in car one"): a car someone pinged burning (GDD §12, note
       739) is remembered (`StopDriver.PingedFire`, kept past the ping's 20 s until that car's fire is out or the car's gone).
       The stop's departure waits on it as on a person. Who goes to put it out is the ping's business. `CrewCalls.Can` gives a
       fire ping to walkers only, so a lone driver's crew has nobody to take it. That's Finn's #639 (agreed through the Token
       Budget Manager: Finn has CrewCalls.Pings.cs, Nora has StopDriver's departure and the driver's own routine).
