1024. **A town's standing lowered by what's brought into it (n1-S1, nathaniel1; queue #652; GDD §3.1 "A town remembers" and v1.6's "Not yet"; the audit, note 949, question 2).** *In review* ([#1784](https://github.com/nsmith-gd/darkterritory/pull/1784)).
   - **Before.** A creature ridden in through a town's gate is fought in its yard and burns houses by the train (notes 608, 958, 966); the town kept only those burnt houses (`TownAt.Burnt`, the campaign save's `Burnt`), and its gatekeeper and guards said so.
   - **The director's call is open** (the Director's Inbox, `nathaniel3-gdd-town-standing`: a colder welcome, dearer upgrades, fewer guards, or nothing more). Under the standing orders this builds the most reasonable default behind a flag, towns.json `standing.mode`: `welcome` (the default: a colder welcome and dearer upgrades), `guards` (that and fewer guards), `off` (nothing more than the burnt houses).
   - **Built** (Sim, host-side and deterministic; every machine makes the same cold town from the route spec; no protocol bump):
     - **The standing** (`StandingTuning`, towns.json `standing`): each creature brought in makes the town `perCreature` (40) percent colder to the crew, up to `most` (100), easing `easePerNight` (10) a night settled, wherever the crew is: one creature is four nights, two are eight.
     - **Kept per town in the campaign save** (`CampaignState.Standing`, percent by `TownAt.Key`; `Campaign.Arrived` adds the night's `RunReport.BroughtIn`, `Campaign.Settle` eases through `Campaign.Eased`). An old save has none: every town is warm.
     - **Carried in the town** (`TownAt.Cold`, after the burnt count in the spec: `Fort Boudreau/coal/1234/1/40`; an older 3- or 4-part spec reads as warm), through `LinePlan`'s `PlanFortress.TownCold` and `TownSite.Cold` to the plan (`TownPlan.Cold`), so the departure town is built cold. The arrival town gets it from the setup's `Cold` (a JSON field of the Welcome's setup, beside `Burnt`).
     - **A colder welcome** (`TownGenerator.Cold`, on a stream of its own, `"standing"`, so a warm town's plan is byte for byte as before), each scaled by how cold (0 to 1):
       - the gatekeeper says why, after the gate's law: "The last crew brought one of them in through that gate. Your train, your number on the cab. {town} hasn't forgotten." (world/towns.json `broughtIn.standing`; the burnt houses' gate line is left out while it's cold, so it isn't said twice);
       - of the other townsfolk (not the gate's or the guards), `wary` (1.0) x cold open on a wary line (`broughtIn.wary`);
       - of the lived-in doors the crew knocks on (not the open houses, which are walked into), `doors` (0.75) x cold stay shut, with what's said through them (`broughtIn.door`);
       - of the crew's waves at the townsfolk (note 730), `waves` (0.8) x cold are stared at, not waved back (`World.Waves`, drawn from the wave and the person, the same on every machine).
     - **Dearer upgrades** (`Campaign.UpgradeCost` / `BuyUpgrade` with the standing; `Campaign.Dearer`): the blacksmith of the town the crew's in asks (1 + `dearer` (0.5) x cold) of the list price, and says so (`broughtIn.smith`). Only that town's forge: every other town's is the list price. The fortress menu's catalogue, the forge's and a joiner's view all show it.
     - **Fewer guards** (mode `guards`, off by default): the yard fights a creature brought in (1 + `fightLonger` (1.0) x cold) times as long (`Run.FightSeconds`), it burns round(`moreBurns` (2) x cold) more houses (`Run.BurnsEach`; the scene's and the audio's fires follow it), and (`fewerGuards` (0.75) x cold) of its guards keep back from the fight (`Town.TurnsOut`); the gatekeeper always turns out.
   - **Seen:** `dt town standing [--route frontier:7] [--brought 1] [--nights 6] [--mode welcome|guards|off]`: the crew brings creatures into the route's terminus town, then rides nights elsewhere; each night how cold it is, the forge's price there and elsewhere, the gatekeeper's first lines, the blacksmith's, how many townsfolk are wary, doors shut, the share of waves returned, and with fewer guards the guards turning out, the fight's length and the burns. Frontier:7's Grieve, one creature: 40% cold, the lamps 234 scrip there against 195 elsewhere, 23 of 81 townsfolk wary, 49 of 153 doors shut, a wave returned 68% of the time; four nights on, warm. `dt campaign show` lists the save's `standing` and the forge's `dearer`; `dt campaign nights` each night's `fromCold`/`toColdOnArrival`.
   - **Tests** (`TownStandingTests`, 8): the default; the standing falls with a creature brought in and eases night by night (and through the spec to the next night's departure town); the forge asks more there and nowhere else, and is paid it; the lines go cold, the gatekeeper says why, doors stay shut, and a warm town is unchanged; the same town on every machine, everyone in their place; off, only the burnt houses; fewer guards: a longer fight, more burns, fewer guards out; the save round-trips and an old save is warm.
   - **Not done** (the director's call first): townsfolk keeping away from the crew on their rounds (they still walk where they walked); fewer jobs or tips (#657's buyer, unbuilt, would be where a tip is paid).
