1043. **The visual tell audit's staging gaps: `dt tells` stands every creature (n1-G1, nathaniel1's Token Budget Manager's worker; queue #742; note 1037's ranks 8-9).** *In review.* Note 1037's audit couldn't stand four creatures (the Follower, the Track Doll, the Choir, the Tapper) and had two measuring problems (the Freight Beetle, Hotbox). Every tell is now staged and measured, each on a gym night of its own: 45 tells, 180 frames, a full run in about 8.5 minutes on lavapipe.
   - **How each is staged** (`src/DarkTerritory.Dev/Tells/TellAudit.cs`: `Stage`, `Prime`, `Ready`, `Toward`; the numbers in `content/tuning/tells.json`, whose new `moving` is the speed the train is held at from the call to the shot):
     - **Follower.** The caller steps down onto the stop's follower ground, the only place it takes anyone (it's in the yard's shed at the gym's stop). Once it's on them, they walk out beside the engine, 8 m off its side, walking away from the train. The eye is someone at the cab's side, behind them.
     - **Track Doll.** The train is held at 12 m/s (a standing train makes her go as she shows). The shot is taken when she's 60 m ahead of the cab, from outside the cab's side window, past the engine's flank, not inside the boiler.
     - **Choir.** The audit waits (up to 30 s) for its ghosts to come; at 1 s it was still gathering.
     - **Tapper.** The caller is seated at the first gun on a train held at 12 m/s (it leaves a train under `leaveBelow`, 3 m/s). The shot is taken when it's up on its knees tapping (its telegraph), from up the train, the way it came; from the gunner's side, the gun and the gunner are between.
     - **Freight Beetle.** The eye stands where it's heading, the freight coming at you.
     - **Hotbox.** Its heat comes only with its wheels turning; at a stand it comes half out of its truck after 2 s instead. That's why note 1037 saw nothing glow 120 s in: what it measured was the unfolded body. The audit gives it its `knockSeconds`' heat (90 s fed at its 18 m/s reference speed) and holds the train at 10 m/s, so it glows. Driving the 1.6 km instead tipped a car on the way. The eye is on the side of the car it's in. The glow matches `dt screenshot --view hotboxbug`'s: the axle box lit orange, its light on the ballast.
     - **Tippy Toesie** (it was staged in note 1037's run only by luck). Hidden, it waits at the line's start and comes out behind whoever's stood still and alone its `idleSeconds` (5 s). Note 1037's run got it only because the tells before had kept the caller standing long enough; alone (`--only tippyToesie`) it never came, on main too. In the cab, "behind" is clamped into the cab's few feet; toward the engine is the gym's safe ring, which drives it off. So the caller stands still for 5 s on the ground beside the cab, facing the train, before the call.
