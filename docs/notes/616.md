616. **The app's bots are the harness's (D1.4, queue #348, at D1's ask after #345 and note 613: "the director plays with the app's bots, and every bot sweep we run is the harness's").** Note 613 had bots hosted as the app hosts them (`NetPlaySession`, `dt campaign nights`) dying far more than the harness's (`Harness.Run`, `dt campaign play`) on the same contracts. They were dragged off roofs, smothered, eaten on the line, and burned in cars, and three nights of four were lost at dawn.
    - **The cause: the host's own seat, left idle.**
        - The app always has its own player in the crew, joining after the bots. `dt campaign nights` stepped it with no input, so all night it stood where the yard put it: on car 3's roof.
        - The idle player costs the bots:
            - The director's census makes them a walker (`Census.PostOf`) piling up slack, so Draggers, Fire Flies, Climbers and car fires are weighted their way, and pressure comes on from 150 s of slack (enemies.json `census`).
            - `Heed.Rescue` sends bots for whoever holds them.
            - Dead and freed from a Holdout, they stand on the ballast, and the driver backs up for the left-behind and stands 120 s (`ConductorBot.ForTheLeftBehind`).
            - At a stop they're a pair of hands the bots count on (facilities.json `peopleAreHands`), so the crew takes on loading it can't finish and stands, doors open, for `peopleWait`.
            - The kit run can pick them, and a hot box near them is left.
        - On night 1 of note 613's campaign (local:435, 3 bots):
            - The idle seat lost 2 to 3 crew, the idle player first (dragged off car 3's roof, then smothered once a Holdout freed them).
            - With the seat played by one more bot (`dt campaign nights --human bot`, now the default), nobody died, net 985.
        - Nobody plays idle, so no bot is changed for it. An idle host (somebody away from the keys) is a hazard to their crew in the game, as it would be with people.
    - **What else the two hosts did differently, now the same.** `dt campaign play` hosted its night unlike the app:
        - the crew walking aboard from 400 m back in the yard, where the app stands the train at the gate (run.json `departShortOfGateM`) with the crew aboard, cab first and the rest on the roofs;
        - the director on the harness's seed (1, every night), where the app's is the route's.

        Both now go through **`HostedAsApp`**:
        - `HostedAsApp.Campaign` makes a campaign night's `SessionSetup`, the one the app's Start now takes too.
        - `HostedAsApp.For` makes the harness's night as the app hosts it: the line, tunings, consist and freight, the start and boarding, the director's seed (`HarnessOptions.DirectorSeed`, new), the run, facilities, Holdouts, lineside, upkeep and finds.
        - `dt campaign play` and `dt campaign nights` both use them.
        - The harness's own defaults, and every sweep and test on them, are as they were. `--as-harness start,seed` puts the old start or seed back beside the app's, to compare.
    - **A night is a throw of the dice.** On night 1, the old harness, the app's start with the harness's seed, the walk aboard with the route's seed, and the app's way with 4 bots were all delivered with nobody lost. The app's way with 3 bots lost 2, on the same contract: one night measures little. Over four nights, the same crew size each way:
