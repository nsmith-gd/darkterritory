1013. **The Close: a soft grey sack that lies on the roof of a car the crew have crowded into, bows it in, pours in and pins them; burst it from outside, or get out (queue #716; the director's playtest of 23 Oct, line 36; Sloe's design, note 1004; Rook, the Token Budget Manager's worker, nathaniel2).**
   - **The ask:** the director, 23 Oct, line 36: "if everybody's crowded into one car ... something that does a little bit of
     crowd control, but I really don't like that the air goes bad. That feels gimmicky. It should always be a creature with a
     clear tell and a clear counter." Sloe's `docs/design/creatures/the-close.md` (note 1004) answers it. It foils turtling in
     one shut car.
   - **Off by default** (`enemies.json` `close.on`): the director hasn't given a verdict on Sloe's creatures. The design doc's
     status says "built behind a flag, off, awaiting the director's verdict". The `r716-close` edition turns it on.
   - **Kind 51** (reserved for it), **protocol 91** (main's 90 + 1).

   **What's built.**
   - **Sim** (`Enemies/Close.cs`):
     - **Its own census** (`Crowding`, read in the director's once-a-second `Read`, only with `close.on`): the behaviour reader
       (note 999 §2b) has no crowding behaviour, so the Close counts for itself. Each car with `crowd` (3) of the living crew
       on its floor (inside its `Interior`, not its roof, steps or plates), or `crowdSmall` (2) on a crew of `smallCrew` (3) or
       fewer, and its four doors shut (the hatch is its way in, so not counted), and the seconds running it has been.
     - **Comes:** its spawn rule holds at `boardAbove` (8 m/s) or more, out of the forts, none about, and a car crowded and
       shut for `settle` seconds by tier (60, 40, 35, 30): the longest such car. Weight `tierWeights`.
     - **Bow** (the telegraph, from the moment it settles): on that car's roof over the hatch, `bowSeconds` (10; never under
       the reaction window). **Pour** (the commit): `pourSeconds` (4). The car down to fewer than the crowd at any time and it
       slides off (`BreakOff`, `Gone`).
     - **Pin:** the spine's grab on the weakest in the car (lowest health, then lowest id), its window `killSeconds` (20);
       everyone else on the car's floor held each tick too (`EnemyContext.Hold`). `crushAfter` seconds pinned by tier (8, 6,
       5, 4) each takes `crushPerSecond` (5) a second as a bite (never the last point). The window out: the weakest is killed
       (`DeathCause.Crushed`, the spine's punish) and it slides off sated.
     - **The door:** a door of the car open (either end, either side; opened by the pinned or by a friend outside), every
       `getOutSeconds` (1) the pinned crewmate nearest an open door is let go (never the one it's crushing first). Fewer than
       the crowd left and it lets go of everyone. **The pinned can still Use a door of its car in reach**
       (`Close.DoorInReach`, in `World.CrewAct`): otherwise a held player's Use is the struggle's alone (note 681). From
       replicated state, so a predicting client works the door as the host does.
     - **Burst:** a blow from anyone not on the car's floor (its roof, the open door, the next roof across the gap: its
       `MeleeRadius` is its car's half length, `Extra`, since it lies the car's length) counts one; `health` (3) burst it
       (`Slay`, not for the night: it can come to another crowd). A gun's round (or a crewmate fired from the cannon) bursts it
       at once (`Hit`). Blows from inside go into its thickness: nothing.
     - In the creature gym (`World.CallOnly`) it doesn't slide off for want of a crowd: it lies bowing until cleared, and pins
       whoever goes in.
   - **Bots** (`Heed.TheClose`, through intent, in `RoofWalkerBot.Decided` ahead of the guns' manning): in the car and free,
     out by the nearer end door with footing beyond it (never a side door at speed, note 380), opened if shut; pinned, the door
     in reach faced and worked; outside, on its roof, clubbing it (`Heed.Strike`), or along the roofs to it from up to two cars
     off. A car with a Close on it is `WarmUp.Troubled`: walkers warming in it come out, and none go in.
   - **Art** (`CreatureArt.Close.cs`): procedural, a sack with no limbs and so no rig; its clips are the sim's modes drawn as
     lumpy superellipsoids that breathe on the tell's 3 s: `bow` (slumped along the roof; the roof's planks bowed in over the
     room, the skin pressing through the seams), `pour` (bulging down through the hatch), `pin` (filling the room, drawn from
     inside too), `burst` (sagged off the side). `CreatureBodyTests` checks its body against what's drawn (with the stand-ins);
     `CreatureArtTests` draws it in every phase. `dt art clearance` has nothing to check: it has no model and no limbs.
   - **Views:** `close` (off the car's side, the sack on its roof) and `closeroof` (from the room's floor up at the bowed
     boards); `dt screenshot --close bow|pour|pin|burst` stages any mode.
   - **Sound** (`tell-close.bow`, `GameAudio.Close.cs`): a synth stand-in, the boards' creak every 3 s while it bows, its weight
     at 150-400 Hz and the groan that carries at 480-680 Hz (`AudioBench.FlaggedTellBands`): 16.4 dB over the bed from the cab
     and 19.9 dB from the last roof of a 20-car train at 22 m/s.
   - **The gym** (`dt gym --only close`): called onto the car with the most of the crew in it, else the nearest car with a room.
   - **Tests** (`CloseTests`): off does nothing; the census's settle, an open door and a thin car; the bow's timing, the pour,
     the pin and the weakest held; no walking, the crush, the kill; a friend on the roof bursts it and blows from inside don't;
     a gun's round; the pinned against the door get out; bots leave a bowing car before it pours; the same night twice.
     `SpecTableTests.TheCloseMatchesB23` pins spec B.23.

   **Not built, for the director's verdict or a later pass.**
   - The design's late column's "one on every crowded car at once": one at a time on every tier.
   - The pinned aren't pushed to the walls (held where they stand), and a held player's Use works only a door of the car.
   - The lamp swinging on its hook (the car's own lamp; the bowed boards and the creak are the tell now).
   - A modelled sack with a skin shader and a swell blend (the art chat's), and AU1's takes for the creak.
   - Bot gunners don't lay on it (`GunAnswers` stays false: the car's body is in the way of most arcs).
