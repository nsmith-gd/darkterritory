1011. **The Tapper: a long folded thing that comes over the roof behind a gunner left alone on their gun, taps twice, and lifts them off the train (queue #714; the director's playtest of 23 Oct, line 36; Sloe's design, note 1004; Rook, the Token Budget Manager's worker, nathaniel2).**
   - **The ask:** the director, 23 Oct, line 36: "I am sitting on the back gun alone for too long ... every creature's purpose
     is to foil some sort of behavior", always "with a clear tell and a clear counter". Sloe's `docs/design/creatures/tapper.md`
     answers it. It foils note 999 §2b's **gun alone**, and the behaviour reader decides when it comes.
   - **Off by default** (`enemies.json` `tapper.on`): the director hasn't given a verdict on Sloe's creatures. The design
     doc's status says "built behind a flag, off, awaiting the director's verdict". The `r714-tapper` edition turns it on.
   - **Built on two open PRs:** `rook-bruin-712` (#1707: kind 48, protocol 88) and `rook-behaviours-703b` (#1700: the
     behaviour reader). So the Tapper is kind 49 and protocol 89. Whichever lands first, this takes main's version + 1
     when it merges (COORDINATION rule 10).

   **What's built.**
   - **Sim** (`Enemies/Tapper.cs`):
     - **Comes:** its spawn rule holds at `boardAbove` (8 m/s) or more. The behaviour reader (note 999 §2b,
       `Behaviours.At`) must show a crewmate seated at a gun as `gunning` for `dwell` seconds by tier (90, 90, 60, 45), with
       nobody within its `aloneM` (12 m). One at a time, `tierWeights` (none on Local). It reads the reader, so it needs
       targeting on (on by default).
     - **The approach:** it comes over that car's roof `comeFromM` (6 m) behind the gunner, against the way their gun
       faces: the rear gun faces back down the line, so behind is the car's front. It crawls (`crawl` 0.8 m/s) to
       `tapBehindM` behind them.
     - **The tap** (the telegraph). `tapSeconds` on by tier (5, 5, 4, 3; never under the reaction window), the lift: a
       grab, `liftSeconds` (10), then they're dropped off the train (`DeathCause.Dropped`).
     - **The look:** the gunner facing it within `lookDegrees` (30°) for `lookSeconds` (1). It flinches flat and backs off
       the way it came for `holdSeconds` by tier (30, 30, 20, 10), then comes again. **Seated, the view stays inside the
       gun's arc** (`Guns.Sit`), and the rear gun's doesn't reach behind, so looking round means getting up. That's the
       design's "the gunner has to stand".
     - **Company:** anyone else on its car, or within `companyM` (4 m) of it, and it folds and goes over the side.
     - **A blow** knocks it off (not killed). A friend's blow while it lifts drops the gunner on the roof. `health` (3) at
       once kills it (`Slay`).
     - **It goes** when the train is under `leaveBelow` (3 m/s), or its gunner is off their car.
     - Staged without a car (a look, a test), it stands where it's put.
     - Deterministic and the host's: no draws; it reads state.
   - **The director's tables:** `costs.tapper` 2. `target.answers.gunning` and `target.behaviours.foils.gunAlone` +
     `tapper`, so the aim weighs it up for a lone gunner. Eligibility's gates (`dt eligibility`), `HitConfirmTests`' roster,
     `bodies.tapper`.
   - **The tell** (`tell-tapper.tap`, a synth stand-in; `GameAudio.Tapper.cs`): two dry knocks on the gun's iron, once
     each time it comes to tap.
     - **Moved from the design's 600–900 Hz** to a knock at 900–1400 Hz with a short iron ring at 1.25 kHz. That clears
       the Bruin's chest whistle (~820 Hz, note 1007) and the gun's own laying (`gun-lay`, 160–500 Hz).
     - Its rhythm (two, dry, overhead) tells it from the hounds' howl and the Hotbox's knock (low, in a truck, a run).
     - Checked with `dt audio render --sound tell-tapper.tap`: two transients, the ring a line just above 1 kHz, where
       the Bruin's whistle sits just below it.
     - The audio chat's to replace with takes (not yet a line in `tools/audio/cues.py`).
   - **Greybox** (`GreyboxScene`, `Staging.Tapper.cs`). Flat and folded like a broken umbrella, its long arms spread,
     crawling. Up on its knees with an arm out over the seat at the tap, both arms up lifting. Facing the gunner along its
     car. Views:
     - `dt screenshot --view tapper`: crawling along the guard car's roof toward the rear gun.
     - `dt screenshot --view tappertap`: at the tap, beside the gun.
