## The big forests

- **Where (`DeepWoods`, Sim/LineGen).** Long stretches of the main line where the woods close in, a pure function of the
  night's plan and seed (a stream per run down the line; the plan's JSON round trip gives the same woods), so the sim, the
  art, the sound and anything coming out of the trees all find them alike. Each biome that has them says how much of its line
  they take and how they stand (biomes.json `deepWoods`: `share`, `lengthM`, `edgeM`, `cover`, `nearM`, `extraTries`,
  `wallM`, `shade`, `gatesM`): black spruce 0.75 of its line in 0.8-2.2 km stretches, the spruce-and-fir edge 0.45, the drumlins 0.2,
  the coast and the highland 0.15, the valley farmland 0.1; none in the bog, the barrens, the dykelands, the dead towns,
  the coal country. The plan's own Dark Forests (setpieces.json `darkForest`, §7.2) are deep woods too wherever the country
  grows any. Trimmed to the country that grows them, joined where they nearly meet, and kept 600 m clear of either town's
  gate (`gatesM`): out on the line, never round a fortress (300 m, local:5's departure square drew 1.67 M triangles, over
  `maxFrameTriangles`' 1.5 M; at 600 m it draws main's 1.36 M, FogLoadingTests). frontier:7: 43% of the night between the
  gates (km 0.6-1.6 and 2.3-4.3); deadLines:3 38%; local:5 6% (local's farmland and edge woods have the fewest).
- **How they stand (LinesideProps.Slot).** Eased in over `edgeM` (80 m) at either end: the stands cover 0.97 of the land,
  the first trunks 5.2 m from the line's middle (a car's side is 1.6 m out: the crew walk the 3.5 m between; past the telegraph poles at 4.5 m), 40 more tries a
  12 m slot within 30 m of the line (drawn after the slot's own 50, so out of the woods not a tree moves), all grown as
  tall as the thickest forest's. Measured (DeepWoodsTests): in 200 m of frontier:7's deep woods clear of any stop, 29 trunks
  within 8 m of the line and 193 within 20 m, against 2.8 and 52 in its other woods. Every one is the sim's wall (note 371):
  walked into, not through. A stop's yard is still cleared of them.
- **Dark under the canopy (the art).** The forest floor under the stands darker by `shade` (WorldArt's duff), the trees too,
  a little (PlanArt); the stands' masses behind them at the deep woods' cover.
- **What it costs.** In the deep woods only the first rank is modelled (`DeepModelledM`, 14 m), the rest are the crossed
  cards already drawn past 40 m elsewhere. All modelled, a cell uploaded 98 MB (main's 29 MB) and `dt perf --ride 8`'s
  worst frame was 605 ms; as instances, the cab drew 1,788 draws (`maxPassDraws` 1,500). As landed, `dt perf --route
  frontier:7 --ride 8 --at 3700` (8 s at 25 m/s through the second stretch, llvmpipe): median 1.74 ms (main 1.69),
  p99 29.9 ms (main 16.9; five frames, the cells' uploads: 28 MB a cell, main's 29 MB), worst 59 ms (main 48), 5 hitches
  (main 6). The cab at km 3.1: 0.65 M triangles (main 0.55 M), 45 draws (main 45); trackside 0.82 M, 173 draws.
- **Checked.** `dt linegen generate` lists them (`deepWoods`: share, stretches by km) and maps them (a dark green band
  under the main line). `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --view cab|trackside --at 4300`: the woods a wall either side
  from the cab, the trackside camera stood inside them. `dt holes --route frontier:7 --from 1500 --to 5600 --every 300
  --edges main`: 42 frames down both stretches, no holes.

## The wilds heard

- **`world-woods`** (tools/audio/cues.py; recipes/world_woods.py, modelled throughout: no recordings, so CC0 by
  construction): `hush`, the deep woods round a stood train (the wind up in the canopy, a trunk creaking as the tops sway,
  needles and drips, 20 s loop, −31 LUFS); `far`, four far calls 150-300 m off (a barred owl's "who cooks for you", a great
  horned owl's five, two barred owls answering, a dead tree coming down); `branch`, five things going close by in the
  trees (a twig snapped, a limb cracking down, a cone dropping, a bough shaken, two twigs a beat apart); `heavy`, four of
  something big moving 50-80 m out (heavy steps in the duff, a sapling whipping back, a dead limb broken underfoot, a long
  low breath, then still). No throat, no call: nothing a tell sounds like. Installed as a fresh line (ci_build.py's
  `fresh_items`, then install.py: each cue's one candidate, "first").
- **Wired (`GameAudio.WorldWoods`).** By how deep in the woods the engine stands (`DeepWoods.Depth`) and only with the train
  stood (under 0.5 m/s): the hush in over 6 s, out in 2 s once it moves; a far call every 15-35 s, a branch every 6-16 s,
  something big every 40-90 s, out in the trees beside the line. The open country's night bed thins to 0.4 under it. The
  woods shelter the ear from the weather's gale (its sound alone; the sim's wind on the roofs is as it was). Muted down a
  tunnel and a mine, as the night is (spaces.json).
- **Never drowning a tell.** `dt audio render --scenario woods` (the train stood in frontier:7's first deep woods, every
  demo tell round it, heard from the ballast beside car 2) against `--scenario woods-open` (the same, stood out of the
  woods): every tell is as clear or clearer in the woods (AudioTests `StoodInTheBigForestsTheWoodsAreHeardAndNoTellIsLostInThem`).
  Stems in the woods: hush −31 dB, the open night −34.9, heavy −46.8, branch −49.4, far −54.3. **For AU1:** stood on the
  ballast in frontier:7's open night (no woods), its gale (`world-wind.gale`, −32.7 dB) and the night bed (−28.8) leave the
  Sleepers' writhe at −3.2 dB and the Dragger's scrape at −8.4 dB over the bed in their bands, on main as here; the woods
  make both a little clearer (−2.0, −7.9), but it's the open night's to fix.
- **The Audio Checklist.** Row `world-woods` ("The deep woods", World & hazards, level 1, review) written as a new document
  (version 1), its four candidates' previews in the page's asset store (tools/audio/assets.json).
