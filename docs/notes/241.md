241. **The wind on a roof and a mended radio, heard (note 201's hazards; mechanic telegraphs and confirmations).** Note 201 made the wind push roof standers and the repair kit mend a broken radio, both silent.
    - **The gust that's pushing you** (`GameAudio.RoofGust`): up on a roof, a gale's roar held off the side the wind blows from (a push to the car's right comes off its left), as loud as `PlayerMotor.WindPush` says it pushes. That's the sim's own sum, so you hear the gust you're in, building before it walks you to the edge and easing as it passes. Only your own: it's what your footing feels.
    - **The line's gusts, not dice:** the gust cues (`world-wind.gust`) had been at random times from a random side, which would now mislead. On a line with weather they're the line's own gusts (`PlayerMotor.Gust`, the field the sim pushes with), each heard as it rises past 0.6 of full, from its side. `bed-wind`'s random gusts stand down there; a hand-laid line without conditions keeps them.
    - **A radio mended:** at a broken radio, the kit opened as the hands go to work (`MendTicks` rising off 0, on the body record, so everyone near hears it), its ratchet held while they stay at it, and once it's whole the kit shut and the set's squelch: the radio's back. Let go early and the ratchet stops.
