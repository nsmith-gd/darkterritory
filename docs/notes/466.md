466. **The conveyor line heard (AU1, queue #202; A1's #446, note 400: "its sound is AU1's: the belt, the drive and the jam, a line on the audio checklist"; spec D.2 "start machinery at a powerhouse, then clear jams as they occur").** The grain elevator's conveyor is started at its drive house, carries grain up its riser into the car under its head, jams every 30–60 s of carrying, and stalls if a jam's left 20 s. Note 400 has it started "loudly", and it's machinery the Choir hears. None of it made a sound.
    - **Reading:** C1's drive house (note 430's `drive_house`) is an engine house: an exhaust stack through its roof, sooted, and a flywheel turning beside it. So the drive is heard as a big single-cylinder oil engine, started off the yard's power (note 400: a dead yard's powerhouse is restarted first). The starter's hold cranks it over, and when the hold's done it catches and the clutch puts the belt in.
    - **How** (`GameAudio.ConveyorSounds`, off the site's replicated record: `Running`, `Carrying`, `Jam`, `JamFor`, `Start`, `Clear`, alike on every machine):
        - **Starting:** `place-conveyor.cranking` at the drive house while someone holds the starter (`Start` > 0, not running). `place-conveyor.catch` once as it starts. The running loop comes in under the catch 3.2 s on, as the catch reaches the engine's beat.
        - **Running:** `place-conveyor.engine` at the drive house, `place-conveyor.belt` at the point of the belt nearest the ear (along the low run or up the riser; quieter with nothing on it), and `place-conveyor.pour` at the head while it carries into a car.
        - **A jam:** `place-conveyor.jam` once where it is (`Site.JamAt`). Until it's cleared the drive labours (`place-conveyor.labour`: the belt slipping on the drum, the engine bogged down) in place of the engine and the belt, louder the longer it's left toward the stall. `place-conveyor.clearing` at the jam while someone's at it (`Clear` > 0). Cleared with the drive turning, `place-conveyor.free` where the jam was; cleared while stalled, nothing (nothing pulls the belt).
        - **The stall:** the line stopping with a jam still in it is the jam left too long (the sim's only stop with one in), and `place-conveyor.stall` plays at the drive house.
        - The edges are read wherever the ear is, so a line that started or jammed out of earshot plays nothing late; within 250 m of the drive house it's heard.
        - **The HUD's hold ticks:** the starter's hold and clearing a jam (`GameAudio.Holding`: `StartBelt`, `Unjam`), done when the line's running or the jam's gone, as the prompts read them.
        - **Captions:** the engine starting, the belt jamming, the belt squealing and the engine labouring (the jam's call, which goes on out of sight down the belt), and the engine dying.
    - **The sounds** (`tools/audio/recipes/conveyor.py`): ten candidates on the Audio Checklist's new `place-conveyor` line, one a cue, all installed. The machinery's iron is the packs' real metal, pitched to its size and choked where it's bolted. The exhaust, the starter and the belt's rubber are modelled. The drive's loops stay under 2-2.2 kHz (spec A.4 rule 1: the tells' band clear while a stop's work goes on round the crew).
        - `engine`, `oil-engine`: one cylinder at 300 rpm firing every other turn, a slow pop-pop out of the stack (a pressure pulse rung by the stack's quarter-wave modes, the valve's crack and the thump off its mouth), the crank's knock, the tappets, the flywheel's swing, the drive belt's joint slapping, the hut's tin buzzing. 8 s exact cycle.
        - `labour`, `slip`: the belt slipping and catching on the drum (a squeal near 700 Hz with each catch, a judder 20 times a second), the engine dragged down to 2.25 firings a second, firing hard.
        - `cranking`, `starter`: the starter motor's hum and its pinion's whine, slowing 25 % into each compression; the pinion grinding; the engine breathing out of the stack.
        - `catch`, `fires`: a hard first pop with the starter in, the pinion thrown out and its whine running down, the pops quickening to the beat, the clutch thrown and the belt taking up with a groan.
        - `stall`, `dies`: the pops slowing and weakening, the squeal dying with them, a last cough, the flywheel coasting and the belt going slack.
        - `belt`, `idlers`: the idlers' rumble, a dry bearing chirping, the splice ticking over each idler, the return roller, the grain shifting on it.
        - `pour`, `head`: grain off the head pulley down a short chute into the car, lighter than the spout's, the pulley rumbling in its hood.
        - `jam`, `bunch`: the grain heaping, the belt riding up onto the stringer with a bang, rubber dragged on steel, grain spilling, the belt shuddering to a stop.
        - `clearing`, `hands`: grain dug out by the double handful and flung down, the belt hauled at, the idler frame knocked.
        - `free`, `slap`: the belt jerking free with a slap, the idlers spinning up, the heap sliding off down the belt.
    - **Not yet:** the drive's speed on Low power (note 400: half rate) isn't heard; the engine runs as it does on Live. The head's gate shutting over a full car isn't heard either: the pour just stops.
