723. **Bringing them home (K1, queue #457; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md` §3, GDD App. F.6).**
The friendslop audit's Friends goal (note 733: "It's extremely important that friends exceeds our target meaningfully") has a pillar, nobody gets left. The game's tension is "the engine can leave without you" (GDD §17); this gives friends the physical verbs to answer it. Approved with the rest of friendslop.md §3 ("The rest ... goes ahead"), and on 14 Oct for throwing a carried friend: it's griefing, so it's behind the CREW MISCHIEF switch, as the shove is.

   - **What it was:**
     - Someone left on the ballast beside a moving train could only climb a car's ladder or steps on their own, at whatever speed they could match. A friend at the door could do nothing for them.
     - A living crewmate couldn't be carried. Only bodies (App. C.4, D.9) went over the shoulder.
     - A healing find (note 272) healed only whoever held it.
     - There is no knocked-down state. A fall's knock (note 573, spec B.3) is health only and nobody is left lying. So **help up isn't built** (the spec said to skip it if there's nothing to get up from).
   - **Now** (player.json `rescue`, loot.json `healing.onAFriend`; `Sim/Player/Rescue.cs`, `Sim/World.Rescue.cs`, `Bots/Heed.HandUp.cs`; GDD §17, §23.5, App. F.1's damage model line, F.6):
     - **The hand-up** (`rescue.handUp`):
       - A crewmate on a car (in its open door, on its end platform or its steps) holds Use looking at a friend on foot beside it: within `reach` (1.6 m, from the helper's chest to any of the friend, shins to crown), within `coneDegrees` (40°) of their view, and with nothing of the car between them (a wall, a shut door: `Rescue.Clear`).
       - The car must be going at least `fromSpeed` (1 m/s; at a stand the steps are there to climb). The two must be moving within `maxRelativeSpeed` (7 m/s) of each other along the ground: a runner's sprint and a little, so a friend standing on the ballast isn't snatched up at 40 km/h.
       - Held `seconds` (0.6) with the friend in reach, they're hauled up (`Rescue.HaulAboard`, a host move: `Placed`). They land in the car's frame, `landInboard` (0.45 m) inboard of the helper, away from the side they came up, on the footing there; if that isn't clear, they land where the helper stands and the crowd parts them. Nothing goes through the world but where they're put, so the moving-frame rule holds.
       - While it's held, Use is the hand-up's alone: the door the helper stands in isn't worked. A Use held on at a shut door works that door as ever, and once it's open the runner is in reach.
       - The report: on a train going `reportFrom` (2 m/s) or faster, a rescue line, "Hauled aboard in car 3 at km 4. Hauled up by Dave at 40 km/h." (`IncidentKind.Rescue`, as a Holdout's breaking out is). *Came Back For Me* is one of the commendations players give each other on the run-end screen (App. D.12). It has no automatic rule to count anything towards, so the report line is what it answers to: the run-end screen shows the crew who came back for whom.
       - HUD: "HAUL UP : HOLD [E]" at the crosshair, before a door's prompt.
       - Bots: a walker or gunner on a moving car with a crewmate playing (not a bot) running beside it in reach turns to them and holds Use till they're up (`Heed.HandUp`, after the grab rescues in `BotCrew.Think`; the same intent a player's is). It doesn't go to a door for them: one that's at a door, a platform or the steps when they come alongside does it. Not the driver.
     - **Carrying a friend** (`rescue.carry`):
       - To lift: empty hands, a press of Use looking at a living crewmate on their feet in the same frame, within `reach` (1.2 m chest to chest) and `coneDegrees` (30°), with nothing Use works in reach (a door, a lever, the fire). Held `liftSeconds` (1).
       - Then they're over the shoulder (`PlayerFlags.Carried`, the carrier `Carrying` and `Heavy`: carryHeavy's pace, no ladders, no jump). They sit `shoulderUp` (0.7 m) over the carrier's feet and `shoulderBack` (0.15 m) behind, in the carrier's frame. They go where the carrier went each tick (`World.StepRescue`, after the motor, so they're never a tick behind), and from one frame to another as the carrier crosses (`PlayerMotor.Reparent`, made public).
       - The carried can look and talk. Their intent is stripped of the rest (`Rescue.CarriedIntent`: no keys, no driving, `CabControls.CanDrive` refuses them). Jump wriggles free to the carrier's feet, and the motor leaves their feet alone (`Move` returns early).
       - Use puts them down at the carrier's feet (the crowd parts the two). That hold is then spent (`_useSpent`), so it doesn't lift them again or pick up a crate.
       - **Throw** throws them along the carrier's view at `throwSpeed` (4 m/s) and `throwLift` (1.5 m/s) up, on the carrier's own motion, in their frame (`Rescue.Throw`). They're off their feet as a shove puts them (`PlayerFlags.Shoved`, `World.ShovedBy`), so the landing is the world's rule (the train's speed or the drop) and the throw's own speed knocks nobody. A death is `DeathCause.Shoved`, and the report says "Thrown off by Dave at 65 km/h." (`World.WasThrown`). A throw that kills marks the thrower as a shove does (note 717's mark reads `ShovedBy`).
       - Throwing is gated behind `World.CrewMischief` (`Mischief(by)`): with the switch off, Throw puts them down. Putting down and the wriggle always work. The thrower's arm goes out as a shove's does (`KnockKind.Throw`, a horseplay event: the push clip).
       - Safe from nothing. The carried come down at the carrier's feet if the carrier dies, is held by a creature, is staggered (a shove, coal, a bonk), takes a ladder, a gun's seat or a crane. A creature can take the carried off the shoulder: held, they're left where it holds them.
       - Bots can be carried; bots never carry anyone (`World.BotIds`). Carried crewmates aren't solid in the crowd (`Horseplay.Solid`), and neither carrier nor carried can shove.
       - HUD: "CARRY : HOLD [E]" at a friend in front, with nothing else at the crosshair ("CARRY : HOLD [E]   SHOVE : [LEFT ALT]" with mischief on). Over the shoulder, the corner says "CARRIED / WRIGGLE FREE : [SPACE]". Carrying, it says "A FRIEND ON YOUR SHOULDER / PUT DOWN : [E]", and "THROW : [RMB]" with the switch on.
     - **A bandage on a friend** (`rescue.bandage`, loot.json `healing.onAFriend`):
       - With a healing find in your hands, hold Use looking at a hurt crewmate within `reach` (1.4 m chest to chest) and `coneDegrees` (40°), for the find's `useSeconds`. It goes on them (`Bodies.Dose`'s new `patient`, from `World.RescueAct`) and heals `onAFriend` (1.5) times as much, up to their full health: bandages 45, medicine 75, morphine 120 (capped).
       - Used on yourself, it heals what it always did. Bots don't bandage others (a hurt bot's own find stays its own).
       - HUD: the find's own verb and whose, "BANDAGE PRIYA : HOLD [E]", "GIVE PRIYA THE MEDICINE", "GIVE PRIYA THE MORPHINE", with its progress once it's under way.
     - **Presentation** (`CrewActs`): a carrier is drawn in the body-carry's shoulder clip. The carried are drawn across the carrier's shoulders, square to the way the carrier faces (the pinned clip, `CrewActs.OnTheShoulder`). A crewmate in a moving car's door looking at a runner in a hand-up's reach is drawn hauling up (the Dragger rescue's clip). The views are `handup` and `carryfriend` (`Staging.Rescue`), in CLAUDE.md's list and the review gallery.
   - **Protocol 61**: two player flags (`Carried`, `Carrying`, in the record's flags long as before) and `KnockKind.Throw`.
