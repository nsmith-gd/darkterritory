583. **The Lodger, and what lives in the village houses (G1, queue #316; the director, 9 Oct 2026: "Every time I go into a house, it should feel like there could be something lurking in there that wants to kill me, wants to chase me", and on the Lodger, "one of those terrifying hides in the darkness ... a one-hit kill. It telegraphs with its shrill shriek"; design `docs/design/creatures/lodger.md`; GDD §21).**
    - **Which houses** (`Sim/Enemies/Dwellings.cs`: `Dwelling`, once a second on the host in the director's beat; enemies.json `dwellings`). As the train comes within `placeWithin` of a stop, each of its open houses rolls its own dice (the route's seed, the stop's feature and the building, so nothing else's draws move): `share` of them by tier have something in them, never the Gaunt's roost, its kind by `weights` (a Householder only where the stop has a find in that house). Put in asleep, and dismissed once the train's `forgetPast` past the stop. Not the director's: never `Engaged`, they cost it nothing.
    - **A house as the creatures see it** (`StopWalls.OpenHouses`, `InHouse`, `OpenHouse.Plan`/`Middle`). A pair's cottage is its own home (note 453), and an L's middle can be out in its notch, so a home's middle is its own part's (the biggest, for an L). `HouseWays` gets a creature in and out of a house by its doors, and says whether a wall (a shut door among them) stands between two points.
    - **The Lodger** (`Sim/Enemies/Lodger.cs`). Hidden in the floor furthest from its doors; someone in its house `noticeSeconds`, or within `shriekWithin` with no wall between, and it shrieks for `shriekSeconds` (the spine's least telegraph), then lunges where they were; within `killWithin`, no wall between, it grabs with a `killWindow` of 0.25 s and the punish kills: the one-hit kill is the director's call, and it still goes through the spine (App. A.1's "kills go through GRAB"). Missed, it chases (`chase`, under a run), shrieks again before every lunge, breaks a shut door in its way in `breakSeconds`, and gives up on anyone `pursuitRadius` from its house. Only blows as it walks back hurt it.
