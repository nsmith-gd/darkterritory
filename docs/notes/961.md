961. **Notes found out along the line (Ezra, queue #653; found by Wynn's audit, note 949).**
   - **GDD:** §3.1 "Not yet"; ROADMAP's "notes along the line".
   - **What it is.** The world's story was only in the towns' boards and the villages' postbox letters (note 730). Now a
     note is left out at the stops. It's a sheet folded twice, weighted by a stone on the doorstep of one of a village's
     three houses nearest the line, on the line's side. Hold Use and the note is picked up: it's read out on the HUD as
     a paper card (the postbox letter's card), goes in the night's report ("A note found by the line … Picked up by
     {actor}."), and is gone from the step.
   - **Content** (`run.json` `oddities.note`, data not code):
     - `chance` a village is 0.6; `holdSeconds` 1.0; `reach` 1.3.
     - Twelve `notes` (`heading`, `text`), in the world's voice from the World Building board's Cultures and canon
       threads: the fall, Dispatch, the dead lines' switches, the lost night mail, the clerk's fees, the penal cars, the
       sea's empty boats, a fortress only begun, the Flooders' water mark, and the pit, growing and foundry towns' trades.
   - **Placement:**
     - `Oddities.Of`, on every machine alike from the route, on the stop's own stream. The note's draws come after the
       can's, scarecrow's and postbox's, so those stand where they did.
     - `Oddities.NoteSpot` steps out from the house toward the line until the note is 0.8 m off the wall, clear of every
       building and more than 5 m from the main line.
     - The notes are dealt round the night's stops by a shuffle of the route's seed (`NoteIn`), so none is read twice
       in a night till all twelve have been out.
   - **Rules** (`World.Oddities`):
     - Hold Use within reach to pick it up; once picked up, it's done.
     - A pick-up is the `OddKind.Letter` happening, so the letter's sound and card serve both.
   - **No wire change:** the things are built alike on every machine, and their done bits already replicate in the
     oddities record (`Capture`/`Mirror`).
   - **Drawn:** `GreyboxScene.Note`, the paper under a stone; not drawn once picked up.
   - **HUD:** "A NOTE   PICK UP : HOLD [E]", then the card.
   - **Check it headless:**
     - `OddityTests.ANoteOnADoorstepIsPickedUpReadIntoTheNightsReportOnceAndGone`: a tap doesn't pick it up; a hold
       does; it's in the report once; it's gone.
     - `OddityTests.TheNightsNotesStandOffTheLineAtHousesAndNoneIsReadTwiceTillAllHaveBeenOut`: across frontier
       nights 1-12, no note repeats in a night and none lies on the line.
     - `dt screenshot --view oddities --odd note --route frontier:7` shows a note on its doorstep.
     - `dt screenshot --hud --note --route frontier:7` shows one picked up and read.
