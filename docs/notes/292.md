292. **The in-night menu (F1, UI/UX 3; queue #30).** In a night, Escape freed the mouse and a second Escape ended the night at once, with no word: a host's, for the whole crew. Nothing in a night opened the settings (B3's "settings take effect from the next night", #206). Now Escape opens Lethal Company's quick menu over the night, which goes on (it's the crew's, not this player's to stop): **RESUME**, **SETTINGS**, **INVITE** and **LEAVE**.
    - **One menu, the front end's.** It's `FrontEnd` with two more pages (`Screen.Night`, `Screen.Leave`) and a `NightMenu` record the app reads off the session as Escape opens it: hosting or joined, the other people aboard (bots not counted), a campaign night, a lobby that takes invites, the address friends type, the night over. So the keys, the mouse, the cues and the settings rows are the front end's own, and `MenuInput.Apply` works both. The settings' BACK, and Escape on them, come back to the menu; Escape on its first page shuts it, and where the front end was is put back.
    - **Nothing reaches the night while it's open.** The app's `Held`, `Hit` and `Pressed` read nothing, and the mouse doesn't look, so a player standing in the menu stands still. The night is drawn under it, dimmed, in place of the HUD (the HUD's corners under a list were a muddle), with no nameboard.
    - **It follows the night while it's open:** who's aboard, the report coming up, the gate passed (`FrontEnd.RefreshNight`, each frame), on the page it's on.
    - **Leaving is asked twice.** LEAVE (END THE NIGHT, for a host with others aboard) opens a confirmation with STAY lit, so the Enter that chose LEAVE, pressed again, stays. Both rows say what leaving costs: the night ends for the others aboard (a host), the crew carries on (a joiner), the slot carries on from the last stop the train left (a campaign night), or nothing from tonight is kept. These are a menu's plain words about the session, not a prompt foretelling the night (GDD §32 on #206). Over the report, LEAVE goes at once, as Enter does, keeping what you were commended for.
    - **Settings take effect at once.** The app takes a changed `Settings` up the same frame: the mouse speed, every rebound key, the HUD, push to talk, the mic level and the display (`ApplyDisplay`, which the menus already used). The volumes were always live. The microphone itself is still from the next night (its row says so). The player's name is sent as you join and the headset's comfort is set as a night starts, so those rows aren't on the in-night page.
    - **INVITE** opens Steam's overlay (F2 still does) when there's an open lobby with room. A host on the network without one is told its address to type on JOIN. A night nobody can join (alone, loopback only) has no INVITE.
    - **Not here:** in a headset the mirror window keeps the old Escape (VR is on the backburner), and the night never pauses, solo included.
