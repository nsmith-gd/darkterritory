436. **The hand lamp's shadows (queue #172, E1; the art checklist's `crew-lantern` "next", "the light's flicker swinging its shadows"; GDD §31, §27).** A carried lantern swung in its carrier's fist and its light went with it (PR #133), but its light was one of the practical lights, lit per pixel and unshadowed: nothing it lit threw a shadow, so the swing showed only as a pool of light sliding about.
    - **One lamp casts:** the hand lamp nearest the eye, carried or lying (`GreyboxScene.HandLamp`), is the frame's `MeshBuilder.ShadowLight`; the rest stay practical lights. One is what's seen: the lamp in your own hand or the crewmate's beside you, and a second would cost a second cube for shadows mostly lost behind the first's light.
    - **Its cube:** the renderer's `handShadow` pass draws six 512² faces (+X, −X, +Y, −Y, +Z, −Z, each 96° so the filter's taps at an edge stay on its face) into the layers of one depth array, a pass a face, the face each draw's first instance (`shadow_hand.vert`). `scene.frag`'s `handShadowAt` takes the face by the axis a point lies furthest along from the flame, four taps of its layer. Near plane 0.1 m, and the lantern itself drawn `Shadowless`: its cage and cap round the flame would otherwise shut its own light in.
    - **What it costs, and what it draws:** a first cut drew the cube in one multiview pass (six views), which puts everything near the lamp through all six faces, and culled by the instances' spheres, which every cell of the land (hundreds of metres across) overlaps: 185k triangles a face on the roof, 1.1M a frame. Now each face draws only what it sees within the lamp's reach (`DrawGeometry`'s `reach`): no piece more than four times the reach across (the land's cells: ground that hardly shades itself so close), the skinned pieces culled too (loosely, `SkinSlack`: their bind pose's sphere doubled), and of the soup only the triangles within reach (`_handSoup`, cut on the CPU). On the flat screen the worst view with a lamp in your own fist is the roof's, 1.33M of tuning/perf.json's 1.5M (1.05M without).
    - **Not yet, a headset's:** `VrView.HandShadows` is off, so the lamp lights unshadowed there (`GreyboxRenderer.HandShadows`). Both eyes on the roof draw 1.4M already and the cube there is near 0.3M more. VR is on the backburner; when its budget's looked at, this is one of the things to fit.
    - **The flicker:** the light now flickers with the flame's glow (`SceneArt.Flicker`, which only the glow had) and the flame shivers a centimetre or so in its glass on the same beat (`SceneArt.FlameDrift`), so the shadows' edges tremble as well as swinging with the walk.
    - **Verified:** `HandShadowTests` (behind a post on each side and under a plate the floor is dark with the lamp shadowed and lit without; the open floor lit alike either way, no acne or seam where faces meet). `PerfBudgetTests` draws every view again with a hand lamp in your own fist. `dt screenshot --act idle,lanternwalk,crouch --view lampshadow [--scene-time t]`, a new view over the staged crew's feet (its JSON gives `handTriangles`); staged crew carrying lamps now light them (they had only the glow).
