796. **The Mouse Moose modelled and rigged (queue #523, C1; the director, 15 Oct 2026: "We have a new asset added by gameplay, you need to do a blender pass to make it more in line with the games aesthetic. It's called Mouse Moose"; note 790's "a modelled and rigged Mouse Moose").**
   - **The model** (`tools/blender/mouse_moose.py`, after the brief, `docs/design/creatures/mouse-moose.md`: "a mutant rat the size of a dog, mangy and hunched, with a pink rope of a tail and a little pair of palmate moose antlers"):
     - One fused skin from the hind feet to the upper lip (flesh volumes, settled, QuadriFlow), the Pickers' way:
       - a sewer rat's pear shape, the back one arch over the loins down to a short thick neck, the belly slung low and the thighs and upper arms in the body, so only the shins and forearms show;
       - three growths under the pelt;
       - a broad skull tapering to a long blunt snout with full jowls and a pink nose.
     - A jaw of its own. Four orange incisors, chisel-ended. Small wet eyes in their orbits. Six stiff whiskers a side.
     - The ears round and thin and cupped forward, the left one torn.
     - Pink hands of four clawed fingers. Long pink hind feet of five toes.
     - A bell of loose skin at the throat, a moose's.
     - A bare tail as long as the body, on six bones.
     - **The antlers:** a moose's, small, and not a pair. Each is a beam out of the skull with a burr, a brow tine forward over the eye, and a cupped palm turned up and out, fringed with tines. The left one is grown big (five tines). The right is stunted and curled in (three). Velvet hangs off both in torn shreds.
     - 32 bones (SK_MouseMoose): root, pelvis, two spine bones, the neck, the head, the jaw, the bell, two ears, four bones a foreleg and a hind leg, and six down the tail.
     - 6,249 triangles; a distance copy of 2,645.
   - **The atlas** (`tools/models/recipes/mouse_moose.py`, one 1024 atlas, the Pickers' way):
     - Relief baked from a sculpted high copy: the pelt short and matted, gone in patches, scabbed; the tail's scales in rings; the hands' and feet's creases; the ears' veins; the antlers' bone grooved and pitted.
     - Colour painted from where each texel is on it at rest:
       - a sewer rat's dark grey-brown, grizzled, darker down the spine, the belly a dirty pale grey;
       - mange: bare grey-pink skin in patches, with scabs in it; the growths bald and pinker, veined;
       - grimed from the ground up the legs;
       - dark rings round the eyes;
       - the tail grey-pink with darker rings, browner toward the tip, a scar near the rump;
       - the ears' insides pink-grey and veined;
       - the incisors orange, brown at the roots;
       - the antlers' velvet mousy brown-grey, torn back raw red at its edges and bloodied under the shreds, the tines' tips worn through to pale bone;
       - the shreds wet red inside.
     - The eyes keep their own lit glass: a dark red that throws a lamp back.
   - **The clips** (30 fps):
     - `idle` (3 s): hunched, the nose going; the head round to a sound, down sniffing, then up on its haunches with its forepaws at its chest (a rat's).
     - `creep` (0.4 s): the low slinking walk at the sim's 1.2 m/s, the legs in diagonal pairs planted by reach, the head weaving.
     - `bite` (1.5 s, the sim's `biteSeconds`): a sniff at the casting, the head drawn back and the jaw open, the lunge, the teeth in, a worry.
     - `drag` (0.8 s): its teeth in the casting's lower edge (0.7 m ahead, the sim's `grip`), braced back between its planted forefeet and its hind feet. Each heave yanks the body back, then the hind feet shuffle a step after it.
     - `flee` (0.27 s): a bounding gallop at the sim's 5 m/s, flat out, ears pinned.
     - `dive` (0.5 s): nose first into its hole, then turning to peer out.
     - `hide` (3 s): down its hole, up it nose first and steep (the root pitched 80°), only the head over the ground: the antlers, the laid-back ears, the eyes and the nose; it looks round, twitches, ducks.
     - `emerge` (0.8 s): up out of its hole: a look round, then a scramble out.
     - `hit` (0.4 s): a flinch and a squeal.
   - **In the game** (`CreatureArt.MouseMoose.cs`, `MouseMooseClip`): the sim's mode picks the clip:
     - Creep: creep, or idle if it's stood. Leaving its hole, it plays the emerge's scramble first.
     - Bite: bite. Drag: drag, its heaves played up to 2.2 times faster as it drags faster (`dragAway`). Flee: flee.
     - Hide: hide, after a dive if it came from a bolt or with a casting.
     - In its hole (and climbing out), the hole is drawn: a black mouth and a ring of dug clods, the spoil heaped behind it so the head over the low front rim is seen against it.
     - The box stand-in stays as the fallback where the model isn't there.
