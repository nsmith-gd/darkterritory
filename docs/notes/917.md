   **What it does.**
   - **`dt telemetry summary <dir> [--out out/telemetry/summary.json] [--tables dir]`** (`DarkTerritory.Dev/Telemetry/TelemetrySummary.cs`): every `*.jsonl` under the folder read back (`TelemetryFiles`), a session each, by id. A line that isn't JSON, or is of a newer schema than v1, is counted (`skippedLines`) and skipped, never guessed at; a field it doesn't know is ignored. The summary: sessions, nights, minutes; builds, editions, VR, roles, routes, tiers, crew sizes; how nights end (`nightEnd.end`; a night left mid-way, its end `None` after a quit, is `quit`; a night with no end at all, closed hard or crashed, `unfinished`); quits (all, mid-night, by minute; closing the game between nights isn't a mid-night quit); deaths (total, a night, the player's own, by cause, post and minute); encounters (total, a night, by kind and channel, afoot, grabs, punishes, by minute); incidents by kind; the funnel, counted in sessions: launch, a night, first move, first job, first stop, first delivery (a `first` or a night `Delivered`), a second night; each first's median minute; purchases, rebinds, prompts (shown, share acted on); how it ran (the minutes' median p50/p95/p99, the worst minute's p99, hitches a minute, a client's ping and loss); and a row a night. Nothing names anyone: the recorder never writes a name.
   - **`--tables dir`**: the same as flat row tables, a JSON file each (`TelemetrySummary.Tables`: overview, funnel, ends, tiers, deathsByCause, encountersByKind, encountersByChannel, timeline, nights) for the dashboard, which reads row datasets.
   - **`dt telemetry map <session.jsonl> [--out out/telemetry/map]`**: each night of a session on its route's `linegen` map (`PlanImages.Map`'s new `overlay`), the line rebuilt from the night's route (`frontier-7` is `RunParameters.RouteId`'s; the spec is `frontier:7`): deaths red with their cause and minute, quits amber with how and the minute, encounters violet at the train's km when they began (interpolated between the minutes' km). A hand-made route has no plan and is said so, not drawn.
   - **`dt telemetry record --me n`**: records as crewmate n's machine (a player among bots: `humans` 1), so a bot night has a player's firsts (move, job) and their own deaths, as a playtester's would. Without it, as #624 left it.
   - **Tests** (`TelemetrySummaryTests`, 8): two hand-written sessions (a player who delivered, died and quit the second night for the menu; a joiner whose game closed with no end, a v2 line and a broken one) give the counts, ends, quits by minute, deaths and encounters by cause, kind and minute, the funnel (narrowing), how it ran, the flat tables; a folder reads as its files; and every field the summary reads (`TelemetrySummary.Reads`) is in `TelemetrySchema`, so a field renamed there fails here.

   **The dashboard** (claude.ai/artifact/7TGs6GykMTAruojtfELE6E, "Dark Territory playtests", from the Artifact tool's Dashboard type): the takeaway, six headline figures (nights, delivered, deaths and encounters a night, mid-night quits, p95 frame time), the funnel with the biggest drop in red, how nights end, the night minute by minute (encounters, deaths, quits), encounters by creature and by channel, deaths by cause, and a table of every night. Its datasets are the `--tables` files, attached (fixed files, no connector: the playtest files are on testers' machines until #626 uploads them). To refresh it: `dt telemetry summary <the playtest's folder> --tables out/telemetry/tables`, then upload each file to the dashboard and update its dataset (or ask an agent to). Until the first real playtest it shows sample sessions, bot nights, and says so.
