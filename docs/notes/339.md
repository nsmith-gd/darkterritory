339. **The Moose: a hyper-aggressive, territorial moose, too big to get on the train (queue #77, G1; the director's decisions of 7 Oct 2026; design `docs/design/creatures/moose.md`).**
    - **What it is.** GDD §21's eighteenth enemy (Outside, Split, cost 3; App. A.6, B.6; spec B.12). `Moose` (src/DarkTerritory.Sim/Enemies/Moose.cs) is loose in the world: `Extra` its target, `Extra2` its aggro (0–100), `Lateral` its heading, `Height` its `MooseMode` (graze, home, hunt, search, ram, square up, charge, wheel, snag, pin) for the clips, so a client draws it from replicated state alone. Its home, who put what into its meter and its search are the host's.
    - **Its temper.** Grazing, its meter fills from anyone on the ground it can see within 20 m (much faster within 12 m), anyone talking within 15 m (by the Voice byte, over a whisper's floor), a train going by within 25 m (once a pass); a hit fills it. Listening at 20 and warning at 50 are its tells; given room and quiet it falls 15 a second. Full, it's after whoever put the most in (lowest id on a tie, never dictionary order: `SortedDictionary`), and whoever hits it after that. Crowding counts only what it can see (`Moose.Clear`: no stop wall or car on the line between), so hiding behind the train or round a building works; voices it hears through them.
    - **The bullfight.** In sight within 30 m it squares up 2.5 s (the telegraph; the spine refuses a commit any sooner) and charges a straight line at 11 m/s, its heading locked; anyone on the ground it runs through takes a 60 hit (`ctx.Bite`: can't kill), and someone at 40 or less is pinned for 12 s (the shared GRAB). A friend's blow (`Struck`) breaks the pin and makes them its target; nothing hurts it (`Struck` passes 0 damage), so it can't be killed, by the director's decision, an exception to queue #25. Its body is a disc the rack's 3.2 m across: it can't come within half that of a stop's wall (`StopWalls`) or a car's box, so the alleys between buildings, doorways and the coupling gaps are out of its reach by geometry, not a rule. Charging into a wall snags it 4 s; into a car, or the track's clearance, it pulls up; three such charges without reaching its target and it gives up. Trees aren't solid in the sim yet (queue #14), so for now only buildings and cars are cover.
    - **Out of sight, aboard, away.** Out of sight it searches where it last saw or heard them for 25 s, and anyone talking within 15 m is found (and is its target). Lost within 5 m of a car they're aboard, it rams that car every 3 s for 15 s: only a ram (`Rams` on the host; every client reads `RamCount`, carried in the replicated `Health` as 1 + the rams, since nothing hurts it: the audio lands a boom on each, AU1 on #247; nothing in or on the car is touched). It gives up past 80 m from its ground and when the train moves off; it's gone 600 m behind.
    - **Never on the rail.** No part of its path comes within 3.2 m of any track's centre (`RailLine.Nearest`, the main line or a branch), 6 m while the train moves and wherever it's put down, so the line is a boundary it never crosses: the train can never hit it, and it can never stop or derail the train. The first draft let it cross a track at a run during a chase; this is the simplest rule that can't put it in the train's way.
    - **Where it comes from.** The director's spawn rule (`Spawns`, B.6): stopped at a facility or a dead settlement, one about at a time, every tier weighted 1/1.5/2/2.5 and by the line's biome (`moose.biomeWeights`, the plan's main-line biome), 30–60 m out from the consist's middle wherever `Moose.Place` finds ground it can stand on (dry, clear, outside the forts). A grazing moose isn't engaged (`Director.Engaged`), so it holds no cap and isn't dismissed for want of company. Beside the line on the run, `World.LinesideMoose` puts one down 300 m ahead, 8–22 m out, 2–5 per 10 km by tier times the biome's weight, on its own dice from the route's seed (so the director's draws, and every seeded test, are untouched) and costing the director nothing. Conflict pairs: the Gaunt, Ribbits, Whistler and facility loading. Its primary sense is movement (App. A.1 allows one), keeping it out of the Ribbits' (sight) and the Gaunt's (sound) one-tell-type cap so the pairs can happen. `minCrew` is 1: at a crew of one the solo rule (`grab.soloStruggle`) frees a pinned player, as from every grab.
    - **Its art** (`tools/blender/moose.py`, 39 bones; rebuilt on 8 Oct the Gannet's way, at the director's word that the first pass was "super low quality, very boxy and rigid, not organic"). The trunk, hump, neck, head and lower jaw (the mouth the gap left between the drooping lip and the jaw), legs, bell, tail and the ridge's crest are one skin through `rig.fuse` (voxel union, six relax passes so the joins fillet, QuadriFlow, smooth-shaded); the rack is a second fused skin (beams, burrs, cupped and twisted palms turned forward like open hands, rim tines, slag lumps), collapsed rather than QuadriFlowed (`quads=False`) and shaded hard only at its plates' edges; the tick sacs are rebound to the skin's weights so they ride it. A second pass the same day (the coordinator: it read as a deer or a llama) made it a heavy, front-loaded bull: the barrel about twice the girth, massive shoulders under a hump that is the back's high point, the back sloping to smaller hips and a tucked flank, a short very thick neck, a bigger head and muzzle, a heavy dewlap, thick legs with big knees and hocks, flattened engorged ticks in place of popcorn clusters, and palms that spread out to the sides in a broad flat span. 14,219 triangles (LOD 5,955), 3.23 m across the rack, 3.37 m to its top. Its colour is baked into one 2048 texture by `tools/models/recipes/moose.py` (a mottled, bald-patched ghost-moose hide crusted with painted ticks, darker legs and muzzle, slag rack with bloody velvet), so `tools/blender/build.sh` leaves it out; built with Blender as a Python module, bpy 4.5 (bpy 5 drops `Action.fcurves`, which `rig.bake` uses). The rack is its own bone a side (so `dt art clearance` measures it apart); a ridge of sacs on three spine bones swells when it warns; the charge is a rotary gallop with the head held at a fixed world rotation, so the rack stays level on the line. `CreatureArt` picks its clip from the replicated `MooseMode`, aggro and pace (graze, listen, warn, walk, trot, squareUp, charge, overrun, snag, search, ram, pin, strut; trainPass when a moving train is within `trainPassAt`, decided on the presentation side since the sim has no mode for it); the one it pins lies under its rack in `HeldPinned`. Views: `dt screenshot --view moose|moosecharge|moosepin` and `--moose <mode>`; stills in docs/design/creatures/. `CreatureArtTests` and `CreatureBodyTests` pin its budget, clips, size, clearance and gun body.
    - **Not yet.** Its sounds are the audio chat's (requested on the Audio Checklist; spec A.4's row is "pending"). The bots don't give it room or hush near it: they walk past it to the loading and rescue a pinned crewmate with a blow (`Heed.Rescue`), which the grab audit uses. Queue #48 may move its ground to a laid-out site.
    - **Verified:** `MooseTests` (14: left be it never charges; crowded it listens, squares up 2.5 s and charges a 60 hit; backed off at its warning it settles; a talker is its target; it pins the hurt and a friend's blow takes it off them; pinned with nobody coming, trampled; nothing kills it; out of sight and quiet it gives up; lost at a car it rams it and touches nothing; the train leaving ends it; it never comes within the track's clearance chasing someone along the line; a passing train makes it listen, never warn; every tier, more the harder; its charge is a hit, not chip). `SpecTableTests.TheMooseMatchesB12`. `dt audit grabs --only moose`: freed at every crew from 2 to 8 (1.2 of 12 s). A harness night (`dt harness --route frontier:7 --bots 4 --enemies --insist moose,ribbit --start 5090 --seconds 200 --trace`): placed at the stop, the look-out walks up (`LookErrand`, balance.json `look.mooseM`), it listens, squares up and charges twice, and the bot, gone back to its post, is missed both times. The combination sweep (`dt balance --pairs --only moose,... --at-stops`) still reads it "dormant" against the Ribbits and the Whistler: its one look-out goes to the Ribbits first and is busy for the night, so the sweep under-exercises it.
