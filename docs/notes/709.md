709. **Speed feel (A1, queue #441; the director, 13 Oct evening, `docs/playtest/2026-10-13-evening.md` line 20).**
"It says we're going 27 kilometers an hour. I think by world standards we are, but when I look out the front, it just feels so agonizingly slow". The director gave A1 this line on 14 Oct.

   - **What it was:**
     - From the cab, the track ahead starts 15 m out, past the boiler, and the ground near the line is bare.
     - The eye rode the car dead level, at a fixed field of view.
     - At 7.5 m/s nothing close went by to say how fast.
   - **GDD §31 first:** "Speed is felt, not just read off the gauge". The sim's speeds are unchanged.
   - **Now** (`SpeedFeel`, look.json `speedFeel`, the app's eye camera; aboard only):
     - **The view widens:** by up to 8° at 20 m/s, eased by (speed / 20)^0.6, so the director's 27 km/h already gets about half of it. Never in a headset (a widening view makes a rider sick), and not when watching a crewmate.
     - **The rail joints:** a 12 mm knock down through the floor at each 12.5 m joint, under each bogie (8.5 m apart), over 0.35 m of travel, so the beat quickens with the train.
     - **The sway:** the car sways 10 mm side to side every 34 m at full speed.
     - Both the knock and the sway scale with speed and with the settings' CAMERA SHAKE, down to none.
   - **Checked:**
     - `SpeedFeelTests`:
       - standing, nothing changes;
       - at 27 km/h the view is past a linear share of the widening, and full at full speed;
       - off the train, nothing;
       - down a rail's length at speed, the eye dips by up to the jolt at the joints and is quiet between them.
     - The app run headless on frontier:7 with it on.
   - **Left for later:** more near the line to pass the window (fence posts, snow poles, the near ballast's grain), and wind-blown snow past the glass. Those are the strongest cues there are, and art work for the line (B2's, the world's).
