606. **The crew goes on together (D1.4, queue #338, at D1's ask after #336 and note 604: "it's a co-op game, and town to town only holds if the whole crew goes together").** When the host goes on to the next night, its joiners go with it, by way of the town's card, not their menus.
    - **Before.** Each night is its own session. When a host left a night its joiners lost the link, tried note 253's rejoin (back into a session that was gone), and ended on their menus. The host's next night in the same town (notes 591, 604) was the host's alone till each joined again by hand.
    - **The word** (protocol 50). As the host leaves a delivered campaign night (the app; the campaign's town set), it tells everyone aboard and waiting it's going on from that town:
        - `MessageType.CarryOn`, reliable, carrying `TownAt.Spec` (`HostSession.CarryOn`, `NetPlaySession.CarryOn`);
        - the app then gives it `NetPlaySession.CarryOnSeconds` (0.5) to reach them before it hangs up.
        - A joiner keeps it (`ClientSession.CarryingOn`). From then on, a lost link is the host gone to pick the next night, so note 253's rejoin stands down (`Redialable`): rejoining would put the old night's world on the new night's session.
    - **The follow.** The joiner's night ends when the link goes (`NetPlaySession.GoingOn`), and its next launch is `Launch.Follow`. It waits on the town's card, drawn over the town it arrived in (note 604's backdrop): "GRIEVE · THE HOST IS PICKING THE NIGHT AHEAD".
        - It dials the same host the same way it came: its address, or the lobby owner online. This is note 253's `Redial`, with the run's password key, so a private run (B3's #275) stays private.
        - It dials every rejoin.retrySeconds for up to rejoin.followSeconds (600, player.json) (`NetPlaySession.Follow`).
        - A Welcome back into the night just left (the host still closing) is hung up on and dialled again.
        - Welcomed by the next night, it joins as any joiner does: its yard and its town (built from the Welcome's setup, the route spec carrying the town, note 591). The card names the night ahead, "GRIEVE · THE NIGHT TO FORT WEXLEY", before it opens.
    - **The host's next night** is hosted as ever, from the campaign's board in the town (note 604), on the same port (and a new lobby online). The joiners dial its owner, not the lobby, so a new lobby doesn't lose them.
    - **Verified:** `CrewGoesOnTests`, a host and two joiners over loopback:
        - the host says it's going on from night 1's terminus town and closes;
        - both joiners hear it, lose the link without trying to rejoin, and follow;
        - the host's next night, its contract from that town on the same port, has both aboard on its route spec, departing from that town by name and seed, and not on the night they left.
        - `TownToTownTests`: the card's words while following.

        - Leaving the wait by hand (it gives up after followSeconds, back to the menu).
