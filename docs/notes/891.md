   - Claimed 2026-10-20 15:55 UTC by n1-H1 (nathaniel1), [#1339](https://github.com/nsmith-gd/darkterritory/pull/1339).
   - The director, 9 Oct: "This is too much. Cinder Hounds should reignite every minute and a half they are left aboard at the car they are currently in. Crew should have time to fight fires effectively while cinder Hounds are on board. Attacking a cinder hound should reset its ignite burst from its body that sets the fire. Attacking should cause it to retreat. Cinder Hounds attack the player when the player is first spotted with a lunge attack by jumping at the player to bite, then retreating to do it again. Cinder Hounds stop attacking and leave the train if they are pushed to the back of the train and there is a valid exit (door open or roof of car)."
   - **What's built** (`CinderHound` in `src/DarkTerritory.Sim/Enemies/Rear.cs`; `enemies.json cinderHounds.aboard`, `on: true`; off is note 472's hound exactly):
     - **Every minute and a half:** `igniteEverySeconds` 20 → 90. With `aboard.on` the clock runs the whole time the hound is aboard (not only while it's alone, as note 269's did), and lights *the car it's on* when it runs out. Set back to nought by a blow on it, or by a fire it lights (or finds already alight). **#1232's rule kept** (note 851): with a crewmate within 12 m on its ground it never lights the car; the clock runs on, and it lights once they're gone. Reading: "left aboard" = not dealt with, so time spent fighting the crew counts; only a blow resets it.
     - **A blow resets it and drives it back:** `Struck` (a blow that doesn't kill it, not while it holds someone): clock to nought, then driven away from the striker `struckSeconds` 1 s at `struckSpeed` 3.5 m/s (`HoundMode.Retreat`), over the gap onto the car behind (the leap), then a `crouchSeconds` 2 s crouch before it comes again.
     - **Lunge, bite, retreat:** someone it can reach on its ground (notes 471, 528) within `lungeFrom` 4 m: it leaps at them over `lungeSeconds` 0.5 (`HoundMode.Lunge`, to `lungeShort` 0.8 m short of them), bites as it lands if they're in its reach (`biteDamage` 35; down to the last, the pin, as before), backs off `retreatMetres` 3 at 2.5 m/s, crouches 2 s watching them, and again. The first lunge comes at once ("when the player is first spotted"); further off it chases them along the roofs (note 472).
     - **Off the back:** driven (a blow, or the extinguisher's backing off, note 728) to the back of the rear car with a way off, its roof's end or, inside, any door of that car open, it leaves the train, counted in `DrivenOff` as the spray's drive-off is. Driven toward the engine it stops at its car's end.
     - Two new aboard modes (`HoundMode.Lunge`, `Retreat`) ride in `Lateral` as the others do (mode × 4 + facing): no wire change, no protocol bump. The art draws Lunge with E1's `lunge` clip, Retreat as its patrol walk (`CreatureArt`).
   - **Not changed:** when hounds are sent (the spawn director, #600–#613's), their run behind the train, the gun's answers, sounds (AU1: a lunge plays no sound of its own yet; it could take the leap's, `GameAudio.Creatures.HoundMove`, an audio checklist line for AU1).
