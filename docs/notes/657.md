657. **The Tweaker's estimate under a roster (D1, queue #387; note 654's "not yet").**
The Creature Tweaker's estimate counted every combination of the kinds with a night's chance, but only the first ten of
them; the rest it took as about every night. That was the Knotter alone until the director's roster (note 652) put a
chance on every kind: then 17 of them counted as always about, so the estimate kept the old mix and showed no pile-up
(on Deep Territory, 1.7 Draggers a night against 4.0 measured).

- **Above ten kinds with a chance, the estimate samples the nights:** 256 of them, each kind drawing once a night in
  name order from one seeded generator (mulberry32), the same nights for the director's values and the game's. Two
  estimates differ by the values alone, not by the draw. Ten or fewer are still counted exactly. `tools/tweaker/
  calibrate.py` and the page (`creature-tweaker.html`) do it draw for draw; `apply.py --self-test` runs the page's
  estimate under node against calibrate.py's, sampled and counted, and they agree to 1e-6.

- **Still short:** each night's creatures are shared among those about by their 12 Oct rates, and those were measured
  with everything about. A kind the others crowded out then (the Hounds, the Tippy Toesie, held back by the one-a-zone
  rule and the director's pairs) comes far more often in the game when few are about than the estimate says. The page
  says so under "Estimates and measurements"; the Measured column is what happens. A closer estimate would model the
  zones (`Director.Room`) per sampled night, or calibrate each kind's rate from nights with few kinds about.
