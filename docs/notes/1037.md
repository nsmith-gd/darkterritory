1037. **The visual tell audit, `dt tells` (n1-V1, nathaniel1's Token Budget Manager's worker; queue #262, after W1's stale claim, note 1026; systems-spec A.4a; GDD Part Eleven q6).** The twin of A.3's audio audit: a tell that can't be seen is a bug.
   - **How it measures.** `dt tells` (`src/DarkTerritory.Dev/Tells/TellAudit.cs`, developer builds only) hosts the creature gym's night (note 676, no lamps set down) and calls each creature as the gym calls it. It puts a crewmate's eye at the tell's warning distance from the creature (on foot, in a gap, at the cab window or on a roof; on the crew's side first, then its other three sides if that frame doesn't have it at all) and draws the frame twice: with the creature, and without it (`WorldShot.Render`'s new `drawn` filter). It does this in four nights: **clear** (the route's fog, every lamp lit), **dark** (the headlamp and every car's lamp out), **rain** (wet, at a Dead Lines night's thickest haze, 75 m; no drops are drawn yet) and **fog** (Deep Territory's thickest, 60 m). What differs is what the creature puts on the screen, measured in CIE lightness (L*). A tell is **seen** when at least 60 pixels of a 720p frame differ by 3 L* or more and the brightest tenth of those differ by at least 6 L*. The numbers, the nights, each tell's warning distance and viewpoints are in `content/tuning/tells.json`. It writes the table (`out/tells/tells.md`), the JSON and a contact sheet per tell (a row per viewpoint, a column per night, framed green or red). `--frames` keeps every frame. A full run is 160 frames, about 12 minutes on lavapipe.
   - **The test.** `TellAuditTests` draws the fast few (tells.json `fast`: the Moose, the Ribbits, the Gannet, the Car Hugger: a body coming at you, a pack low in the grass, overhead, a mass at the train's end) in every night and fails if any is under the floor. It also pins the measure itself. It skips without Vulkan. A run takes about 1.5 min.
   - **Hidden by design** (tells.json `hidden`: drawn and measured, never counted a failure). The Dragger is under the roof's lip until its limb comes up; its scrape is the tell (A.4). The Gaunt is asleep in its roost; its dark house is the tell. The Passenger is inside its car. The Lodger is in a room until it shrieks. The Waker lies as a hill until the stir, and the sky's bruise is its warning.
   - **Not staged by the gym** (the audit can't stand them yet; #742): the Follower (it rides whoever is off the train), the Track Doll and the Choir (gone before the shot), the Tapper (nobody at a gun).
   - **Seen in every night**: the Cinder Hounds, the Car Hugger, Tippy Toesie, the Ribbits, the Grumbler, the Moose (fog at 50 m is its thinnest margin, 6.6–7.3 L*), the Gannet, the Mourners, Tower Jaw, the Brakeman, the Knotter, the Mouse Moose, the Bruin, and the three house creatures (the Householder, Hollow House, the Hanger; the house itself is what differs, so their numbers are the whole house's).

   - **Cheap fixes made:** none held. The only content-level lever found was the figure rim on the Soot Child's model, and it measured no better. Raising the lamps or the moon would change the whole look and `dt perf`'s views, so it wasn't tried. The fixes left are art and effects (#740, #741). `dt perf` is unchanged: the only change outside the developer tools is a `drawn`/`grade` parameter on the dev-only `WorldShot` and `CreatureGym.Of`.
   - **What it found about the nights.** "Dark" equals "clear" for anything more than ~30 m from the train: the lamps never reached it. The moon and the fog's pale line are what make a silhouette, and the fog thins both. Rain helped every dark creature on the ground: its wet sheen is a pale floor behind them. That's an argument for a little sheen on dry nights too, but that's the look's call, not this audit's.
