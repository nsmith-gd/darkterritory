731. **Painted masks (K1, queue #465; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`, GDD App. F.6).**
The audit's Friends goal: who's who in a masked game. Note 58 already had it: "friendslop horror so we need to be masked but still have some fun to us", with each player's colours.

   - **What it was:**
     - A crewmate's only marks were the outfit (note 298: one of eight looks, the cap or helmet and scarf, `World.Outfits`) and the armband in its colour.
     - The gas mask itself was the same black rubber on everyone.
     - A crew had no mark of its own.
   - **Now** (GDD §29 "Painted masks", §9's yard):
     - **The paint** (`Sim.Player.MaskPaint`: coat, stencil, ink, each from 1, 0 none). There are ten paints (look.json `maskPaintNames`/`maskPaints`) and twelve stencils (`maskStencils`): stripe, cross, teeth, star, number (the wearer's, id + 1), moustache, heart, crow, bolt, smile, X eyes and anchor. Initials were skipped, since they need text. A stencil with no colour picked is painted in `maskInk` (bone).
     - **Chosen** on PAINT YOUR MASK, a page in F1's menu style: COAT, STENCIL, STENCIL COLOUR, DONE (`Screen.Paint`). It's reached from the settings' YOU section and from the night menu in the yard. It's kept in the settings (`Settings.MaskColour`/`MaskStencil`/`MaskInk`). DONE or Escape goes back to the page it was opened from.
     - **On the wire as an outfit is, but in bytes of its own**, so U1's hat rack (outfit byte = hat × 9 + colour, PR #967) can land on either side:
       - three bytes in the Hello, after the flags and before a private run's key;
       - `MessageType.Paint` (client → host; the host takes it only in the yard);
       - `MessageType.Paints` (host → everyone, sent with names and outfits).
       The host clamps each part under `MaskPaint.Max` (32), keeps `World.Paints` and gives a wearer's paint to a Passenger wearing their face. Past the gate a change is kept for the next Hello, and the crew go on seeing the yard's paint.
     - **The crew's sigil** (`CampaignState.Sigil`, a stencil from 1). It's set on the fortress board as CREW SIGIL, by whoever hosts from it, and saved with the crew at once. It goes to every machine in `SessionSetup.Sigil` and is kept on `World.CrewSigil`. Because the save travels (note 724), every crewmate's copy has it.
     - **The art** (`Art.MaskKit`, `CreatureArt.Mask`): flat paint, lit a breath like the armband (Emissive 0.14), so it reads at 5 m by a hand lamp. Each design is bold shapes in a square from −1 to 1, laid onto a surface.
       - **The coat** goes over the gas hood's face (crew.py MASK_C's ellipsoid), cap rim to collar. The eyepieces and filter drums stand proud, so they come through it.
       - **With the welder's visor down** (variants 1 and 3), the coat goes on the visor instead, with its slot of glass left bare.
       - **The stencil** goes over the coat. X eyes and smile are placed on the eyepieces.
       - **The sigil** is a badge on both earflaps. It's in the wearer's stencil colour over a coat, else in `sigilInk`.
       - **On the engine**, the sigil is a 1.1 m ring and design on both sides of the big cab's waist, under the gallery windows (`MaskKit.SigilSpot`). The hood behind is all braces. The cab's side is the canvas note 716 left.
