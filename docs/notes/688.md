688. **Chimney smoke runs faster as the player moves (D1, queue #420; the director, 13 Oct evening, `docs/playtest/2026-10-13-evening.md` line 4).**
"When I'm moving, the smoke out of the chimney stacks is speeding up. So that might be a hint as to why we're getting
some frame drops ... the smoke speed up is quite dramatic when I start running, and when I'm walking, not even when I'm
running."

**What it was:** the smoke's clock was the world's, but its seed wasn't. `GreyboxScene` seeded each chimney's plume
(`Effects.Chimney`) with `(int)(top.X * 7 + top.Z * 13)`, where `top` is the chimney's place relative to the eye. Every
step changed it, so every frame re-picked the plume's emission rate (0.9–1.5 puffs a second) and every puff's place,
drift and size. The smoke churned and raced while you moved, harder the faster you went. The works' stacks were the same.

**The fix:** `WorldArt.Chimneys` and `Stacks` give each chimney its own seed (its house's index and which chimney; the
stack's fixture), wherever the eye is. `TownSmokeTests.AChimneysSmokeIsSeededTheSameWhereverYouStand` builds frontier:7's
departure town (the director's) and pins it.

**Was it the frame drops?** Not this: the smoke's cost per frame didn't change (the same billboards, about 7 per chimney
within 160 m), only which ones. Lines 1–3's drops are the performance audit's.
