668. **frontier:7's departure town reads as a town, not a row (P1, queue #399; the World Building board's `pt13-seed7-row`; the director, 13 Oct, playing the morning build at main 8fc43b58).**
"The Seed 7 first spawn area still has a long row of houses. It doesn't feel like a town, so that's a bit of an issue."

**What it was.** A town's houses went on the line's own street first, one every `houses.every` (15 m) either side of
the track, and only took streets of its own (the walled town's, note 335) when that row ran out of lots. frontier:7's
departure town (Fort Gantry: 97 people, 41 houses) fitted the row, so it was all row. B2's #599 (note 490: a small
town's green in the square's rear end, houses off true) doesn't change that: its houses are still the line's row.

**What changed.** towns.json `houses.lineRow` (10; `StreetTuning.LineRow`, no limit by default): a town of more houses
than that keeps ten on the line's own street, the nearest its square, and the rest stand on streets of its own as a
walled town's do: a row either side, facing across the street, lanes jogging between, the green across from the square,
the works across the line, the wall round it (inside the yard's level pad: linegen `fortress.padM`, 70 m). A hamlet of
about twenty people stays a row by the yard. A town that needs streets anyway keeps its line row as it was.

**Looked at:** `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --town square|street|houses|green|over [--survey]`, and over the gate
(`--cam 1000,-20,90 --target 870,-20,0 --survey`), before and after; on the World Building board's "A town, not a row
down the track" section.

**Verified:** `WalledTownTests.ATownOfMoreThanAHamletHasStreetsOfItsOwnNotOneRowDownTheTrack` (frontier:7 at 97 and
50 people, local:1 at 140): at most `lineRow` on the line's street; each side's street built on both rows, facing it;
the houses round the square. Three tests that used frontier:7 at 40-60 people as "the small town that's the yard" use a
hamlet of 20 now. The Sim suite passed (1756 of 1756).
