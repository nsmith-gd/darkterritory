1045. **Tower Jaw's fall is a punish (Ines, the Token Budget Manager's worker, nathaniel3; queue #743; note 1036's finding; GDD App. A.1, A.9, D.12; docs/design/creatures/tower-jaw.md §5).** *In review* (branch `ines-towerjaw-743`).
   - **The fault** (note 1036): Tower Jaw gnaws in its Alert phase, and on gnawing through `TowerJaw.Collapse` entered Punish straight from Alert. The spine refuses that (App. A.1: no punish but out of a commit, no commit but out of a telegraph its reaction window long), so the structure fell (the chute empty or the crane dead, the wreck across the line) with no telegraph, no punish event, no auto-bookmark, no attribution record, and the HUD's "the tower's down across the line: clear it by hand (Use)" never shown.
   - **The fix, as tower-jaw.md §5 has it.** Its tree gives the gnaw a TELEGRAPH of its own: "the chiselling; chips; then the creak and the lean (from half gnawed), the groan (the last 5 s)". So the groan is the telegraph, and the fall is its commit and punish:
     - `TowerJaw.Tick`: in Alert at its gnawing, from `groanSeconds` (5 s) short of through, it enters Telegraph; through, it commits and the structure falls (`Collapse`, then Punish out of the commit). With the groan cut short (it reared at someone, or turned to a knock: Alert again), it holds at the last splinter until the telegraph's had `minReactionSeconds`, so no fall comes unwarned.
     - Its rear at a crewmate during the groan joins the telegraph already running; its lunge waits its own `threatSeconds` from the rear (the mode's clock, not the phase's).
     - The record: the base `Punished` ("Punished by the tower jaw", where it stands by its fallen structure), and the bookmark of whoever can see it (one that holds nobody is pictured only when someone sees it, as before).
     - **Its art and hints by its phase:** the art already draws from its mode (mirrored from the sim; a groaning Tower Jaw is still at its gnaw clip, the lean from `leanFrom`). The HUD line is read by its mode where the phase means two things, as the Gannet's (`PrototypeSession.Cue`): Telegraph still gnawing is "the tower groans and leans over the line: get clear of it", Telegraph reared is "it rears and slaps its tail: four blows drive it off" as before; the fall's commit says nothing (its punish, the same tick, says the tower's down).
     - Nothing about #732's gantry fall changed (`TowerJawTests.GnawedThroughTheGantryFallsAcrossItsTrackAndClosesIt` and its joiner test pass as they were).
   - **Measured:** the repro from note 1036, `dt balance --conflicts --routes frontier:7 --seeds 1 --crews 2 --only towerJaw+facilityLoading --loading-seconds 130 --trace out/t`: Telegraph at 93 s (gnawed 0.96), Punish at 98 s with `towersDown=1` (before: Alert throughout).
