202. **The dead's vote as a screen, from a headset, and the bots' votes (GDD v1.4 App. D.11, D.12; WP11, note 180's "not yet").**
    - **The ballot's screen (D.11):** dead with a ballot (`ClientSession.Ballot`), a plate of its own high on the right, clear of the dead card and the yard's lobby list: "THE DEAD'S VOTE: ONCE A RUN", each creature with its key and, at the right, the want it serves (the vote only moves weight inside a want), then what to do next. The trim's lit while there's a vote to cast. `Hud.BallotRows` says it; `BallotPlate` draws it.
      - **Pick, then cast:** a number key picks its creature (lit on a bar), and the same key again or Enter casts it: "[2] AGAIN OR [ENTER] CAST IT / IT'S LOCKED ONCE CAST". Then "CASTING ..." until the host's ballot comes back cast, then "CAST, AND LOCKED" with the choice in green. The dead card keeps "YOU CALLED THE FIRE FLIES. THE LIVING WON'T KNOW TILL THE END".
      - **Reading: "locked on submit" wants a submit.** Before, a number key cast at once, and one stray key spent the night's only vote. The pick is this machine's alone (`BallotPicker`); only the cast goes to the host.
      - **Same intent path:** the cast is still the option's number as `PlayerIntent.Select` (the dead carry nothing). `NetPlaySession` sends it every tick until the host's ballot says it's locked, so a lost packet can't lose it. While the ballot's open the number keys are the ballot's, not the hotbar's.
    - **From a headset (T36):** the left stick's up and down step through the ballot (its left and right still change whom you watch), and clicking it casts. The trigger and A already mean something to the dead (next crewmate, Live Mic), and the click means nothing. `VrMenuInput` reads the click as `VrMenuPress.Click`, which the menus ignore; `BallotPicker.Headset` applies a press. With `Hud.Headset` the hints say "[STICK UP/DOWN] PICK ONE", "[CLICK STICK] CAST IT".
      - The run-end screen's commendations work the same way in a headset: the stick picks who and which, and its click gives it. A, which leaves the screen, now also keeps your commendations in your profile, as Enter does (before, only Enter did).
    - **Bots vote (D.11: "dead players", and a bot is a crewmate):** `BotCrew.Vote`, the last step of `BotCrew.Think`. A dead bot (not one waiting to board) casts `enemies.json director.vote.botSeconds` (6 s) after the host first offered its ballot, timed by the host's tick (`ClientSession.BallotOfferedTick`, the newest snapshot's when it came), for option `id mod options`: no dice, and a crew of bots spreads its votes. It sends the same `Select` a player's cast does, until it's locked. botSeconds isn't a GDD number: it's the bots' deliberation, so a harness night with deaths votes. The harness's threat report now counts `votes` by creature.
    - `dt screenshot --hud --spectating --ballot pick|cast [--headset]` stages it: the watcher dead, their second option picked, or cast and locked by the host (`--vote` alone: offered, nothing picked).
    - **Not yet:** a profile screen showing the tally; bots don't commend.
