669. **The notice board in the town's own words (P1, queue #400; the World Building board's `pt13-notice-board`; the director, 13 Oct, playing the morning build).**
"The notice board is interesting, but I think we're going to want to have actual like custom writing for this."

**What it was.** The board's papers were one stock texture, "FORM 19 - TRAIN ORDER", on every sheet, whatever was
posted; the words were only in the HUD on reading it, and most of them the generic notices any town might carry.

**What changed.**
- Each custom has four notices of its own (towns.json `cultures[].board`, `TownCulture.Board`), in its own voice: its
  rotas, lost and found, wanted and for trade, meetings and burials, warnings, corrections, a private plea. Written for
  each custom from its law, hall, centrepiece and its people's lines; none tells anyone how to play.
- The board posts the custom's order, then `boardOwn` (2) of the town's own (on their own stream, `papers.board`), then
  as before.
- The sheets carry their own words (`SquareKit.Board`): the heading large, the words under it in the pixel font's
  capitals (a printed handbill's), wrapped and sized to fill the sheet, each sheet tacked a little off true; a piece per
  town (`square-board-<town>-<n>`). Read close to; the HUD still reads them out in full.
