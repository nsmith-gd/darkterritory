866. **The extinguisher puts out the flame it's aimed at, stretch by stretch down the car (Faye, queue #593; the 19 Oct playtest, line 10).**
   - Claimed 2026-10-19 17:40 UTC by the Token Budget Manager (nathaniel3) for Faye.
   - **The director's words:** "the fire is still an issue, its not going out on the grid Im aiming at, its still going out once the whole car is done only so I cant work down the car for the fire out. Think about how an extinguisher actually works, it aims at a flame, puts it out, then I aim at a different flame and put it out, etc. etc."
   - **What the sim does (unchanged):** the fire has been a grid since note 267 (16 cells in a cargo car since note 634: 4 stretches along it, each its floor, two walls and roof). The spray works the stretch it's aimed at (note 662, `sprayWorksAcross`) at `sprayPerSecond` 1 a cell and wets it for `dampSeconds`; each cell's heat is replicated (4 bits a cell, `CarFire.Pack`). Measured on a car at full blaze, one stretch sprayed for 3 s: it's at 0 after 1 s and stays out about 8 s with the rest of the car burning beside it, the next stretch knocked down to ~0.1 by the spray's share, then creeps back (spread unchanged). So in the sim the cell aimed at did go out.
   - **What was wrong (the drawing):** a car fire is drawn twice: its cells (`GreyboxScene.FireGrids` → `Effects.FireCell`), and its heart (`DrawEnemy` → `CreatureArt.Enemy` → `IncidentArt.Draw` → `Effects.CarFire`) at the fire's heat-weighted middle, sized by the whole car's mean heat. Note 267 meant the heart to give way to the cells (`Effects.CellFlames`: no tongues, no creep at the heart), but the scene set that flag only on the art's own `Effects` (`Look.Art.Effects`), and `CreatureArt` draws the heart with **its own** `Effects` (`_fx`), whose flag was never set. So the heart has always drawn a whole fire: two ranks of up to 13 tongues, the creep toward the ends, the base glow, the cinders, 30 puffs of smoke and the roof's pool of it, and two lights reaching 8.5 m, standing in the middle of the car until its last cell was out. Spray the stretch under it and the stretch's own flames went, but the heart's flames, light and smoke stood on in it; the whole car lit orange and smoky to the end. That's "it's still going out once the whole car is done only".
   - **Now:**
     - `CreatureArt.CellFlames` passes the flag to the heart's effects, and the scene sets both each frame. With it, `Effects.CarFire` draws nothing at the heart (it still draws the whole fire for a fire with no cells yet: a client's copy before its first record, and the unit tests' stand-alone fire).
     - Each stretch (`Effects.FireStretch`, from `FireGrids`, by the hottest of its cells) draws its own smoke (8 puffs rising to the roof), its share of the pool under the roof, a glow on its floor, cinders and one flickering light reaching 2.5 + 3 x its heat m (5.5 m at full blaze, so the stretch beside it put out goes dark); smouldering (short of `burnFrom` x 0.8), pale smoke and a small red light; out, nothing. Its flames are its cells', as before. Four lights a burning car at most (the frame carries 32, nearest first).
     - **The jet goes to the flames it's putting out** (`GreyboxScene.Extinguishing`). It was drawn from the nozzle to the fire's heart, wherever the sprayer pointed: the white jet always flew to the middle of the car. The aim isn't replicated (the scene has no crewmate's pitch), the cells are: each jet now goes to the cell within 4.5 m of its nozzle whose heat is falling fastest (the stretch being sprayed falls at the whole `sprayPerSecond`, its neighbours at the share), held 0.4 s between snapshots, its flames 0.35 m off the boards; a fire with no cells, to the heart as before. The staged `--spray` goes to the burning cell nearest its nozzle.
     - The car's outside smoke (`Fires`/`Burnt`) still follows the car's mean heat, so it thins as the crew work the car down; the sound follows the mean as before.
   - **Spread and relighting stay as #579 (note 851) left them.** They don't stop the work: a stretch put out stays out ~8 s while the next is worked (a stretch takes about a second to put out from full blaze), so working from one end to the other puts the car out. Started in the middle (by the side door), the stretches either side can creep back into a wet one after it dries; that's the fire as designed (note 267's "dampSeconds"), told by the flames now coming back where they actually are.
   - **No wire change, nothing in the Sim changed:** `Messages.Version` stays at main's.
