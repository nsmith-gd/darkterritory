      - The stove is its own heat (`PlayerMotor.BesideStove`), steam or none. Shut in, the whole car's warm; with a door open, only within `stoveReach` (2.5 m) of the stove, like standing at the cab's firebox.

    - **Armoured car conversion (§26 "reinforced plating, heavier mass"):** one car, the van, `Vehicle.Armoured`. It weighs `armourTonnes` (10) more, so the same engine pulls it slower (spec B.5's forces are the loaded consist's). `Vehicle.Batter` lets `armourDamage` (0.5) of a blow to its shell through: the Car Hugger's feeding and a knock at the couplers. Drawn in the armoured livery under riveted plate, a skirt over the trucks and angle iron at the eaves (`TrainKit.ArmourPlate`).
    - **Second guard car (F.3 "a third gun, but it costs a cargo slot"):** `guardCars` 2. It's a guard van with cars behind it, so it has no rear platform; its gun faces back, slides on its rail and is restocked like the others.
    - **The small ones:**
      - *Roof handrails* (F.3 "Dragger resistance"): with a hand on the rail, a Dragger's grab range is `rails.draggerGrab` (0.6) of what it was, and it hangs on `draggerHang` (1.5) times as long before it has you, which gives friends longer to haul you back. A bend has to be taken `throwOver` (1.5) times further over its limit to throw you off the roof. Drawn on every car's roof (`TrainKit.RoofHandrails`), with gaps at the ladder heads.
      - *Car insulation* (F.3 "(cold)"): in a car without heat (a door open, or the steam gone) the cold comes on at `insulation` × `player.json cold.indoorsRate`: halved.
      - *Coupling reinforcement:* the closing speed a coupling takes before the ends are damaged goes ×1.5, so a coupling-up at the most that couples (1.5 m/s) does none. The Passenger takes ×1.5 as long to unhook the caboose (6 s).
      - *Reinforced couplings* (F.3 "uncouple under load"): cutting a working train takes no longer than cutting a slack one (`uncoupleUnderLoadSeconds` ×0.5: 6 s to 3 s).
      - **Reading:** "uncouple under load" read as being able to cut under load as readily as slack, since cutting under load was already possible, just slower. "Coupling reinforcement" read as the couplings taking harder knocks. The wreck's `couplingBreak` stays as it is: a derailment's film is not a coupling the crew can save.
