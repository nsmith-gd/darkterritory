687. **Hot reload out of a player's build, and watched not polled in a dev one (D1, queue #419; the director, 13 Oct, on note 666's suggestions: "Clear to do this once I get a build to test").**
Note 666 found the sound bank listing its 681 files every frame for the hot reload (now once a second), and every
other hot-reloaded file asked the disk too. A player never edits `content/`; a dev build and a mod author do.

What changed:
- **`Ballast.HotReload`** (Ballast.Core): `Enabled`, off by default. The app turns it on in a dev build (`#if DEVTOOLS`)
  and with `--hot-reload` (mod authors), before any content loads.
- **`FileWatch`**: one `FileSystemWatcher` per folder, shared by everything reloading from it. Every change bumps a
  generation. A watcher that can't start or errors is `Failed`.
- **`HotCheck`**: a reload reads the disk only when its folder's generation has moved, or on a slow poll (5 s, or 1 s
  when the watcher failed) for what a watcher misses.
- **`HotData<T>.Refresh`** (the session's train, player, boiler and combat tuning; the audio's mix, spaces and walls)
  does nothing when hot reload is off, and otherwise asks its folder's watch first.
- **`GameAudio`**: the sound bank's listing of its 681 files happens only when `content/audio`'s watch says so. This
  replaces note 666's once-a-second timer.
- A game running on mods runs on the mounted copy (`content-with-mods`). Its reload sees edits to that copy, not to the
  mod's own folder, as before.
