346. **A lamp guttering, the second upkeep job while the train runs (queue #83, D1; GDD App. F.3, the director, 7 Oct 2026: "on the train, still relatively boring from point A to point B"; [orchestrator.md](design/orchestrator.md) §5.1 U3).**
    - **What it is.** Every `everyMetres` (3 km ± half) of the train's running, a lit car with a room has its lamp start to gutter: `Vehicle.Gutter`, the seconds it's been at it. None in the first 3 km, none in the yard or a fort, never more guttering than there are crewmates alive. All in `upkeep.json` `lamp`; host only (`Gutters`), the vehicle record carries it (protocol 36).
    - **The tell.** The lamp flickers: drawn out for moments a few times a second, at random, more of the time the nearer it is to going out (a twelfth of the time at first, over half at the end; `GreyboxScene.Sputtered`, the car's lights and its lamps alike); and the wick sputters by the ceiling lamps (`lamp-gutter`, a synth loop: uneven crackles over a low flutter, quicker as it goes). The prompt in the car says TRIM THE LAMP.
    - **The fix.** The lamp key, in the car: guttering, the press trims it (it burns steady, and stays lit; a trim isn't a light seen in the dark, note 287, so it draws nothing). Burning steady, the key is the switch it always was.
    - **Left alone.** After 45 s it goes out: the car is dark, and Climbers make for an unlit car (App. A.4) until someone lights it again. The outline's ×1.15 on the pressure is the train's own lamp's dark, not a car's, so a car lamp out adds none.
    - **Bots.** One indoors in that car presses the lamp key every half second while it's guttering (`Heed.Gutter`): the host's trim reaches the client a snapshot later, and a second press on a lamp burning steady would put it out. Since 8 Oct (a 41-minute bot night with upkeep: 7 of 8 guttering lamps went out, nobody in their cars) a walker goes to one: with no trouble in a car to answer, the nearest car guttering with nobody inside is where its legs take it (along the roofs and in at its door, `RoofWalkerBot.GutterCar`), and in there `Heed.Gutter` trims it. Before a mail bag (the lamp's on a clock; a bag is pay); a gunner only with nobody else to send. `GutterTests.AWalkerGoesInToAGutteringLampNobodysWithAndTrimsIt`.
    - **Verified.** `GutterTests` (4): one starts by the train's running; left alone it goes out; the key trims it, then switches it as ever; a bot in the car trims it, and leaves a steady lamp alone. `dt screenshot --view inside --gutter 44 --scene-time 0.45` (out for that moment) against `--gutter 0` (lit), looked at; `dt audio render --sound lamp-gutter --param gutter=1`, the spectrogram looked at.
