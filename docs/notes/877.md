877. **The encounter rate λ(progress, crew, cars, tier): `EncounterPacing` (the director's brief §1, §3; plan note 874; nathaniel1's n1-C1, queue #606).**
   - Claimed 2026-10-20 by nathaniel1's Token Budget Manager for n1-C1 (PR #1330); note 877 taken on the claims branch.
   - **The contract** (for #608 and the others; `src/DarkTerritory.Sim/Enemies/EncounterPacing.cs`, pure statics, nothing
     drawn, no world read, no state):
     - `EncounterPacing.Progress(elapsed, dawnSeconds, distance, runLength, previous = 0)` → 0..1: the further of the clock
       toward dawn and the train along the line, never under `previous` (hold the last value and pass it back, so a stopped
       or backing train can't take the night back). No dawn: the line alone; no line: the clock alone.
     - `EncounterPacing.Rate(progress, crew, cars, tier, EncountersTuning)` → opportunities a minute; `PerSecond(...)` the same
       a second. `Ramp`, `CrewFactor`, `CarFactor` its terms, for telemetry (#600) and the sweep.
     - `EncounterPacing.ActiveCrew(world.CrewThisTick)` → the crew alive in the night: bots and players alike; a disconnected
       player has left the session's crew; the dead and one waiting for a Holdout (`DeathCause.Waiting`) aren't counted.
       `Rate` counts fewer than 1 as 1, so the rate never collapses to nothing.
     - `EncounterPacing.Night(minutes, crew, cars, tier, t)` → the opportunities a night holds with progress running evenly:
       what #601's and #613's measured encounters are read against.
     - The tuning: `director.encounters` in `enemies.json`, `DirectorTuning.Encounters` (`EncountersTuning`, partial: #607 adds its `Debt` in its own file, `EncounterDebt.cs`).
       The two land as one `encounters` block; whoever merges second joins them.
   - **The formula** (the brief's prototype, every coefficient tunable):
     λ = (base + (ramp + rampPerCrew·(P−1))·p^power) · (1 + perCrew·(P−1)) · (1 + perCar·max(0, C−carsFrom)) · tier, to at most
     max. Shipped: base 0.12, ramp 0.33, rampPerCrew 0.035, power 1.2, perCrew 0.07, perCar 0.05, carsFrom 3, tier local 0.8 /
     frontier 1 / deadLines 1.2 / deepTerritory 1.4 (the pressure's own tier table's values), max 3 a minute (eight crew, 18 cars,
     Deep Territory at the end is 2.54, so the cap binds only past the matrix).
   - **A 20-minute Frontier night's opportunities** (`Night`): solo 2 cars 5.4, two 5 cars 6.7, four 8 cars 9.6, eight 12
     cars 16.5 (brief §7's bands: 5–8, 7–11, 10–15, 14–20). Two and four sit just under their bands: opportunities aren't
     encounters (some fail to place; #607's debt brings part of those back), so #608/#613 should measure before raising them.
   - **Determinism:** p^1.2 needs a real power, and the Sim can't call `Math.Pow` (`DeterministicMathTests`). New
     `DMath.Pow(double x, double y)` (Ballast.Core): the whole part by squaring, the fraction by its binary digits as repeated
     square roots (IEEE-exact), so the same bits everywhere; within 1e-13 of `Math.Pow` (pinned). The int overload is unchanged.
   - **Tests** (`EncounterPacingTests`, the brief's six requirements over every tier, crews 1–8, cars 2–24): the shipped
     tuning is the prototype; monotonic in progress; rising with crew; the late rise steeper with crew (absolutely and as a
     ratio); rising with cars in even steps with no breakpoint, short trains not below the floor; a solo floor (≥ 0.09 a
     minute at the gate on Local; a solo Frontier night in 5–8); bounded by max and the tier ordered; progress the further
     of the two and never back; the active crew the living.
   - **Not done here (by the plan):** nothing drives the director with it yet. #608 wires it in, removes the pressure's
