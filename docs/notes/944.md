944. **Dread at the stops: the longer you loot, the less safe (queue #646; the director's playtest of 20 Oct evening, line 15; n1-C2, 21 Oct 2026).**
   - **The ask:** "You start safely collecting loot and then suddenly it doesn't feel so safe. You've been in there just long
     enough." A crew afoot at a yard or an abandoned village should meet rising danger the longer it stays: first signs, then
     a scare, then a real threat, each kind's telegraph kept. Inside the overhaul's placement (#609) and caps (#611), not a
     new channel.
   - **Built** (`src/DarkTerritory.Sim/Enemies/Dread.cs`, pure; thin calls in `Director.Watch`). Behind
     `director.afoot.dread.on`, which ships off until the director confirms ("test creature spawn first").
     - **An outing:** the seconds anyone's been afoot (note 327's afoot) since the crew was last all back aboard for
       `forgetSeconds` (30).
     - **Signs:** note 327's eyes come more often (the wait × 1 → `signEveryAtScare` 0.5 by `scareAt` 90 s) and nearer (the
       distance × 1 → `signOutAtScare` 0.7). The same sign stream: only the drawn values are scaled, so the dice don't move.
     - **A scare** (from `scareAt`): the signs at `scareOut` 0.45 × their distance, close by. Still never a spawn.
     - **A real threat** (from `threatAt` 180 s, once an outing): the encounter clock is brought up to due (note 908). The
       director's own channels then send one: met out there by `afoot.outsideWeight`, placed by #609, under #611's caps and
       every kind's own rules and telegraph. `Director.DreadThreats` counts them.
   - **Measured, `dt afoot --route frontier:7`** (a crewmate kept on the ground at Foundry's yard, 2.5 km), off → on:

     | seconds afoot | signs | spawns | engaged |
     |---:|---:|---:|---:|
     | 60 | 3 → 4 | 3 → 3 | 1 → 1 |
     | 180 | 4 → 15 | 6 → 6 | 2 → 2 |
     | 300 | 9 → 27 | 8 → 8 | 2 → 3 |

     - **The threat:** brought due at 180 s, it came at 184 s as a Ribbit pack, under the caps. By its own rules it sat
       dormant by its warren, since the crewmate stood still. A Whistler came on at 288 s.
     - **Off is note 327 as it was:** the same signs and spawns, tick for tick.
     - **Defaults:** 27 signs in 5 minutes is a sign every 11 s on average, near the end every 6–12 s. That's the director's
       call when they try it; `signEveryAtScare` is the knob.
   - **Tests:** `DreadTests`:
     - the stages and the signs closing in;
