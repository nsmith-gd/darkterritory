         - **The eight kinds**, three of each, after the renders (`tools/models/recipes/corrupted_trees.py` on `tools/models/treekit.py`: tubes on parallel-transport frames, barnacles, blobs, quartz points, bracket fungus, hanging cards, membranes, holes, boxes):
             - **barnacle:** a spruce crusted at its foot and roots with barnacles and mussels, wrack and beard lichen hanging off spiky dead limbs;
             - **lantern:** bracket fungus stacked up the trunk, amber pods glowing on threads and heaped in a hollow, grey webs in the crown;
             - **tar:** a twisted trunk run with tar, every limb coated and dripping, a pool at its foot;
             - **iron:** rail spikes all over it with the rust run down from each, an I-beam through it, fishplates, holes; a rail bent through the third one's crown;
             - **crystal:** bleached like bone, hollowed, white quartz breaking out of it and hanging in clumps, red nodules, thorny twigs;
             - **webbed:** a birch (one, two or three stems) with oval holes and black marks, pink membranes between its limbs, dead leaves hanging;
             - **thorn:** a twisted trunk throwing claw-curved limbs, thorns, red webs, cocoons, sprays of needles;
             - **lattice:** a trunk of nine roots woven round nothing, skin in some gaps, dead leaves.
           Each is 1,100–2,300 triangles, built 12 m tall with its foot at the origin. It's worn in the texture library's layers with a tint of its own per part (`dt_tint`, which `PropArt.Resolve` now honours; no prop set it before).
         - **New library layers** (`tools/art/texgen/mat_blight.py`, imported last so no other layer moves; `textures.py --only`):
             - `wrack_card`: knotted wrack on its left half, beard lichen in locks on its right;
             - `leaf_card`: dead leaves on a hanging twig;
             - `membrane_web`: a holed, veined skin, painted pale so each tree's tint colours it;
             - `quartz`.
           The two hanging cards keep their roots at their image's foot, because scene.vert bends a card from v 1. They hang from the limb and swing at the tip.
         - **The cards** (`tools/models/impostor.py`): past `CorruptedTreeArt.ModelledWithinM` (28 m) from the line, a tree is two crossed cards of itself, 8 triangles. They're rendered in Blender from the model in its own layers and tints: the colour darkened by its hollows, its normals as seen from the card, and what glows. Each kind's three trees from two sides each fill one 512 layer, `ctree_<kind>_far_0` (cut out; the lantern's pods glow in it).
         - **The Sim** (`LinesideProps.Slot`): a tree is corrupted on the roll every try already draws, at the biome's share. That's `biomes.json` `corruptedTrees`, 0.3 in every biome, the dead and the living alike; it was a third of the dead in the four biomes with "corrupted" in their trees. No tree moves. A corrupted tree's wall is its model's trunk: `LinesideProp.Radius` 0.024 h, clamped 0.1–0.55.
         - **The art** (`PlanArt`, `CorruptedTreeArt`): which kind is the biome's to weigh (`corruptedKinds`), from the tree's art seed: barnacles on the coast, the dykelands and the fishing towns; iron and tar in the colliery towns and the slag; tar and lattice in the tar ponds; lantern, thorn and lattice in the black spruce; webbed birch on the farmland and the edge; crystal on the barrens and the highland. Which of the three is the seed's too. The stands' art-only crossed-card spruces out past the woods (55–165 m) are corrupted at the same share, by where they stand, and drawn as cards (cheaper than the spruce cards).      - **Found:**
         - scene.vert bends a card by `1 - fract(v)`, so a vertex at exactly v = 1 has `fract` 0 and bends the most: a hanging card's root is put at v 0.999 (Blender's 0.001), not 1.
         - Cycles' camera space looks down +Z, not Blender's -Z: a normal pass through a Vector Transform to camera space comes out facing into the card (the first cards were lit from behind, near black). The cards' normals are baked in world space and turned onto each card's axes in numpy.
         - Cycles in this container renders an emission-only pass at 320×512 in under a second on four cores. A kind's whole build and bake (three trees, six shots, three passes each) takes about 10 s; all eight about 70 s.
