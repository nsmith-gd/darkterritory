     - **Found:**
         - scene.vert bends a card by `1 - fract(v)`, so a vertex at exactly v = 1 has `fract` 0 and bends the most: a hanging card's root is put at v 0.999 (Blender's 0.001), not 1.
         - Cycles' camera space looks down +Z, not Blender's -Z: a normal pass through a Vector Transform to camera space comes out facing into the card (the first cards were lit from behind, near black). The cards' normals are baked in world space and turned onto each card's axes in numpy.
         - A vertex shader that moves a piece as one needs one number on all of it, and a prop drawn into a world cell keeps nothing of which piece it was but its UVs and its tint: so a drip's (and a pod's) whole part of u says which it is (the layers tile, so the texture never sees it) and the tree's tint which tree. `Vertex.Surface` won't do: a prop's is its object position in texels (128 a metre), different at every vertex and the same in every copy.
         - Cycles in this container renders an emission-only pass at 320×512 in under a second on four cores. A kind's whole build and bake (three trees, six shots, three passes each) takes about 10 s; all eight about 70 s.
