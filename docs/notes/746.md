Make sure the player doesn't die when they are fired to a location because we're going for fun in this one.    - **The plan:**
     - **The reload order:** for every gun, powder, then pack it, then the ball (App. C.3's powder, ball, ram becomes powder, ram, ball). The last step is a ball or, on the guard van's cannon with `dress:humanCannonball`, a crewmate who climbs in.
     - **Consent:** only a living crewmate, only themselves. Use held at the muzzle with the gun packed and no ball in it ("CLIMB IN : HOLD [E]"); Jump climbs out any time before the shot. A bot never climbs in.
     - **The flight:**
       - The gunner's shot fires them at a landing point along the gun's aim, on dry ground, never off the map. A point past the map's edge or in water comes back along the aim to the last good one.
       - They face that point all the way.
       - The landing never hurts or kills: a fired crewmate lands as a roll, whatever the speed.
       - A creature in the flight's way takes the cannonball's damage.
     - **Feedback:** the radio ("Sam's been fired from the rear cannon"), the report ("Fired from the rear cannon by Dave."), the HUD in the barrel and at the gun, and the muzzle's flash and boom.
