746. **The human cannonball (U1, queue #447; docs/design/upgrades.md §8.3; the director, 15 Oct 2026: "the reload flow to be powder, pack, then either a ball or a player jumps in. Make sure the player doesn't die when they are fired to a location because we're going for fun in this one. The fired player needs to be facing the impact location when they are flying through the air. Don't ever allow a player to be fired off the map. If a player is fired into a monster they need to do damage to it."). Notes 715, 744, 745.**
   - **The plan:**
     - **The reload order:** for every gun, powder, then pack it, then the ball (App. C.3's powder, ball, ram becomes powder, ram, ball). The last step is a ball or, on the guard van's cannon with `dress:humanCannonball`, a crewmate who climbs in.
     - **Consent:** only a living crewmate, only themselves. Use held at the muzzle with the gun packed and no ball in it ("CLIMB IN : HOLD [E]"); Jump climbs out any time before the shot. A bot never climbs in.
     - **The flight:**
       - The gunner's shot fires them at a landing point along the gun's aim, on dry ground, never off the map. A point past the map's edge or in water comes back along the aim to the last good one.
       - They face that point all the way.
       - The landing never hurts or kills: a fired crewmate lands as a roll, whatever the speed.
       - A creature in the flight's way takes the cannonball's damage.
     - **Feedback:** the radio ("Sam's been fired from the rear cannon"), the report ("Fired from the rear cannon by Dave."), the HUD in the barrel and at the gun, and the muzzle's flash and boom.
   - **Handoff** (15 Oct 2026, U1): **not started; anyone's to pick up** (after #967 merges: it needs `dress:` upgrades, `WhimsyTuning`, `World.Whimsy`). The plan, from U1's reading of the code:
     - **Reload order:** `GunState.ReloadNeeded` stays 3, 2, 1, 0; only the steps' names change (`Hud.LoadStep`: 0 LOAD POWDER, 1 PACK IT, 2 LOAD BALL) and the reload cues' order in `GameAudio.CrewGuns` (AU1's, tell them; C1's reload clip `crew-cannon` too).
     - **Climbing in:** on the guard van's gun (`Facing` +1) with `dress:humanCannonball`, at `ReloadNeeded == 1`, a crewmate nearer the muzzle (pivot + barrel aim × `TrainKit.CannonMuzzle` 1.5 m) than the pivot, within `climbReach`, holding Use `climbSeconds`: a flag `InCannon` (next free `PlayerFlags` bit), feet pinned in the barrel (the motor's still-list beside Seated), `ReloadNeeded = 0`; Jump climbs out (`Placed++`). `Guns.Working` must not take that Use as the ball step. Bots never climb in.
     - **Firing:** the seated gunner's Fire with a crewmate in its gun: the same gates as `Guns.TryFire` (factor its bookkeeping out: cooldown, `LastShotTick` for the flash, reload, choir), no `GunShot`; a launch queued like horseplay's `_knocks` and applied with them (`World.StepHorseplay`), since the gunner's `CrewAct` can't touch the passenger's state.
     - **The flight:** a landing point `range` (40) m along the barrel's level aim, stepped back toward the gun until it's on dry ground (`Guns.Water`, `PlayerMotor.GroundAt`), not off the line's ends (`RailLine.Nearest`'s along), within `LateralRoom` and a `mapHalfWidth`; the world velocity that lands there in `T = dist / speed` under gravity (the motor's air step is gravity only, so it lands where it's aimed). Flag `Fired`: no damage in `PlayerMotor.Land` (fall or speed), cleared on landing; while it's set the look is overridden to face the landing point (re-solved each tick from position, velocity and the ground: deterministic on host and client).
     - **Hitting a creature:** host, each tick in flight, an enemy within its hit radius takes the cannon's damage once (`struck.Hit` / `Confirm(..., HitSource.Cannon)` as `World.StepEnemies` does for a shot), and the flyer's horizontal speed stops (they drop, still unhurt).
     - **Feedback:** the report and radio line ("Fired from the rear cannon by Dave", an `IncidentKind.Whimsy` event), HUD lines (CLIMB IN : HOLD [E]; IN THE CANNON   CLIMB OUT : [SPACE]; the gunner's LOADED: SAM), a view `cannonball`, `PetTests`-style tests (consent, the arc lands where aimed, never off the map or in water, no damage at any speed, a creature hit is hurt).
