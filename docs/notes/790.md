790. **The Mouse Moose (A1, queue #515; the director, 15 Oct 2026, in A1's chat).**
"A creature that wanders yards stealing loot. It does so very slowly, and once it gets out of position so that the crane can't reach it, then you can't get it as a player ... If you get within, let's say 6 meters of the creature, then it runs away and it will run away and hide for a bit before it comes back to try and steal the same piece of loot again ... Once you scare it away 5 times, it will target a new piece of loot. This creature is called the Mouse Moose. It's a mutant rat with little tiny moose antlers."

   - **GDD first:** a §21 entry, and the change list's paragraph. The design is `docs/design/creatures/mouse-moose.md`.
   - **The loot is a crane's castings.** "The crane can't reach it" is the crane's reach, measured, not a radius:
     - `Crane.Reaches(at)`: the hook can be put within `rigReach` of the point. It uses the crane's own bridge and trolley search, unremembered, since a dragged casting is somewhere new each time.
     - A casting dragged past that is `CastingState.Taken` (a new state, protocol 72):
       - Drawn where it lies (`Crane.Where`).
       - Not rigged (`Riggable` takes only stacked ones), not a bot's (StopCrew picks only stacked ones), and not in `Left`.
     - Dragged to the hole, it's `Lost`, and `World.MouseMooseTook` counts it.
   - **`MouseMoose` (Sim/Enemies/MouseMoose.cs):**
     - A creature loose in the world. Its modes are creep, bite, drag, flee and hide (`MouseMooseMode`, in its Height).
     - `Extra` is the casting it's after; `Extra2` is the scares off that one.
     - Its crane and hole are the host's. The casting's place replicates in the Crane record, as before.
     - The spine:
       - Telegraph is creeping in.
       - Commit is its teeth in the casting, after the reaction window.
       - BreakOff is bolting, and it returns to Telegraph when it comes back.
     - `StaysAboard`, so the director's linger doesn't dismiss it: it works when nobody's near.
   - **Tuning:** enemies.json `mouseMoose`:
     - `scareWithin` 6, `scaresToGiveUp` 5, `hideSeconds` 25.
     - `creep` 1.2, `drag` 0.15, `dragAway` 0.5, `run` 5.
     - `biteSeconds` 1.5, `holeOut` 22, `reachEvery` 15 ticks, `leaveBeyond` 300.
     - `tierWeights`.
     - Director: `costs` 2, `nightChance` 0.5. A body for the cannon.
   - **Spawn:**
     - The director's (`Spawns`): stopped at a facility whose crane (its own or a yard's: `Site.Cranes`) has a casting stacked. One at a stop.
     - The gym (`Calls.MouseMooseCall`): after the nearest stacked casting of any crane on the line, within the gym's 200 m.
   - **Art:** `CreatureArt.MouseMoose.cs` draws it from boxes (no Blender here). It creeps low, braces back and tugs, bolts stretched out, and in its hole only the antler tips and eyes show over turned earth. There is also a greybox stand-in.
   - **Checked:**
     - `MouseMooseTests` (6 tests):
       - Left alone, it takes a casting out of reach, then to its hole.
       - A crewmate by its casting scares it off 4 times, then the 5th sends it for the other. The casting never moves, and nobody's hurt.
       - Scared while dragging, it lets go and comes back for the same one.
       - A blow scares it and never kills it.
       - It goes with the train.
       - The director sends it to a standing train at a crane.
     - Shots: `dt screenshot --view mousemoose` and `--view mousemoosedrag` (staged; a heavy crate stands in for the casting, since the view has no crane). Also `dt gym --only mouseMoose` (a real yard and its casting).

   - **Beside the Pickers** (the director, 15 Oct, asked whether the two overlap too much: "Keep both as they are"): the Pickers race in a group for the loose crates; the Mouse Moose, alone, drags the crane's castings slowly off.
