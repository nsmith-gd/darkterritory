1039. **Corruption seen growing on the train: black, wet and veined at the couplings, under the cars and in the tender, where boarders came on, a little more each night; kept in the campaign save, scraped off at the blacksmith; the grown cars creak wetly at speed. Look only, behind a flag, off (n1-C4, queue #730; GDD Part Eleven q8; note 1026 rank 6).**

Part Eleven's question 8 asks: "Is corruption ever visible on the train itself: growth in the couplings, in the tender,
under the cars?" Note 1026 ranked it 6th, and the director's call is open in the inbox (`n1-A4-train-corruption`: look
only, or does it also draw creatures, or no). Until this note, the train was the only thing that forgot: damage and fire
char it (C.5) and burnt houses stay burnt (§3.1), but nothing showed where creatures got on or how long a crew had been
out. This builds note 1026's default meanwhile: look only, behind campaign.json `growth.on`, **off**, with a developer's
switch to see it.

## What changed

- **The measure** (`Sim/Campaign/TrainGrowth.cs`). `TrainGrowth`: how grown each vehicle is (0 the engine, whose growth is
  in its tender), and each coupling (the one behind each vehicle), 0 clean to 1 black all over. Deterministic, from the
  night's report alone.
- **Where the boarders came on** (`Run.Boarders`, `RunReport.Boarded`). The host counts each creature once, at the car
  it first got onto (by vehicle id). One running the roofs afterwards is still one boarder. Hazards and incidents aren't
  boarders.
- **The night settled** (`Campaign.Settle`, with campaign.json `growth`; GDD Part Eleven q8):
    - a boarder adds `perBoarder` (0.18) under its car, `toCouplings` (0.6) of that at each of the car's two couplings,
      and `toTender` (0.05) of it in the tender;
    - every spot already grown spreads by `perNight` (0.04) a night, up to `most` (1).
  Cars lost in the night take their growth with them. Off, a night grows nothing.
- **The save.** `CampaignState.Growth`, null in a save from before it (a clean train).
- **To every machine.** The night's `SessionSetup.Growth`: the spec in percent, cars then couplings (`0,5,40/9,9`), left
  out when there's none. The world's `World.Growth` is presentation only, and is drawn only with `growth.on`, so a save
  grown before the flag went off looks clean.
- **The blacksmith** (`Campaign.Scrape`, the forge's catalogue). `SCRAPE THE TRAIN: 35 SCRIP`, with the line "It's worst
  under car 3". It clears the whole train for `scrapeCost` (35, about half a spare lamp), dearer in a town cold to the
  crew as its upgrades are (note 1024). Not mid-night, not a clean train. The yard is hosted again clean, as after a
  refit. `dt campaign buy scrape` does the same, and `dt campaign show` lists `growth`.
- **The look** (`Art/GrowthKit`, `SceneArt.Car`; look.json `trainGrowth`). One piece per vehicle, over its body, in
  `levels` (4) steps from `showFrom` (0.05). The car's seed picks the layout, so the same car grows the same way night
  after night. Its parts:
    - **at a coupling:** a black crust round the draft gear and the knuckle, the hose coated, sagging strands across the
      gap to where the knuckles meet (each car draws its own half), drips, and veins up the car's end;
    - **under a car:** clumps on the frame, strands hanging toward the rails clear of the bogies, a black bead along both
      sills, and veins up its sides from the sill, longer the more it's grown;
    - **in the tender** (the engine's bunker): a crust over its rim and down its walls, strands, and veins.

  The crust is the tar texture, near black and wet. The veins are a bruise's red, faintly lit from inside as the
  Corruption's tree pods are (note 696): black alone doesn't read on a dark car at night. Holes (#717, note 1014) and the
  cracked cargo doors (#727, note 1025) are untouched: the growth is its own piece over the body.
- **The sound** (`GameAudio.GrowthCreak`; `train-growth.creak`; look.json `trainGrowth`). A car grown past `creakFrom`
  (0.5, under it or at either coupling) creaks wetly at `creakSpeed` (9 m/s) and over. That's up to `creaksPerSecond`
  (0.8), the more the faster and the more it's grown. The sound is the frame's creak muffled, a wet suck and a few
  bubbling clicks. It's captioned **A WET CREAKING UNDER THE CAR**.
- **Developer's switch** (note 514). The dev app's `--growth 0.8` (or a spec in percent) grows the train for a look,
  whatever the flag says; `dt screenshot --growth` does the same. A player's build has neither.
