845. **The crane's keys as seen from its cab (T2, queue #573; the director's playtest of 16 Oct, line 14).**
The director: "It's like I move left, and it goes up, like when I hit A, and then I hit S, and it goes to the right. I hit D, and it goes down. I hit W, and it goes to the left. So it's a little unintuitive in terms of the controls ... I would just fix the controls so that it makes a little bit more sense."

**What was wrong:** the stick ran the bridge (W/S, along the track) and the trolley (A/D, across) directly. The cab's view (note 736) looks in across the span from the bridge's far end, a quarter turn off those axes, so every key went 90° round from what was pressed.

**What changes**
- `Crane.Stick` turns the stick into the bridge's and trolley's ways from the gantry's own axes and the cab's view: W takes the hook away from the cab, S toward it, A and D to the left and right on the screen. Worked out from the axes, so a crane laid either way along the line reads the same. `Crane.StickFor` undoes it for the stop crew's bot operator (`StopCrew.Steer`).
- `CraneTests.TheKeysGoTheWayTheCabSeesThem` holds each key's way; the other crane tests drive by bridge and trolley through `StickFor`.
