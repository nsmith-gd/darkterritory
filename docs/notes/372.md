372. **Toys a lamp finds (queue #109, C1; the director, 8 Oct, on what's found at a stop: "a bit of a brighter more unique look to them so that they stand out from the background as interactable objects. Do this with good texture work, not VFX").** B4's finds atlas (note 326) answered the director for the village finds. The toys are found in the same cupboards and cellars and on the same doorsteps, but they were still drawn in their first paint: library wool and plaster tinted dark, averaging 37–68 (sRGB) against the atlas's 172. On a house's boards under a lamp they were brown lumps.
    - **Their own paint and cloth** (`tools/models/recipes/train_stores.py`, `toy_*` materials). Each part is tinted to come out at a chosen colour on its library layer: the bear in honey mohair, worn paler at the paws, with a brass button eye; the horse in ivory, a pillar-box red saddle and a pine board; the doll's porcelain and a dusty-rose smock with auburn hair; the pig's pink; the music box in polished walnut, red velvet and bright brass; the drummer in red, blue and green tin. The textures now average 93–145. The layers keep their wear, so they still read as old. The extinguisher, the kit and the shot locker keep their own materials.
    - **Not done by:** a glint, an outline or a light. That's the director's "not VFX". The gloss is the library layers' own: the spec bake takes gloss from the layer's map, not the material's roughness, so lowering roughness on the glazes changed nothing and isn't relied on.
    - **`dt screenshot --toys [h]`:** the six on the floor `h` m (default 1.6) under the eye, in an arc in front of it. In one of B4's searched houses: `--searched --toys --route frontier:7 --at 10250 --cam 10254.5,88.6,1.6 --target 10252,95,0.4 --lantern --fov 75`.
