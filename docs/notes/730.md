730. **Odd outcomes to find (K1, queue #464; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`, GDD App. F.6).**
The audit's Hackers goal: things that answer when poked, "never in the Wiki" (Nicki's wine, note 571, the precedent). The director approved the friendslop items on 14 Oct: "The rest of docs/design/friendslop.md §3 goes ahead." **This note is the wiki, for the team only.** The GDD says only that odd things happen, by design, unexplained (§3.3).

   - **What it was:** the towns and the stops had nothing that answered a poke beyond talking, reading, knocking and searching. Nicki's glass and Jacob's word were the only hidden holds.
   - **The rules for all of them:** small, in the world's grain (the Maritime towns, the railway, the dead villages), a payoff seen or heard, deterministic and the host's. Nothing hurts anyone or solves a creature. None is horseplay, so the crew mischief switch doesn't gate any of them.
   - **Now** (run.json `oddities`, `enabled` switches them all off; `Sim/Run/Oddities.cs` places the stops' things, `Sim/World.Oddities.cs` holds the rules):
     1. **The town bell** (`oddities.bell`). A walled town's council house has a bell rope on its front, beside the door, on the far side from the lamp (`Town.BellRope`). Use held 1.2 s pulls it. It rings at most every 20 s a town. Everyone in the town within 90 m stops where they are and looks up at the cupola for 6 s (`Town.React`; their round waits for them, as for a talk), then goes on. The rope's prompt comes before the door's knock: "THE BELL ROPE   PULL : HOLD [E]".
     2. **Waving back** (`oddities.wave`). The wave emote at a townsperson within 12 m and 30° of where you face: they turn to you and wave back for 2.5 s (the townsfolk's "wave" clip).
     3. **Dave's painting** (`oddities.painting`; GDD §3.2). Sit with him: Use held 10 s within 3 m of him while he's painting. If you've never struck him, he puts a painting at his feet, once a night: "A painting signed D" (loot.json `davePainting`), worth 0.08 of the tier's per-car value. The tally names it like any find. No prompt says so: his is still "DAVE   TALK : [E]". The app now sends Dave's press on to the host while he has a painting to give, as it does Jacob's.
     4. **The well** (`oddities.well`). A village's well (the layouts already had them). A find or a toy in hand at its mouth: "DOWN THE WELL : [E]", and it's gone. 2.8 s later it's heard hitting the water. The night's first drop gets something back: 6 s after its splash an old coin comes up out of the dark onto the ground beside the well ("An old coin", 0.04 of per-car). Empty-handed and sooted, "THE WELL   WASH : HOLD [E]" (2 s) washes the soot off.
     5. **The tin can** (`oddities.can`). On a village's road near its houses, 0.6 of villages. Walk into it faster than 2 m/s and it's kicked 3 to 6 m on the way you were going (the night's seed and the kick's count), rattling. 1.2 to 2.4 s later something answers from the dark, 90 m out beyond it, away from the line. Only the living hear that.
     6. **The scarecrow** (`oddities.scarecrow`). At a farmstead village, 0.8 of them, between the line and the houses, its back to the line, looking out over its fields. Once someone has been within 15 m of it, and then nobody living is within 30 m or looking its way (60° of their view), it's found turned to face the line.
     7. **The postbox** (`oddities.postbox`). A red pillar box at 0.5 of villages: at a halt's platform end, else where the village road comes in nearest the line. "THE POSTBOX   OPEN : HOLD [E]" (1.5 s) takes out the letter nobody collected (nine in run.json, the night's seed picks). It's read out to whoever took it as a paper card, for 14 s or until they walk 4 m off. It also goes in the night's report: "A letter left in a postbox at km 12. To Peggy: "…" Read by Dave." (`IncidentKind.Letter`). Then "THE POSTBOX   EMPTY".
     8. **The coaling chute's soot** (`oddities.chute`). While the chute pours, anyone within 1.8 m of where the coal falls comes out of it sooted (`PlayerFlags.Sooted`, horseplay's black smudges, note 717). This is the spec's "ride the chute down". There's no climbing the tower, so standing under the pour is what does it.
     9. **A lurch on the blades** (`oddities.lurch`). A switch thrown with a crewmate stood on its blades: on the ground, within 1 m of the main line, between the toe and the points' end. They're thrown off their feet toward the way it went, 1.6 m/s across and 1.4 m/s up, and staggered 0.5 s. It's a host move (`Placed`, as a shove is). It's their own hop off the ground, so no knock (note 573).
   - **The well's wall** is now its stone ring, 0.95 m half-width as drawn (`StopWalls.WellHalf`), not its 5 m plot, so a crewmate can walk up to it. `SolidWorldTests` says so.

The report's letters are `IncidentKind.Letter`. Clients take the townspeople's looks from the bell and the waves themselves, alike (`World.Notice`, `World.NoticeWaves`).
