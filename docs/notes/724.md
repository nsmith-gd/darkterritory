724. **Sessions (K1, queue #458; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`, GDD App. F.6).**
The audit's Friends goal ("It's extremely important that friends exceeds our target meaningfully") and its guardrail: mischief is fine among friends, and a public lobby needs kick and mute so horseplay (note 717) can ship there. Friend groups break on friction: uneven attendance, slow joins, strangers in public games.

   - **What it was:**
     - A campaign lived in the host's three slots: a crew whose host didn't turn up couldn't play its next night.
     - A friend joining mid-night waited at the train for the next stop, or (past the gate, with Holdouts) went dead into the respawn queue (`HostSession.Admit`), with bots crewing the night they could have taken over; a full crew of bots turned them away.
     - No mute and no kick: the only tool against a stranger was leaving.
     - Horseplay's switch was `player.json horseplay.enabled`, the same for every lobby.
   - **Now:**
     - **The crew's save travels** (GDD §11): as the host settles a night into the save (the app's `Autosave`), it stamps it with who hosted (`CampaignState.LastHost`) and when (`SettledAt`, the host's clock), and sends its bytes to every human aboard or waiting (`HostSession.ShareSave`, `MessageType.CrewSave` in chunks; bots skipped). Each joiner keeps it under the crew's id (`CampaignState.CrewId`: `Crew`, or an older save's seed) in `saves/crews/`, never in a slot (`SaveSlots.Keep`, slot 0). The campaign screen lists them under the slots, "CREW OWLS · NIGHT 8 · LAST HOSTED BY DAVE" (one in your own slots only when the copy has gone further); chosen, it's the fortress board for that crew, and its nights are hosted from it (`Launch.CampaignNight.Crew`; saving goes back to the crew's file). Of two copies, the one with more nights settled wins, then the newer settlement, then the one held (`Campaign.Prefer`); a train taken at dawn (note 632) closes every copy, listed as TAKEN AT DAWN.
     - **A friend takes a bot's seat** (GDD §33.1; `player.json crew.joinTakesBotSeat: true`): a human joiner who'd wait for a stop, go into the Holdouts' queue or be turned away from a full crew takes a bot's id where it stands, with its body, slots and what it carries, health and post (`HostSession.TakeSeat`): a living bot, the director's census's least busy (fewest threats on it, then most slack), else the newest, the one at the cab's controls last; a full crew takes a dead bot's seat before refusing. The joiner's welcomed into the id with a fresh token and adopts the state as a placement; the bot's link is hung up and its machine's `BotCrew` drops it (`HostSession.BotLeft` → `BotCrew.Retire`). No bot, or the flag off: the old way.
     - **Mute** (this machine only): Q held, a crewmate's number toggles MUTED beside their name (`NetPlaySession.ToggleMute`, by id and by name for the next nights). `VoiceChat.Muted` drops their frames as they come, on every path: near, radio, the speaking tube, the dead channel, a Soot Child's mimicry (a death's hard-cut still lands).
     - **Kick** (`player.json crew.kick`): Q held, Shift and the number. The host's own player puts them off outright. In a public lobby (listed, no password: `SessionSetup.VoteKicks`) anyone living and playing calls the crew's vote, or agrees to the one open (`MessageType.Kick`); open `voteSeconds` (30), carried by more than `majority` (0.5) of the living humans aboard, the one voted on not counted; the host and yourself can't be voted on; one vote at a time. The vote's across the top of every screen (`MessageType.KickVote`, `Hud.KickVoteLine`). Put off (`MessageType.Kicked`): their crewmate dropped as for a Leave (the place not held), hung up on after `refuseLingerSeconds`, the menu saying "THE CREW PUT YOU OFF AT THE NEXT STOP", no redial; coming back is refused (`RefusalReason.Kicked`), barred by their Steam account or LAN address, or by name where there's neither. Private and friends-only runs: the host's kick only.
     - **CREW MISCHIEF** (GDD §23.4): on the host screen and, hosting, the fortress board; in the yard, the host's night menu (`Launch.SetMischief`). It starts on, but in a public run FEELING COMPETITIVE (`player.json horseplay.competitiveOff: true`; `Moods.MischiefByDefault`). It goes to every machine with the night's setup (`SessionSetup.Mischief` → `World.CrewMischief`) and, changed in the yard, as `MessageType.Mischief` (and to every joiner as they're welcomed). `World.MischiefOn` gates the shove, the bonk and coal at the crew (all decided by the host alone) and the HUD's SHOVE prompt.
   - **Protocol 62** (A1's #1017 and #1024 took 60 and 61 first; rule 10): message types CrewSave, Kick, KickVote, Kicked and Mischief, and `RefusalReason.Kicked`. Nothing in the snapshot or the input changed.
