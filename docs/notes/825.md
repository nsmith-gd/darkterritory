825. **A night market on the square (queue #552, B2; the director's playtest of 14 Oct, line 3, in frontier:7's town: "The town's looking better. It's looking a little more explorable here. Still a bit basic. I like the little park").**

**What it was.**
- **The square:** the custom's centrepiece, the board, the plaque, two benches, two fire barrels and a flag on a lot of open ground between the line and the buildings (the clerk's office, the hall, the stores).
- **The market:** two stalls at its ends, shut for the night.

**What changed.**
- **The market** (`TownGenerator.Market`; towns.json `square.market`, `marketPer`, `marketIn`, `marketEvery`):
  - **Where:** a row of stalls lit and open, 3.6 m in front of the buildings' fronts, between them and the centrepiece.
  - **Each stall:** stands where it's clear of everything in the square (the benches, the barrels, the centrepiece, the green's things) and of the buildings' doors, at least 4.6 m from the next.
  - **How many:** four in a small town, one more for every 400 people, up to eight, as many as there's room for.
  - **Which places:** drawn from the clear ones on its own stream (`market`), laid in order along the square.
- **What's on them:**
  - the town's trade's own stalls (industries' `stalls`, none that a shut stall has);
  - the market's own (world/towns.json `market`): the fish stall (every fish's eyes taken out), the candle stall (nobody lights one after midnight), the lamp-oil stall (the book of who bought it), the shoe stall (single ones, never a pair), the knitting stall (a name in every hem), the clock stall (a quarter of an hour fast), the bread stall (a loaf put by for whoever's coming in on the train), the tea stall (the cups on strings), the old things stall (keys to doors in towns that aren't there), the mask stall.
  - None twice in a town.
- **A stallholder behind each** (role `stallholder`, bynames "{first} the Stall" and "{first} Penny"):
  - facing the square, with eight lines of their own;
  - at their post as the stores' keeper is, away once in the middle of the night (`TownRounds`).
- **Nothing else moves:** the stalls are listed after every other fixture but the forge, and the stallholders after every other townsperson but the blacksmith and the Flame's keeper (last of everyone, note 697), so every other fixture keeps its index and every townsperson their name and place; those two keep their place (`TownMarketTests`, `BlacksmithTests` hold it).
- **The art** (`SquareKit` "marketstall"):
  - a counter heaped with its wares in crates, jars and sacks, and a shelf at the back with more;
  - four posts and a striped canvas awning, red, green or blue on cream by where it stands;
  - a lantern under its edge, lit (`WorldArt.Square`: its light on the counter and the ground in front);
  - a slate on its post.
- **Solid and lookable:** each stall is a `TownFixture` (1.4 × 1.2 × 2.7 m), so it's solid, and it says what it is.

**Measured.**
- **`TownMarketTests`** (frontier:7 at 97, local:3 at 1200, a harbour town at 140):
  - between four and eight stalls, in the square's open ground;
  - none on anything else, none in front of a door, none twice;
  - a stallholder behind each, facing the square, most of them talking about their stalls;
  - a bigger town has a bigger market;
  - with no market every other fixture and townsperson is as it is with one;
  - it's the same every time.
- **`TownMarketArtTests`:** a stall inside its box (its awning out over the counter), cheap, its lantern under the awning's edge.
- **The town tests:** `TownTests`, `TownsfolkTests`, `WalledTownTests`, and the Game's `TownsfolkArt`, `TownTalk` and `WorldSound`, pass.

**Looked at:** `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --town market|marketover` (and `--survey`). Both views are new:
- **market:** from the square in front of the first stall, along the row;
- **marketover:** from over the square, the whole row lit.

**Not yet:**

- **Buying:** a stall's wares are to look at. Buying, if wanted, is the stores' and the blacksmith's way (F1, note 697).
- **Townsfolk at the stalls:** folk on their rounds stopping at a stall (`TownRounds`' places).
