Creatures, carries and players must respect the terrain and geometry").** T128 (note 273) put the Whistler's carry over the ground and its nest out of the hills, and T124 (note 274) stood the fortresses up. A survey of what still went through what (players, creatures, carries, balls, bodies; the fortress towns are B2's, #194) found, most visible first: the Hounds and Climbers running beside the train inside a tunnel's rock, in the air beside a bridge and inside a cutting's walls (`Enemy.WorldPosition`: rail + right × lateral, the land never asked); a crew walking through every yard building but the village's (sheds, the hero, the powerhouse, signal boxes, water towers, wells); anyone walking up a cutting's 2:1 wall or a cliff, and out of a tunnel through its lining onto the hill 18 m over it (`TerrainField.Ground` held the bore's floor to 4.5 m either side, the lining is 3.1); a Ribbit keeping the height it was put down at, and a Gaunt, a feral Grumbler and the Choir going straight through houses; the Whistler's run through a building on its way to the nest.
    - **The stops' buildings** (`StopWalls`, run.json `walls`): shut, each its footprint, are now the powerhouse, signal boxes, lamp rooms, water towers, lockups, prison cars and wells (a well's kerb `wellTopM` 0.85 high), beside T114's barns and outbuildings and B4's dead-town station, goods shed and derelicts (note 302); B4's open houses keep their walls, door and furniture (note 326).
