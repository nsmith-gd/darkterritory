728. **The sense table, and thrown things do what they are (K1, queue #462; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`, GDD App. F.6; and the playtest of 13 Oct evening, `docs/playtest/2026-10-13-evening.md` line 24).**
"I'm pretty sure I can take a fire extinguisher and use them on the cinder hounds, can't I? ... they still got me, even though I was using it on them. That should have worked." The friendslop audit (note 733) found the Hackers (Bartle's implicit explorers) underserved: most actions are prompts, not systems that answer back.

   - **What it was:**
     - The creatures ran on senses (App. A.1), but the crew's things spoke none of them: a noisy toy fed the Choir only in the hands, the extinguisher only cooled fire, a lamp only lit a car.
     - A thrown lamp landed and lay there; only a powder keg had an impact rule (note 182's kegs).
   - **Now** (`content/tuning/senses.json`, `SenseTuning`; `World.Senses.cs`):
     - **One table, one query.** `items`: what each thing gives off, a sense, a radius and a strength (a voice's scale), negative for a masker. `World.Emitters(sense, at)` / `Loudest` / `Sprayed` read it: hand lamps (carried or set down), car lamps, noisy toys playing, the extinguisher's spray (Fire held, a cone `spray.coneDeg`), knocks on the rail, burning lamps, food finds (`foodFinds`), bodies. Built once a tick, in a fixed order.
     - **Toys play on:** wound in the hands, a music box (`toys.playsSeconds.musicBox` 90 s) or a drummer (30 s) let go keeps playing where it lies (`Body.Playing`, on the wire; the client's toy voice plays it lying). A squeaker sounds only squeezed.
     - **The Moose** (`answers.moose`): a toy louder than any voice it hears, inside its leash, walks it over to `standOff` m of it, listening (aggro held at `listenAt`, never past; a toy riles nobody); run down, it goes home. Searching for someone, a louder toy is where it searches. A lamp is nothing to it.
     - **The Choir** (`answers.choir.lyingToysCount`): a toy playing where it lies counts as carried, in the name of whoever last held it (`Body.LastHeld`), while it's within its reach of a living crewmate.
     - **The Gaunt** (`answers.gaunt`): a toy playing within `listenRadius` isn't silence (its anger holds) but doesn't talk it down (`toyTalksDown` false). Decided per App. A.6: it wants to be talked to; a tune is company, not conversation.
     - **Cinder Hounds** (`answers.cinderHounds`): sprayed aboard (in reach and cone, the same side of a car's walls), a hound lets go of whoever it holds, can't bite, and backs off down its car at `backOffSpeed`, keeping off `repelSeconds`; sprayed `driveOffSeconds` within `driveOffWindow`, it leaps off (`World.DrivenOff`). The charge goes only as the spray reaches something (once a tick). Weaker than a gun's suppression: it reaches only hounds aboard, and one backed out of reach must be followed.
     - **Fire Flies** (`answers.fireFlies`): a hand lamp lying lit in a car is that car's lamp (`World.CarLit`: the spawn and the break-off); one lying out in the world within `lureRadius` draws a swarm off the car (`lureSpeed`), and its igniting breaks that lamp into a ground burn, no car alight.
     - **Vibration:** anything heavy landing on the ground within `knock.railWithin` of a track's centre at `knock.speedFrom` m/s or more, and a lump of coal (note 717) landing there, knocks the rail (`World.KnockRail`, heard `knock.seconds`). The Car Hugger lurking (`answers.carHugger`) crawls to the knock and out off the formation's edge, and latches onto nothing while drawn (`drawnSeconds`). Tower Jaw (`answers.towerJaw`) with nobody near stops gnawing and rears at the knock `listenSeconds`. The Knotter and Hotbox answer the train's running, the Switchman a train on its switch: they ignore it.
     - **Thrown things** (`thrown`): a hand lamp brought up short at `lampBreakSpeed` (the change in its speed; the tick it's let go is the hands') breaks: on or in a car with a fire grid, its oil lights the cell nearest where it broke at `lampFireHeat` (`CarFire.Kindled` / `Kindle`; C.9 records the thrower); anywhere else it lies `Broken` and burning `groundBurnSeconds` (light and heat; not to be picked up; drawn as a puddle of flame). A powder keg thrown hard goes up by its own rule (facilities.json `kegs.blowSpeed`). Glass: the lamps are it; #453's fragile finds are #453's.
     - **No damage between crewmates** from a thrown thing. What can hurt is the world's own: a car fire a lamp started (the fire grid's burns), and a keg's blast.
     - **Wire:** `Messages.Version` 60 (from 59): the body record's ninth field carries a toy playing (noise + 0x100) and a lamp broken and burning (1).
   - **Checked:**
     - `SenseTableTests` (14): the table loads and says each sense; a music box lying 20 m off draws a grazing Moose over a quiet crewmate, and it goes home when it runs down; a drummer is where a riled Moose searches; a Moose ignores a lamp; the extinguisher on a hound in range backs it off without a bite and, followed, drives it off the train; a sprayed hound lets go of who it holds; aimed away, nothing (and no charge spent); a thrown lamp on a car floor starts a fire cell where it broke, one set down doesn't break; a thrown lamp on the ground burns 25 s and is gone; a keg thrown at a car's side goes up on landing, not on the way; a crate dropped on the rail knocks and draws a lurking Car Hugger off; a coal knock is heard its while; a music box set down plays on, on the Choir's meter in its last holder's name; a music box by the Gaunt holds its anger, run down it climbs.
     - Views `musicbox` (the box in the grass, the Moose stood over it) and `extinguisherhound` (the jet on a hound backing off down car 3's roof), rendered and looked at.
   - **Left for later:**
     - Sounds (AU1's): `rail-knock` (a ringing clank down the rail), `lamp-smash` (glass, and the oil going up with a whump), `lamp-burn` (a small oil fire's crackle, looping while `Broken`), `hound-doused` (a hiss and a yelp as the spray hits). A toy lying playing reuses `toy-musicbox` / `toy-drummer` (now played lying while `Body.Playing`).
     - Scent: food finds give it off, but no creature yet answers it (the Cinder Hounds' scent, App. A.3's food cargo, is the director's spawn rule, not a find's).
     - The Lodger and the Soot Children (sound) could answer a toy; their rules weren't touched.
     - The bots don't use any of it (throwing a box to the Moose, spraying a hound).
     - A flare: there isn't one in the game.
