Ribbits go for the nearest group smaller than their pack (App. A.6), and a hand alone at the points is a group of one.
   - Claimed 2026-10-20 by n1-w7 (nathaniel1's worker).
   - **The cause.** `Heed.Ribbits` (note 658): a bot on the ground with Ribbits within `crewBots.ribbitWithin` (12 m) turns and clubs the nearest. Against a pack sitting about, or with the count even, that's right. Alone, walking back to the train with a pack of three hunting it, the shunter turned on them and was tongued.
   - **The fix** (tuning flag `crewBots.ribbitsOutnumberedRun`, on). A bot on its way somewhere, hunted by a pack (its leader's mark) that outnumbers it and the crewmates on foot within the pack's `groupRadius`, runs on (App. A.6, "outrunnable, if you run": 5.5 m/s against the hop's 4) instead of turning, **when its way leads away from the pack**. Toward the pack or past it, it clubs as before. A first version that always ran (v1) lost seed 2's shunter: its way to its ladder ran past the pack, and clubbing it had killed one and got clear. Bot behaviour only: nothing on the wire, deterministic.
