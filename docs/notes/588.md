588. **Dawn: the Wakers (queue #321, D1; the director, 9 Oct 2026, approving D1's proposal: "The new Dawn ending design is excellent. This is exactly what I was looking for. The gargantuan creatures, the picking up of the train, especially with you in it, you should experience that if you're still playing. And then the tension before it is really, really important"). The design: docs/design/creatures/wakers.md.**
   - **What it replaces.** At dawn "the line is live" and, `run.json dawnGraceSeconds` (120 s) later, the night failed with nothing seen: the director's "run lost with nothing happening feels bad". With `enemies.json wakers.enabled` that timer is off (`Run.Step`), and the night ends when a Waker has eaten the train to the engine (`World.TakenAtDawn`), or its crew (`World.WakerHolds` with nobody alive). `RunEnd.DawnMissed` stays the value (saves, the harness, the balance reports); on screen it's "taken at dawn".
   - **The Sim** (`Enemies/Wakers.cs`, `World.Wake`). At dawn, with the train out (underway or at a facility), `count[tier]` of them are added, once a night. Each is a hazard (not the director's to spend, count or cap), sent to every client (`Far`), free on the engine's path. The first rises `riseBehind` behind the train's rear, and the rest `riseAside` off either side, `asideBack` further back, closing on the line over `joinSeconds`.
     - Rising (TELEGRAPH, `riseSeconds`), then running (COMMIT): a place that's a function of the seconds since it rose (`Waker.Covered`: `startSpeed` building evenly to `topSpeed` over `rampSeconds`), never past `stopShort` short of the terminus's gate.
     - The lead one at the last car takes the train (PUNISH). It drags it at `dragFactor` × the engine's pull (`Drags`, the Car Hugger's drag), and `Extra` counts the seconds it's held it.
     - From that: cars lifted (`Lifted`, one every `liftSeconds`) and eaten (`EatenCars`, from `eatAfter`, one every `eatSeconds`). Whoever's in an eaten car dies with it, and anyone off the train dies `groundSeconds` in (`DeathCause.Woken`, attributed to whoever had the controls).
     - No randomness and no pathing: the host and the clients agree, and a bot can reckon it.
   - **Drawn** (presentation only, as the tipple's tilt, note 423).
     - `WakerLift` raises the held cars in the drawn frames, from the rear. Each rear end is the sum of its own and every car's in front of it, so the lifted cars are a ramp up to its mouth; an eaten car is gone. The crew's eyes are drawn from those frames, so whoever's in a lifted car goes up with it, in first person.
     - The greybox stand-in (`GreyboxScene.DrawEnemy`): a hill's worth of back, its head down to its catch, arms up at the last car. It's drawn from any distance. Its model is asked of C1/E1 on the art checklist.
     - The HUD's dawn line says how far behind the lead one is, or that it has the train (`Hud.DawnLine`).
     - `dt screenshot --view waker|wakerlift|wakerinside` (`--waker chase|lift`, `--behind`, `--held`).
   - **The stir, felt** (`DawnStir`): the ground's thuds through the train in the last `stirSeconds` before dawn, a few seconds apart and growing, and after it as near and as quick as the lead Waker's stride (the eye only, scaled by the settings' CAMERA SHAKE).
   - **Not yet:** the rest of the stir (the cold line on the horizon behind; their calls and the thuds' sound, AU1's), the night's creatures going to ground in the last minute (the director's call: wakers.md §2), the town's guns on them at the walls, and what the company puts under a crew whose train was eaten (wakers.md §7, for the director).
   - **Verified.** `WakersTests`: a train standing at dawn is run down, lifted and eaten with its crew, and the night is lost "taken at dawn" (`DeathCause.Woken`). It's caught in 55–95 s, not before. Stood just outside the terminus's gate, they stop `stopShort` out and the crew is home. Their pace builds evenly to its top and holds. With the Wakers off (no enemies, a mod), `RunTests` keeps v1's timer.
