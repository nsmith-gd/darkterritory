390. **Credits for what the game is built on (queue #127, B3; the checklist's `credits`; GDD App. E.6; CC BY's terms).** The CREDITS screen listed the opera alone (E.6: CC0 asks for no credit, the performers listed anyway). The build also ships CC BY models (Iman Aliakbar's Boy Room, Allay Design's Interior Scene, Eric Chadwick's two Wayfair pieces, Lee Perry-Smith's head scan, note 58), CC BY voices (LibriTTS by way of Piper, note 240), Three D Scans' scans, CC0 textures and sounds, fonts rendered into textures, and MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD and zlib code. None of it was credited, in the game or beside it, and CC BY and MIT need the credit.
    - **From the provenance, not a list kept by hand** (`Credits`, Game): the models are the sources with an id in `art/textures/index.models.json`, each credited by its own attribution string, whole (title, author, where, licence, as the source asked). The voices are a sample cue's licence that names its work ("CC BY 4.0: LibriTTS, Zen et al. 2019") in `audio/samples/index.json`, with the cues that speak through it. The sound packs are the part of a source before its ':', with how many cues use each. The textures are `art/textures/index.json`'s authors, and the type its fonts with what they letter. What ships is what's credited: a new model or pack comes in with its line.
    - **What the indexes can't say** is in `content/credits/credits.json`: each section's line (changed work says so, as CC BY asks), the packs' names (`earlier`, a cue's own earlier take, is the game's own and not credited), every licence by the name a source gives it (a display name, where to read it, its text when one must ship, whether it's owed), and the code that ships (DarkTerritory.App's packages and what they carry: SDL 3, shaderc, Opus, the Steamworks SDK).
    - **The screen:** after the opera, a heading a section (THE MODELS, THE SOUNDS, THE TEXTURES, THE TYPE, THE CODE), greyed so the selection steps over it, its line under it in faint print, then a row a credit with its licence, source and what has it; the owed first. The selected credit is shown whole under the list, broken across as many rows as fit above the hints (a Commons address too long for a row is broken rather than cut).
    - **THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt** (`content/credits`, generated by `dt credits --write`): the same in plain text, each licence's address, and the texts the code's licences ask to be carried (MIT's under its holders' copyright lines; Apache 2.0, Opus's BSD and zlib whole). The content carries it into every build and `tools/package.sh` puts a copy at the top of the folder.
    - **Kept true by tests** (`CreditsTests`): every licence a source names is described in credits.json; every sourced model is credited by its attribution, and a CC BY one is owed; every voice whose licence names its work has its line; every PackageReference in src/ is in the code; the notices are current. `dt credits` lists them (JSON), `--notices` prints the file, and `dt screenshot --menu credits --down n` shows the screen scrolled.
    - **Found on the way (CI):** main's `dotnet format` had been red since #367 (`dt screenshot --shed`'s else body unindented: fixed). Windows CI then failed `CaptionsTests.TheHudWritesThemAtTheFootOfTheFrame`, which compares two HUD builds vertex by vertex, on two races between parallel tests: every `Hud.Build` wrote the static prompt scale another thread's build was reading (now `[ThreadStatic]`, read through `Fine`), and HudPaletteTests, QuietHudTests and FrontEndTests set the static `Hud.Keys` (the palette, the corner's hints) while it built (those four classes now share xunit's `Hud.Keys` collection, so they don't run at once).
    - **Not done:** a mod's own assets credit themselves (the credits are the base content's). The code's licences are read from each package's nuspec as of this change; a version bump that changes a licence needs credits.json changed by hand (the test catches a new package, not a changed licence).
