674. **Town flags: the cloth in the wind, and a flag for every town (E1, queue #405; the director, 13 Oct, playing the morning build; the World Building board's note `pt13-flags`).**
"The flag on the flagpole is cute, but it needs to be waving if it's going to be there. And we need to do a series of flag designs for the towns."

**What it was.** The square's flag (note 353) was two quads in the flagpole's cooked piece, held out stiff whatever the night did: the town's blue with an amber lamp, the same in every town.

**The cloth** (`Art/FlagKit.cs`).
- It's a 12 × 4 grid, both faces, skinned to a chain of six bones from the hoist to the fly. `WorldArt.Civic` draws it from the pole's ring each frame (`MeshBuilder.Skinned`); the pole's piece keeps only its ring and cleat.
- `FlagKit.Pose` poses the chain from the world's wind, the same `WindTuning` that bends the foliage (look.json `atmosphere.wind`: calm 2 to full 16 m/s from one quarter, gusting). How the cloth behaves is look.json `atmosphere.flag`:
  - **It turns downwind.** In a calm it keeps wherever the wind last blew, wandering a little.
  - **It lifts out of its hang** as the wind rises past `liftFrom` (1.5 m/s), and flies nearly level by `liftFull` (10 m/s).
  - **Slack, the fly hangs** `droop` (1.9 rad at the fly) down the pole, and the hang breathes on its own slow swing. So a calm night's flag hangs; it isn't held out.
  - **A ripple runs out from the hoist,** nothing at the pole and widest at the fly. It snakes side to side (`ripple` 0.22 calm to `rippleFull` 0.6 rad) and, flying, billows up and down in its own plane (`flutter` 0.22). It runs faster in a stronger wind and in the gusts (`pace` 2.2 to `paceFull` 10 rad/s, `waves` 1.6 on it at once), and the fly flicks. It's never still, and never a board.
  - Each town's flag is out of step with the next by its name.
- The screenshot tool's `--wind w` now reaches the scene too (`GreyboxScene.Wind`, `StagedWind`), so a still frame's flag flies in the wind asked for.

**The designs** (`tools/art/texgen/mat_flags.py`, the `town_flags` texture; `tools/art/flags.py`). One 1024 atlas, a hero layer, with a cell for every custom (towns.json `cultures`, 14) in every trade (`industries`: coal, farm, foundry), 42 in all, 3:2, sewn the way a walled town would sew one from what it had:
- **The field is the trade's:**
  - a pit town's coal black with the lamp's yellow along its foot;
  - a growing town's green over the ochre of turned furrows;
  - a foundry town's oxide red bound in iron grey.
- **The canton is the old province's, the same on every flag:** New Scotland's blue saltire on white, as the engine's lamp carries it (TrainKit), so the towns read as one country's.
- **The charge is the custom's,** cut from bone-white sheeting and stitched on. Each is the custom's own centrepiece:
  - the bell, its clapper bound (`hush`);
  - two iron rings (`pairs`);
  - the tally with one mark too many (`count`);
  - the shuttered lantern (`shutters`);
  - the reading horn (`talkers`);
  - the eye (`eyes`);
  - a toy horse (`offerings`);
  - the rule book open at 9 (`rulebook`);
  - the banked brazier and its bent poker (`banked`);
  - the white line and a left boot (`crown`);
  - a coat on its peg, its back seam out (`backs`);
  - four lamps, one hook empty (`fours`);
  - the bitten van (`lastCar`);
  - the back gun (`backGun`).
- **All of it weathered:** faded toward the fly, a patch or two sewn into the field, rain-stained, sooted, and the fly frayed into tongues (alpha-tested).
- **Provenance:** the texture's index.json entry (procedural, CC0) lists every cell. `tools/art/flags.py` writes `content/art/flags.json` (which cell is whose, its charge and field) and, with `--sheet`, a labelled contact sheet.
- **The index's order:** `textures.py --only town_flags` rewrote index.json in its registry's order, which isn't the file's. The entry was appended by hand to keep every other layer where it was.

**The pick** (`FlagBook.For`):
- A town flies the flag for its custom (`TownPlan.Culture`) in its trade (`TownPlan.Industry`, by the trade's name in it).
- A custom or trade the series hasn't got yet takes one from what it can, chosen by the town's name (FNV-1a, its seed's). So the same town always flies the same flag, and no change was needed in B2's `TownGenerator` or towns.json (P1, carrying B2's towns, told on #906).

**Seen.** `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --town flag` (across the square) and `--town flagclose` (side on to it, across look.json's wind), with `--wind 0|0.5|1` and `--scene-time` for its motion. `python3 tools/art/flags.py --sheet out/flags/sheet.png` makes the contact sheet. The shots are on the World Building board, the checklist's `wind` line and the Look Review.

**Tests** (`FlagTests`):
- every custom in every trade has its own flag, inside the atlas;
- a town's flag is fixed by its name;
- the cloth is skinned along its chain;
- in a calm the fly hangs below 60% of its length down the pole, and in a gale it's out downwind three quarters of its length and about level;
- in any wind it moves in a quarter of a second.
