967. **The Look Review rebuilt for verdicts (Sage, queue #667; the director, 21 Oct).**
   - The ask, in the director's words: "The Look review artifact is not set up well for me to give notes on latest, approve, mark for change, or reject (redo)."
   - The item: COORDINATION.md row #667.
   - **What the director sees** (the rebuilt page). One card per subject (63) and per model (25): the subject's newest
     shot large, the rest of that round beside it ("Also in this round"), every earlier round folded under "History ·
     N earlier shots from M rounds". Under the newest shot: **Approve**, **Change…** and **Redo…** (both need a note),
     the latest verdict with who and when, "New since your verdict" when a newer shot has come in after one, Undo for
     their own verdict, the card's checklist lines, its open notes and its round log. Every clip has its own three
     buttons. Filters at the top of Captures and Animations, with counts: Awaiting you (the default), Changes asked,
     Redo, Approved, All; the index chips show each card's state and follow the filter; a link to a filtered-out shot
     shows All so it lands. The notes boxes, the before/after sliders, the Log tab and deep links are as they were.
   - **How it's built.** The page's markup is unchanged in kind: rounds still add `<article class="shot">` entries, newest
     first; a script (`tools/review/look_review/verdict.js`) makes the cards when the page loads, so a round posted the
     old way shows up as the new latest by itself. Verdicts are rows in the page's db, collection `verdicts` (the `db` and
     `user` capabilities it already declares; COORDINATION.md's "Look Review verdicts" has the fields and how art agents
     act on them).
   - **Light.** The live page was 15.5 MB (132 inline images) in 508 of its 512 files. `tools/review/look_review.py`
     re-packs every still (the 13 old packs, the 132 inline images and the 288 `stills/*.jpg`) into two packs per
     subject (its newest round, and its History, cut at about 3 MB), fetched as a card nears the screen: the page is
     1.26 MB, a card's newest shot comes with one fetch of 0.1 to 1 MB, History's packs only when it's opened, and the
     version holds about 330 files. `--ui` puts the cards' CSS and script in (or brings them up to date); from the live
     version 1792535213-d458 it rebuilds the page byte for byte, and a second run changes nothing.
   - **Checked** headless (Chromium, a stand-in db): the cards, the filters' counts, a Change sent with its note (the
     row as written: subject, shot, round, verdict, note, checklist, author, at), its card moving to Changes asked, a
     stale verdict showing "New since your verdict", History's images loading when opened, the model and clip verdicts;
     no sideways scroll at phone width (the live page had one: wide images in grid figures, fixed).
   - **Handoff:** built, not published. The publish to the Look Review (three calls: half the new packs with the live
     page unchanged; the new page with the rest; then removing the 301 old packs and stills) was refused by the session's
     permission check (a publish to another owner's artifact), so it waits for a session allowed to publish it. The
     steps are in `tools/review/look_review.py`'s docstring: a fresh `read`, the stills saved, the tool with `--ui`, the
     publishes. The verdict round trip through `ArtifactData` waits on that publish too.
