290. **The guns' effect on creatures: every body a ball can meet stops it, and a ball lands as a blow (GDD App. F.1, build 1121: "Bug: the guns do nothing. Rounds don't collide where they land and have no visible effect on the monsters"; queue #28, D1).** Note 171 gave four creatures a hit volume (Cinder Hounds on the line, the Switchman at his lever, Climbers in the open, the Track Doll on the rail), each a single sphere centred at the feet, sized in code. The rest had none "by design", so a ball laid on a Gaunt, a Grumbler, a Ribbit pack or a Whistler carrying a crewmate went straight through it and came down on the ground behind. A ball that did find one of the four, and didn't kill it, did nothing at all in the sim.
    - **Bodies.** `enemies.json` `bodies`: every creature's body as a stack of spheres, each [radius, height over where it stands], fitted to its model as drawn (`content/art/models`; the Ribbit at its 1.4 scale). A tall thing is three or four spheres up its height (the Switchman 1.8 m, the Gaunt 3.5 m), so a ball laid on its shoulders no longer passes over a sphere at its feet. A long, low one (a hound, a Climber, the Whistler) is one sphere round its middle. A sleeping Gaunt curls to `gauntAsleep` (0.35) of its height. The body stands while the creature is in the open, `Enemy.Exposed`: by default wherever a tool's blow can land on it (`MeleeRadius > 0`), so what the crew can club, the gun can find. The exceptions: hounds only loose on the line (aboard, the car is round them; unchanged), Climbers on the roofs or at the gap (unchanged), the Switchman revealed at his lever, the Track Doll on the rail with `cannonShatters` (unchanged), the Car Hugger and the Gaunt whenever they're about (a great body by the line; asleep in a village). No body: the Fire Flies (a ball goes through a swarm) and the Choir's ghosts (every round still feeds the meter). A real child among the Soot Children never has one; a Soot Child only once it's shown itself. `World.Targets` holds every sphere (`Enemy.Body`), lag-compensated as before. The gun's arcs, the train's own body and the land still decide what a ball can reach, so the flank (Draggers under the cars, a Whistler folded in its gap) is still "no cannon answers" (§21), and anything inside a car is behind its walls. `HitRadius`/`HitHeight` in code and `trackDoll.railHitRadius`/`railHitHeight` are gone.
    - **A ball lands as a blow.** `Enemy.Hit(ctx, by, damage)` is now the creature's own rule for a blow (`Struck`) by the gunner, of `combat.json` `guns.damagePerRound` (4 of a tool's 1): hurt (a ball takes a Ribbit, a Follower, a Whistler or a Soot Child; two take a Gaunt; a Grumbler heals between one gunner's shots, so only a ball and a crewmate's blows together kill it), a crewmate held by it freed as a friend's blow frees them (a ball on a Whistler running off with someone, or a Grumbler mauling them), and a Grumbler struck by a ball goes feral on the gunner (App. A.8: "hunting whoever hit it last"). The Track Doll is still shattered, the hounds still killed or driven off by the rounds. Whether each creature can be killed at all, and by how much, stays with each creature's health and with queue #25 (A1's "driven off by their rules, killable by a coordinated team"): the ball rides whatever rule a blow has.
    - **The gunner bot** lays only on what the gun answers (`Enemy.GunAnswers`: the four of note 171) or on anything holding a crewmate, to free them. It never fires at a Gaunt asleep in a field or a Ribbit pack minding its own business, so the Choir's meter isn't fed for nothing.
    - **Seen.** A ball on a creature now throws its insides (`Effects.Ichor`): a dark cloud at the wound, a spray out the far side along the ball's way and a third of it back at the gun, arcing down to spatter the ground under it (`GreyboxScene.Strikes` passes the ground), and a splash there that lies as long as the scorch. Near-black blood for most, a Cinder Hound's embers and ash, soot for a Soot Child and the Stoker; the doll's porcelain as before. A ball on a wall leaves its scorch on the wall's face (`WallDecal`), as one on the ground always did. The hit flash, the flinch, the creature's hit clip and the gunner's marker (note 171) now play on every creature a ball can find. `dt screenshot --impact creature:gaunt --impact-at 60,2 --impact-age 1.5 --view ahead` stages one; `--impact structure` the wall's.
    - **Not yet:** a ball on the train's own body leaves no scorch (its impact is in the world, the car moves on). Per-creature pain sounds for a ball belong to the audio chat; a ball plays the generic hit cue as before.
    - **Verified:** `HitConfirmTests.EveryCreatureABallCanFindConfirmsTheHit` (all 15 kinds with a body, staged on the line ahead: the ball lands on the body, within its reach of its feet), `ABallGoesThroughASwarmAndAGhost`, `EveryBodyIsUprightOverWhereItStands`, `ASleepingGauntIsCurledUpLowAndWakingItStandsTall`, `ABallOnAGrumblerMaulingACrewmateFreesThemAndTurnsItOnTheGunner`; `CreatureBodyTests` (each body inside its model, up its height); the Sim suite (NetcodeTests included); screenshots of the ichor at 0.15, 0.5, 1.5 and 5 s and the wall's scorch looked at.
