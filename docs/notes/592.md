592. **The Pickers: scavengers that race the crew for a yard's loot (G1, queue #315; the director, 9 Oct 2026: "other stuff that is more frequently active in the yard", and of G1's proposal, "The picker is interesting. I think that's up there for something that we should prototype and put in"; design `docs/design/creatures/pickers.md`; GDD §21).**
    - **The stop's own, not the director's** (`Sim/Enemies/Pickers.cs`: `Picker`, and `Picking` the watcher, once a second on the host in the director's beat). A train standing at a facility with loose crates within `facilityReach` brings one group up `after` seconds in, `count` by tier and `perTwoCrew` more for every two of the crew past two. They're never `Director.Engaged`: they cost the caps nothing, and the linger rule leaves them be.
    - **Their drains** are read from the stop's own yard sheds (`Picking.Drains`): up to `drains`, `drainOff` out from a shed's wall on the track's side, settled where one of them standing there would be put (`Solidity.Clear`, new: `Settle`'s walls-and-land pass for a point), so one can always get back to it. No stop generator change, so no layout or seed moves.
    - **What each does:** the nearest crate on the ground nobody's guarding (a living crewmate within `guardWithin`) and no other Picker has; carried over its head (`Bodies.TakeAlong`) to its drain, where the crate's removed and `World.PickersTook` counts it; up again `downSeconds` later. A heavy crate waits for a second (`Heave`). Unladen, anyone within `shyWithin` sends it down its drain `shySeconds`; laden, a crewmate within `biteWithin` is bitten (`EnemyContext.Bite`, never a kill) and it drops the crate and scatters. A blow kills one and scatters those within `scatterWithin`.
    - **They go home** when the train's left the stop: the facility changes, not the train moving (a crew shunting the yard is still at the stop: the first pass sent them down on any move, and in a bot night they all left as the crew set back). Gone after `leaveSeconds` whether or not they reached a drain.
    - **Replicated** as any loose enemy: `Extra` the crate, `Extra2` the drain, `Height` the mode. Protocol 50 (with the house creatures, notes 583–586: five enemy kinds).
    - **Verified:** `PickersTests` (7): the nearest unguarded crate down the drain; a guarded crate never taken; a heavy crate takes two; the bite never kills and drops the crate; a blow kills one and scatters the rest; the train going sends them all down; deterministic. A frontier:7 bot night (seed 2) brought a group up at the first facility 20 s after the stand; the bot crew had loaded and gone before they took anything.
    - **Not yet:** the bots don't answer them (their crate hands load around them); their art is G1.10's.
