    - **The live crew** (`Director.Census`, once a second with the director's think; `Director.Active`): the crew alive in the night now. Until anyone's been seen it's the crew the night was planned for.
    - **The budget** (`liveCrew`): App. B.1's crew multiplier, `0.7 + 0.12 × crew` to 1.6, now takes `Active`, so `Director.Budget` is live.     - **The engaged cap** (`on`; `Director.EngagedCap`): the least of App. B.1's flat cap (4, or 6 at crew 6+), `ceil(active × engagedPerActive)` (0.75) and `perPlayer × active` (1), and at least 1. That's 1 at crew 1, 2 at crew 2, 3 at crews 3–4, 4 at 5, 6 at 8. The director holds "at the cap" on it, and the hound run (note 328) waits while the crew's at it, its count banked, so a crew of one never meets the run and the director's threat at once. The left-behind's hunts (note 273) and the Stoker (App. B.5, uncapped by design) stay outside it.
