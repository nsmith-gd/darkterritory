824. **Ruins: the beginnings of fortress towns the Wakers stamped flat, out beside the line (queue #551, B2; the World Building board's Cultures tab, the director, 12 Oct, "Beyond town walls near the lines": "Ruins of fortress towns that once were. They're always small beginnings of a fortress that were destroyed by the Wakers.").**

**What they are** (`RuinKit.FortRuin`).
- **The fortress that was begun:** new wall in the fortresses' own dark dressed stone (`StructureKit.Wall`'s), never finished to its height. Each mason's stretch stops at its own course, so the top steps.
- **What a Waker did to it** (GDD §9's titans, wakers.md): one came down the line at dawn and a foot came down on it.
  - A run of the wall is trodden into rubble, slabs thrown out from it.
  - The footprint is pressed into the ground across it: the earth shoved up round its heel and three toes, ten metres heel to toe.
- **Two of them:**
  - **A corner:**
    - the front wall along the line, broken in its middle;
    - a squat round tower at its end, sheared off higher at the back than the front, a chunk of its top lying beside it;
    - a side wall only begun, knee to shoulder high, ending in the stone waiting to be laid;
    - its scaffolding, the far third fallen in.
  - **A gatehouse:**
    - two tower stumps either side of where the arch was to go, a wall stub out from each;
    - the left stub stamped flat, the footprint across the gateway;
    - the gate's iron leaf, never hung, lying in the mud;
    - scaffolding round the right stump, and the shear legs that lifted its stone, a block still in the sling.
- **Cost:** about 1,700 and 1,500 triangles, under a large prop's budget (the art catalog's `fortruin-0`, `fortruin-1`).

**Where** (`content/linegen/biomes.json`, every country's `props`: `fortRuin`, 0.025 a 150 m block, 28–48 m out, one).
- **How often:** where there's room for one, about two nights in three. It's dealt as a building is (`LinesideProps`: its own stream, clear of the stops and the fortresses, room round it).
- **Facing:** its front wall faces the line.
- **Where it shows** (the nights' seeds): local:3 at 3985 (a gatehouse, 35 m out), local:3 at 6243 and frontier:3 at 7912 (a corner, 31 m out).

**Solid where it stands** (`LinesideFootprints` "fortRuin", `Shape.Cells`; `dt art footprints --write`):
- what it draws at a crewmate's height, cell by cell: its walls, towers, rubble, slabs and footprint's ridge;
- open where it's trodden through, so a crew can walk in by the breach.

**Measured.**
- **`FortRuinTests`** (nine nights' lines):
  - ruins stand beside the line, 28–48 m out;
  - never on a stop's ground or inside a fortress;
  - each stands more than twenty walls;
  - between two and one a four kilometres;
  - the same every time.
- **`LinesidePropsTests` and `LinesideFootprintTests`** still pass, with the file written.
