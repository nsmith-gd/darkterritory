   - **Before:** a person's temperament (note 474's matrix) only chose their byname and their lines. Their round was their
     role's (note 353), and the talk card typed everyone at towns.json `typePerSecond`.
   - **Rounds** (`TownRounds.Away`, towns.json `rounds.temperaments`, `RoundTuning.Temperaments`). On a slot away from their
     post, a temperament's pull comes first, at its `chance`. Its `to` targets are tried in turn (comma-separated):
     - `gate`: stood inside the town's gate (`TownBounds.Gate`, 2.5 m in, 4.5 m off the line) looking out down it;
     - `wall`: the nearest of the walls, 2.5 m in, looking out, five places along it tried;
     - `lamps`: down their own street to a lamp, mending (as the lampman walks the line's);
     - a kind of the square's places (`garden`, `vigil`, `warm`, `read`, `work`, `mend`, by key or act);
     - `talk`: a word with somebody.

     A place needs room, a free spot and a clear straight way within 40 m (outdoors has no pathing, so a garden behind houses
     goes unvisited); otherwise the slot goes as anyone's does. It applies to everyone but the gatekeeper and the porter, so a
     watcher guard leaves their beat for the wall.
     - Tuned: restless to the gate 0.6; watcher to the wall 0.6; mender to the garden then the lamps 0.6; mourner to the vigil
       0.5; faithful to the vigil 0.35; comforter to a fire 0.5; talker to a word 0.6; frayed to a fire 0.4.
     - Deterministic: each slot's stream as before (`rounds`, name, slot). It's the plan's, so every machine has the same rounds.
   - **The talk card** (`TalkVoice` in TownContent.cs, towns.json `voices`, `TownContent.VoiceOf`). A temperament's lines type
     at `rate` × the town's pace, with a pause after a comma or dash (`comma`) and after a sentence's end (`stop`), and in bursts
     of `burst` characters with `burstPause` between. The values:
     - frayed: quick bursts of 9 with 0.35 s stalls;
     - hollow: half pace, 0.5 s at commas, 0.9 s at stops;
     - mourner: slow;
     - talker: quick;
     - watcher: measured;
     - and lighter touches for the rest.

     `TownTalk` types the card by it and keeps it up for the voice's whole line plus `lingerSeconds`. A pure function of the line
     and the time, presentation only.
   - **Tests** (TownsfolkTests):
     - `TheirRoundsGoByTheirTemperaments`, over six towns (7 watcher stops at walls, 2 restless at the gate, 14 menders
       mending): nobody but watchers and the restless out at the wall, and the same plan twice gives the same rounds;
     - `TheirLinesTypeInTheirTemperamentsVoice`: hollow is slowest, talker quicker than plain, frayed in bursts, every voice
       gets the whole line out monotonically, and `VoiceOf` gives their temperament's.
