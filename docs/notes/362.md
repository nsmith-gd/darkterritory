362. **The Mourners: the ones that come for your dead (G1, queue #99; the director's brief of 8 Oct 2026; design `docs/design/creatures/mourners.md`; GDD §21, A.6, B.6; spec B.14).**
    - **Not the director's.** A body (a ragdoll, not a disconnect's) lying off the train (not carried, not taken, not in a car the engine pulls) for `after` seconds brings a group (`Mourning`, on the host, once a second): `count` by tier, each its own `Mourner` loose in the world, the first id the group's lead (`Extra2`). They cost nothing and are never `Engaged` (`Director.Engaged`): they hunt no one.
    - **Each decides from the same state, in id order.** Shy of the living (`shy`, startled to `startleTo`), the nearest to an unwatched body takes it (`Bodies.TakeAlong`, the spine's commit after the long telegraph of their coming) and hauls it straight off the nearest track; its partner hauls beside it. A crewmate within `dropWithin`, or a blow on any of them, and it's dropped; a blow kills one (`health` 1) and scatters the rest. Hauled `lostAt` from the track the body's removed (`World.MournersTook`): no refund at the report (`Run`'s `bodiesHome`), its kit gone with it. A body in a car the engine pulls sends them off.
    - **Verified:** `MournersTests` (8).
