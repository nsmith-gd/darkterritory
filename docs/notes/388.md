388. **G1's six new creatures heard, ahead of their sims (AU1, queue #125; G1's #99–#104, notes 362–367, and its nine lines on the audio checklist).** G1 claimed six creatures on 8 Oct and asked for their sounds on the audio checklist before any of them landed. As for the Moose (#73), the candidates come first, from G1's requests, and each creature is hooked off its record, tested and installed as its sim lands. Until then nothing plays, and the lines stay at L0.
    - **The candidates** (52 for 25 cues: `tools/audio/recipes/g1_ground.py`, `g1_train.py`; `cues.py` "queue #125"):
        - **The Mourners:** a keening of several thin voices, each on its own wavering line and never in unison, 600 Hz–2.5 kHz. Synthesised small throats, or door-hinge squeals held into tones. That keeps it apart from the Choir's swelling chorus and the Soot Children's call. Startled, dry clicks. A body dragged in tugs over gravel, under breathless keening.
        - **Tower Jaw:** a deep chiselling into a timber leg, chunks splitting off, held at 200 Hz–1.3 kHz so it's never the Grumbler's 1.4–2.2 kHz gnaw. The tower creaks, then groans in its last 5 s, then a crack like a shot echoes off the stop. Its tail slap, its lunge (a snarl and the incisors), and a water tower crashing across the line.
        - **The Freight Beetle:** a crate shoved in surges with each tripod of its legs, split by surface (`ground`, `wood`). Startled, its plates clatter and it hisses.
        - **The Brakeman:** a brake wheel's pawl ratcheting on (1.5–5 kHz). The shoes' steady binding squeal, which never catches once a wheel turn the way the hot box's does. Iron-shod limping steps, the good leg's takes first and the bad leg's after, and a chain dragged on the roofs. Cornered: a wheeze through a crushed throat, the chain hauled up, and its lash.
        - **The Knotter:** wet rope creaking in surges at a coupling, three in its 4 s warning. Then the long stretch, the buffers parting and clanks running down the train. Taut, the hawser's creak on a jerk.
        - **Hotbox:** a dull knock at 150–900 Hz, one take a knock for the game to fire once a wheel turn. Never the hot box's 1–3 kHz squeal (`state-hotbox`, note 358). The glow is the knock gone ragged and wet with a sizzle; seized, a long grinding scrape.
    - **When they land:** each hook goes in `GameAudio.Creatures` off what the clients are sent, as G1's notes describe it, and AU1 asks on G1's PR for any event a sound needs. The Brakeman's steps limp only if the good-leg and bad-leg takes alternate on a long-short beat, so the hook fires them that way, or the cue splits in two.
    - **Not yet:** the hooks, tests and installs; the director's Keep or Redo; and real chain, rope and voice recordings (Sonniss, when the network allows).
