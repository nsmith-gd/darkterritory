Open for the audio chat: the clerk's words, below.*
   - **The split.** §7.3's five crew tools come in two PRs: [#1874](https://github.com/nsmith-gd/darkterritory/pull/1874) builds 1-3 (the cord, the telltale, fusees); the stretcher and the roof gangplanks follow in a second PR on the same row (`n1-t3-crew-tools-2`). Each tool takes a share of a trouble, never all of it (upgrades.md §2), and each has its three §8.1 tells: a thing on the train, a sound and a word at the moment, a line in the tally.
   - **The communication cord** (campaign.json `commCord`: a small tool, 0.15 of the next car, from Frontier, the trouble being a long train's cab that can't hear its van, §15):
     - *Use.* The whistle's key [H], pressed aboard any car of the engine's rake behind the engine, pulls it. In the cab the key is still the whistle. On the engine outside the cab, or in a car cut off the train, there's no cord to pull.
     - *What it does* (`World.PullCord`, host): the world record's `Cord` (car, who, tick) is set, so every machine's cab gong strikes the car's number of times (train.json `kit.cordBell.strokes`, 9 at most, `strokeSeconds` 0.55 apart) and the HUD says "THE CORD: PULLED IN CAR 4 (DAVE)" for `showSeconds` (8). One pull a crewmate every `every` (3 s).
     - *The share.* It says which car, never why.
     - *Its tells.* The cord runs along every car's right-hand eave through iron eyes, over each coupling to the car ahead, and along the engine to the cab. Each car has a red T pull. Its gong is a small brass dome high on the cab's right wall (`TrainKit.CordLine`, `CordSpan`, `CordGong`). The sound is `up-tells.cord-bell`, a synth: a strike at 820 Hz, lower, rounder and slower than the overspeed bell's two 1.2 kHz strokes, so the two are never mistaken. The clerk is to read "Cord: pulled, 3." or "Cord: not pulled." (for now a placeholder, below).
   - **The end-of-train telltale** (`trainTelltale`: a small tool, 0.15, from Local, since the Passenger is the first night's trouble):
     - *What it does* (`Telltale.Lit`): the lamp is lit while the train's tail (its highest-numbered car of the night's train; no yard car, no handcar) is in the engine's rake, and out the moment it isn't. It lights again once the train is coupled whole. It's worked out from the rakes, which every machine has, so it needs nothing on the wire.
     - *The share.* It says the train has parted, not where. A second parting while it's out shows nothing more.
     - *Its tells.* A brass-bezelled bullseye lettered TRAIN at the end of the dials' row over the front glass: green and glowing while lit, dark red glass when out (`TrainKit.TelltaleLamp`). In the cab the HUD says "THE TELLTALE'S OUT: THE TRAIN'S PARTED BEHIND YOU". As it goes out, `up-tells.telltale-out` plays: a relay dropping out, a small click heard in the cab. The host counts each time it goes out, and the clerk is to read "Telltale: out, 2." or "Telltale: lit all night." (for now a placeholder, below).
   - **Fusees** (stores, like the flare pistol, note 871; campaign.json `stores.fusees`: 40 scrip a box, 2 a night at most, 4 fusees each, from Local, under a spare lamp's 60 since they burn out):
     - *Aboard.* The boxes are in the guard van beside the flare pistols (`BodyKind.Fusees`, the fusees left in `Charge`).
     - *Use.* Fire [LMB] with the box in hand strikes one and drops it at the feet (`World.StrikeFusee`; one a crewmate every `kit.fusee.every`, 1 s). A fusee dropped off a moving car goes down with the car's speed.
     - *The burn.* `BodyKind.Fusee` burns `kit.fusee.burnSeconds` (180 s), its burn left in `Charge` for every machine, then it's gone. Nobody picks a burning one up.
     - *The share and the cost.* It's a light (senses.json `fusee`: light 25 m at strength 0.4, further than a hand lamp's 12 but fainter, far under the flare's 60), so whatever answers light answers it. It marks the way back; it doesn't lead anyone there.
     - *Its tells.* A red waxed-card box lettered FUSEES. A red stick lying in the dirt, with its flame, red glare and lit smoke (SceneArt) and a red practical light out to 12 m (`GreyboxScene.FuseeReach`). The sounds are `up-fusee.strike` (scrape, sputter, whoomp) and `up-fusee.burn` (a hiss and sputter held on it, under the flare's roar and the car-fire tell's band). The clerk is to read "Fusees: struck, 4." on a night some were struck (for now a placeholder, below).
   - **The stretcher** (`stretcher`: a small tool, 0.15, from Local, since bodies home pay 75%, App. D; second PR):
     - *What it does.* With it fitted, anyone carrying a body gets `SoloCarry`, so they can take a ladder and climb it with the body. It's no longer only the last one alive (D.9). The climb is train.json `kit.stretcherCarry.climb` (0.8 m/s): twice the solo remainer's 0.4, half a free climber's 1.6 (`PlayerMotor.Climb`).
     - *Walking pace.* On the ground a body already goes at `carryHeavy` (2.8 m/s, over the walk's 2.4), so that half of the design is already true. The ladder is the stretcher's share.
     - *Its tells.* A canvas stretcher hangs on the last guard van's left wall (`TrainKit.Stretcher`). The HUD calls a carried body "A BODY, ON THE STRETCHER". `up-tells.stretcher` (poles creaking, canvas drawing taut) plays as a body is taken onto a ladder. The clerk is to read "Stretcher: carried up, N." (the bodies taken onto a ladder) or "Stretcher: unused." (for now a placeholder, below).
   - **The roof gangplanks** (`roofGangplanks`: a small tool, 0.15, from Frontier, since the gaps are the longer trains' roofs, GDD §13; second PR):
     - *What it does.* train.json `composition.gangplanks` (2) puts an iron plate (`CarShape.WithGangplank`, `PartKind.Gangplank`) on the roofs of cars 1 and 2. Each plate runs off the car's front over the coupling gap, `overlap` (0.3 m) under the roof ahead, `width` 0.8 m along the middle.
     - *Up to the engine.* Its roof is 0.4 m over car 1's, past the 0.35 m step, so car 1's plate ends in treads, none more than `tread` (0.2 m) over the last.
     - *Walked, not jumped.* Walked off car 2's front, a crewmate stays on the roofs all the way to the engine's.
     - *The share.* The rest of the train's gaps are jumped as ever. The plates are narrower than the roof, and a car cut off has none in front of it.
     - *Its tells.* Chequer-plate plates with angle-iron edges and a bright nosing (`TrainKit.GangplankPlate`). They sound as grate underfoot (`Footing`). The clerk is to read "Gangplanks: two, bolted." (for now a placeholder, below).
