356. **A coupling working loose, the third upkeep job while the train runs (queue #93, D1; GDD App. F.3, the director, 7 Oct 2026: "on the train, still relatively boring from point A to point B"; [orchestrator.md](design/orchestrator.md) §5.1 U2).**
    - **What it is.** Every `everyMetres` (6 km ± half) of the train's running, the pin in one of its gaps works loose: `Vehicle.Loose` on the car ahead of the gap, the seconds it's been at it. The metres count `roughFactor` (3) times over while the engine is on a bend tighter than `roughRadius` (800 m): the outline's "rough track". Never the engine's own coupling (that would be the whole train) and never behind the last car; none in the first 4 km, none in the yard or a fort, never more loose than there are crewmates alive. All in `upkeep.json` `coupling`; host only (`Couplings`), the vehicle record carries it (protocol 37).
    - **The tell.** The pin knocks in its knuckle (`coupling-loose`, a synth loop: a dull 340 Hz clank with a ring over it, 1.4 knocks a second at first, 5.5 at the end, louder as it goes), and the gap is called out as every break is (note 301's amber glow and sparks, `BreakKind.Coupling`, the sparks spitting up out of the gap). The prompt in the gap says TIGHTEN THE COUPLING, or the key that puts the wrench in hand.
    - **The fix.** The wrench in hand (bare hands where the wrench isn't the repair tool), Use held 4 s with the hands within 2.2 m of the pin: from the plate in the gap, an end ladder over it, or the ground beside. It comes before the plate's doors, so at a loose pin with the wrench in hand it's the pin that's worked; Uncouple is its own key and is untouched.
    - **Left alone.** After 90 s the pin drops and the rake parts behind it (`TrainOnLine.Uncouple`): §24's cut-car rules, what's behind rolls free at the speed it had, and the knuckle's release and the hoses parting are heard (`CrewCouplings`). The worst job, so the rarest: one a 24 km night on the flat, more on a night of bends.
    - **Bots.** A walker goes to it as to a hot box (`RoofWalkerBot.Grease`, now either job: along the roofs, down the end ladder into the gap), puts the wrench in hand and tightens it; any bot in reach does the same (`Heed.Coupling`).
    - **Verified.** `LooseCouplingTests` (7): one comes loose by the running, never the engine's nor behind the last car; a bend looses it a third as far on; left alone the rake parts behind it; the wrench tightens it in its time and the shovel doesn't; not from the roof nor inside; a bot in the gap puts the wrench in hand and tightens it; a three-bot crew's walker goes down into the gap and tightens one before it parts. `dt screenshot --view gapside --loose 60`, looked at; `dt audio render --sound coupling-loose --param loose=0.1` and `=0.9`, the spectrograms looked at (clean knocks, a peak near the hot box's).
