809. **The Mouse Moose heard (AU1, queue #537; A1's #1042, note 790's "Left: 2. Its sounds (AU1)").** The yard's casting thief was silent. It's heard off its record (`GameAudio.MouseMoose`, its `MouseMooseMode` in Height, as the Pickers are) and off the crane's castings, which replicate; its crane and hole are the host's, so nothing waits on them. A pest, heard as one: it never hurts anyone and nothing kills it, so it has no hurt or death.
- **The tell** (`tell-mouse-moose`): its claws held while it creeps in toward a casting (faster than 0.3 m/s flat: it steps), and a too-big rat's squeak about every 3-7 s while it does; one within the first second when it comes back out of its hole for another try, so the crew guarding that casting hear it's back.
- **The acts** (`cs-mouse-moose`):
  - `bite`: its teeth fastened on the casting, once, as it bites.
  - `drag` (a loop): held while it hauls the casting off, its level by its speed: 0.7 at the slow drag in the hook's reach, rising to full at `dragAway` (three times that) once the casting is `Taken`. Getting away with it sounds like it.
  - `bolt`: scared off (a crewmate within 6 m, or a blow), a squeal and claws scrabbling away.
  - `dig`: down its hole after a bolt; and where the train leaves the yard and it bolts home and is gone there (`Vanished`).
  - `lost`: a casting dragged all the way, tipping into the hole, off the casting's own record (Stacked or Taken to Lost, `GameAudio.LostDown` in `PlaceSite`), so it's heard whether or not a client still has the creature. A casting the hook sets down off the line (Hooked to Lost) is the crane's own `place-crane.load-set`, as before.
- **Candidates:** two for each of the seven cues (`tools/audio/recipes/mouse_moose.py`), on the Audio Checklist (new rows `tell-mouse-moose` and `cs-mouse-moose`, L1). Installed: the packs' real ones (cinders, a hinge's squeal shaped to a rat's, a tooth's iron tink, gravel, a squeal, earth, the thud with the weight); the modelled ones are candidates. All are well under the squish rule (the thud the most, 24% below 150 Hz).
- **Captions:** "CLAWS TICKING, SNIFFING", "A BIG RAT SQUEAKING", "TEETH SCRAPING ON IRON", "IRON DRAGGED OVER GRAVEL", "A SQUEAL, CLAWS SCRABBLING AWAY", "EARTH SCRABBLED", "IRON THUDDING INTO A HOLE".
- **Tests:** `CreatureSoundTests.TheMouseMooseCreepsInSqueakingBitesDragsAndBoltsForItsHole`, `WorldSoundTests.ACastingTheMouseMooseDragsDownItsHoleIsHeardTippingInAndOneSetDownOffTheHookIsNot`, and `CaptionsTests` (the squeak and the drag).
