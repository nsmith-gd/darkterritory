740. **The horn calls the bots aboard; the driver hears who's aboard (A1, queue #474; the director, 14 Oct, `docs/playtest/2026-10-14-second.md` lines 3 and 4).**
"When I pull the horn, the bots should understand that they need to get back on the train." "We need some way to signal to the driver that everybody is on the train. It would usually be vocal with players, but with bots, we need some sort of way of telling people."

   - **Why:** with two bots, the director had no way to call them back, and no way to know they were on. The horn only meant something to people. Bots left with the train when their own stop's work said to (note 574's Leaving), not when the driver did.
   - **GDD §17 first:** "The horn calls the crew aboard; 'Aboard!' is called back". Open question 2 stays verbal. A bot can now answer the roll call, but nobody is counted for you.
   - **The horn is the call** (`BotCrew.Think`, `CrewCalls.CallAboard`). The horn, pulled with the train standing (`CabControls.StandingBelow`) by someone playing (`WhistleBy` not a bot, not the Whistler's −1), sets `CalledAboard`. Every bot then reads `Leaving` as true, so the stop's walk-back (note 574) brings it aboard. `BotCrew.Decide` also drops the Holdouts' search and the six errands (houses, loot, the ground) while it holds. The call ends when the train moves off.
   - **"Aboard!" is an emote** (`Emote.Aboard`, the wheel's fourth spoke, up; `emotes.aboard` 3 s in player.json). A bot aboard after the call raises it once (`CrewCalls.CallBack`/`HasCalledAboard`). Someone playing uses the wheel. It wears the Wave pose and its sound.
   - **Protocol 60:** the intent's emote field had two bits, so a fourth emote needs one more byte. That byte is marked by the select byte's 0x30 bits. Emotes 0-3 are written as before.
   - **The roll** (`Hud.AboardLines`, `Hud.Aboard.cs`):
     - Everyone sees each call with its name: "JOE: ABOARD!".
     - In the cab, a horn at a stand starts a roll for `hud.aboardRollSeconds` (90 s): "ABOARD: JOE (1/2)" or "ABOARD: NOBODY YET (0/2)", then "ALL ABOARD" in green once every living crewmate has called.
     - A call before the horn doesn't count, and neither do the dead. The roll is kept per world, so a new night starts clean.
   - **Checked:**
     - `HornCallsAboardTests`: the horn at a stand calls the bots aboard and each calls back once; a bot on the ground comes aboard to it.
     - `EmoteAndOutfitTests`: every emote, Aboard included, round-trips the wire.
     - `AboardRollTests` (Game): the roll's lines and ALL ABOARD; nobody counted who hasn't called, nor the dead, nor a call before the horn.
