270. **One night length for every tier: 24 km, a 51 min dawn; the tiers differ in density, and quiet is counted in kilometres (the director's decision of 6 Oct 2026, GDD App. F.1 and §11, spec B.8; T125).** "The time a run takes should always be the same. Difficulty scales not by time but by monsters and density of challenges." Before this, T125 was only the GDD's lines and note 13's open design call: routes ran 18–40 km by tier (route.json 18–22 / 24–30 / 30–36 / 36–40; the line generator's columns 18 / 23 / 29 / 34 / 40 km, lerped by severity), so a Deep night was twice a Local one.
    - **The length** (route.json `nightLengthKm` 24, gate to terminus, read by the line generator's `Limits` whatever the tier or severity). The reading that keeps spec B.8's own table true: 24 km at the 14 m/s cruise is 28.6 min, the table's 28 min floor (no stop made), and the total run's 45 is that plus a few stops. It's also the length of the Frontier night the director has been playing (frontier:7 was 24.3 km).
    - **The dawn** (spec B.8: route ÷ 11 m/s + 40%). The generated lines' dawn read its own copy of the formula in linegen/tiers.json `conflicts` (`dawnAverageSpeed` 11, `dawnSlack` 0.18), which the 100-night playtest's change to +40% (route.json) never reached: frontier:7's dawn left 2.4 min over the ideal run (note 188), and `StopDriver` skipped every stop. Those two numbers are gone from tiers.json; the line generator reads route.json's. Every night's dawn is now 3,055 s (51 min); the sweep's ideal transits are 23–35 min.
    - **The density.** Over one length, each line-generator column's counts are its density, and the director's per-night `baseBudget` (90 / 150 / 230 / 330, unchanged) is 3.8 / 6.3 / 9.6 / 13.8 a km. But the deeper columns were set for 29–40 km: at 24 km `dt linegen sweep` put Dead lines at 20–22% and Deep territory at 2–4% within two attempts (8 and 44 of 50 unpassed), every failure "no room on the main line" for a long climb. The space-hungry counts were cut until every tier's quotas fit (tiers.json, its comment lists each): facilities 3 on every tier (Dead lines and Deep 4, deep max 5 before; a stop is time, so more of them would lengthen a deeper night again), fewer junctions, alternates, dead lines, brass fields and momentum banks on Dead lines and Deep, and Dead lines' `climb_long` quota 2 → 1. What still rises steeply with the tier is what goes on each kilometre: the ruling grade and tightest bend, the terrain budget (0.6 → 2.6 a km; laid per night, measured, 6.8 / 9.3 / 14 / 17), washouts, weak bridges, Corruption, fog, cold, signage and the director's budget.
    - **Reading (ARCHITECTURE §8's rule).** B.8's "POIs per run 3–5" now reads 3: the decision makes time the constant, and a stop is the thing that costs it. If the director wants the deeper tiers to keep more stops, the length has to grow for every tier (a Deep night's quotas don't fit 28 km either: 8% first attempt, 33% within two at Deep's old counts).
    - **The legacy generator keeps its own lengths.** `RouteGenerator` (route.json `tiers`, "routes": "legacy") isn't the game's nights; it's the fixture much of the sim's suite is pinned to (StopCrewTests' stops, SwitchTests, HoldoutTests, SpurDrillTests: 20 tests failed on a fixed 24 km, one through a real bot overshoot at a stop that only the new geometry found). Left as it was, and said so in route.json.
    - **Quiet in kilometres** (enemies.json `pressure.quietRampMetres` 1,260, `quietBackstopSeconds` 120). Note 266's quiet term ramped over `quietRampSeconds` (90 s) since a threat was engaged; now it ramps over the line run since then (1,260 m: the old 90 s at cruise), or the seconds over 120 if that's sooner, so a stopped train can't wait it out. A train at 20 m/s meets its full pull in 63 s, one at 7 m/s or standing in 120 s. `quietRampMetres` 0 is the old ramp. `World.QuietMetres` counts the same for the pacing log beside `QuietSeconds`.
    - **Found, not fixed:** on every generated line about 1–2% of 20 m samples of the ballast 2 m out sit 0.1–0.65 m off the rail's height, in runs of 100–300 m, flat where the rail is on a grade (frontier:7 at 6,480–6,640 m beside a junction pad; frontier:9, frontier:1, frontier:4, local:2 alike). `ThereIsGroundWithinReachOfTheTrackAndTheFormationIsAtRailHeight` samples every 500 m and had missed it; on the new frontier:7 it lands on one, so the test now reads frontier:3 (clean at 500 m). Not pads, roads or the junction's own pad: a terrain follow-up.
    - **Tests.** New: `LineGenTests.EveryTierIsTheSameNightAndTheHarderOnesAreDenser` (every tier's line 24 km, its dawn 3,055 s, its ideal run inside it; challenges and terrain budget per km strictly rising by tier; the director's budget a km rising; a Deep night lays more than a Local one), `SpecTableTests.RunLengthMatchesSpecB8`, `PacingTests.QuietIsCountedInLineRunWithTimeTheBackstop`, `RunTests.AQuietStretchIsCountedInLineRunAndInTime`. Changed: `LineGenConfigTests` (the length no longer lerps; Deep's facilities), and tests pinned to one generated line's geometry re-pointed: `CabRideTests` (deepTerritory:1's first alternate; every alternate's run in to its rejoin on frontier:3), `LeftBehindAlongTests` (deepTerritory:1's last alternate), `WatersideTests` (frontier:2 has no shore now, so deadLines:3; deadLines:2 for the river; a dyke's fields skip a branch's own formation: frontier:7's dead line runs off across them). No spec test pinned the old lengths.
