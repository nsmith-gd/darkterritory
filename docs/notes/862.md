862. **An open cab door lets voices out a bit more, not completely (the director, 19 Oct, on #583's call) (Theo, queue #589).**
   - Claimed 2026-10-19 14:45 UTC by the Token Budget Manager (nathaniel3) for Theo; the note and its Handoff block are Theo's to write as the work goes (rule 9).


   - **What it was:** the cab counts as a closed space for sound whatever its doors (`PlayerMotor.Space`), so a voice between the
     cab and anyone outside it came through the whole wall: spec A.3's -12 dB and 900 Hz low-pass, about 3 dB over the bed at
     speed after #583 (note 855). Note 856's side doors (#1259: engine door bits 2, by the coal, and 3, across the front) changed
     nothing for sound.
   - **Now:** `VoiceRouting.ThroughOpenCabDoor` (Sim, from replicated state only: the two positions and the engine's door bits):
     one of the two in the cab, the other outside, and either side door open. The listener's client (`VoiceChat`) then plays
     the host's `Occluded` voice through player.json `voices.openCab.occlusion` (0.5) of the wall instead of all of it. In the
     mixer that's half the wall's 12 dB and its low-pass up from 900 Hz to about 4.2 kHz, so the consonants come through. No
     wire change (rule 10 not needed): the host still says "occluded" and the client knows the doors.
     - **Either door, not only the listener's side:** the cab is small and a door open in it lets the sound out wherever you
       stand outside it. It's the simplest reading of the director's words; a side rule is easy to add if a test night asks.
     - **Voice only:** the train's own sounds (`Walls`), the Choir and the tells are as before.
   - **Measured** (`dt voice bench --car 0 --listener-car 0 --speed 22 --mic -20 --seconds 3 [--cab-door left|right]`: the
     driver in the cab, a crewmate on the engine 7.5 m off, a quiet mic, 22 m/s; "outside" is `--car 3 --z 7.5`, the same voice
     at the same distance with no wall):

     | | voice | over the bed |
     |---|---|---|
     | door shut | -27.6 dB | +3.1 |
     | a side door open (left or right) | -19.0 dB | +11.7 |
     | no wall | -13.1 dB | +17.0 |

   - **Held by:** `VoiceTests.AnOpenCabDoorLetsTheVoiceOutABitButNotCompletely` (both ways, from the cab and into it; either door):
     open at least 4 dB over shut and 6 dB brighter above 2 kHz, the open air at least 3 dB over open, shut still 2 dB over the
     bed. Fails with `openCab.on` false. Systems-spec A.3 (tier 2) and A.5 (occlusion) say it.
