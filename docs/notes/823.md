- Much of the "choppy" is also in the clips themselves:
    - These creatures were keyed sparse and stepped (`"CONSTANT"`), for §31's "still, then abrupt". The director's notes now ask for fluidity.

**The Climber (done)** (`tools/blender/climber.py`, rebaked with `tools/models/build.sh climber`)
- **Scrabble** (0.8 s, loop): it was seven stepped snatches. Now a lizard's diagonal climbing gait:
    - the right fore and left hind reach up together while the other pair hauls, the middle pair between them;
    - each hand is lifted off the steel, thrown up and hooked on, then dragged down the length of its pull as the body's hauled up past it;
    - the body snakes side to side with the pulls, rising and sinking on them, and the tail whips;
    - the head is pressed up and turning, with a jerk at the top of each pull.
- **Grab** (1.2 s, loop): it feeds in two long pulls a loop:
    - the body rocks back and drives down, the sucker opening wide as it comes back and clamping as it drives in;
    - the forehooks clench and the middle hooks dig in;
    - the tail lashes slowly, the hind feet shift their grip, and a tremor runs through it.
- Keyed every two frames and interpolated, so nothing steps.
- `dt art clearance --only climber`: nothing through itself, as before.

**The Cinder Hound (done)** (`tools/blender/cinder_hound.py`): its stepped keys made linear, so its pops and holds are quick eased moves (prowl, crouch, lunge, bite, board, patrol, leap, drop, climb, sniff); the run kept.

**The Gaunt (done)** (`tools/blender/gaunt.py`, rebaked with `tools/models/build.sh gaunt`)

**The Ribbit (done)** (`tools/blender/ribbit.py`, rebaked with `tools/models/build.sh ribbit`; about 11 minutes)
- **Sit** (4 s, loop), keyed every two frames: the throat pulsing slow, three breaths a loop, the body settling a little on each. Once the head turns a few degrees, quick, the way a frog's does, and holds there; once an ear flicks.

    - the arc of it keyed through the air, the ears streaming back;
    - the hands reached down for the ground and the hind legs swung under;
    - landed heavily, squashed down on its hands, the throat thrown out and the ears flopped forward;

- **Creep:** keyed every three frames (was four keys a loop). Each hand is lifted and placed, and the body rolls with them.
- **Devour:** the jaw snaps shut in two or three frames, and the head wrenches on through the bite (was a stepped hold on each bite).
- **Swell:** the body shudders with each pulse of the sac.

- **Bite:** it draws its head back before the lunge, snaps shut in two frames, shakes what it has, and draws back (was a stepped hold on each end).
- **Maul:** three blows a loop, each head drawn up, driven down into them in two frames, the jaw working there; keyed every two frames.
- **Hit:** a jerk round in two frames and on past, and back over the crate.

**The Soot Child (done)** (`tools/blender/soot_child.py`, rebaked with `tools/models/build.sh soot_child`; `CreatureArt`, `SceneArt`, `GreyboxScene`)
- **Its telegraph, a last-moment save (`rise`, `CreatureArt.SootPoise`):**
    - The sim lunges the moment someone afoot is within `sootChildren.lungeWithin` (5 m), so the telegraph comes by distance, before that, presentation only.
    - From 2.5 m outside the lunge, as they come on, the call stops dead and its head snaps round to them; it comes up off its heels onto its toes, the hands off its knees and clawed; at the lunge it's crouched forward, poised, the jaw unhinging, trembling.

    - Only a Soot Child does it; a real child goes on calling.
- **The kill (`pin`):** it springs from poised out in the dark, the last of it in the air at them with its arms out for their neck; it hits them and locks on, its weight carrying it up past where it holds; its head rears back with the jaw unhinged, and drives into their throat; then it drinks. (Was a crouch at their feet popping up onto them.) The drink's last stepped key is eased.
- **The two-handed carry in your own eyes (`held`, `SceneArt.InYourArms`, `CreatureArt.OwnCradleLift`):**
    - **Why you didn't see it:** the child you carry was drawn at the crate carry point, turned the third-person clutch's way, clinging to a neck out in front of you, its face down on a shoulder. Your own cradling arms were drawn where your body really holds it, 0.6–0.7 m under your eyes, below a forward look.
    - **Now:** in your own eyes the child is drawn where your cradle holds it, lifted with your arms (0.08 m up, 0.2 m out) so its face sits at the bottom of what you see ahead, looking up at you, its hands on your shoulders either side of your neck. You see over it to walk. Look down and it's in your arms. Everyone else still sees the third-person cradle.

**"It stands right on the player when triggered"** (`CreatureArt.GauntStoodBack`, `GreyboxScene.StoodBack`)
- **Why it happened:** the sim keeps a woken Gaunt at its waker's back at `gaunt.followAt` (1.2 m, inside its 1.6 m reach). Its forefeet are a metre ahead of its middle and a metre to each side, so that stands the waker inside the cage of its legs, under its neck.
- **What changed (presentation only):**
    - Out in the open, following, listening or striking, it's drawn 1.7 m from them on the same bearing, facing them, its neck let down to them. They stand a step clear in front of its forefeet.
    - When they turn, the sim walks it straight through them to their new back. Drawn, it goes round them (at 2.5 radians a second).
    - Further off than 1.7 m, it eases back to where the sim has it, all the way by 2.7 m.
- **Why that much and no more:** the sim's point stays in its drawn chest (`bodies.gaunt`, what the guns hit, 0.5 m) and straight ahead of anyone facing it (a swing's 70° cone and 3 m reach). Stood any further off or round to one side, the guns would miss what they're aimed at.
- **The sim gap:** standing it right beside them, round at their shoulder, needs the sim's `followAt` near 2 m and its `reach` to match (its stab lands 1.5 m ahead of it). Those are design numbers, so they're left to the director. Aboard, the car is too narrow to stand it back; it stays where the sim has it, folded.
