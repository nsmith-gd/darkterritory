581. **Loot into the cars, forgivingly (D1.4, queue #313; the director, 9 Oct 2026, playtest: "There's no way it seems to actually unhook loot when its in the car finally. Also it's extremely difficult to get loot into the cars, the game should be more forgiving with this", with a screenshot of a car's narrow aisle with a crate in it).**
    - **What was found.** A find carried into a car and put down with a tap of Use is stowed from every spot of every car's aisle on a standing train (a probe: 393 of 393). So the hands work. What didn't forgive:
        - *The crane's hook* (T48, T99): a casting brought down onto a cargo car's roof by its open hatch, overlapping the opening but not clear of it, stopped on the roof. Let go, `Crane.Release` kept it on the hook and said nothing. This is the "no way to unhook it", in the car or as good as.
        - *A car's door with your arms full*: Use put down what you carried on the press, before a door could take it. So a crate or a find couldn't be got through a shut door without setting it down, opening the door, and picking it up again.
        - *The doorway*: anything put down from the steps or the ground outside an open door landed outside it.
    - **Now:**
        - **The crane** (`Crane.Guided`): a casting low over an open hatch (its base within `crane.guideAbove`, 2.5 m, of the roof) that overlaps the opening but doesn't clear it is eased over it. The bridge and trolley are drawn to where it goes in at `crane.guideSpeed` (1 m/s), as a hand on the rope would steady it. Let go there, it's put through where it clears and set down inside, and loads as any casting does. Higher up, or over a shut hatch, it's as before (T99's rule that nothing stands in the opening holds: it goes all the way in or not at all).
        - **A door with your arms full** (`Bodies.Handle`): Use held is the door's, as empty-handed; a tap still puts down what you carry. This is the crew lockers' rule (note 267).
        - **The doorway** (`Bodies.TakeIn`): a find or a crate (loot, cargo, a heavy crate) set down within `Bodies.DoorTakeIn` (1 m) outside a car's open doorway, from its steps, the ground or the plates, is taken in onto the floor a step inside. There it's stowed or loaded as anything put down inside is. Thrown things fly as they did; a shut door takes nothing in.
    - **Verified:**
        - `LootIntoCarsTests` (3): a find put down anywhere in a car's aisle is stowed; one put down outside an open side door from the ground is taken in and stowed, and outside a shut one it isn't; at a shut door with a find in the hands, a held Use opens it and keeps the find, and a tap puts it down.
        - `HatchTests.HungOverTheHatchsEdgeItsEasedInAndLetGoThereItGoesIn` replaces T99's `...ItWontGoInAndWontBeLetGo`: eased in with the stick still, and let go at the edge it's loaded inside.
        - CraneTests unchanged and green; the Sim suite.
    - **Asked:** D1 was asked what the director's screenshot shows (a crane's casting or a hand crate). All three are forgiving now, whichever it was.
