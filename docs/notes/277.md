277. **The art checklist's open rows to L1 (C1, the art session; the director: "keep getting L1s in there").** Eight rows had nothing of their own drawn, or only part. Each is drawn now, with a `dt` path to look at it and a test.
    - **The boiler rupture** (spec B.6, GDD §23 "loud, spectacular, run-changing"). The sim had it (the drag down to coasting, the fire and pressure to nothing) and the audio, but nothing was drawn.
        - **The burst** (`Effects.Rupture`): a flash, then a white cloud blown out of a seam torn in the boiler's left flank (`TrainKit.RuptureSeam`, between the first two straps behind the cab, seen from the left doorway and the line). A hard core of steam stands out of the tear. The jet is checked by the air and laid back down the train by its going, full for 4 s, then a hiss that thins over half a minute. Scale and grit fly from the tear. In the cab, the fire is blown back out of the firehole in steam, ash and embers.
        - **The tear** (`TrainKit.RuptureTear`, drawn while ruptured): a ragged black hole in a scorch of soot, a lip of raw bright iron, the plate peeled back in rusted petals, and burst copper tubes curling out.
        - **The seized engine:** the stack dead (a last grey wisp, no cinders), no whistle, valve or cylinder-cock steam. While she's still dragging down to `ruptureCoastBelow` the drivers are locked (`GreyboxScene.DriversLocked`: the rods stop turning and pick up where they stopped once she's coasting). Each tyre slides in a stream of sparks over a glow on the rail. The cylinder cocks' steam now comes from the cylinders (it was at the old front).
        - `dt screenshot --view rupture --ruptured s` (0.8: the blast; 30: the hiss; the train dragged down from `--speed` as the sim does), and `--view fireman` for the cab.
