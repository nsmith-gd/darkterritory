289. **The switch audit (queue #26, A1.11; the director, 7 Oct: "audit switches, in a recent playtest I felt like they weren't working").** Everything a switch does, end to end: the generated junctions, the levers, the lamps, the prompts, replication, the facility drill, the bots and the Switchman's hook. The points, the routing and the replication were sound. What was broken was around them: the bots stopping for points set wrong, the Switchman's hook, the prompt at an alternate, and the lock on the branch's leg.

    - **The Switchman (A1.8's, #22; note 286 landed beside this).** Note 286 fixed the same vanishing from the Switchman's side (it checks `Diverging` before it throws), so both fixes stand: the hook now means what its callers read, and the Switchman doesn't lean on it. Reported, not changed:
      - With `switchman.throwsUnderTrain` set (off by default since note 286), the throw "under the train" never moves the points: `SetSwitch` honours the lock, and with the engine over the toe they're locked. The split or derail still happens, but the lamp stays green and the lever doesn't move.
      - While it's `Dormant` it throws the points again whenever they're set back, so a crew that walks out and sets them back before it reveals itself (`revealAt`, 300 m) has them thrown again.
    - **Not yet:** the driver bot doesn't use the powered thrower (note 196). Holding Use at a stand with something to pick up in reach does both: the pick-up and the throw (the HUD offers only the pick-up).
    - The frontier:7 harness night (8 bots, 9 cars, enemies) delivered with no facility stop worked: two Switchman set-backs (75 s and 88 s, each down the dead line and back) ate its slack. Not re-run here after the fix (the machine is shared); `SwitchAuditTests` covers the stop.
