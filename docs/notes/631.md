631. **A dawn cushion for the run home: measured, and not kept (queue #361, D1.2 for D1; D1's ask after #826, from D1.2's own flag on note 628: "is 'in after dawn but before the catch' what the reckoning means to aim for, or should the driver keep a small cushion so those nights don't sit one bad stand away from being taken?").** Note 621 reckons every spare to the dawn and the Wakers' catch of a stood train after it (≈72 s, `Waker.CatchAfterDawn`).
   - **The catch isn't the yardstick.** With no cushion (main after #829; 4 bots, enemies, upkeep, Release, seeds 1–16 at 2,000 s), 10 of frontier:6's nights and 4 of frontier:7's ended past dawn + 72 s, up to +383 s, and none was taken. A train still running is caught later than a stood one. And a night's end isn't when it was safe: most of those trains were in under the terminus's guns before the dawn line, and their yard moves ended them later.
   - **The closest call** (new): `dt harness`'s dawn report has `closest`. It's the fewest seconds the lead Waker would have needed to take the train had it stood just then (`Waker.SecondsInHand(world, speed: 0)`, the speed now an argument), sampled from the dawn line until the train is in under the guns. It's −1 when the train was in before the line. That is what "one bad stand away" means.
   - **Measured**, cushion 0 → 60 s off the deadline (`Deadline` less a temporary knob, sweeps only):

     | | frontier:6, 0 | 60 | frontier:7, 0 | 60 (seeds 1–5) |
     |---|---|---|---|---|
     | stops (pick-ups) | 24 (8) | 16 (0) | 18 (2) | 5 (0), as at 0 |
     | cargo (car-loads) | 22.22 | 16.41 | 22.47 | 6.61, as at 0 |
     | deaths | 4 | 7 | 6 | 0, as at 0 |
     | nights with a closest call | 5 | 2 | 4 | 1 |
     | under 60 s in hand | 1 (10 s) | 1 (10 s) | 1 (40 s) | 0 |

     The frontier:7 sweep at 60 s stopped at the background limit after five seeds, all five identical to 0 s. Its pick-ups are rare on ordinary nights anyway.
   - **Why not.** A cushion buys nothing against the close calls there are. frontier:6 seed 13's 10 s is the same night either way. It made no pick-up, so the reckoning had nothing to cut; it came in late from two Fire Flies car fires at the Voss Grain Elevator and a Whistler's grab on the line. What a cushion does cut is every pick-up frontier:6 makes, and a quarter of its cargo. **The reckoning stays aimed at the dawn and the catch**, as note 621 has it.
