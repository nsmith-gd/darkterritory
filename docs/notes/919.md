919. **Playtest telemetry T3: the local spool and the `file` sink; the upload an interface until the director picks a backend (queue #626; the plan, note 907; n1-A1, nathaniel1).**
    - **Builds on** n1-H1's session recorder (#624, note 913, #1386) and n1-N1's consent gate (#625, note 914, #1381). It
      changes neither. Everything is in `DarkTerritory.Game/Telemetry/`: nothing in the Sim, no wire state, nothing
      recorded or sent without the gate's yes.
    - **The spool** (`TelemetrySpool.cs`): one JSON-lines file a session in the gate's `SessionsDirectory`, the folder #625's
      DELETE empties.
      - **Per session:** `CappedTelemetrySink` stops a session's file at `maxSessionBytes` (2 MB). A line that would pass the
        cap, and everything after it, isn't written, so a capped night keeps its start whole. A 15-minute 4-bot night is
        8.9 KB (note 913), so the cap is a guard, not a limit anyone meets.
      - **The folder:** `Trim` holds it to `maxTotalBytes` (32 MB) and `maxSessions` (200). It drops sent sessions first,
        then the oldest unsent, and never the session being written.
      - **Sent:** a session is renamed `<id>.sent.jsonl` in the same folder, not moved to a subfolder, so DELETE (which
        clears the folder's files) still takes it.
      - **The app** makes its recorder with `TelemetrySpool.Recorder(gate)` in place of `SessionRecorder.ToFile`, and trims at
        launch.
    - **The upload** (`TelemetryUpload.cs`):
      - `ITelemetryUpload` is a sink for finished sessions.
      - `TelemetryUploader.PumpAsync` sends nothing without consent and nothing during a night. It trims, sends the oldest
        pending sessions in one batch of at most `maxBatchBytes` (4 MB), and marks them sent. A failed batch waits
        `backoffSeconds` (30, 120, 600), and after `attempts` (3) failures, or one refusal, it's left for the next launch.
    - **The `file` sink** (`FileTelemetryUpload`, the one there is): the batch's sessions in one file,
      `playtest-data-<stamp>.jsonl`.
      - **In the app**, when a player writes a problem report (note 452, `Report.Problem`, never a crash) with consent on,
        the finished sessions and the session so far (flushed, left unsent) go beside the report. The tester's mail
        carries them.
      - That bundle is the player's own doing, so it goes mid-night too; the between-nights rule is for what the game sends
        of its own accord.
    - **`https` is not built.** The backend is the director's call (note 907, the inbox): a small self-hosted ingest, a
      hosted analytics service, or no upload for now. `upload.sink` "https" makes no sink yet (`SinkFor` returns null).
      Whichever is picked implements `ITelemetryUpload` and holds at most a write-only ingest key, never note 516's
      feedback token.
    - **Tuning:** `content/tuning/telemetry.json`'s `spool` and `upload` blocks (`TelemetryTuning`).
    - **Headless:** `dt telemetry spool [--dir d] [--trim] [--export out]` lists a spool's sessions against the caps, trims
      it, and bundles the pending sessions as the `file` sink does. `TelemetrySpoolTests` (7) pins:
      - the per-session cap;
      - the trim order;
      - sent sessions staying where DELETE clears them;
      - no consent and in a night holding the upload;
      - batching, backoff and giving up;
      - a problem report's bundle carrying the night so far, unsent;
      - the tuning file.
