815. **Houses that don't all look alike (queue #543, B2; the director's playtest of 13 Oct evening, line 7, frontier:7's town: "A lot of the house models are too same same").**

**What it was.**
- frontier:7's departure town, Fort Gantry, is a *company* town (houses.json `company`: a foundry's or a pit's town; two
  in five of the coal towns are company towns).
- A company town's houses were one design (`uniform`). The first house was drawn, and every other house was
  `HouseDesigner.Copy` of it, with the same width and depth; only its paint, door and roof colour were its own.
- So its streets were rows of the same small gable-front box in different paints.
- The other characters already draw each house afresh, and already passed the measure below:
  - a cove, an old painted town, the loyalist row, a farm town and a town of all sorts;
  - the fortress towns of note 692.

**What changed.**
- **A company's plans** (houses.json `plans`, 3; towns.json `houses.planRun`, 45 m). A company built to a few plans, not one.
  - Each street's houses stand in runs of one plan, each run about `planRun` long.
  - The plan for a run is drawn from its own stream (row, side, run), so the next run may be another plan.
  - Each plan is drawn, with its size, the first time a run of it is built (`HouseDesigner.Draw`, the company
    character's odds). The rest of the run are `Copy`s of it.
- **What the families have done since** (`HouseDesigner.Alter`; houses.json `altered`, the share of a town's houses
  changed, and `alterations`, how many changes each):
  - **The changes:**
    - a porch added (a stoop), or closed in (the enclosed porch);
    - a lean-to built on the side: a one-storey wing toward the back, where the lot has room;
    - a shed dormer cut into an eave-front roof;
    - shingled over the clapboard;
    - shutters hung;
    - a stovepipe for the chimney;
    - a storey built up.
  - **Only where it fits** the house as it stands. An open house's porch stays a stoop: you go straight in.
  - **How many:** a company town most of all (0.75 of its houses, one to three changes each: built alike, lived in
    apart). The others less: from 0.15 (an old painted town that keeps its houses) to 0.4 (a farm town that builds on).
  - **Nothing else moves:** the alterations draw from their own stream (`houses.altered`), so every house is otherwise as
    it was.
- **Solid as drawn.** A lean-to is the design's ell (`TownHouse.Parts`), so it stands in the Sim as the wing does. The rest
  is how the art builds the house (`MaritimeKit`, unchanged: it already draws every one of these).

**Measured** (`TownHouseVarietyTests`).
- **What's compared:** each house's silhouette beside the next one down its row. The silhouette is gable or eaves to the
  street, storeys, roof, dormers, wing, porch, chimney and its size; not its paint.
- **What passes:**
  - few twins next door: at most a quarter of neighbours;
  - never three alike in a row;
  - a town of many kinds of house: a third as many as there are, up to a dozen.
- **The towns:** frontier:7 at 97 people (the company town), and local:3, local:5 and frontier:3 at 1200.
- **Before and after:**
  - On main, frontier:7 failed, with three alike in a row: the same 5.5 m two-storey gable-front.
  - The other three already passed.
  - Now all four pass.
- **A company town is still its plans:** 2 to 3 shapes as built, with most of its houses changed since. It comes out the
  same every time.
- **What changed in an old test:** `TownTests.HousesVaryWithinATownAndTownsDifferInCharacter` pinned a company town to one
  shape. It now holds its built shapes (gable or eaves to the street, and its roof, which no family changes) to its plans.

**Looked at:** `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --town row --survey`, before and after. It's a new view: down the first
street on the line's other side from the square, from over it. On the World Building board's "The houses" section.
