876. **Bounded encounter debt: `EncounterDebt`, the spawn director overhaul's #607 (C2) (the director's brief, `docs/design/spawn-director-overhaul.md` §3 "Encounter debt"; the plan, note 874; n1-C2, 20 Oct 2026).**
   - **What it is:** `src/DarkTerritory.Sim/Enemies/EncounterDebt.cs`, a `readonly record struct EncounterDebt(Owed, Allowance)`,
     pure and deterministic (plain arithmetic, no draws, no clock, the tuning passed in each call). Nothing calls it yet;
     #608 (C3) wires it into the director beside #606's rate.
   - **The contract (note 874's, as built):**
     - `debt = debt.Owe(unmet, DebtCause cause, t)`: of `unmet` opportunities (fractions allowed), `t.Share[cause]` is owed,
       to at most `t.Cap`.
     - `debt = debt.Decay(dt, t)`: the owed decays with a mean life of `t.MeanLifeSeconds` (stepped linearly, `owed × (1 −
       dt/life)`, so it is the same arithmetic on every machine; no `Math.Exp`), and the release allowance refills at
       `t.ReleasePerMinute` up to `t.ReleaseBurst`.
     - `debt = debt.Hold()`: the director held (safe zone, grace, recovery). The allowance empties; what's owed stays and
       keeps decaying. Call it every tick the director is held, so after the yard repayment starts from an empty allowance:
       the release rate, never a burst.
     - `debt = debt.Take(allowed, t, out taken)`: repays `min(allowed, Owed, Allowance)`. `allowed` is the caller's headroom
       under the engagement caps (#604/#611), so the debt never pushes past them. The caller adds `taken` to its own
       opportunities this tick.
     - `DebtCause`: `NoPlacement` and `Ineligible` owe their share; `SafeZone`, `Grace`, `Recovery`, `Terminus` and `Capped`
       are intended quiet and **never** owe, whatever the tuning names (`EncounterDebt.Intended`). #603's `SpawnFailure`
       reasons map onto these at #608 (a placement failure → `NoPlacement`; nothing eligible → `Ineligible`; a hold →
       its own cause).
   - **Tuning:** `enemies.json` `director.encounters.debt` (`on`, `share`, `cap` 2, `meanLifeSeconds` 240,
     `releasePerMinute` 0.5, `releaseBurst` 1). `EncountersTuning` (the `director.encounters` block, `DirectorTuning.Encounters`)
     is a **`partial record`**: #606 adds its rate fields in a partial declaration of its own file, so the two items never
     edit the same lines. Starting values, not measured: #608/#613 tune them with #601's sweep. What they mean: at most 2
     encounters remembered, half forgotten in under 3 minutes, and repaid at most one every 2 minutes over the night's own rate.
   - **Tests:** `EncounterDebtTests`: an unplaced encounter owed in part; intended quiet owes nothing even with a greedy
     tuning; capped; decays to nothing and never negative (about 1/e at a mean life); `Take` bounded by owed, allowance and
     headroom; no burst after ten minutes held in the yard with a full backlog (no 60 s window repays more than the release
     rate); `on: false` inert; deterministic bit for bit.
   - **No night changes:** nothing reads it yet, so no sweep was run (note 874's rule: measure the items that change nights).
   - **Handoff:** done in its PR (20 Oct 2026). Nothing left on #607; #608 wires it in: build each tick's debt with `Decay`,
     `Hold` while held, `Owe` the unmet part of λ's opportunities by cause, `Take` with the caps' headroom.
