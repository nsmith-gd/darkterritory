973. **A houseboat's ladder climbed (queue #673, B2; the director's playtest of 16 Oct, line 28, its open half: "they have little ladders to go up to them, but the ladders aren't climbable. Those should be climbable"; note 841's handoff).**

**What it was.**
- **Drawn, not climbed:** a Flooder houseboat's ladder (note 822) was drawn, leant against its bow at the door, and was nothing else.
- **Why:** a ladder was the train's alone. `PlayerMotor` grabbed and climbed only a car's (`CarShape.Ladders`), its climber parented to the car.
- **No deck to stand on:** the deck at the top was 0.4 m of hull in front of the house, less than a body. And the house's door was at the ground's height.

**What changed.**
- **A ladder standing in the world** (`WorldLadder`, beside a car's `Ladder`):
  - its foot on the ground, its top at the deck's edge, in world coordinates;
  - which way is in over the top;
  - how far in a climber steps off;
  - `At(y)`: the point on it at a height, as it leans.
- **A town's ladders** (`Town.Ladders`): each houseboat's, at the door 1.0 m out from the bow (`HouseDesign.LadderOut`), up to the deck's edge. `World.EnableTown` gives the town's and the arrival's to the train (`TrainOnLine.Ladders`, beside `Walls`), so they're alike on the host and every client.
- **Climbed as a car's is** (`PlayerMotor`):
  - **Grabbed** (`TryGrabLadder`) by the Ladder key, Use and forward, or walking into it facing up it. It's reached for where it is at the climber's height, so from the deck it's taken at its top.
  - **Held:** the climber is parented to the world (`PlayerState.World`) on `Surface.Ladder`.
  - **Climbed** (`StepWorldLadder`) along it as it leans. Over its top they step 0.6 m in onto the deck, stood there; off its foot onto the ground. Let go as a car's.
  - **A car's ladders are as they were.**
- **A foredeck to step off onto** (`HouseDesign.HullBow` 1.2 m): the hull's bow stands further out at the door. `MaritimeKit.Hull` draws it, and the ladder where the Sim's is.
- **The solid split at the deck** (`TownHouse.Parts`):
  - the hull's box to the deck, whose top is stood on (note 817's wall tops);
  - the house's box on it, up to its roof.
- **The door on the deck** (`Town.Door`): lifted by the hull, at the top of the ladder.

**Decisions.**
- **A ladder of the world, not a stair of steps:** with a 0.3 m body and a 0.35 m step-up, a 1.0 m run up 2.2 m can't be walked. The car's ladder (the key, the climb, the let-go) is what the director knows a ladder by.
- **Bots:** on a ladder of the world they only press up or down. Every place in the bots that looks up the car a climber's on (`Frames[self.Parent]`) guards against the world's parent. A bot doesn't go to a houseboat.

**Measured.**
- **`StiltTownTests.AHouseboatsLadderIsClimbedUpOntoItsDeckAndDownAgain`** (local:1 at 600, local:2 at 1500):
  - every houseboat has its ladder, and the train has them;
  - three ladders each walked into from the stage and climbed up onto the foredeck;
  - stood there a second;
  - turned round, taken from the top with the Ladder key, and climbed down to the stage.
- **The rest of `StiltTownTests`** (the hull solid at its corners, the Flooders out) pass.
- **The motor's own:** `PlayerMotorTests` (a car's ladders run down, boarded, the cab steps), `MovingFrameRegressionTests` and `NetcodeTests` (prediction against the host).
- **The town tests:** `FloodersTests`, `TownTests`, `TownsfolkTests` pass.

**Looked at:** `dt screenshot --route local:1 --form dykeland --town houseboat` (and `--survey`): the foredeck at the door, the ladder up to it from the stage.

**Not yet:**
- **The ladder heard:** footsteps on its rungs, as a car's.
- **Other ladders of the world:** a hayloft's, a water tower's, the wall's. `WorldLadder` is theirs to use.
- **A bot that climbs one:** no bot's task takes it to a houseboat.
