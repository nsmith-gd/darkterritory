937. **Leaving a game leaves it (Quinn, queue #640; the director's playtest of 20 Oct evening).** Line 27: "So I escape and leave, and I've been put into automatically, like, another lobby ... I suppose it's a bot. Or the bot from my game ... you can't actually leave a game".
   - **What it was.** No lobby was joined or hosted. On a campaign night, LEAVE (Escape > LEAVE, or END THE NIGHT) went
     where Enter on the night's end goes: the crew's board at the fortress (`ShowFortress`, or `ShowCrew` for a crew ridden
     with, note 724). With the night unsettled, that board's rows are CARRY ON, CREW: YOU AND 1 BOT, PLAY: ALONE/HOST FOR
     FRIENDS. That reads as a lobby with the night's bot in it, waiting to go again. Shown headless: the app, a hosted
     campaign night with one bot (`--campaign 1 --host --bots 1`), LEAVE taken at 20 s, opened `Screen.Fortress` on main
     and opens `Screen.Title` with this change.
   - **Now** (`DarkTerritory.Game/AfterNight.cs`, the app's loop after a night): what comes after a night is decided in one
     place, `AfterNight.Decide`, which the app follows and tests call. A night left on purpose goes to the title, whatever
     it was: hosted, joined, a campaign's, a crew's. Its after-effects are skipped:
     - no refit (note 697) and no following a host that went on (note 606);
     - a host that leaves a delivered night doesn't send CarryOn (note 606), so its joiners aren't sent to wait for a night
       it won't host. They lose the link and go to their own title.
     - An invite accepted during the night (queue #272) is still taken: the player asked for it.
     - A night seen to its end (Enter on the end screen) goes on as before: the fortress, the crew's board, the refit, the
       next night from the town. Put off by the crew (note 724) still says so.
   - **The night's state** is kept as the campaign keeps it: autosaved at the last stop it left (spec E), CARRY ON on its
     slot. A delivered night was settled as it ended, and the town it reached is the slot's.
   - **A client leaving** says Leave (note 254), so its place frees at once, and goes to the title. That was already so.
     It now holds even when its host said CarryOn just before.
   - **The menu:** LEAVE on a night that's over has a line saying where it goes ("Back to the title. Enter on the night's
     end goes on with the crew."). A headset's Escape out of a night counts as a LEAVE.
   - **No wire change** (protocol unchanged).
   - **Tests:** `LeaveGameTests` (Game.Tests):
     - a left night goes to the title, whatever it was, and an over night goes on as before;
     - an accepted invite is still taken;
     - a joiner on a night hosted with a bot leaves after the host's CarryOn: it's the title, not a follow; its place frees
       and the host and its bot carry on;
     - a host that leaves: its joiner, with no CarryOn, ends lost and goes to the title.
