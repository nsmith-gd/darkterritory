937. **Leaving a game leaves it (Quinn, queue #640; the director's playtest of 20 Oct evening).** Line 27: "So I escape and leave, and I've been put into automatically, like, another lobby ... I suppose it's a bot. Or the bot from my game ... you can't actually leave a game".
   - **What it was** (the director's words, from the transcript: "Okay, so I'm going to leave that built, and joining.
     What the hell am I joining?"). They left after dawn, mid-chase, the night not over. Three things in a row:
     - **LEAVE went to the crew's board.** On a campaign night, LEAVE (Escape > LEAVE, or END THE NIGHT) went where Enter on
       the night's end goes: the fortress board (`ShowFortress`, or `ShowCrew` for a crew ridden with, note 724). With the
       night unsettled, its first row, selected, is CARRY ON.
     - **The press that chose LEAVE chose again.** The night's loop broke out on LEAVE's Enter (or click) before the frame
       ended (`Window.EndFrame` clears what was pressed). The menu's first frame read the same press as a choice of its
       own: CARRY ON, so the night was started again at once ("BUILDING THE RUN").
     - **The resumed night had the host joining it.** Resumed from its checkpoint past the gate, the night's bot boarded
       first. The host's own player then came in as a mid-run joiner (D.1, `HostSession.Board`): dead, waiting in the queue,
       the card saying JOINING. With the bot aboard, it looked like someone else's game.
   - **Now:**
     - **The press is spent.** The app ends the frame (`input.EndFrame()`) as a night's session comes back, so nothing
       pressed in the night chooses anything on the menu. By the code, the end screen's Enter took the same path (not checked
       in the app).
     - **The night's own crew boards alive** (`HostSession.Board`, K1's note 724): while the game brings its bots and its
       host's player aboard (`BotSeatsOpen` shut), none of them is lobbied past the gate. A friend joining a resumed night
       still takes a bot's seat (note 724), or waits in the queue if there's no bot.
     - **Where a night goes** (`DarkTerritory.Game/AfterNight.cs`): what comes after a night is decided in one
       place, `AfterNight.Decide`, which the app follows and tests call. A night left on purpose goes to the title, whatever
       it was: hosted, joined, a campaign's, a crew's. Its after-effects are skipped:
       - no refit (note 697) and no following a host that went on (note 606);
       - a host that leaves a delivered night doesn't send CarryOn (note 606), so its joiners aren't sent to wait for a night
         it won't host. They lose the link and go to their own title.
       - An invite accepted during the night (queue #272) is still taken: the player asked for it.
       - A night seen to its end (Enter on the end screen) goes on as before: the fortress, the crew's board, the refit, the
         next night from the town. Put off by the crew (note 724) still says so.
   - **The night's state** is kept as the campaign keeps it: autosaved at the last stop it left (spec E), CARRY ON on its
     slot. A delivered night was settled as it ended, and the town it reached is the slot's.
   - **A client leaving** says Leave (note 254), so its place frees at once, and goes to the title. That was already so.
     It now holds even when its host said CarryOn just before.
   - **The menu:** LEAVE on a night that's over has a line saying where it goes ("Back to the title. Enter on the night's
     end goes on with the crew."). A headset's Escape out of a night counts as a LEAVE.
   - **The app, headless, with real keys** (xvfb and xdotool, about 1 fps under lavapipe): slot 1's board, CARRY ON,
     the night, then Escape, Up, Enter, Down, Enter (LEAVE, LEAVE). On main the log shows the night started a second time
     at once, and the screen is the yard's "THE LOBBY: 1 ABOARD OF 8". On this branch, the title stays up and nothing
     starts.
   - **No wire change** (protocol unchanged). The boarding change is the host's alone (who it lobbies), deterministic.
   - **Tests:** `LeaveGameTests` (Game.Tests):
     - a left night goes to the title, whatever it was, and an over night goes on as before;
     - an accepted invite is still taken;
     - a joiner on a night hosted with a bot leaves after the host's CarryOn: it's the title, not a follow; its place frees

     - a host that leaves: its joiner, with no CarryOn, ends lost and goes to the title;
     - a night resumed past the gate with a bot: the host and the bot board alive. Without the fix the host's player was
