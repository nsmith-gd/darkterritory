601. **The bots load at the stops: the Brakeman's pincer leaves a stop's hands to their work (D1.5, queue #333; D1's ask after note 575: on frontier:7 4-bot nights the bots brought home 0–4 car-loads at 45 min, and almost nothing on an empty train).**
    - **What was there.** The Foundry, the stop in reach on frontier:7, was given up on half the 4-bot nights: the driver held short of its points for note 550's 240 s, the switch was never thrown, and the train went on with nothing loaded. The shunter was "to the cut" the whole time, on the roofs.
    - **Why.** `BotCrew` runs each bot's intent through the crew's heeds after its own work, and `Heed.Brakeman` (note 364) takes the two lowest ids of the bots on the roofs as his pincer, the shunter among them. While he's hidden, each keeps its end of the train. On seed 2 the shunter's cut, behind car 7, had it squaring up over car 7's plate; the pincer had it walk on to the tail, jump to car 8, and come back, round and round for four minutes. It reached the plate by chance at 615 s and the switch at 618 s, 8 s before the give-up.
    - **The rule.** A hand whose part in a facility stop is under way (`StopHand.AtAStop`: the train standing for the stop, or in at it, and not yet away), its rake at a stand (under 0.05 m/s), is left to that work by the Brakeman's heed. He stops a moving train, and a wound brake costs nothing until it moves: the same reasoning the heed already used for the Knotter's stand (note 545). Any other stand is still his to work and theirs to stop him at, and under way the pincer and the wheels are theirs, as before. A first cut that left every hand at every stand alone let him wind the train's brakes unopposed wherever it stood, and its nights went on slower: 8 crew deaths in eight nights against main's 1. Bot behaviour only: no tuning, nothing on the wire.
    - **Verified.** `BotsAnswerTheSixTests.AtAStopTheBrakemanLeavesTheHandsToTheirWork` (fails without the rule); the Brakeman's tests at speed unchanged. On seed 2 the stand now takes 64 s, not 240, and the train goes in. Seed 3's stand still runs out, for a real reason: Fire Flies and a Whistler set cars 1 and 2 alight at the points, and the shunter fought the fires first (note 330), cutting at 704 s, 14 s short.
    - **Eight full nights** (frontier:7, 4 bots, enemies, upkeep, 2700 s, Release, seeds 1–8, against main).
