   **Shared contracts.** Each wave 1 item defines its contract first and pushes it early (a stub PR, or the types in the
   row's first commit), so the wave 2 items can build against it.
   - **#603's `SpawnAttempt`:**
     - `SpawnRule.Spawn` returns an outcome (placed, with the members it put in; or failed, with a `SpawnFailure` reason)
       instead of `bool`.
     - The director has one commit point (e.g. `Director.Commit(SpawnAttempt)`). It charges pressure, starts cooldowns,
       writes the log and counts the night's caps only for a placed attempt; a failed one returns its opportunity.
     - It is used by the pressure, free, stop and special channels alike (the Stoker's and the hounds' own spawns
       included).
   - **#604's `ThreatState`:**
     - Each enemy reports its engagement state (`Dormant`, `Ambient`, `Threatening`, `Engaging`, `Resolved`), derived from
       what it is doing in the Sim, never from distance alone.
     - An enemy damaging a train system (a Hotbox, a feeding pack, a Knotter's work) counts as Threatening even with nobody
       near it.
     - The census and the engaged cap read it.
   - **#606's `EncounterPacing`:**
     - `Progress(...)` returns the max of the night's time fraction and the route's distance fraction, never decreasing.
     - `Rate(progress, crew, cars, tier, tuning)` gives encounter opportunities a minute.
     - Both are pure statics, the tuning a new `director.encounters` block in `enemies.json`.
   - **#607's `EncounterDebt`:**
     - A value type with `Owe(unmet)`, `Decay(dt)` and `Take(allowed)`, capped and decaying.
     - It holds back after a safe zone (a release rate, never a burst) and is deterministic.
     - Its tuning is a `debt` sub-block of `director.encounters`.
   - **#600's `EncounterLog`:**
     - Records for attempts, placements, failures (with #603's reasons), engagements (with #604's states), severities
       (#610), debt (#607) and suppression reasons.
     - Reported as an `encounters` block in `dt harness` and `dt playthrough`, read by #601's sweep.
     - Measured facts and inferred awareness are kept in separate fields (§8): a creature near a player is "near", not
       "seen".

   **Rules every item keeps** (CLAUDE.md, COORDINATION.md, §9):
   - Numbers in `content/tuning`, each block citing its spec section.
   - A deterministic Sim: seeded `Pcg32` streams, a new stream for any new draw, so the night's other draws stay as they
     were. `NetcodeTests` stay green.

   - Safe zones, departure grace, post-crisis recovery and the terminus ban are kept. #602's guard tests prove it.
   - Never weaken an existing test's assertions. Re-picking a night for a test whose night moved is allowed, as #595 did.
   - Every item that changes the nights measures them with #601's quick sweep and says what moved, in its note.
