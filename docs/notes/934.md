   - **Why the driver passed them** (traced 20 Oct on main):
     - The driver's stops are `StopDriver.Decide`'s cruise leg (`Bots/StopCrew.cs`): a wrong switch (`SwitchPlan`), a coaling
       tower (`CoalPlan`), or a facility's spur (`StopPlan.Ahead`, over `Run.Sites`).
     - `Run.Sites` is built from `FeatureKind.Facility` features only (`Run.EnableSites`, `_facilities`), and each target is
       a spur's points with work the crew can do there (`CrewCalls.CanWork`). A halt (a `FeatureKind.Village` stop with
       `StopLayout.Halt` laid) has neither, so it was never a target. It wasn't the loot plan: `--loot` stocks the halts'
       houses (`Run.EnableLoot`), but a bot searches only a facility stop's own village (`StopHand.Village` takes a
       `StopPlan`).
     - Nothing marks a halt as "called for": the route card lists every halt as a timetable row (`LineBuilder.LayRouteCard`,
       kind "halt") with its whistle and name boards, but nothing in the Sim reads them; a player stops there by choice.
     - The only stands at a halt the bots made were by accident: a lit Holdout at its lockup (`ForAHoldout`), a lamp signal,
       or someone left behind.
   - **What this adds** (behind `facilities.json` `crew.haltStandSeconds`, **0, off**):
     - `HaltPlan` (beside `CoalPlan`): the nearest halt ahead not stood at tonight, its hold the train's middle over the
       village's (front at least at the village's start, rear no further than its end: `Director.StopAt`'s stand).
     - The cruise leg takes it when it's near enough to start stopping, before any facility stop or coal nearer on, and only
       with `crew.haltAllowance` (600 s) of the night in hand past the run home (the reckoning every optional stop uses).
     - Two legs: `ToHalt` (the approach, creeping back if it overran), `AtHalt` (stood `haltStandSeconds`, then everyone
       aboard, or `AboardGiveUp` past it, and away). Logged as a `"Halt"` stop record (facility −1).
     - The director counts every halt stood at (`Director.Halts.Stood`), the halt's own encounter on or off.
     - Not in this: the crew doesn't get down to search a halt's houses (`StopHand.Village` is a facility stop's); they stay
       aboard and answer what comes. A halt search for the bots is the next step if the director wants halts worth
       stopping for.
