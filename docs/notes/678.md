678. **The fortress's catalogue: tools, whimsy, fun and silly (U1, queue #410; the director, 13 Oct 2026: "We need train upgrades that make sense for the game loop that will balance against the increase in difficulty that each new run should bring. Some upgrades need to be simply for fun, some purchases need to be silly, some purchases need to have comedic whimsy, and some purchases need to be legitimate tools that will push runs further"). The design is docs/design/upgrades.md; spec F.3 and GDD §11 carry a paragraph each.**
   - **The catalogue's fields** (`UpgradeDef`, campaign.json `upgrades`): `kind` (tool, the default; whimsy; fun; silly), `fromTier` (the tier the consist must work before the fortress sells it: spec F.4, the cars set the tier), `excludes` (upgrades it won't go with, either side naming the other), `line` (its card in the shop) and `tally` (what the yard clerk reads into every delivered night's tally while it's owned). `Campaign.Unavailable` says why one isn't for sale; `BuyUpgrade` refuses on it.
   - **The shop** (`FrontEnd.UpgradeEntries`) shelves them under TOOLS, WHIMSY, FUN and SILLY headings (a shelf with nothing on it isn't shown: no fun is sold yet), each with its line; one stocked from a tier the train doesn't work yet reads "SPEED RECORDER: FRONTIER AND ON" with why. `dt campaign` lists each one's kind and why it's refused.
   - **The clerk** (`Radio.Tally(report, owned)`, `Campaign.TallyLines`): the owned upgrades' tallies, in the catalogue's order, after the salvage and before the costs. `NetPlaySession.OwnedTally` reads them from the setup's upgrades on every machine. The clerk's word bank doesn't have their words yet ("whistle", "gold", "horn", "comedic", "horseshoe", "still up", "commendation", "framed"): the set breaks up over them, as over a typed name, until the audio chat records them (`tools/audio/clerk.py`).
   - **Ten tools** (each a share of a trouble, never all of it): speed recorder (overspeed.leadSeconds ×1.5, Frontier), composition brake shoes (brakeFade ×0.5, Frontier), sprung cradles (fragile ×0.4), pre-cut boards (breach.boardSeconds ×0.5, Frontier), spare radios (kit.radios +2), big extinguishers (carFire.chargeSeconds ×1.5, rechargeSeconds ÷1.5, Frontier), axle oilers (hotbox knock and glow ×1.5, repair ×0.5, Dead lines), feedwater heater (coldEfficiencyPerStep ×0.5, Dead lines), gun rammers (reloadStepSeconds ×0.8, Dead lines), trailing-truck booster (major; performance accel ×1.2, Dead lines). New effect names in `Campaign.Apply`: overspeedLead, brakeFade, fragile, boardUp, radios, accel, coldBoiler, reload, whistle, hotboxKnock, hotboxMend, extinguisher. Spec F.3's seventeen are unchanged and all from Local.
   - **Two whimsy, two silly:** the honk (whistle ×0.35 to the Choir; not with the gold whistle), the lucky horseshoe (foul ×0.85), the gold-plated whistle (2.5 of the next car; whistle ×1.25) and the framed commendation (0.5; nothing but its tally line). The honk still draws the night's first threat as a whistle (note 287's draw has its own weight): only the Choir hears it quieter.
   - **Not sold yet** (note 196: a purchase that does nothing is a bug): the fun shelf (livery, nameplate, festoon lamps, three-chime whistle, hats, emotes, the photographer, a bell), the rest of the whimsy (Gerald, the canary, the pigeon loft, the calliope, the gramophone, the human cannonball, the yard cat, the hot tub), the rest of the silly and seven more tools. docs/design/upgrades.md §4 says what each needs and whose it is.
   - **Balance.** `dt campaign sim` and `CampaignTests.TheStandardCrewReachesTwentyCarsInFiftyFiveToSixtyRuns` are unchanged: the yardstick crew spends shares of the next car, not items. The tools come to 3.6 cars' worth of smalls and 11 of majors against the standard crew's 5 and 15 over a whole campaign, so a crew that lives to the Behemoth can own them all, by the end and not before.
