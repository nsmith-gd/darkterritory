599. **Dawn, seen and felt coming (D1.4, queue #331, at D1's ask over its Wakers, #321 and note 588; docs/design/creatures/wakers.md §2-§4; the director, 9 Oct 2026: "the tension before it is really, really important").** Presentation only: the Sim's rules (D1's and the director's) are untouched.
    - **The sky.** Over the last `stirSeconds` (180) before dawn, a cold line comes up low on the sky behind the train, where they'll rise. It starts as nothing, becomes a wide dim bruise, and by dawn is a thin grey band clear of the treeline and the far ridges.
        - The sky shader (`sky.frag`) adds it toward a flat direction at a strength: `FrameLighting.Stir`, `StirDirection` and `StirColour`, passed as the frame constants `stir` and `stirColour`.
        - Its colours are in look.json `atmosphere.dawn.stir` (`bruise`, `line`, `strength`).
        - `DawnStir.Sky` is how far the stir has come, from the run's dawn clock. `DawnStir.SkyFrom` is behind the train, off its last car, and once one is up, toward the lead Waker.
        - The app and `dt film` pass it to `Views.Lighting`.
    - **The rise.** `WakerRise` is the rise both stand-ins draw (greybox and art), now timed by `riseSeconds` instead of a hard-coded 8. It comes out of the ground quickly in three surges, slowing as it stands.
        - Over the rise and 6 s after (`Effects.WakerRise`), clods are heaved up off its back and flanks and fall back: dark turf and boulders up to metres across, against the pale stir. A bank of earth-dark dust rolls out low round it.
        - On the move (`Effects.WakerStride`), each foot raises dust as it comes down, so the side ones crossing the land are seen by what they raise.
        - From `PhaseSeconds` and the enemy id alone, as every effect is.
    - **The guns at the walls.** A Waker stopped `stopShort` short of the terminus's gate stands under the town's guns (`Effects.WallGuns`). There is one on each of the gatehouse's towers and on the first two wall towers each side (`GreyboxScene.WallGuns`, Fortresses' layout).
        - Each fires on its own beat: the flash and powder smoke of the train's cannon (`CannonShot`).
        - The shot lands on the Waker's back a moment later (`CannonImpact` against a creature), somewhere over its body each time.
        - (No tower has a gun model on main. B2's tower guns are on its open #599, on the departure town; the flashes stand at the towers' tops.)
    - **The tremor in the train.** On each thud of the stir (`DawnStir.Thud`, the same beat as the camera's), two things move:
        - Every car's lanterns swing on their chains about the ceiling, along the car (`DawnStir.LampSway`, up to about 0.2 rad, dying away before the next thud, each car a moment after the one ahead). Their lights swing with them (`SceneArt.LampSwing`, `GreyboxScene.LampSwayOf`).
        - Coal is shaken off the cab bunker's heap onto the footplate (`Effects.CoalSlide`).
    - **Looked at** (`dt screenshot`):
        - `--view stir --stir 0|0.4|0.8|1`: the line behind the train.
        - `--view wakerrise --rise 2|4|7 [--behind 120] [--aside 1]`: `--waker rise`.
        - `--view wakerwall [--clock s]`: `--waker wall`, the train home and one stopped outside, from behind the gatehouse.
        - `--view inside --sway r`, `--view fireman --thud s --stir 1`.
        - At its rise distance (`riseBehind`, 1,200 m) the Waker itself is past the night's fog and behind the land. What reads from the train is the line coming up behind and the land heaving where a gap shows it. Up close (120-200 m) the earth and dust read against the stir.
    - **Verified:** `DawnSeenTests`:
        - the line comes up behind the train over the stir (nothing before it, half up halfway, toward the lead Waker once up);
        - the lanterns are knocked about by the thuds and never swung half over;
        - the rise's curve (under, then quickly half up, then stood, and its seconds run on through the chase);
        - the guns that bear on the walls stand inside the gate, at the towers' tops.
