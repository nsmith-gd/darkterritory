793. **The town flag's flutter never races (C1, queue #520; the director, 14 Oct, `docs/playtest/2026-10-14.md` line 2: "The flag blowing in the wind looks good. It's a little fast sometimes, though").**
E1's town flag (queue #405, note 674) flies on `FlagKit.Pose`'s chain: a ripple running out along it at a pace that rises from `pace` to `paceFull` as it lifts, and with each gust. Its phase was the scene's clock times that pace. So each time the pace changed with a gust, the phase jumped by the change times the whole time since the night began. A few minutes in, the fly was flung about from frame to frame instead of rippling: "fast sometimes", and worse the later it got.
- **The fix:** a `FlagClock` for each town's flag, kept by `WorldArt` from frame to frame, runs the ripple on from where it was at the pace of the moment.
    - A gust now quickens the flag without jumping it.
    - After more than a second unseen, or with the clock run back, it starts as if it had run at that pace. One-off poses (a screenshot, the tests) have no clock and are as before.
- **The tuning is unchanged** (look.json `atmosphere.flag`): the speeds were right, and only the jumps were wrong.
- **Verified:**
    - `FlagTests.ItNeverRacesAsTheGustsComeAndGoLateInTheNight`: drawn at 60 fps for 20 s through a gusting wind 45 minutes in, the fly's corner moves at no more than 9 m/s.
    - Without the clock, the same run reaches 14.1 m/s. It's 13.7 from ten minutes in and 14.5 at two and a half hours, and 7.3 at the night's start.
    - The rest of `FlagTests` is green.
