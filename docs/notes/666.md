666. **Skips in the morning build (D1, queue #397; the director, 13 Oct, playing the morning build).**
"I'm getting audio skips. The audio seems buggy. The performance seems a little skippy ... I am getting some performance
skips."

What it was (D1 profiled the game itself, 13 Oct, without waiting for the log: the app on frontier:7 with three bots
riding, under `dotnet-counters`, `dotnet-trace --profile gc-verbose` and `dotnet-gcdump`; and `dt harness --bots 4` for
the sim's own share). Under lavapipe the app draws about one frame a second, so a hitch can't be timed here; what follows
is what the frames spend their memory and time on, and why a long frame is heard.

- **The audio skips are slow frames heard.** The mixer rendered on the game thread: `FeedSpeaker` filled the device
  to about 60 ms once a frame, so any frame past ~65 ms (a GC, a town's houses built, a cell's mesh uploaded) emptied the
  queue, and the device played a gap. AU1's #398 (note 667, PR #914) feeds the speaker from a thread of its own
  (`AudioPump`), which is the fix; D1's stopgap here (80 ms, topped up mid-frame) was dropped for it.
- **Every frame read the sound folders.** `SoundBank.Refresh` (the hot reload) listed all 681 files under
  `content/audio` every frame: about 60,000 file-system calls a second and 92 MB of garbage in four minutes. The walls'
  and spaces' reloads were the same. Now once a second (`GameAudio.ReloadEverySeconds`): an edit is still heard within a
  second.
- **The ground's height made a list every time it was asked.** `TerrainField.Height` (everything standing on the
  ground: the dressing, the towns, the crew's feet) built a `List<Near>` per call: 18% of everything the game allocated,
  ~265 MB in four minutes. Now each thread borrows one from a small pool (Height is reentrant through the roads, and the
  cells are built on workers). Same heights to the bit.
- **The large-object heap.** 2.4 GB of it, 400–630 MB fragmented; the working set 5.9 GB. Its churn is the mesh building:
  `Vertex[]` arrays from `WorldArt.BuildCell`, `MeshBuilder.Append`, `PlanDressing` and the kits' houses
  (`MaritimeKit.House`): 367 MB in four minutes, every one a large object, so each gen-2 collection is a long frame.
  Pooling those arrays and building a town's houses off the frame is B2's (#215/#595, loading in the fog).
- **The rest, smaller:** the net snapshots (`WorldRecords` Capture/Apply/ReadDelta/ToBody, ~200 MB in four minutes on
  the host), `Bodies.Contact`, `Town.Around`, boxed enumerators (`Solid`, `Door`), Opus decoding. And the JIT is still
  compiling ~800 methods every 5 s minutes in (ReadyToRun or tiered PGO in the package would take that off the frame).
- In the harness (host and four bots, 600 s): ~30 MB allocated a sim second, 128 gen-0, 2 gen-1 and 4 gen-2 collections,
  0.51 s paused in all. The app's steady rate was ~10 MB/s.
