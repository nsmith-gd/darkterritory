560. **The line's boards read from the cab (queue #299, E1; the director, 9 Oct 2026: "I cannot read any of the signs with words on the rail, they need to be massive like billboards and have clear sight lines and be illuminated by the lamp for a long while").** The boards were the size of real ones (a facility board 2 m across, its letters 0.4 m), 3.2 m off the rail on the right behind the telegraph poles, among the trees, and lit only within the headlamp's 120 m. From the cab's 75° a name was a few pixels until it was alongside, and by then it was behind the cab's corner pillar.
    - **As big as their words** (`content/linegen/signage.json` `letterM`, `lineChars`; `BoardLayout`, which the sim and the art share):
        - A name's capitals are 2.8 m: a font pixel of 0.4 m, about a screen pixel at 175 m at 720p.
        - A speed's figures are 3.2 m and a km post's 1.4 m.
        - A facility board is about 18 m by 12 m, in the HUD's 5x7 font.
        - A tunnel's plate spans its portal over the arch.
    - **Stood back and turned in** (`billboard`, `LinesideProps.Place`):
        - On the left: across the line from the telegraph poles, which stood in every right-hand board's sight line, and where the cab's front glass sees widest. `billboard.side` 0 gives back the plan's side.
        - The near edge is 4.5 m out. The bottom is 3.5 m over the rail, or 2.5 m over the land if that's higher, so a board sits up out of cuttings. It's turned 10° toward the cab.
        - Boards within 30 m of each other hang on one set of posts, the warning on top, and none stands within 30 m in front of another.
    - **Where it's seen.** The driver's eye is checked every 15 m from the board's reading distance to 40 m short of it. From each point it must see the face's middle and lower corners past the land and the stops' buildings, within the lamp's 18°.
        - A board seen over less than three quarters of its approach moves 15 m at a time, up to 150 m, nearest first (a warning only back toward the train).
        - Failing that, it goes to the other side. It also goes there where it can't stand on the left: an alternate's track, a road or water.
        - A board never stands on a bridge, by a tunnel's mouth, or up over a bore.
        - Seen over its approach: frontier:7 96%, deadLines:2 97.5%, deepTerritory:2 95%. One mail board on frontier:7, on a ledge round a hillside, can't be seen from anywhere it may stand.
    - **Sight lines kept clear in the sim** (`LinesideProps.InBoardsSight`).
        - Trees (their crowns), boulders and kit pieces in the triangles between those eye points and the face's ends aren't dealt, so the art and the walls agree. The branch lines' pines go too; the telegraph poles stay.
        - It's pure and `DMath`-only, so every machine clears alike. The lineside checksum and the woods change slightly.
        - Standing a night's boards takes about half a second, once per route.
    - **Lit by the lamp a long while** (`WorldArt.Glow`). The paler of a board's paint and its letters is reflective (emissive 0.9); the other is plain.
        - In the beam, the glow rises from 0.8 close to 2.0 at the reading distance, since the fog takes more of it further off.
        - Off the beam, or with the lamps down, it's 0.06: nothing in the dark until it's close, as sight.json's `darkSignRange` has it.
        - At 60 km/h, a name board is made out for about 15 s and read for 9–12 s.
    - **The sim's own boards** (sight.json's advance limits, LOW, MAIL, END) stand and stack the same way. The lineside clears for them at the default tuning, which the content matches; a test holds it.
    - **Hand-laid lines and weak bridges.** A hand-laid line's boards stand back from the rail. On a generated line, a weak bridge's car count is its plan's `bridgeLimit` board, and WorldFeatures' hand-laid pair isn't drawn there.
    - **Headless.**
        - `dt linegen boards --route r` lists each board: its words, where the plan put it and where it stands, its size and height, how much of its approach sees it, and the cost.
        - `dt screenshot --view cab --board type[:n] --ahead m` frames a board from the cab. The gallery has board-cab, board-speed and board-tunnel.
        - Tests: `BoardSightTests`, `SignKitTests` and `LinesideArtTests`' glow.
    - **Readable as you drive past them, `pt13-signs`** (the director, 13 Oct, on the morning build, which didn't have this: "I still can't read the signs when I'm driving past them. They're not big enough, and there's no clear enough sight lines to see them. Some signs are overlapping."; carried on by B4 from E1's #835).
        - **No board in front of another.** From 250 m in (`billboard.clearFromM`, where a name's 2.8 m capitals are made out) until it's passed, no other board's face covers any of a board's words. The test is a 5 × 3 grid over the lettering (`BoardSite.FaceGrid`), from every 5 m of the approach (`AboutEye`), against every board (the plan's, the sim's mail, limit and END boards, the km posts). A board that would stand in front of one already stood (`Hides`) doesn't stand there either. On main the resume "60" stood in front of RENWICK YARD 0.5 KM on frontier:7, and the sim's MAIL board in front of the Maddox Bridge board.
        - **Merged where apart they'd hide.** Boards within `stackM` (60 m) on a side are one board on one set of posts, if that's read better than apart (`StandRun`: metres of approach seen, less those another board is in front). At most `stackMaxM` (24 m) tall. If the first can't be read anywhere near (frontier:7's mail board on a hillside ledge), it's stood after the rest, out of their way. Two boards saying the same thing aren't both stood. Nor is a plan board whose words already stand within its reading back down the line (a bridge's plate at each end, now both facing the train).
        - **The cab's own frame.** The driver's eye is the cab view's, `eyeAcrossM` 1.1 m right of the line's middle. Where any of a board's words is behind the right front window's outer pillar (8.6°–14.2° right of the heading) or the middle post (−35.3° to −32°), it isn't seen there (`cabBlindDeg`; `CabSightTests` hold both to `TrainKit.FrontWindow` and the side slits). So boards go left wherever the left will take them. A board that must stand on the right (the left is a yard's or a road's ground) is behind the pillar from about 85 m to 45 m: it's read before that.
        - **The km posts.** They stand at their km (`fixed`: nudged at most 30 m to be seen), on the right by the poles (`side: 1`), low (0.8 m over the rail, its own `overGroundM` 0.5), with their tops under the driver's eye line, so they're never in front of a billboard's words. A billboard keeps `postClearM` (20 m) from one on its side, not `stackM`.
        - **A bridge's name** warns now: only ever moved back toward the bridge (frontier:7's had gone on past it and was read through the truss).
        - **Size, unchanged from E1's.** At 1280 × 720 (469 px to the radian) a name's 2.8 m capitals are 8 px tall from 164 m and a speed's 3.2 m figures from 187 m. At 60 km/h that's 7 s of reading before the 40 m mark (more at 1080p).
        - **Measured** (`dt linegen boards`, its `overlapped` list and each board's `inWay`): no board in front of another on frontier:7, deadLines:2, deepTerritory:2, deadLines:1 or frontier:1; one 15 m stretch on local:3's J2 1.0 KM. Seen over their approaches (stricter now: the cab's frame and the other boards count): 87%, 95%, 91%, 89%, 92%, 86%. Standing a night's boards takes about 1 s on the cloud box (0.4–1.1 s), cached land answers included.
