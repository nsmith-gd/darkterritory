634. **Bots fight a burning car together: two hands, before it's well alight (D1.4, queue #364; the director, the inbox, 12 Oct: `D1-bots-two-extinguishers`, "a"; built as two before it's well alight, D1's call of 12 Oct).** A car's fire is two hands' at most. The first goes in by the car's front door to its own extinguisher; the second goes in by the rear with an extinguisher brought from the car behind. A car already well alight nobody goes into: it burns itself out while the cars either side are kept from catching, and nothing is cut loose.
    - **The decision.** The director chose "a, two together", whose ask read: "When a car is well alight and two hands are free, they go in together, one from each end, and fight it out. A lone hand still keeps out and the car is cut loose."
    - **Why it's built as two before it's well alight.** As built first, the pair worked: each walker took an extinguisher from the car on its side and was at its end door within 17 s. But under the fire as it plays (enemies.json `carFire`), two can't put out a car that's well alight.
        - **Inside the end door**, the nearest cells burn 6–10 a second. Two walkers, one in at each end of car 3 at full blaze, fell from 100 to 20 in 7 s without getting the spray on it.
        - **From the plate**, spraying in through the open end door (prototyped behind a flag, not shipped): two full 15 s charges took the fire from 0.97 to 0.79, and it was back at 1.0 within 15 s. With 30 s or 45 s charges it still burned after 160 s, keeping 0.68–0.71 of the cargo against 0.67 left alone; at 45 s, cars 2 and 4 caught through the open doors.
        - **The arithmetic:** a car is 72 cells (`carFire.cellSize` 1.5). The door's spray reaches about 32 of them, and the cells further in keep relighting the near ones.
        - **Left alone**, a whole car at full blaze burns itself out in about 110 s and loses about a third of its cargo; the walkers put out what it sets going in the cars either side.
        - **Cut loose**, it would lose all its cargo, and for any car but the last, every car behind it. So nothing is cut loose.
        - **Caught early**, two win: with a third of the car alight, two walkers have it out before it's well alight (the test below), where one alone lets it reach full blaze.
    - **What the bots do now** (`RoofWalkerBot`, `CrewCalls.ClaimFire`, `WarmUp.HoseEnd`):
        - **Two hands at most** (`CrewCalls.ClaimFire`): in the order they say so, held while they say so each tick, as for a loose pin. A third leaves the fire to them. A walker claims down its own list of trouble, nearest first, and takes the first fire it gets a hand on.
        - **The first hand in by the front door** (`RoofWalkerBot.FrontIn`), where there's a car ahead (not the engine) and the front end isn't ablaze. The car's own extinguisher hangs 2 m in from its front end (`World.ExtinguisherMount`). In by the rear, the first hand walked two thirds of the car's length through the fire to it and burned. This was so on main too, and is the commonest way a fire got away from one hand. The warm-up drops onto the plate behind the car ahead, from that car's roof or this one's, and goes in through the front door (`WarmUp.Plan`, `ToDoor` and `In` by `_inDoor`).
        - **The second hand brings its own** (`RoofWalkerBot.Second`): into the car behind (or else the car ahead), one with a charged extinguisher and no fire, for that extinguisher (`Fetch`). Then it goes out by that car's end door facing the fire, across the plate, and in at the fire's door (`WarmUp.HoseEnd`, `Hose`, `Toward`). It goes only to a fire that's alight (smoke is one hand's), and only with a way to bring one. At a stop it goes in at that car's side door from the ground (`StopHand.IntoTrouble`), as the stop's hands do.
        - **D1.5's stop case** (deadLines:7 seed 1's wreck yard): both winch hands went to car 5's fire, the second with nothing to fight it with, and the winch stood for the whole loading leg. Now the winch's second hand goes only with an extinguisher to bring.
        - **A car well alight** (`RoofWalkerBot.WellAlight`, 0.85, unchanged from note 539): nobody goes into it while it is. Once it's burning down it's a fire like any.
        - **A walker keeps to its side of the fire** (`Fight`): the side it stands off is its side of the fire's heart (the heat-weighted middle), not of the nearest cell. With cells alight just in front of it and just behind, the nearest cell flipped from tick to tick and the walker with it. The second hand, in at car 3's rear door, went back and forth on the spot and burned from 100 to 30 without spraying.
    - **Tests** (`TwoHosesTests`):
