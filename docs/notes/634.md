634. **A burning car beaten by two together: 16 fire cells, twice the spray, and the bots' pair (D1.4, queue #364; the director, the inbox, 12 Oct: `D1-bots-two-extinguishers`, "a", and `D1.4-burning-car-beatable`, "b").** A car's fire is 16 cells and the spray's twice as strong, so one extinguisher has a car at full blaze out in about 10 s. A fire is two hands' at most. The first goes in by the car's front door, beside its own extinguisher; the second goes in by the rear, bringing one from the car behind. A car well alight is gone into by two together, never by one, and nothing is cut loose.
    - **The decisions.**
        - "a": "When a car is well alight and two hands are free, they go in together, one from each end, and fight it out. A lone hand still keeps out and the car is cut loose."
        - Built for it first, the pair couldn't win under the fire as it played (72 cells of 1.5 m, 1 a second of spray):
            - inside the end door, the nearest cells burned 6–10 a second, and two walkers fell from 100 to 20 in 7 s without getting the spray on it;
            - from the plate at the open end door (prototyped, not shipped), two full 15 s charges took the fire from 0.97 to 0.79, and it was back at 1.0 in 15 s;
            - a car is 72 cells, and the door's spray reaches about 32;
            - left alone it burned itself out in about 110 s with a third of its cargo.
        - So the director was asked whether a car well alight should be beatable by two (`D1.4-burning-car-beatable`). "b": "72 patches of fire per car is far too many, make it 15 and have the extinguisher extinguish far more of the fire. A car should be extinguished from full blaze within 20 seconds."
    - **The numbers** (enemies.json `carFire`; spec B.11 with them):
        - `cellSize` 1.5 → 3.5: 16 cells in a cargo car, 4 along, and the floor, the roof and each wall one across. The grid is the car's length × (2 across + 2 up), so 15 isn't one it can make; 16 is the nearest.
        - `sprayPerSecond` 1 → 2: a cell at full blaze is out in half a second of spray, and the cells round it cool at 0.8 a second.
        - `burnPatch` 0.75, new: the fire's burn reach is counted from that far round a burning cell's centre. It was half a cell, so 3.5 m cells would have burned 1 m further, with the walls of the stretch a walker stood in right on top of it. 0.75 is half the 1.5 m cells the burn was tuned on.

    - **What the bots do** (`RoofWalkerBot`, `CrewCalls.ClaimFire`, `WarmUp.HoseEnd`):
        - **Two hands at most** (`CrewCalls.ClaimFire`): in the order they say so, held while they say so each tick, as for a loose pin. A third leaves the fire to them. A walker claims down its own list of trouble, nearest first, and takes the first fire it gets a hand on.
        - **The first hand goes in by the front door** (`FrontIn`), where there's a car ahead (not the engine). The car's own extinguisher hangs 2 m in from its front end (`World.ExtinguisherMount`).
            - Going in by the rear, the first hand walked two thirds of the car through the fire to reach it and burned. That was so on main too, and on 3.5 m cells it took `FireSenseTests`' walker to 18.
            - The warm-up drops onto the plate behind the car ahead, from that car's roof or this one's, and goes in through the front door (`WarmUp.Plan`, `ToDoor` and `In` by `_inDoor`).
        - **The second hand brings its own** (`Second`): into the car behind (or else the car ahead) for that car's extinguisher (`Fetch`), then out by the end door facing the fire, across the plate, and in at the fire's door (`WarmUp.HoseEnd`, `Hose`, `Toward`).
            - It goes only to a fire that's alight (smoke is one hand's), and only with a way to bring one.
            - At a stop it goes into that car at its side door from the ground (`StopHand.IntoTrouble`), as the stop's hands do. So the winch's second hand goes to a fire only with an extinguisher to bring: D1.5's dead lines wreck yard (deadLines:7 seed 1) had both winch hands on car 5's fire, the second with nothing to fight it with, while the winch stood the whole loading leg.
        - **A car well alight** (`WellAlight`, 0.85) is gone into by two together.
            - The first hand waits, its claim kept, until a second is on it with an extinguisher to bring (`CrewCalls.FireHands`). Then both go, through ends that are ablaze.
            - One alone keeps out (note 539; `FireSenseTests` with no crew calls, `TwoHosesTests` with them).
            - Nothing is cut loose. The decision's "and the car is cut loose" was for a car that couldn't be beaten. A car cut loose loses all its cargo, and for any car but the last, every car behind it; one beaten loses a few seconds' burn.
        - **A walker keeps to its side of the fire** (`Fight`): the side it stands off is its side of the fire's heart (the heat-weighted middle), not of the nearest cell. With cells alight just in front of it and just behind, the nearest cell flipped from tick to tick and the walker with it. The second hand went back and forth on the spot inside car 3's rear door and burned from 100 to 30 without spraying.
