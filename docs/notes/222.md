222. **The look-out in a crew of two (note 212's "not yet"; GDD §34; WP17).** The look-out was the crew's last walker, and a crew of two has none: below `Harness.FiremanFrom` (6) there's no fireman, so it's the driver and the gunner. The Gaunt, the Draggers and Ribbits stayed dormant at crew 2.
    - **The gunner is the look-out when there's no walker** (`Harness.Run`, `GunnerBot.Errand`, `LookErrand.Wants`). Its legs are a walker's already (`RoofWalkerBot`), so it's the same errand run by the same legs. It isn't the fireman: there's none at two, and the one in a bigger crew can't leave the cab at speed (the tender's full width).
    - **Only while the gun can spare it:** off the gun to look only with no Cinder Hound coming on, after the repair kit, the cold and its part at a stop. `LookErrand.Wants` is the errand's own reading (a step to take, or a lip on another car to make for), so it can't disagree with what the legs then do. Nothing left to look at from where it stands, and it's back to its usual round: the gun, or in for a bag at a crane, as it does without the errand (checked by hand: `dt harness --insist Gaunt,Dragger --bots 2`, and the new `--no-look` for the night without it).
    - Intent only, deterministic, enemy rules untouched. The trace shows the gunner's errand (`gunner[look:Gaunt]`).
    - **Before / after** (`dt balance --pairs --only Gaunt,Dragger,Ribbit --crews 2`: frontier:7, one seed):
      - Before: 3 dormant; never exercised Dragger 2, Ribbit 2, Gaunt 2.
      - After: 2 fair (Gaunt+Dragger, Gaunt+Ribbit), 1 trivial (Dragger+Ribbit / cold); nothing never exercised. `pass` is false on that trivial verdict, as at crew 4 (note 212): left for balance.
    - Test: `AuditTests.ACrewOfTwosGunnerIsTheLookOut`, a 30 s insisted Gaunt+Dragger night with two bots. Without the look-out, both are placed and neither comes on; with it, both do.
    - **Unaffected:** a crew of two with no guns (`--no-combat`) has a walker in place 1, and crews of three and up have walkers too; they keep their look-out.
