199. **A dropped joiner is told, and doesn't see itself (audit order P2's rehearsal).** Before the two-machine test, the 4 Oct rehearsal ran it here: two real app windows (xvfb, software Vulkan), one `--host`ing, one `--join`ing over UDP. At the software renderer's 0 fps the joiner's link timed out (8 s of silence, `DatagramOptions.TimeoutSeconds`), and what it showed then was wrong in two ways, either of which a slow machine stalling for 8 s, the host quitting, or the wifi dropping would hit.
    - **It drew itself.** On a drop `ClientSession` clears `PlayerId`, and `RemoteIds` filtered on it, so the joiner's own player came back as a remote: listed again ("CREW 2" under YOU) and drawn round its own eyes. It keeps who the host said it was (`_was`) and filters on that.
    - **It never said so.** `NetPlaySession.Lost` was set only while the client still read as connected, which the drop had just ended, so the HUD sat on "connecting…" with "HOST" in the corner. `ClientSession.Dropped` (a disconnect after the welcome) sets it: "CONNECTION LOST", and "NO LINK" in red where the ping was.
    - **The joiner's lobby lines.** A joiner was shown the host's "EVERYONE IN? DRIVE OUT OF THE YARD" (and, with no ping, "A PRIVATE NIGHT"); it reads "THE HOST DRIVES OUT WHEN EVERYONE'S IN".
    - **The ping** is just "100 MS" at the big size: "PING 100 MS" ran into the route strip at 1280 wide.
    - Rerun, the joiner held its link ("2 aboard, ping 100 ms") and its screen read right. Tests: `NetcodeTests.ADroppedClientKnowsItAndDoesntSeeItselfAsCrew`.
