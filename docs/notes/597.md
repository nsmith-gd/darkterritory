597. **The bot leaves your steam alone (queue #329, D1; the director's in-game note 2, 9 Oct 2026: the bot venting pressure all the time).**
   - **Why.** With steam driving (T97) pressure is speed. The bot fireman goes out to the blow-off whenever the gauge is more than `VentOver` over the steam for the speed the line allows and the train's over that speed (T106: venting and braking as a pair). That's right when a bot drives to the boards. With someone playing at the controls (note 574), every time they ran faster than the bot would, it went to vent their steam down. It was fighting their driving from the blow-off, as the bot driver had fought them for the brake.
   - **Now** (`ConductorBot.Vent`): while someone playing holds the controls, the steam's theirs. The fireman vents only with the gauge in the red: the safety valve held shut, a Stoker's fire. A bot driving vents with its fireman as before.
   - **Verified.** `VentTests.WithSomeonePlayingAtTheControlsTheFiremanLeavesTheirSteamAloneTillItsInTheRed`: someone playing runs a long descent hard on the regulator. The fireman doesn't vent; without this change it blew the gauge down to 73. With the gauge put in the red, the valve held shut, it vents. `RunningAwayDownhillOnItsSteamTheFiremanVentsWhileTheDriverBrakes` (a bot driving) still holds.
