722. **Emotes for two, and more of them (K1, queue #456; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md` §3, GDD App. F.6).**
The friendslop audit's Friends goal (note 733: "It's extremely important that friends exceeds our target meaningfully"). Emotes that need a second person are a friend's thing by definition. Approved with the rest of friendslop.md §3 ("The rest ... goes ahead").

   - **What it was:** note 298's wheel (hold J): DANCE, WAVE, POINT, and note 740's ABOARD, four spokes. Each emote a record the crew see for its length (`RecordKind.Emote`: tick, who, which), posed by IK (`CreatureArt.EmoteArms`) until clips come. Nothing for two people, and nowhere to sit.
   - **GDD §9 first:** "Emotes for two, and more of them".
   - **The wheel** (`EmoteWheel`): twelve places round the crosshair, like a clock (`EmoteWheel.Clock`). DANCE 12, WAVE 9, POINT 3 and ABOARD 6 stay where they were. HIGH FIVE 1, FIST BUMP 2, SIT 4, LEAN OUT 5. A place with nothing in it isn't drawn; leaning at it picks a neighbour. The labels sit a little further out (30 px, not 22) so they don't overlap at 30°.
   - **U1's slots** (their open PR #967, docs/design/upgrades.md §4, "more emotes"): `EmoteWheel.Kept` keeps the bow at 7, the salute at 8 and the conga at 10, as `Emote` values 9, 10 and 11. 11 o'clock is free.
     - Note 740's emote byte carries them as it is: six bits for the emote (0-63), the wheel's step in the top two. U1 needs no bit 7 of the intent's third byte and no wire change for its three.
     - Values 0-8 are used. After U1's three, **52 values are free (12-63).**
   - **The emotes** (`Emote.HighFive`, `FistBump`, `SitEdge`, `LeanOut`; the shared rules are `EmoteRules`, the host's side `World.EmoteAct` in World.Emote.cs):
     - **The high five:** offered for `emotes.highFive` (3 s). It's answered when a crewmate picks it too within `emotes.reach` (1.5 m, feet to feet, on a level), the two facing each other within `emotes.facingDegrees` (50°). The hands meet on that tick. The offer's record and the answer's both carry the meet tick and each other (`EmoteEvent.Meet`, `With`). It's shown met for `emotes.met` (0.8 s) from the meet. Unanswered, it times out, hanging there and sagging.
     - **The fist bump:** the same, `emotes.fistBump`. A fist bump doesn't answer a high five.
     - **The dance-off** (`EmoteRules.DanceOff`): two or more dancing within `emotes.danceOffReach` (3 m), each pair facing within `danceOffFacingDegrees` (70°). The lead goes round them by id every `danceOffBeat` (2 s), from the host's tick, so every machine draws the same lead. Presentation only: the dance is the same record.
     - **Sit on the edge** (`EmoteRules.SitAt`): a roof within `emotes.edgeReach` (0.6 m) of where its footing ends at the side and drops more than `edgeDrop` (0.5 m). The same goes for anything else of a car's stood on outside it (a door landing, the guard van's platform, the engine's running board). From inside, in an open side doorway, the edge can be up to edgeReach + `sillReach` (1.2 m) out: across the sill and a cargo car's landing (the cab's doorway onto its running board too).
     - **Lean out** (`EmoteRules.LeanAt`): standing on the floor in a car or the cab within edgeReach of the side, with nothing of the car between them and outside at the knee (0.5 m) and the chest (1.3 m) (`Rescue.Clear`). So an open side door or the cab's doorway counts, and a shut door or the cab's waist-high side under its windows doesn't.
     - The sit and the lean are **held** (no clock). They stand up on any move or a jump, or once they're no longer at the edge (`EmoteRules.StandsUp`): shoved, knocked, staggered, lifted, the door shut. **The motor does nothing new:** stood still in a car's frame, you stay in it at any speed, and looking about doesn't stand you.
   - **The shove (note 717) still applies:** a sitter or leaner is as shoveable as anyone standing (`Horseplay.Shoveable`). Off their footing, the sit is over, and a shove at speed off a car can kill, as intended.
   - **Bringing them home (note 723):**
     - A carried crewmate can't emote at all (`EmoteRules.CanEmote`; `Rescue.CarriedIntent` already stripped the pick).
     - **The call: a lift takes a sitter off the edge.** The lift goes ahead, and every emote of theirs ends on the tick they're picked up (`World.StepRescue`). It isn't blocked: picking up a friend sat on a roof's edge is how you get them back from it.
     - Carrying a friend, you can't sit or lean (`Settled`).
   - **Bots** (`Heed.HighFive`, from `BotCrew.Think`): a high five or fist bump held up to a bot (the offerer within reach and facing it, `EmoteRules.OfferedTo`) is answered. It stands, turns to them, and once it faces them and `emotes.botAnswer` (0.4 s) has passed since the offer, it picks the same. This is the same intent a player's answer is. Not the driver of a moving train. Bots never offer.
   - **Drawn** (`CrewActs.WithEmote`, `EmoteDetail` on `Crewmate.EmoteWith`; `CreatureArt.EmoteArms`, `SitOnEdge`; poses `CrewPose.HighFive`, `FistBump`, `DanceOff`, `SitEdge`, `LeanOut`, clips for E1: `high_five`, `fist_bump`, `dance_off`, `sit_edge`, `lean_out`):
     - **The high five:** offered, the hand up and a little forward, sagging and the head tipping the longer it hangs. Met, both right hands to the middle of the gap between the two (leant into when it's wide), then let down.
     - **The fist bump:** the same at the chest.
     - **The dance-off:** the lead's jig thrown bigger; the rest clap it.
     - **The sit:** drawn out at the edge (`CrewActs.SatOn`, to the last footing at their height), facing out over it. The body is let down onto the seat, the thighs out over it, the shins hanging, the hands on the edge. The sitter's own eyes come down to `Eyes.SatOnEdge` (0.92 m).
     - **The lean:** drawn on the sill facing along the car the way they look (`CrewActs.LeantOut`). Tipped out past the jamb, the inner hand on the jamb, the outer arm loose, the head turned to the look.
     - The crew photo (note 721) stands a sitter or leaner in the line without it.
   - **Sounds:** the meet plays once per pair, at the hands. Until AU1's own takes come, the high five uses `crew-mishaps.bare-slap` and the fist bump `crew-mishaps.body-knock`.
   - **Protocol 70** (renumbered at merge): an emote record carries the meet tick and with whom (`[tick, by, kind, meet, with]`). The intent is unchanged: the emote byte carries 5-8.
   - **Views:** `highfive` (a high five met, a fist bump met, one offered to nobody, on car 2's roof), `danceoff` (three in a ring), `sitedge` (a roof's edge and a door's landing), `leanout` (a cargo car's door, and the cab's doorway). All from about 6 m off the train's side (`Staging.Emotes`, `Staging.EmoteCamera`; dt screenshot --view, or --emotes).
