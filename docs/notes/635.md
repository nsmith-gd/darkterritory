   **Built.**
   - **A night's cap and a night's chance, per kind** (`director.perNight`, `director.nightChance`; `Director.Tonight`). The director sends no more of a kind than its cap a night (its own sends, `Director.Log`), and a kind with a chance is rolled once a night from the seed: out, it isn't sent at all. The roll is its own stream from the scrambled seed (the dragger drop's lesson, note 435: a PCG stream's first draw barely moves between neighbouring seeds), in name order, so the director's other draws are as they were. The Knotter: at most 2, about on 3 nights in 4 (0.75). The vote ballot (a dead crewmate's pick), the hound run and the dragger drop all ask `Tonight` too.
   - **How often, per kind** (`director.weights`, ×1 not named): a kind's weight among what could come, multiplied. Nothing set yet; it's the Tweaker's "how often".
   - **The night things run** (wakers.md §2; `wakers.nightFleesSeconds` 60, `wakers.staysAtDawn` the Knotter and Hotbox; `World.NightFlees`). From a minute before dawn every night creature but those named lets go and is gone, as one driven out of a fort is (`Enemy.Dismiss`), and the director sends none of them after. Not a night creature: the hazards (a car fire, the Drift, the Sleepers, the Wakers), the trouble in the cars, Dave and Jacob. Negative seconds turn it off. It runs with the Wakers off too (the example mod's Late Dispatch): the night's over at dawn either way. `World.FledAtDawn` counts them, and `dt harness` reports it (`threats.fledAtDawn`).
   - **The Creature Tweaker** (claude.ai/artifact/TdoxfYnmZTgv2jc9JmRkkw; source `tools/tweaker/creature-tweaker.html`). A card per creature, the 23 the director spends on: how often (×), cost, most in a night, nights it comes at all (%), the tier weights where it has them, "Leaves at dawn", and a note to the agents; the night-wide seconds before dawn. Each card shows what's in the game beside each value and goes amber while the director's values differ from it (or a note waits for an answer). The store: `creatures/<name>` with `want` (the director's) and `game` (main's), `note`/`reply`; `settings/night`. Only the owner and editors write; everyone who can open it reads. COORDINATION's "The Creature Tweaker" says how a change goes in.

   - **The Tweaker as a matrix** (the director, 12 Oct, after it went up: "What does cost to night mean in creature tweaker? Let's get this all set up as a matrix so we can see when one number change influences another"). One table per tier, every creature's numbers and beside them its estimate a night with the director's values, against the game's, and what was measured; under it an influence grid, each creature changed alone (how often ×1.5, cost +1, or taken out) against every creature's count a night. "How the numbers work together" at its head says what cost is: the pressure a creature takes off when it's sent (`pressure.reliefPerCost` 3 × its cost), so the quiet after it; the budget doesn't bind on a 7 km night (the director spends about 45 of its 360 on frontier:7).
     - **The estimate** (`tools/tweaker/calibrate.py`, written to the store's `model/calibration`): the night's pressure is shared, so a night sends creatures until their costs have used up what the measured nights used (`relief`, in cost), split by each kind's measured rate when it can come × How often × its tier weight; a cap or a night's chance takes a kind out and its share goes to the rest; the Stoker comes on its own (a hot firebox). Calibrated from this build's nights, 16 on frontier:7 and 8 each on local:7, deadLines:7 and deepTerritory:7, and the Knotter's own rate from main's uncapped frontier nights. It gives back what it was calibrated on within 0.1 a kind; main's 16 nights from main's settings it puts at 16.5 a night against 18.2 measured (the Draggers 2.5 against 4.1).
