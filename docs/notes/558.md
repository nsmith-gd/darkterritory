558. **The Ribbits as the director met them (queue #297, E1; the director, 9 Oct 2026, playing main: "The Ribbits jumped towards me in a weird diagonal navigation and they overlapped each other a lot. The nav mesh for enemies with them seems off. I didnt see the tongue when they caught me. There was no blood or lay down of me when they started eating me").** Two causes and two gaps:
    - **The diagonal hop and the missing tongue were one bug.** `Crewmate` is a `readonly record struct`, so the scene's `Crew?.FirstOrDefault(c => c.Id == ...)` returned a default crewmate at the world's origin on a miss, not null. The local player is never in `Crew` (that's the others), so a Ribbit after you faced, hopped and lashed its tongue toward a fixed far point. The devour clip, which needs you near, never played. The Moose, Gannet, Knotter and Dave lookups had the same bug.
        - The scene now has `Self`: you, as a crewmate, from `IPlaySession.Self` (`NetPlaySession`: alive and not spectating). `Whom(id)` looks through the others and then you, and returns null on a miss.
        - The Grumbler's and the Soot Child's "nearest of the crew" choice now goes through `Everyone()`, so it takes you in too.
    - **The pile-up was the sim's hop.** Every Ribbit hopped straight at the catch, with nothing keeping them apart (`Outside.cs`, `Ribbit.Place`/`Hop`).
        - The leader (the lowest id) still comes straight in to the hop's 0.8 m.
        - The others each make for a place of their own, `fan`° a rank apart either side of the leader, `ringOut` m from the catch. They blend over to it within the last `fanFrom` m.
        - A packmate closer than `spacing` m pushes them off. A landing that would end too near one is slid round it, not stopped (stopping blocked the flankers).
        - When the leader grabs, the others still lined up ride COMMIT in with it. If the leader breaks off, they do too.
        - All four numbers are in `content/tuning/enemies.json` ribbits: fan 100, ringOut 1.05, fanFrom 10, spacing 1.15. The fan is past a right angle so that, with the catch down, the others are at their sides.
        - `DemoRosterTests.APackSpreadsRoundItsCatchAndKeepsApart` pins it: three Ribbits from nearly one spot stay over 0.86 m apart, spread over 90° round the catch, and end within reach.
    - **Lay down.** `CreatureArt.RibbitEating` is a Ribbit holding its catch (GRAB or PUNISH) within `RibbitDevourReach` (1.2 m) of them. While it holds:
        - The catch is laid on their back (`Pins`, held_pinned), their head away from the one holding them.
        - held_pinned's origin is at the hips (the head 0.6 m behind it, the boots 1.3 m in front), so the figure goes `RibbitLaid` (0.7 m) up their body from where the sim has them. That puts the leader at their shins and the others either side of their legs and middle. `Hung` now places a pinned figure where its pin says; the Moose's and the Gannet's pins are where they were.
        - The pack goes for the nearest part of the body (`Prey.Down`), stooped `RibbitStoop` (0.95 rad) forward over it from its feet, so the devour clip's mouths reach a body on the ground instead of a standing chest.
        - The others close to play devour too (`RibbitClip`: COMMIT within reach is devour). `CreatureArtTests` says so.
    - **Blood** (`Effects.Devour`):
        - a pool soaking out from under their legs and middle over the first seconds;
        - spots thrown round it, landing in turn;
        - spurts of drops off them every 0.6–1 s as the mouths work.
        The pool and spots are darker than a wound's spray: pooled blood, near black at night. The clock is the scene's, from when the pack got onto them.
    - **Your eyes go down with you** (`Eyes.Devoured`): over 0.7 s your camera goes down to where your head lies (`DownHead`, 1.3 m up from where the sim has you, 0.3 m off the ground), looking down yourself at the pack on you. It's client-side, from when this client first saw a Ribbit eating you (`NetPlaySession.Eaten`), and the desktop camera only.
    - **The fall itself is a cut.** You're standing frozen, then laid, as with the Moose and the Gannet; there's no going-down clip.

The `--eaten s` flag says how long the pack's been on them (4 by default), for the blood. `DemoRosterTests`, `NetcodeTests` and `CreatureArtTests` are green.
