   - **Line 18, "We went under a bridge, and I clipped right through the bridge top. That should be treated like ... a tunnel. If I hit my head on that, should have that comedic boink."**
     - **Cause:** the bridge the line runs *through* is the through truss (`StructureKit.TrussSpan`, linegen's `Truss` over 60 m; frontier:7's Maddox Bridge, 1743–1806). Every bay has its portal plate across the top from `TrussHigh − 1.4` (5.6 m over the rail), and its overhead struts at 7 m. A crewmate standing on a 4 m car is 5.8 m to the top of the head. Only tunnels were posted low clearance (`Lineside.Boards`), so the truss was walked through.
     - **Fix:** a through truss on the main, not a weak one (drawn as a trestle, nothing overhead), is posted `LowClearance` over its span, as a tunnel is (`Sign.Bridge` set on it). So it has the same "LOW" board 450 m ahead, the same roof warning and its full lead, and the bots get off the roofs. A crouch gets you under it (note 664). Standing, it takes you as a tunnel's mouth does: `DeathCause.Struck`, with the tunnel's knock and the `crew-mishaps.tunnel-bonk` boink.
     - The HUD says "THROUGH TRUSS … GET OFF THE ROOF" / "IN THE TRUSS: STAY OFF THE ROOF" rather than the tunnel's lines.      - Tuning: `sight.json trussClearance` (true). It's in the Sim from the route's plan, alike on every machine.
