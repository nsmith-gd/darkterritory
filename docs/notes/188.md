    - **Not slow: short.** frontier:7 is 24.3 km with a 43.5 min dawn, and the harness started a route's night 400 m along, so the crawl to the gate (1,180 m, the yard all at 2.5 m/s) was 312 s of the 1,800: 1,488 s of night, where the train takes about 2,330 s at the cruise and the boards.     - **No stop worked, rightly:** frontier:7's dawn leaves 2.4 min over the ideal run (`dt linegen generate`'s `dawnSlackMin`), so every facility is one there's no time for (GDD §18: every stop is optional; `StopDriver`'s reckoning). Only a Switchman's points were set back.
    - **Where the fires came from:** the director sends no car fires; they were the Fire Flies' (App. A.5), five swarms and every one ignited, then spread. Each failed a different way:
      - **The guard van had no way in.** `WarmUp.Plan` went in by a car's own rear plate (a car behind it) or the car ahead's, so the van, last, was only ever entered from car 9's plate, into car 9.       - **Never outrun.** GDD §21: "lamps off when they swarm. Or drive away"; App. A.5's BREAK OFF: "the train pulls away at speed". The cruise (spec B.3, 14 m/s) is a hair under the flies' pull-away speed (enemies.json `fireFlies.pullAwaySpeed`, 15), so a train at its cruise never shook them.
