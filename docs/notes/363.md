363. **Tower Jaw: a corrupted beaver that fells the railway's timber (G1, queue #100; the director's brief of 8 Oct 2026; design `docs/design/creatures/tower-jaw.md`; GDD §21, A.6, B.6; spec B.15).**
    - **Its structures** are what the stops have that do something: a coaling tower (the chute over the line, `Run.ChuteAt`) and the loading crane's gantry (`Crane.Corner`). Water towers (a Holdout shelter only: the boiler has no water) and signal frames (none) join when they do something; never a bridge (a speed board, not an object). Reading: the brief's list, kept to what has a consequence today; ARCHITECTURE's call, for the director.
    - **The wreck is the creature.** Gnawed through (`Extra` 0..1, by tier), the structure falls: whoever's under it is bitten for `crush` (never a kill); the coaling tower's chute is emptied (`Run.TowerDown`); the crane is `Wreck`ed (dead for the night). The enemy becomes the wreck (`TowerJawMode.Wreck`, a hazard): a rake on the main path that reaches it is stopped there (moved back to its edge, its speed zeroed, the end car hurt as a hard coupling is) until the crew's held Use within `clearReach` adds up to `clearCrewSeconds` (`World.TowersDown`, `TowersCleared`). On the host only: clients see the stop in the next snapshot.
