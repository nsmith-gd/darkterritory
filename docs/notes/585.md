585. **The Hollow House: the house is the creature (G1, queue #318; the director, 9 Oct 2026: "start maybe rumbling the ground underneath as a tell, and then start quickly closing all the doors ... like a Venus flytrap ... And then it should collapse down on the player"; design `docs/design/creatures/hollow-house.md`; GDD §21).**
    - **The flytrap** (`Sim/Enemies/HollowHouse.cs`): someone inside `waitSeconds` and it rumbles for `rumbleSeconds` (the telegraph); nobody inside at its end and it's still again. Otherwise its doors shut one every `shutEvery` (`StopWalls.SetShut`, so they stand as walls, replicate as any door, and are held shut), it sinks `sinkDepth` over `sinkSeconds` (`Extra`, for the art), and comes down on whoever's still inside.
    - **The kill is a crush** (`EnemyContext.Harm`, lethal, `DeathCause.Crushed`): the house holds everyone inside at once, and the spine's grab holds one, so it kills as a structure coming down does (Tower Jaw's tower, note 363, is a hurt; the house, by the director's word, kills).
    - **Settled** it stays sunk and shut for the night.
    - **Not yet:** the open houses' windows are openings in the art, not shut by anything in the sim.
    - **Verified:** `HouseCreatureTests`: in it when the rumble ends, shut, sunk, crushed, and settled shut; out in time, still again.
