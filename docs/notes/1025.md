1025. **A car's cargo read from outside it: placards and stencils, doors cracked on the load, the livestock at the crack, what's spoiled or lost shown, what's in it heard through the slats (n1-C3, queue #727; GDD Part Eleven question 7, note 949 rank 14; the director, 25 Oct: "Keep working on the rest of the GDD that's missing").**

Part Eleven's question 7 asked whether a cargo car should show what's in it from outside (powder, livestock, chemicals), or
only from inside. Note 949 ranked it 14th of the GDD's missing pieces, and the inbox's `nathaniel3-gdd-cargo-visible` is
still open. Until this note, a car's load was seen only from inside it, apart from the comet's green at its seams (the art
checklist's comet "next"). This builds the outside reading behind a flag, on by default (look.json `cargoOutside.enabled`),
so the director can turn it off if the answer is "only its inside".

## What changed

Presentation only: nothing in the Sim moves, and nothing a client predicts depends on it.

- **Doors cracked on the load** (`SceneArt.Car`). A loaded cargo car's side doors stand open `crack` of their slide (0.14,
  about 0.25 m). The leaf is drawn there; the Sim's door is still shut, so the cold, the night's sound and the Choir are
  kept out as before, and nobody gets through. Through the gap you see the lit load (crates, sacks, the pen). Opened by
  the crew, a door slides from the crack. Not on the crew car, the dining car, the guard van or an empty car.
- **The livestock at the crack** (`SceneArt.AtTheCrack`). A sheep stands at each cracked side door of a livestock car, its
  head in the gap. It looks out at whoever is there (note 455's look, held to ±0.3 rad so it stays in the gap), bleats on
  its own beat, and breathes out of the gap as much as the night's cold shows (`Effects.Breath`, `SceneArt.Breath`).
  Within `breathWithin` (45 m).
- **Placards and stencils** (`CargoKit.Placards`). Powder (ammunition), chemicals, medicine and the comet each get a
  board on both sides of the car, bolted over the seventh of its eight framed bays (behind the side door, past where its
  leaf slides fully open). On the board is an enamelled diamond on an iron rim, with an emblem:
    - powder: orange, a bursting ball;
    - chemicals: yellow, a flask;
    - medicine: white, a red cross;
    - the comet: black, a ring and tail lit in the comet's green.

  Under it, the cargo's name stencilled in the HUD's pixel font, with the yard's warning: POWDER / NO LIGHTS,
  CHEMICALS / THIS WAY UP, MEDICINE / FRAGILE, COMET / DO NOT OPEN. Within `placardWithin` (160 m). Ordinary cargo
  (goods, grain, food, ore, coal, timber, salvage, machine parts, livestock) has no placard; its crack shows it.
- **On the roof** (the same piece). For a crew walking the roofs, who can't see a car's sides: the cargo's name painted
  along the car either side of the walk, laid on the roof's arch, in the placard's colour (the comet's lit green), its
  foot toward the walk. A third of the way back from the front, behind the roof's number and ahead of the hatch.
- **The comet's crack.** A cracked comet car's doors leak a thin wash of the green in the gap, and the seam lines skip the
  cracked doors (`CometSeams` with the side doors' bits).
- **Spoiled** (cargo integrity under `spoiledBelow`, 0.5; `CargoKit.Stain`). A stain runs out along the floor seam and
  down the side either side of each side door, in the cargo's colour: the livestock's muck, the chemicals' sick yellow,
  the comet's faint green, food's and grain's brown, anything else's wet rot. The door's landing hides what's under the
  door itself. The same car always has the same streaks (four variants, by its id).
- **Lost** (cargo integrity under `lostBelow`, 0.08). No crack. One side door hangs off its top runner instead
  (`DamageKit.AskewDoor`/`Askew` at the failing stage's angle, the same door #717's failing car would drop). A livestock
  car's pen is empty: the herd drawn is the load times its cargo integrity (`herd` in `SceneArt.Car`), so the animals
  thin out as they're taken, inside the car as well.
- **Through the slats** (`GameAudio.WorldCargo`, `WorldLivestock`):
    - chemicals or medicine: `world-cargo.bottles`, a new synth loop of glass ticking at two high partials, a few a
      second at a walk, quicker and louder the faster the car rides ("speed", m/s), near silent stood still. Tier 6,
      heard to 45 m. Captioned BOTTLES CLINKING.
    - the comet: `world-comet.hum`, the fragment's hum, at the car.
    - the livestock: their existing loops.

  All three are as loud as the cargo's integrity, and silent once it's lost. The bottles are a stand-in until the audio
  chat makes a checklist line of them (AU1 owns the sounds; this one is a synth definition, no samples).

## Tuning

look.json `cargoOutside`: `enabled`, `crack`, `placardWithin`, `breathWithin`, `spoiledBelow`, `lostBelow`. `GameAudio`
reads the same section (`GameAudio.CargoOutside`).

## How to see it

- `dt screenshot --view cargolivestock|cargopowder|cargochemicals|cargomedicine|cargocomet|cargogoods|cargospoiled|cargolost`.
  Each is the third car from its yard side, loaded with its kind. `cargospoiled` is chemicals at 0.3, `cargolost` is
  livestock at 0, `cargolivestock` is a cold night at the crack.
- New screenshot flag: `--spoil a[,b]` sets each cargo car's cargo integrity, the way `--cargo` and `--fill` set its kind and load.
- `dt audio render --sound world-cargo.bottles --param speed=12`.
- `CargoOutsideTests`.
- `dt art check` holds `cargo-placard-*` (medium prop) and `cargo-stain` (small prop).

## Overlaps

- #705 (Fern, loose cargo sliding inside cars) is the Sim's bodies; nothing here touches them.
- #717 (Sable, failing cars holed): a lost cargo's hanging door is the same door a failing car drops, never a second one.
  The placard board sits on the seventh bay, and the holes' sites keep off the side doors but can open beside it. A
  holed car's placard stays on its board.
