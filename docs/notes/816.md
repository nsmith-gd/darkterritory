816. **The handcar modelled: its deck, frame and post, wheels that turn by the distance run, the see-saw handle, and the chains it hangs by (C1, queue #544; note 729's "Art: a modelled handcar (the boxes are the greybox's), its wheels turning ... and the hung trolley's chains", K1's friendslop moments, GDD App. F.6).**
Note 729 drew the handcar as boxes in the look's wood and iron (`GreyboxScene.HandcarModel`): planks, sills, steps, crossed boxes for wheels that never turned, a post, the handle swinging with the sim's swing, a box for a lamp. Hung on the van it was the same boxes stood on end, held by nothing. Now it's modelled (`tools/models/recipes/handcar.py`, `tools/models/build.sh handcar`), in four pieces the scene moves where the sim has them:
- **`handcar_body`** (752 triangles, its detail baked into its layers):
    - nine deck planks across on two iron sills (train.json `handcar`: 3.0 × 1.7 m, the deck 0.55 m over the rails), the end beams bolted under them;
    - the axles in their cast boxes on pedestals, the brake shoes at the treads;
    - a side step each side 0.28 m up on iron hangers;
    - the A-frame post with the handle's bearing 0.95 m over the deck;
    - the gear case on the back axle, the brake lever at the front, and the lamp's bracket at the front right corner.
- **`handcar_wheel`** (228): spoked iron with its flange. Four of them on the axle ends (0.72 m across, 0.95 m along), the flange inboard, **turned by the distance the handcar's run** (its rake's distance over the wheel's radius: rolled forward, its top goes forward). Hung, they stand still.
- **`handcar_handle`** (304): the timber see-saw strapped in iron, a grip bar across each end (where `GreyboxScene.HandleGrip` puts the pumpers' hands), tilted about its bearing as the sim's swing has it.
- **`handcar_chain`** (234): a metre of links, stretched between two points. Hung on the van, two chains run from its roof end (`HandcarHooks`, 0.6 m either side of the middle) to the trolley's top end beam, each sagging a little at its middle. Lifted down, they're gone.
- **The lamp** is the hand lamp (`hand_lantern`) hung off the bracket's arm, lit on the rails. Its light is where it was (`HandcarLamp`).
- The greybox's boxes stay for a scene without the art loaded.
- **From the sim:** nothing new. The distance run is the handcar's rake's (replicated), and the chains' hooks are the van's frame.
