591. **Town to town (queue #324, D1; the director, 9 Oct 2026: "the town that we end in in a run should be the same town that we begin in in the next run. Um, we can do a little bit of a loading screen. I don't mind that, but what I want to make sure is that it's the same town so that you feel like you are still living up this fantasy of I'm going from town to town to town").**
   - **Before.** A night's departure town was made from its route's seed and the plan's fortress name (`TownSite.Of`, note 281). The terminus was only a name on the plan, over note 107's generic village. The campaign kept only the last departure's custom (`LastTown`), so the next night's town was whichever the picked contract's line drew.
   - **A town carried** (`Towns.TownAt`: name, trade, seed). Everything a town is (its custom, streets, houses, people and their names) is drawn from those, the gate (the same on every generated line) and the content.
     - `TownAt.Terminus(route, trades)` is a night's terminus as a town: its name from the plan, a trade from linegen tiers.json `fortress.identities` and a seed, each from its own stream off the route's seed. Null for a silent settlement (nobody lives there to leave from).
   - **The campaign.**
     - `CampaignState.Town` is the town the crew's in. `Campaign.Arrived` sets it at settling, when the night was delivered to a town; otherwise the crew's where they were. Both settle paths call it: the app's `Autosave` and `dt campaign play`.
     - `Campaign.RouteOf` is a contract's spec from there: `frontier:7@Fort Boudreau/coal/1234`.
   - **The spec carries it**, so every machine makes the same town from the session's setup, as it makes the same line (the Routes cache keys on the whole spec).
     - `RunParameters.Parse` reads the town after the `@`.
     - `LineBuilder.LayStations` draws the departure's name and trade as ever (so the rest of the line is the spec's, whichever town it leaves from), then puts the carried ones in their place. It keeps the carried name's first word from every other name on the line.
     - `PlanFortress.TownSeed` holds the town's seed (written only when carried, so every other plan and its fingerprint is as it was).
     - `TownSite.Of` makes the town from that seed.
   - **The front end** names the town the crew's in on the fortress screen ("CREW 1 IN FORT BOUDREAU: ...").
   - **Not yet:** the terminus drawn as that town on arrival (it's note 107's village: its yard is 450 m with a 70 m pad, where a walled town needs the departure's 200 m pad and a frame facing the other way). That's B2's procedural end town; `TownAt.Terminus` is what it should make there, so what you arrive at is what you leave from. A loading screen between nights is allowed (the director).
   - **Verified.** `TownToTownTests`:
     - A night delivered to frontier:7's terminus leaves every contract on the next board departing from that town, with its name and trade.
     - The town is the same whichever line leaves it (its custom, people's names and houses), and not the town that line would have left from on its own.
     - The line is the contract's either way: length, dawn, features, seed.
     - A night not delivered (derailed, the crew lost, taken at dawn, stranded), or delivered to a silent settlement, leaves the crew where they were.
     - The spec round-trips.
