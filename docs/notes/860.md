860. **The night's pattern: monsters waiting at the stops, or boarding from them (the director's playtest of 16 Oct evening, line 7) (Ivy, queue #587).**
   - Claimed 2026-10-19 14:20 UTC by the Token Budget Manager (nathaniel3) for Ivy.
   - **The director's words:** "1. prepare the crew 2. Leave 3. Stop for loot 4. Monsters are positioned and ready at yards and abandoned villages, or use those stops to get onto the train 5. Monsters cause funny social problems for players 6. Players overcome or lose 7. Repeat".
   - **Where the pattern broke (measured on main):**
     - **Nothing was sure to be at a stop.** What a stop met was only what the pressure could spend while the train stood there: past its threshold, off its cooldown, with budget, after the 20 s facility lull. B4's census (note 848) shows which creatures' rules let them come to a stop. It doesn't show whether any of them does. `dt playthrough --route frontier:7 --bots 2 --loot` (seed 1) on main: the crew stood at 1 of the night's 3 yards (km 2.88, 232–480 s). The director sent a Whistler there at 232 s, a free Follower at 351 s and a second Whistler at 379 s. It held 460 s of the night at "building" and 175 s at "nothing fits".
     - **The night's lottery can leave a stop with nothing that lives there.** Seed 1 (the playthrough's and many tests' default) is a sparse night: `nightChance` rolls 22 of 28 kinds out (5,000 seeds: 15.4 out on the mean; 22 or more on 0.5% of them). With the Ribbits, Gaunt, Moose, Soot Children, Freight Beetle, Grumbler, Mouse Moose and Tower Jaw all out, nothing that lives at a yard was about, and no channel can send it. The nightChance values are the director's (Tweaker); not changed.
     - **The bots stop at few of a night's stops.** Two bots stood at 1 of 3 yards: the bots' own spare-before-dawn reckoning (notes 609, 617) passes the rest. That's the bots' call (A1's), not changed; players choose their own stops.
     - **Abandoned villages are almost never laid.** The line generator lays a dead settlement on the main line only at its every-so-often (setpieces.json `deadSettlement.everyKm` 1.5–3 km), in what room a stretch has left. The 7 km nights' stretches between three facilities are about 1.2 km, so in `dt linegen generate` over 12 nights (local, frontier, deadLines 1–4) only local:4 had one on the main line. A village stop is laid at a main-line halt or dead town (`PlanStops`), so without one there's no village stop either.
   - **What was built: the stops' own** (GDD App. B.1 "The stops' own"; spec B.10's row; `director.stops` in `content/tuning/enemies.json`; `Director.SpendStop`):
     - From the first second the train stands at a stop (`Director.StopAt`), the director sends `waiting` (1) of the kinds whose rule the stop meets and `boarding` (1) of the `boarders` whose rule it meets (the Whistler, Draggers, Tippy Toesie, the Passenger). A stop is a facility with a stop laid that the train stands at (`Run.StandsAt`: down its spur, or on the main line beside it), or a village stop with the train stood on the main line over it. It sends one a second and keeps trying for `trySeconds` (240) of standing there, so a kind that wants someone on the ground comes the moment one steps down. It does this once a stop a night.
     - Each kind is picked by its rule's weight × `weights` (the Tweaker's) × the night's variety. The roster, `nightChance`, `perNight`, slain and the engaged cap all hold. The draws use their own PCG stream (`0x5709` off the scrambled seed), so the night's other draws are as they were with it off.
     - It runs beside the pressure, as the free spawns do: outside its budget, threshold and cooldown, and outside the facility lull (it was there before the train came; its own rules say when it moves). The grace, the forts, the final stretch, the line's spawn bans and the aftermath still hold. It takes nothing off the pressure. It ends the quiet and counts toward the kind's cap and the night's variety. It's logged as `DirectorSpawn.Stop`.
     - Reading (§8, a tuning flag): App. B.1's facility lull is "calm on arrival before facility threats activate". It's read as holding the director's *spend*. A stop's own creature isn't a spend, and what it does on arrival is its own rule's (the Whistler hides in its gap, the Moose grazes, the Gaunt sleeps). `director.stops.on` false puts the night back as it was.
     - **`dt playthrough` reports `stops`:** every stop of the night, stood at or passed. For each one stood at: what was about within 30 s of arriving (`ready`), everything met there, what got onto a car while it stood (`boarded`), and the director's sends while it stood (the stop's own marked). The totals are `readyAt`, `metAt` and `boardedAt` out of `stood`.
   - **Measured** (`dt playthrough --route frontier:7 --bots 2 --loot --seed 1..4`, 20 min, `--width 320 --height 180 --every 600 --gap 30`; "off" is the same build with `director.stops.on` false, run from a copy of `content/`; the `stops` report, house creatures (Hangers, Householders, Pickers) left out, as they were in their houses already before):
     - **Something of a stop's in place on arrival** (sent within 30 s of standing there): off, at 3 of the 8 stops the crews stood at (the facility lull and the pressure's threshold held the rest: the first spend at a first yard came 92–116 s after arriving); on, at 6 of 8, the stop's own 0–3 s after the train stood at 5 of them (a Ribbit pack as the first crewmate stepped down, a Moose grazing), and a Ribbit pack at 21 and 39 s at two more. The two without one: seed 1's yard (every creature that lives at a yard was out tonight, above) and seed 4's second yard, stood 33 s with its Ribbits at 21 s.
     - **A boarder from the stop**: on, at 5 of 8 stops (a Whistler, a Dragger, the Tippy Toesie twice, a Whistler; 86–124 s after the train stood: each comes when its own rule lets it, a Tippy Toesie only to an idle crewmate). What boarded at a stop (`boarded`: anything on a car while it stood): off at 7 of 8, on at 6 of 8 (most of it what the night had sent before, the Knotters and Hotboxes in the cars).
     - **The cost**: off delivered 3 of 4 nights in 20 min (seed 3 at 6.92 km), on 2 of 4 (seed 2 at 7.48 km, seed 3 at 7.65 km). Seed 2's crew spent 643 s at its first yard against 413 s with a Ribbit pack waiting there, then stood at all three yards (off: two). Seed 4 came in at 1,008 s against 1,171 s (it stood at two yards, off three). The director's sends a night: off 9–10, on 8–12.
     - (`dt trends measure --seeds 1-4`: below, when measured)
   - **Checked:** `StopsOwnTests`:
     - At a Frontier night's first yard, stood braked, a waiting creature and a boarder are sent within the first 5 s (inside the facility lull), neither free. Standing on 300 s sends nothing more of the stop's own.
     - Stood mid-line, or with `stops.on` false: nothing.
     - The same seed sends the same.
     - Every boarder named is a kind the director sends.
   - **Calls for the director:**
     - **More abandoned villages** (level design's, with B1/B4). Two levers were measured, and neither is in this change:
       - The validator's quota `deadSettlementNearFacility` 1 (content/linegen/tiers.json, 0 in every tier) lays a halt or dead town beside a stop on every night tried (8 of 8). But first-attempt pass rates fall from 1.0 to 0.55 (local), from 0.85 to 0.3 (frontier) and from 1.0 to 0.35 (deadLines), and generation takes 2–3× as long (`dt linegen sweep --cars 6 --seeds 20`).
       - A wanted-but-not-validated settlement handed out before the hard bends (a code change in `LineBuilder.Script`) laid one on 3 of 3 local nights and 0 of 5 frontier and deadLines nights (no room). First-attempt pass rates fell to 0.85, 0.75, 0.7 and 0.75 (hard bends squeezed).
       - Either way, the room for it has to come from somewhere on a 7 km night with three facilities: a longer night, fewer hazards or one facility fewer.
     - **Sparse nights:** `nightChance` can roll out every creature that lives at a yard (seed 1: 22 of 28 kinds out). If a stop should always have something waiting, the choice is either a floor on how many stop residents are about each night, or letting the stop's own draw one of them even when it's out tonight. Both change what nightChance means; the director decides.
     - **The rate:** one waiting and one boarding a stop is a first pass (`director.stops.waiting`, `boarding`). It isn't on the Tweaker yet; a "stops" row in the Tweaker's settings would carry it.
