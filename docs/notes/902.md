   - **The ask** (docs/playtest/2026-10-16.md line 29, the creature gym): "it walked kind of over top of the train, and then it
     disappeared, and the train just started levitating ... We want to see the waker lift the train off the ground, sort of like
     stop behind the train and like scoop it up with its hands and pick it up like it's like a two-handed hot dog and just start
     eating the cars ... three bites per car ... If the player falls off the train when it's picked up in the air ... they should die."
   - **What was wrong on main:** the lead Waker caught at the last car's end and knelt over it (a crewmate on the rear cars was
     inside his body, so he "disappeared"); `WakerLift` raised the cars from the rear as a ramp, every car hanging off the one
     behind it with nothing holding the ones in front (the levitating); his hands were wherever the clip had them. `WorldShot`
     (the replay and gym shots) drew the train's raw frames, so its shots never showed the lift at all.
   - **The sim** (`Waker`, deterministic, a function of the seconds he's held the train):
     - He takes the train `wakers.catchShort` (12 m) behind the last car and stays that far behind it (`SecondsInHand` and
       `CatchAfterDawn` reckon the catch there: about half a second sooner).
     - A car at a time: the last goes up from `scoopFrom` (1.2 s) to `eatAfter − scoopHold` (5 s); each after it in its own turn
       (`TurnStart(k)` = `eatAfter + k·eatSeconds`): his hands down to it over `reachShare` of `scoopShare` of the turn, then up.
       `Raised`, `Reached`, `Through`, `Bitten`, `LiftedAt`, `EatenAt`. `Lifted` is now the eaten cars and the one up in his hands.
     - Three bites a car (`bites` [0.55, 0.775] of the turn, and the last as the turn ends, when `EatenCars` has it).
     - The fall (`Fallen`): whoever's down on the ground within `fallAround` (3 m) of the held car's sides and ends, once it's
       `fallFrom` (0.15) of the way up, dies (`DeathCause.Fell`). The sim's frames never leave the rails, so stepping off the
       held car lands you there. Beside a car still on the rails, nobody falls; the pickers (note 710) still take whoever ran.
     - `liftSeconds` is gone (nothing reads it). The enemies.json change is only in the `wakers` block, clear of the spawn
       overhaul's `director` blocks (note 874); where and when Wakers rise is unchanged.
   - **The drawing:** `WakerLift.Of` reckons the held car off the train's rail frames: where he kneels (`Stand`, `catchShort` behind
     that car), the car between the rails and his hands (`HoldOut` 13 m out, `HoldUp` 23 m up, its far end tipped `HoldPitch`), its
     bitten end shoved on into his mouth with each bite, his hands' places on it (right a third of the way along what's left, left
     two thirds), and his mouth's (bowed over it at the rails, on its bitten end once up). Between cars his hands, mouth and knees
     go from the last car's stub to the next. `Apply` moves only the held car; the eaten go out of sight; the rest stay on the rails.
     `GreyboxScene` reckons the hold in `Build(train)` and gives the art `WakerScene.Stand`, `Hand`, `HandLeft`, `Mouth`;

     `Bite.Waker` takes the bites and how many a car takes. `WorldShot` now applies `WakerLift`. No model rebuild (no blender here):
