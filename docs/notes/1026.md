   1. **A voice on the radio that isn't crew** (Part Eleven q4: "the single most disturbing thing available in this design. Possibly too much"). *The moment:* you're on a roof three cars from anyone, your radio crackles, and it's your friend's voice saying a few words they said ten minutes ago, but your friend is in the cab and says they didn't. *Background:* everyone has a radio in their uniform (queue #478–#486). The Passenger's one tell is that it never speaks, so the voice can't be the Passenger's. *Decision:* wanted? If so, whose: the Brakeman (seen on the roofs, out of sight a lot) or the Grumbler, and how often? *Default meanwhile (#729):* built behind a flag, off. At most once a night, a corrupted human out of sight speaks 2–3 s of a crewmate's own recent radio words on the radio channel only, never near, and the report names it.
   2. **Corruption growing on the train** (Part Eleven q8). *The moment:* a few nights into a campaign, the walker crossing the gap sees black growth along the coupling and under car 3's floor, where the Draggers got on last night, and the car creaks wetly at speed. *Background:* nothing on the train shows where creatures boarded or how long the crew have been out. Damage and fire already char (C.5) and burnt houses stay burnt (§3.1), so the train is the only thing that forgets. *Decision:* wanted, and should it do anything (a sound the flank creatures are drawn to, a cost at the blacksmith to scrape off), or be look only? *Default meanwhile (#730):* look only, behind a flag. It grows where boarders came on, a little each night, kept in the campaign save, and the blacksmith scrapes it off for a small fee.
