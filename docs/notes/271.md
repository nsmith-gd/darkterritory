That's the mistake you learn from. The counter is to vent and starve the fire below a set heat; it then leaves the way it came, and a break of two to three minutes follows … A water hose through the open door kills it, at the cost of much of the fire. No chip damage." Note 263 already had the rest of v3: drawn by heat, boarding at the tender with a telegraph, driven off on the way in, and the 150 s break. In the firebox it still worked to App. A.5's old rule (open the door with a shovelful and club it, 12 burn a blow), and it ate the fire.
    - **Territorial** (`Stoker.Tick`, Interior.cs). The door opening on it (a shovelful, `Boiler.FireDoorOpen` rising) hurts whoever's nearest the door within `stoker.doorReach` (1.8 m): `doorBurn` 60 the first time, `doorKill` the second. Both are lethal-class hurts, kept per Stoker, so a fresh one starts over. It can't be clubbed in the firebox: `Reachable` and `MeleeRadius` hold only while it's boarding, so a crowbar does nothing and `burnPerBlow` is gone. That is the "no chip damage".
    - **Vent and starve.** While it's in, it still lurches heat in (`feedRate` ± `swing`), holds the safety valve shut and runs the regulator open (`Tamper`), so the boiler goes in about half a minute if left (`OnceInItEatsTheFireAndSwingsTheGaugeToARupture` unchanged). It no longer eats the fire (`eatPerSecond` 0.2 → 0), so the crew have to starve it. Under `starveFirebox` (2 of 6) it lets the valve go, takes its heat out (`BreakOff`) and goes back over the footplate to the coal over `boardSeconds` (`Extra2` 1 again, drawn there). It's then gone, and `World`'s break begins. The vent (12/s) outruns its heat and the fire's, so with the blow-off held and no coal it's gone before the boiler: 10–90 s in the test, 15 s for the bots.
    - **The hose** is an extinguisher held into the firebox (Fire, within `doorReach`) for `hoseSeconds` (2). The spray opens the door without the burn, kills it, and takes `hoseFireCost` (60%) of the fire. **Director's call:** the GDD says a water hose, and the cab has none, so the extinguisher stands in (one fetched from car 1: the engine has no room to mount one). A cab hose fitting (a slacking pipe off the injector) would be the real thing.
    - **Bots** (`FightStoker`). They club it while it's boarding, as before. Once it's in they never shovel or open the door: the vent held, the brake on, throttle shut, until it's starved out. The old "leave it if hurt" health gate went with the burns.
