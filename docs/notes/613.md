613. **Three nights running (D1.4, queue #345, at D1's ask after #343 and #344: "a campaign played end to end, three nights running, the way the director will play it").** One campaign (slot 1, seed 7) on the 7 km local line with 3 bots, enemies, upkeep and the stops' finds. It was played two ways:
    - `dt campaign play`: the headless harness, each night its own process.
    - `dt campaign nights`, new: the nights back to back in one process, each hosted as the app hosts it.
    - Each night's departure, arrival and results were shot.
    - **The town chain** (`dt campaign play`, five nights). Every night departed from the last one's town, and a night that didn't arrive left the crew where they were:

        | night | contract | from | to | end | net | scrip after |
        |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
        | 1 | local:435 | Radley | Fort Hensley | delivered | 128 | 128 |
        | 2 | local:7849 | Fort Hensley | Oakes | delivered | 914 | 1042 |
        | 3 | local:9491 | Oakes | Fort Yeld (7 houses burnt) | delivered, 7 brought in | −694 | 348 |

        | 5 | local:7998 | Fort Yeld, burnt | Voss | delivered | 1013 | 1337 |

        The crew came home every night (3 of 3). The cars stayed 3. The board offered three contracts from the town the crew was in each time.
    - **What broke, fixed:**
        - **A client's train lost its engine** (#783). Night 3 crashed every time 66 s in: `Run.EngineRake` found no rake with the engine on a client's prediction.
            - Cars a switchyard had put ahead of the engine (note 481) were cut off again, and `TrainOnLine.Rebuild` gave them the engine's own rake object.
            - Fixed, with `ResumeTests`' regression test, which fails without the fix.
        - **`dt campaign play` wasn't a night as the app plays it.** It ran no upkeep jobs (note 331) and no stops' finds (note 326). Both are now on by default (`--no-upkeep`, `--no-loot`). `--lose-at s` forces a night lost at dawn s seconds into the run: the Wakers have the train (note 588).
    - **What it showed, passed on:**
        - **The gate.** Night 3 rode into Fort Yeld with seven creatures aboard: five Cinder Hounds on cars 2 and 3, the Knotter and the Brakeman.
            - The bots didn't stop and clear the train before the gate, even with D1.5's #766 in.
            - It cost 1575 in brigade fees and 7 burnt houses (notes 589, 608).
            - D1 passed it to D1.5 with the replay (slot seed 7, the third contract, local:9491 from Oakes).
            - Trainfolk counting as brought in stays (#322's rule: anything still on the train).
        - **The app's bots are weaker than the harness's.** Hosted as the app hosts them (`dt campaign nights`, `NetPlaySession` with 3 bots), the crew died far more often, and three of four nights were lost at dawn:
            - dragged off roofs by Draggers;
            - smothered by Tippy Toesie;
            - eaten by the Ribbits on the line;
            - burned in cars Fire Flies set alight, drawn by lamps the bots had lit.

            Scrip ended at −1905. The harness's bots, on the same contracts, came home every night.
        - **The stops.** No night found anything (no scavenging, no loot lines). D1's #341 is on the bots' stops.
        - **The Knotter, again and again.** It is killed five to eight times a night ("killed the Knotter together at km 2 ... km 4 ... Sutter Yard").
    - **What carries on.**
        - The results, scrip and board carried on night to night.
        - A lost night costs its coal and leaves the crew in its town; the next night starts from there.
        - The company keeps a crew in debt going: `Campaign.Settle` holds the train at 2 cars at least, and nothing stops a night starting on negative scrip. Only the shop's purchases are refused.
    - **Nothing hung or leaked.**
        - Four nights in one process: the managed heap after each night (collected) was 36.9, 44.7, 39.8, 34.1 MB. The working set was 190, 221, 227, 231 MB: a slow creep, no climb.
        - The app at each night's departure (headless on lavapipe, `--route <night's spec> --bots 3 --quit-after 40`), from the slow-frame log (note 549): each night's first frame took 8 to 18 s (the scene built and uploaded), then 2.4 to 4.2 s a frame, nearly all of it lavapipe's present. That's the same night to night, and Fort Yeld's burnt houses cost nothing. "crew: 4 aboard" each time.
