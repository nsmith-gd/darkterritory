832. **Up out of a turned-round gun, not off the train's front (C1, queue #560; the director's 13 Oct evening playtest, line 23).**
The director, playtesting on the evening of 13 Oct (`docs/playtest/2026-10-13-evening.md`, line 23): "When I jump off a turret, if I turn the turret around, it causes me to jump off backwards, and I end up jumping off the front of the train."

**What's wrong** (found in the sim)
- The gunner's seat is on the carriage, `combat.json seatBehind` (0.75 m) behind the breech, and turns with it (`Guns.SeatAt`, T112).
- The engine's forward gun stands 0.8 m back from the cab roof's front edge, and turns the full circle (note 596). Turned round, its seat, and the gunner in it, is 5 cm from that edge.
- Jump in the seat gets you up (T112: "not a leap off the carriage"). But a press lasts several ticks. The first gets you up; the rest of the same press jumps you, from 5 cm off the edge.
- In the air, anything carries you over: the train slowing under you (you go on at the speed you left at), or a key held back (backward, as the gunner faces, is toward the train's front). You come down off the engine's front, ahead of it.

**What changes**
- Getting up out of a gun's seat stands you `edge.lip` in from any edge of the roof within it, where walking would hold you (`PlayerMotor.StandClear`).
- The press of Jump that got you up (out of a gun's seat, the handcar's handle or a Muskoka chair) isn't a jump until it's let go (`PlayerFlags.GotUp`; flags go over the wire as a number, so no protocol change).
