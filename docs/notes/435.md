435. **Draggers off a truss (queue #171, D1; [orchestrator.md](design/orchestrator.md) §5.2 S4, "dropping onto the roofs from an overbridge or a tunnel mouth"; note 286's boarding-first: a threat that boards at speed does it at a known point, with a tell and a counter).** Since note 286, a Dragger got under a car only with the train under 14 m/s, so a train at speed never had one.
   - **Where.** A through-truss's top chord, 7 m over the rail (`StructureKit.TrussSpan`). Not a tunnel mouth: a mouth already takes anyone standing on a roof (the low-clearance board, `Lineside`, Struck), so a Dragger there would add nothing. The line has no overbridges.
   - **Put there.** `Director.Drops` (once a second): each main-line truss coming up within `draggers.drop.ahead` (500 m) of a train at `fromSpeed` (14 m/s, the speed at which the director stops putting them under cars) or more is rolled for once (`chance` 0.6). A Dragger is perched mid-span on a side (`Dragger.OnTruss`, `Perched`). It is not from the budget or on the caps, and never in the grace, a fort or the final approach. It has its own dice, seeded scrambled: a PCG stream's first draw barely moves between neighbouring seeds, and frontier:7's seeds 1-3 all rolled 0.73 at first.
   - **The tell.** From `tellSeconds` (3) before the engine's under it, a scraping on the steel above (the Dragger's Telegraph, so `dragger-scrape` plays there and its limb reaches down off the chord, drawn upside down).
   - **The drop.** It is never sooner than `minReactionSeconds` into the scrape. As each car passes under, it drops on whoever's on that car's roof, the nearest the chord's side. It is then a Dragger at that car's edge with them hanging over the side (App. A.4: hauled back up, or dragged under).
   - **The miss.** Nobody up there as the train goes by, and it drops on the ballast behind and is gone. The counter: off the roofs over a truss.
   - **Counted.** `Director.TrussDraggers`, and `dt harness`'s `threats.trussDraggers`. `dt screenshot --route frontier:7 --truss --view ahead`: the train short of Stroud Bridge, the Dragger on the chord. At that distance its limb is a few pixels; the scrape is the tell.
   - **Tests.** `DraggerTests`:
     - At 20 m/s it scrapes for at least the reaction window, then drops on a crewmate on car 2's centreline (out of reach from under any edge) and holds them at the car's edge.
     - With nobody on the roofs it is gone once the train is past.
     - Frontier:7's run-fast train finds one on Stroud Bridge's chord, put there within 500 m of it.
   - **Bot nights.** `dt harness --route frontier:7 --bots 4 --enemies --upkeep --express 21 --seconds 900`, seeds 1-3, after: a truss Dragger on every night. One grabbed a walker (seed 2) and was hauled off; the others met empty roofs. Deaths 1, 0 and 3 against main's 2, 0 and 2. Seed 3's three were hounds Mauling, nothing to do with the truss.
   - **Not yet.** Bots off the roofs at a truss's scrape (they heed a tunnel's mouth, T81, not this).
