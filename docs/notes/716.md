716. **The big cab, the crew car (K1, queue #450; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`).**
"Because this is a game, the boiler doesn't have to be accurate in size. We can have a small boiler and make the cab a lot larger so that the cab itself becomes the crew car. It becomes the place where players hang out together. It becomes the place where coordinated driving happens. It becomes, in essence, the captain's quarters, shared by all." "Everybody wants to be where they can see the tracks, because making people feel comfortable is seeing where they're going." Approving the spec: "big cab, 11 meters, smaller boiler. I still want to keep a bunch of the controls the way that we have it set up because we are going to have this game playable by a single player ... the chart table is good. I'm okay with moving the cab map behind the controls. On a table and as long as it's tracking where the train still is and giving our route. In terms of the gallery and the raised benches, this is a good spot ... And the back wall should definitely have some fun, cute gimmicks back there as well."

   - **What it was:** the engine 20 m with a 5 m cab (note 280's work all at its front) and the boiler about 14 m under the armoured hood (note 338). The run map hung on the plate over the front windows.
   - **Now** (train.json `geometry.engine.cabLength` 11; `geometry.engine.crewCar`; `EnginePlan`, `CarShape.Engine`, `CrewCar`):
     - **The cab is 11 m** of the engine's 20; the boiler and the hood the 7.9 m behind it. The engine's length and width are unchanged.
     - **The footplate** (`crewCar.footplateLength` 2.5) is exactly the 5 m cab's: bunker, firebox, console, levers, whistle cord, vent, tool rack, points lever, the hatch ladder to the gun (note 704's ladder and hatch untouched), the gun. `CrewCarTests` builds both cabs and holds every one of them to the millimetre. The vent stood a third of the way back to the doorway; it's pinned where it was (`CarShape.VentAlong`). The coaling spout pours where it did.
     - **The chart table** (`tableFrom` 2.9, `tableLength` 1.75, `tableWidth` 0.9, `tableHeight` 1.0, part `ChartTable`): in the middle behind the footplate, clear of the floor a crewmate is put on (`PlayerMotor.CabFloorZ`). The run map is laid flat on it (`TrainKit.MapToCab`: the sheet as it was cooked, turned onto the table, the night running back to front the way the train goes, read from the driver's side), the train's place moving on it, the bends' figures, the stops. A green-shaded lamp hangs over it, high enough to keep out of the gallery's view.
     - **One lamp per crewmate** along the table's front end (`TrainKit.CrewLamp`, `CrewCar.Aboard`): lit while they're alive and in any of the train's vehicles, dark on the ground or dead. Read from replicated state (each crewmate's `Parent`), so every machine shows the same.
     - **The gallery** (`galleryFrom` 5.1 to `galleryTo` 8.4, `galleryRise` 0.45, part `Gallery`): a raised floor wall to wall with a tread half way up at each end (`galleryStep` 0.45 deep: deeper than a body's radius, or the riser behind it stops the foot first). Two benches down its middle facing forward (`benchRows` 5.9 and 7.3, `benchWidth` 1.0, `benchDepth` 0.45, `benchHeight` 0.45, part `Bench`). The ways down the cab either side of the table and the benches are 0.95 m wide (`EnginePlan.Lane`).
     - **The sides' waist** stands `galleryWaist` (1.1) over the gallery's floor the whole way from the front pillar to the doorways (deck + 1.55, was 1.1), so nobody steps out over a knee-high sill up there and a walker along the side meets no corner. It's under the footplate's slits, so nothing changes from the controls. Long windows along the gallery (`TrainKit.SideWindows`: the slits along the footplate and the table, three tall bays along the gallery, plate behind them to the doorway).
     - **The back wall**, the plate over the boiler's end, drawn warm with a low orange light: atmosphere only, nothing tied to the cold (the director's playtest of 14 Oct, line 14, would drop the cold). On it:
       - a **kettle** on the warming shelf that steams as the boiler nears its redline (`CrewCar.KettleSong`: from `kettleFrom` 10 under boiler.json's redline, full at it; `Effects.KettleSteam`);
       - a **cuckoo clock** whose bird comes out once a minute over the last `wakers.stirSeconds` (180) before dawn (`CrewCar.Cuckoo`, `cuckooEvery` 60, `cuckooOutSeconds` 3);
       - a **photo rail** of six empty frames (queue #455 pins the crew's photos in them);
       - an empty **trophy shelf** on the left wall;
       - a **hook per crewmate** on the right wall, their cap hung on it in their colour (look.json `crewColours`) while they're off the train or dead;
       - the warming shelf's right half **kept clear for U1's gramophone** (`TrainKit.GramophoneShelf`, #967).
     - **The doorways** moved back with the back wall; the running boards, the cab steps and the corridors along the boiler are where they always meet them. The forward gun stays at the cab roof's front; its rail now runs the long cab roof.
     - **The running gear** under the shorter boiler (`TrainKit.Drivers`: the last pair just ahead of the trailing truck) and a pair of small wheels under the cab (`TrainKit.CabTruck`).
     - **What comes into the cab** (a Climber over the roof, the Lamplighter at the brake, the staged doll) comes down on the footplate (`CarShape.FootplateMiddle`), where the 5 m cab's middle was.
     - **The bots** go round the table and the benches by the ways (`EnginePlan.CabWay`, `WarmUp.SteerCab` in their own walks; `Heed.CabFurniture` for any other walk), and get down from the cab at its doorway at a walk (`StopCrew.GetDown`: the stroll is only for the step off).
     - **Down the treads is a step** (`PlayerMotor.UpdateSupport`): in the engine's cab, a floor up to `player.json stepUp` below the feet catches them, as one that high above does. Before, every step down was a drop: off a tread a crewmate was in the air in the world's frame for a few ticks, out of the cab (its space the outside, a lone driver's sanding called off). Only inside the cab box; everywhere else steps down are as they were.
     - **Sitting on the benches** isn't built: they're geometry to stand by. Queue #456's emotes (sitting on a roof edge) is where a sit belongs.
