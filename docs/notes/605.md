605. **The bots stop short and clear the train before the town gate (D1.5, queue #337; D1's ask; the director, 9 Oct 2026, approving monsters brought into town: "It also encourages players to plan a little extra time at the end of the run to stop the train, clear the monsters, and then bring it in").**
    - **What was there.** Since #322 (note 589) a creature aboard as its car comes in through a town's gate is fought in the yard, and the night pays the brigade (`Run.BringIn`: attached to the train, not a hazard, not an incident). The bot driver drove in whatever was aboard. D1.2's #323 (note 590) makes the last `spawnBanM` (1200 m) short of the gate quiet: nothing new comes there (`World.HomeStretch`).
    - **The rule** (`ConductorBot.ForTheGate`, a partial of its own; one line in `DecideDriving`, after the Holdout's stand). Within `crewBots.gateClearWithin` (1500 m) of a town's gate, with something aboard that the gate would charge for (`ConductorBot.Aboard`, the same test as `BringIn`), the driver brings the train to a stand `gateClearShort` (30 m) short of the gate, in the quiet zone. It stands on the brake while the crew clears the train; bots fight what's aboard a standing train as anywhere. Clear, it goes on in, and D1.4's home rule (note 600) takes it from there. That rule is untouched: the stand only lowers the cruise to stop at its own point, as the Holdout's does. It stands no longer than `gateClearGiveUp` (240 s), in case something aboard is never cleared (a hidden Brakeman). Once stood and away, it doesn't stand again that night.
    - **Time in hand, and the Wakers' wall** (`ConductorBot.GateTimeInHand`). The Wakers stop `wakers.stopShort` (300 m) short of the gate: a train whose rear is past that wall is out of their reach at dawn, and the night doesn't end on dawn alone while they're on (only on their catch). So:
        - A stand with its rear past the wall, the rear already past it, has all the time there is; only the give-up bounds it. A 10-car train is about 175 m, so its stand 30 m short of the gate is inside the wall.
        - Short of the wall still, the time in hand is to their catch: they rise `riseBehind` (1200 m) behind the rear at dawn and come at their speeds (`Waker.Covered`). From that comes off the run to the wall at the pace it's making and `gateClearMargin` (45 s). With them up already, there's none: it runs for the wall.
        - A stand that can't put the rear past the wall (a train over about 270 m) has to their catch, less the run in past it.
        - Without the Wakers, dawn and `run.json dawnGraceSeconds`.
      No time in hand: no stand, and in it goes, the brigade's fee paid. A fee beats being eaten.
    - **Tuning.** enemies.json `crewBots`: `gateClearShort`, `gateClearWithin`, `gateClearGiveUp`, `gateClearMargin`. Bot judgement, not in the spec.
    - **Verified.** `GateClearTests`, each a bot crew of four on frontier:7 coming in on its town's gate with three hounds put aboard car 2 100 m out (12 hp each, so they last to the stand):
        - `WithTimeInHandTheCrewClearsTheTrainShortOfTheGateAndBringsItInClean`: stood short of the gate, delivered, nothing brought in, no fee. Fails without the rule.
        - `AStandWithItsRearPastTheWakersWallHasTimeInHandAtDawn`: four cars, dawn 20 s off: it still stands. Fails without the rule.
        - `WithDawnAboutToBreakAndNoStandPastTheWakersWallTheDriverRunsStraightIn`: eighteen cars, dawn 20 s off: no stand, delivered, the hounds brought in.
      Put aboard 250 m out or more, a crew of four kills a pack of three before the stand; the driver slows for it and goes on once it's clear.
