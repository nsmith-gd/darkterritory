242. **The clerk reads the cause card and the Stranded line (GDD v1.4 App. E.5, E.9; note 240's "not yet").** Both were text over the picture. The yard's voice says them now, each once as it comes up (`GameAudio.ClerkLine`), through the set with the channel's static under it.
    - **The words.** The cause card is `IncidentLog.CauseCard`: the derail's cause as the sim words it (Lineside's bends, the Sleepers, the Switchman, TrackRules' washouts and bridges, the Stoker's runaway), C.9's blame line, and where it happened. The places are the line generator's own words (`content/linegen` `names.json`, and `facilities.json`'s types), so the bank (`tools/audio/clerk.py` `CAUSE_PHRASES`, `CAUSE_WORDS`, `places()`) holds every word they can make, 213 entries in all. `ClerkVoice` says the card's units as words: "68 km/h" is "sixty eight kilometres an hour", "at km 12" is "at kilometre twelve", "40 s" is "forty seconds". The Stranded line is `Radio.Stranded`, which the HUD now prints from too, so the screen and the voice can't drift apart.
    - **Reading: the card's 2 s.** E.5's table gives the cause card 2 s, and its own example takes about five to say: read aloud, the card can't hold. The table stays true: the card is 2 s, and the clerk's line runs on over the static into the end screen until it's said (a bend taken too fast is about 15 s). The opera has already faded under the card (E.6), so nothing covers it. The Stranded line (about 6 s) fits the pull-back it's said over.
    - **A rebuild keeps what's there.** The model doesn't read a word the same way twice, so `clerk.py` keeps a take already in the bank (same speaker, same respelling) and reads only what's new: adding the card's words didn't touch the manifest's. `--fresh` reads everything again (a new speaker does too). Respelled for the phonemizer: "Combe" (Coom), "Drury" (Droo-ree).
    - Tests: `ClerkVoiceTests.EveryCauseCardAndTheStrandedLineAreSaidWithNothingBreakingUp` (every cause template with every blame line through `CauseCard`, every word of the name lists, the units), `TheClerksOneLineIsSaidOnceOverTheStatic`. `dt audio clerk --line "..."` renders one.
