334. **The Moose heard (AU1, queue #73; G1's note 339, docs/design/creatures/moose.md §4).** G1's nine lines on the audio checklist have 30 candidates (`tools/audio/recipes/moose_tells.py`, `moose_acts.py`), each in its own band and rhythm, so no other creature's tell is mistaken for it (spec A.1, A.4). The first of each is installed, and `GameAudio.MooseSounds` hooks them off the record alone, as G1 answered on #247: its `MooseMode` in Height, its aggro in Extra2, its rams in Health (1 + rams).
    - **Grazing** (aggro under `listenAt`): the slow chew held, with browse torn, a sac's wet creak and a low grunt now and then. **Listening** (`listenAt` to `warnAt`): the chewing stops dead and nothing plays; the silence is the tell. **Warning** (`warnAt` up): a cough-like grunt, a run of teeth clacks and a hoof raked back, every 1.2–2.8 s.
    - **Squaring up:** two stamps 0.38 s apart and a snort, as it begins. **Charging:** its hooves and its wheeze held, brush breaking now and then. **Snagged:** the wood groaning and a bellow, then the rack grinding, held. **Searching:** its heavy breathing and sniffing held (not a snort, which is the square-up's), the rack knocking on a wall now and then.
    - **A ram:** a deep iron boom through the car on each `RamCount` step, so it lands on the shudder, and the rack scraping the plates 0.2 s after.
    - **A train going by:** grazing and short of warning, with the train moving over 1 m/s and a car within `trainPassAt` (the art's `trainPass` clip's rule, and the sim's +25 aggro on the same condition), a long call after it and the verge thrashed, once a pass.
    - **Pinned:** `CreatureSoundTests.TheMooseIsHeardGrazingFallsSilentListeningWarnsAndChargesAndBoomsOnEachRam`. All eight lines are at L1 (`levels.py`).
    - **Not yet:** the director's Keep or Redo; the hooves stay one loop whatever the ground (the charge is on ballast and earth).
