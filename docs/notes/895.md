895. **Encounter selection and player-relevant placement: the next threat a problem somewhere else, and spread along the train (the spawn director overhaul's D1, queue #609; n1-A3, nathaniel1).**
   - The brief: [docs/design/spawn-director-overhaul.md](../design/spawn-director-overhaul.md) §3 "Encounter selection", §4
     level 3, §5; the plan, note 874. Built on #603's `SpawnAttempt` (note 885), branch `n1-a3-609` off `n1-b1-603`.
   - **What was already there** (read on #603's branch, so not rebuilt):
     - The brief's §3 priority list is mostly the spend's weighting already: (1) legality and eligibility are each rule's
       `Weight`/`Free` and the director's gates (`Allows`, `Tonight`, slain, `Room`); (2) the pacing need is the pressure and
       the night's curve (#606–#608's); (4) variety is `VarietyWindow`, `NightVariety` and the repeat hold (note 742); (5) the
       crew's places and jobs are the census's `WhoseNext` (notes 345, 753, 867: who's next, empty posts, collisions); (6)
       combinations are the conflict table's pairs (App. B.1, `PairWeight`, `PairRepeats`); (7) the tier and the context are
       the cargo, residents', the line's affinity and the want shares.
     - **Missing:** nothing spread the night's threats over the train. A second threat was as likely in the zone the first
       already held as anywhere, and each rule's place (a car, a gap, a coupling) was a uniform pick, whatever was already on
       that car and whoever stood there.
   - **What this adds** (`Enemies/EncounterPlacement.cs`, pure; tuning `director.encounters.selection` and `.placement`):
     - **The zone spread** (`Director.ZoneSpread`, in the pressure's and the curve's choices, before `WhoseNext`): each kind ×
       `zoneRepeatWeight` (0.5) for every engaged threat already in its zone (`Enemy.Zone`: forward, rear, flank, interior,
       structural, outside). Which kind only, never to nothing; the pairs still weigh their combinations up, so a pair
       comes as two problems in two places (§4 level 3).
     - **The placement spread** (`SpawnContext.Spread`, `EncounterPlacement.Pick`): the Climbers' gap, the Draggers' car
       (none up yet), the Whistler's gap, the Fire Flies' lamp car, the Hotbox's car and the Knotter's coupling are weighed
       away from the threats already about the train (× down to `nearThreatWeight` 0.2 right at one, 1 at `threatSpreadM`
       40 m) and from on top of a crewmate (× `onTopWeight` 0.3 within `onTopM` 6 m). Among the places the rule allows only:
       the rule still decides where it may go (a gap nobody's in, a coupling nobody's at).
     - **Deterministic and the same dice:** `Pick` spends the rule's own single draw, and with every place weighing the same
       (nothing about, nobody near) it returns exactly what the uniform pick did. `on: false` on either block is the
       director before this note.
   - **The halt's own** (`director.encounters.halt`, off; GDD D.15 question 3, the director 9 Oct: "Needs a dedicated
     response", and 20 Oct: "What's best for gameplay?"; the manager's pick: one stop encounter after a short lull, behind a
     flag, "test creature spawn first"):
     - `Director.SpendHalt`, after the stops' own in `Choose`: stood on the main line at an abandoned village's halt (a
       `FeatureKind.Village` stop with `Halt` laid; `StopAt` ≥ 1000), once `lullSeconds` (15) have been stood there, one
       encounter if the stop's own haven't put one there: whatever `FreeOptions` (rule-met or free) offers, under the
       engaged cap, the zone spread, who's next and the votes, on dice of its own (`0x4A17`). It's a Stop-channel attempt on
       the halt's key, so `Commit` counts it in the stop's waiting slots: the halt has its one, and a stop's own placed there
       first is the halt's one.
     - The harness reports `threats.haltsStood` and `haltsSent`.
     - The question for the director, with the default: **should a stop at a halt bring one creature of its own?** Default:
       off until the numbers below say it's fair; then on.
   - **Tests:** `EncounterPlacementTests` (neutral = the uniform pick, draw for draw; three threats at the front of twelve
     cars: the next is put there under half as often as a uniform pick and every car stays possible; the nearer a threat
     the less; not on top of a crewmate; the zone factor; on a real twelve-car train with three Draggers at the front, the
     Hotbox's car is picked there under half as often as with placement off); `HaltEncounterTests` (none before the lull, one
     after it, no second, none with the flag off, off by default). The guard tests (#602, note 887) green on top.
   - **Measured** (`dt harness --route frontier:7 --enemies --seconds 1200 --seed 1|2`; 1 bot on 3 cars, 2 on 5, 4 on 8, 8 on 12;
