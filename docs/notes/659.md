    - **A casting let go high off the crane** ("it just disappeared. (`CastingState.Lost` is kept in the enum for the wire; nothing sets it now.) #908.
    - **The lockers hold four** ("we really should be able to put multiple things in lockers"): train.json `kit.lockers.slots` 2 → 4, four shelves to a locker; `LockerTests` read the number from tuning. #910.
    - **The design: what's put in a car stays there, solid (Lethal Company style).** Agreed with D1 13 Oct, written before the change:
        1. **Nothing is pulled in, nothing vanishes.** Note 581's take-in (`Bodies.TakeIn`, `DoorTakeIn`: a find set down within a metre of an open doorway was moved onto the floor inside) goes. A find set down stays exactly where it was put. It counts as aboard, and pays, when it rests inside a car's room, not because it was near a door. A find resting aboard is no longer removed when it's banked (`RunLoot.StepLoot`): its body stays, flagged `Banked`, paid for while it lies there, unbanked if it's picked up, taken or lost (branch `d1.5-finds-stay`).
        2. **The load is the real things in the car.** The decorative load goes: the two `PartKind.Cargo` solids down a cargo car's right-hand side (`CarFrame`, `CargoDepth`/`CargoHeight`) and `TrainKit.LoadProp`'s stacks drawn from `Vehicle.Load`. A car is empty until something is put in it.
        3. **Solid, for players and bots.** A body resting in a car (its `Parent` the car, asleep, inside the room, not carried) is a box in that car's frame (its particles' bounds) that `PlayerMotor.Collide` pushes a player out of, as it does the car's fittings; low enough (under player.json `stepUp`) it's stepped onto. The boxes are read from the bodies every tick, in body-id order, so host and clients see the same ones (a body's resting place is sim state every machine has; `NetcodeTests` must stay exact). Bots plan their way down a car past them: `Bots.TakeFrom` and the aisle walking treat them as the car's fittings, and a car whose aisle is shut by them is blocked for a bot (it doesn't walk through), so bad stacking costs a bot crew as it costs players.
        4. **Bulk loads are a heap; a casting is the casting.** A chute, conveyor, tippler or skip load (`SetPieces`, `Vehicle.Load` 0 → 1) is drawn and collided as a heap on the car's floor, its height from `Load` (deterministic from the one number every machine has), not as packages. A crane casting stays the casting body where it landed in the car, instead of being folded into `Vehicle.Load`.
        5. **Unrig (reading (a)):** a ground hand holds E at a casting still on the crane's hook, set down in a car, to unhook it there; it then lies as any casting set down.
    - **Order of the PRs:** (1) take-in gone, finds stay banked where they lie (`d1.5-finds-stay`); (2) the decorative solids and stacks out; (3) resting bodies solid for players; (4) the bots round them, or blocked; (5) heaps for bulk loads, the casting kept as itself; (6) unrig.
