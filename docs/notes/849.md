849. **The hybrid spawn system: the night's curve and the free spawns (H1, queue #577; the director's playtest of 16 Oct line 17 and 16 Oct evening line 6).**
   - **The director's words (16 Oct, solo):** "I spent all this time in the crane. I still haven't seen a monster ... I do like the whole Lethal Company. The longer a run goes, the more monsters just spawn in general, forcing you to kind of push towards the end"; "only a moose. No other monsters are really showing their faces ... Where's the rest of the content?"; the call: "rules-based is good ... I want more monsters to spawn the longer the run goes that don't have a rules system. And then I want the weighting of the likelihood of spawn for monsters with rules that they have to follow ... to increase." Evening, two players: "Once again not a lot of monsters came and caused trouble for us even though we stopped at a yard and rode the train a lot, even rolling into the end town exactly at dawn."
   - **What was built** (GDD App. B.1 "The night's curve"; spec B.10's marked rows; `director.curve` in `content/tuning/enemies.json`):
     - **The curve:** how far into the night, along the line or toward dawn, whichever is further (the pressure's own progress), to `curve.power` (1): 0 at the gate, 1 at the end. `Director.Curve` holds it each second it thinks. The pressure's escalation reads it (`pressure.escalationPower` is gone; `curve.power` is the one shape).
     - **The rule-bound come on more:** every spawn rule (A1's) is kept as it is. The escalation climbs by the curve as before (×4 at the end); and in the free draws a kind whose rule is met now weighs its rule's weight × `curve.bound` (1) × the curve.
     - **The free spawns** (`Director.SpendFree`): beside the pressure's spend, outside its budget, its cooldown and its holds (building, listening, the census's "one on each", saving for something new), the director sends a free spawn at `curve.atGate` (2) an hour at the gate rising to `curve.atEnd` (14) at the end along the curve, × the crew's share (`crewFade` 0.2 a crewmate alive over `crewFadeFrom` 2, never under `crewFloor` 0.5: a crew of four 0.6; added after the 4-bot trends below), rolled a second at a time on its own PCG stream (`0xF4EE` off the scrambled seed: the night's other draws are as they were with it off). A free spawn is a kind sent **without its situational rule**: `SpawnRule.Free` is the structural gate (what its `Spawn` needs: cars, gaps, someone on the ground, its once a night), and `SpawnContext.Free` tells the placement to come from the dark when no site of its kind is near (the Ribbits' warrens, the Followers' grounds). The rule-less are `curve.kinds` with weights: Cinder Hounds 1 (behind a moving train at any speed; never a stood one: on 4-bot seed 4 the free packs boarded the train stood at a yard with the crew on the ground and lit it, pack fires 0 → 8), Climbers 1 (any gap), Ribbits 1.5, Followers 1, Soot Children 1 (whoever's on the ground, anywhere), the Moose 0.7 (beside the train wherever it stands); the Gaunt has a free gate too (its tier, crew and once a night kept) but isn't named. A kind not named never comes free.
     - **The fill** (`curve.fill`, `Director.Spend` at "nothing fits"): the pressure past its threshold with no rule met (a moving train lets few kinds in; a crew of one or two fewer: "nothing fits" was 424 of a two-player night's 1,200 seconds, below) spends on the rule-less instead of nothing, by `curve.kinds`, charged, paced and relieving as any spend; placed free. `FreeOptions` is the one builder both use.
     - **What holds for both:** the hard caps (`EngagedCap`, `Room`: a crew of one meets one thing at a time), `Allows`/`Tonight` (the roster, `nightChance`, `perNight`), slain, the night's variety (`NightVariety`), `WhoseNext` and the votes; a Dragger or Whistler (silent until met) only with nothing else; never before the night's first threat, which is drawn (`curve.afterFirst`); never within `curve.spacingSeconds` (20) of anything sent. A free spawn takes nothing off the pressure and nothing off the budget; it ends the quiet and counts toward the kind's cap and the night's variety. Logged as `DirectorSpawn.Free`.
     - **Measuring:** `dt playthrough` now reports `director`: every spawn in order (kind, seconds, km, free), `byKind`, `kinds`, `free`, `ownWay` (the house creatures, the lineside moose), the curve at the end, and `held` (why the director sent nothing, by the second).
   - **The Creature Tweaker** (`tools/tweaker/apply.py --check` still applies; its fields keep their meaning: `weight` is the kind's weight among what could come in both channels, `nightChance` its chance of being about tonight, `perNight` its most a night across both). What a row would need for this: a **"free weight"** column (`director.curve.kinds.<name>`, blank or 0 for never free; only kinds with a free gate can take one), and `settings/night`: `atGate`, `atEnd`, `power`, `bound` (`director.curve`). Not on the page yet; D1's (or whoever holds the Tweaker).
   - **Measured** (frontier:7, 6 cars, 20 min, seed 1; `dt playthrough --route frontier:7 --minutes 20 --bots N`, its `director` report; "before" is the same build with `curve.free` and `curve.fill` false, run from a copy of `content/`):
