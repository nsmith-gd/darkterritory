1023. **Creatures, followers and bots on the houses' floors (n1-H2, nathaniel1's Token Budget Manager's worker; queue #726; #710's PR [#1717](https://github.com/nsmith-gd/darkterritory/pull/1717), note 1005, left it; the director, 23 Oct 2026: a basement is "high risk, high reward").** *In review.*
   - **The ways between floors** (`StopWalls.Floors.cs`): which floor of a house with floors a point is on (`LevelOf`, the
     nearest of its floors), the flight's foot and top and the hatch's ladder in the world (`Flight`, `Hatchway`, read from
     note 1005's `Stair`/`Hatch`/`StoreyedLadders`), and `Way(from, to)`: the next leg where floors are in the way (walk to the
     flight's foot or top, or the hatch's ladder; up or down the flight; up or down the hatch), by the ground floor between
     the basement and the upstairs; null on one floor, or on the ground floor going out (the doors are there). A crewmate
     upstairs is reached by the flight and nothing else; one in the basement by the hatch (the cellar windows are the crew's
     way out, not a creature's way in). `FloorsApart` and `FloorUnder`: nothing reaches through the boards, and a body on foot
     indoors stands on the boards of its floor. All read from the house's own geometry (`StopWalls.Storeyed`, the
     `OpenHouses`), so #725's fortress-town houses get it if they're built the same way.
   - **Creatures** (`HouseWays`, every creature that goes indoors after a crewmate: the Lodger's chase, the Householder's
     hunt): `Toward` takes the floors first; `Step` walks the boards of the floor it's on and takes the flight at enemies.json
     `dwellings.stairsPace` (0.7) of its pace and the hatch at `hatchClimb` (1.2 m/s), never through a floor; `Blocked` is
     true across a floor, so no lunge, shriek-at-close-range or grab goes through one.
   - **Who lives on floors** (`dwellings.storeyed`, ["lodger"]): a house with floors rolls its own dice as a house of one floor
     does (each kind as often); a kind not on the list leaves it empty. The Householder, the Hollow House and the Hanger stay
     in houses of one floor because their art draws a one-floor house round them (`GreyboxScene.Houses`: the kept house, the
     sinking house, the Hanger's ceiling at 2.6 m over a floor at 0.17 m); they can go on the list once the art draws a house
     with floors. The Householder's chair stands on the ground floor's boards if it's ever put in one (the gym can).

     chest), or upstairs furthest from the flight's top, clear of the stairwell, where there's no basement. With
     `lodger.ownFloor` (true) it notices only who's on its floor (and anyone close and in reach): the ground floor over it is
     safe; the basement isn't. A target gone up or down out of its reach at the shriek's end isn't lunged at: it comes after
     them by the hatch and the flight. Its lunge stays on its floor.
   - **Found and fixed on the way** (`HouseWays.Toward`, any house creature): a hunter whose quarry was behind its own house
     stepped out of its door, turned straight back for them through its own wall, was "in its house" again and led out by

     corner (0.8 m out), and goes in from its step at 0.7 m.
   - **Bots** (facilities.json `crew.villageFloors`, true): the hands searching a village's houses (note 326) take the finds
