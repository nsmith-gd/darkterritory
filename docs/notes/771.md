771. **The Pickers heard (AU1, queue #501; G1's #315, note 592; pickers.md §4, "a request to the audio chat").** The Pickers
come up out of a yard's drains and race the crew for its loose crates, and make no sound of their own: their coming up,
the tell, is silent. Each is to be heard off its record (`PickerMode` in Height):
- **Coming up:** the grating shoved aside and a chitter. A Picker's first tick is its coming up (`Picker.Up`), and a
  creature's first sight plays nothing, so it's heard there.
- **About the yard:** its small feet held while it moves, and a chitter now and then.
- **Carrying:** the crate's knock and scrape and a chattering pant, held (at half while a heavy crate waits for its second).
- **The bite:** a snap and a hiss.
- **Bolting and going down:** a burst of squeals, and the grating's rattle as one goes down. Each is once for the group at
  a time: a blow scatters every one near at once, and the train going sends them all down.
- **A kill:** a squeal cut short (`World.Hits`' Killed).
