294. **Blocked sidings: derelict cars on a yard's sidings, by tier (B4, queue #32; level-design D.1, D.2, P18; I.4's "derelict cars on the sidings").** D.2's lever "Blocked sidings 0 / 0–1 / 1–2 / 1–3 (never all)" and D.1's cost ("two more [throws] for every blocked siding that has to be cleared", "one [reversal] per clearance", "clearances ×5") were in the design and in nothing else. Note 187's standing rakes (`TrainOnLine.Stand`) made it a generator change, not an engine one.
    - **The layout** (`StopGenerator.BlockSidings`, its own seed `StopSeed.Blocked`, after the yard and its power, so a stop's track and buildings are what they were): the tier's `blocked` count of tracks (stops.json), each holding `derelict.cars` (1–3, at most what its face takes) at the buffer stop: `YardTrack.Derelicts`. **Never the facility's own track** (its loading modules are there), so never all; a new Z.5 check, "Blocked sidings leave a way in", holds it. **Never at a switchyard:** its sidings have its own standing cars (note 187).
    - **The measure** (`StopGenerator.Measure`, P15): a trip into a blocked siding clears it first, `StopMoves.Clearances`, two more throws (the switch of the track the derelicts are put away on, set and restored) and a reversal, plus `score.clearance` (5). The planner still picks tracks by supply, so a blocked siding costs only if the yard needs it.
    - **The bands moved up by about one clearance** (stops.json, the sim's own calibration, level-design I.3): frontier yard 14–36 → 14–38, dead lines 28–56 → 28–64, deep territory 34–100 → 34–112. D.2's own bands (16–38, 36–64, 62–125) assumed blocked sidings all along. `dt site sweep --seeds 60`, kept medians before → after: frontier yard 23.8 → 26.7, dead lines 38.1 → 46.1, deep 58.1 → 71.9; every tier and kind 100% in band (dead lines yard + village was 98.3%), mean attempts 1.05–2.13; the clearances a kept yard's measure makes, on average, 0.43 frontier, 1.08 dead lines, 1.87 deep.
    - **In the world** (`Run.StandCars`, after every switchyard's cars so theirs keep their ids; host and clients alike, from the route): each blocked track's derelicts stand `derelicts.back` short of its buffer stop, handbrakes on (facilities.json `derelicts`). They're `Vehicle.Derelict` (and `YardCar`): empty, unlit, their shells at `integrity` (0.4, so the bite and scar kit draws them battered: the consist kit "in damage states", linegen plan §11.3), anything loaded into one paying `pays` (0.3) of itself (`CargoIntegrity`).
        - **Never the crew's:** a rake of derelicts alone is `Standing` wherever it's been put away (on any track, the main line too), so it's never a car lost (`Run.Tally`, the report's lost-car lines), never in the wreck's bodies, never boarded at the start. Coupled up and brought home they're delivered cars, paying what's in them at 0.3; they never cost repairs (they came battered).
        - **Clearing one** is the crew's own shunting: run in onto them at a crawl (they couple, as note 187's), back out, set them on a track already worked (or keep them, ahead of the engine, for the rest of the night: the cab's view of the line goes with that choice).
        - **Bots don't fetch them:** `Run.StandingOn` (the stop crew's pick-ups) counts a switchyard's cars, not derelicts; `Run.DerelictsOn` counts those. The bots work a facility's own track (I.4), which is never blocked, so they're unaffected.
    - **Readings:**
        - "Blocked" is derelicts on the loading face, standing at the buffer stop: the siding's useless for loading until they're out.
        - "Cleared" puts them away on a track already worked: that's D.1's two more throws and a reversal. Keeping them ahead of the engine instead is legal and costs the crew in a different way, so the measure doesn't count it.
        - "Never all": the facility's own track is always open, so a yard with one track never has one blocked.
    - **Headless:** `dt site --route deepTerritory:2 --stop 5` (three of four tracks blocked; `derelicts` on each track, `clearances` in the moves; the plan PNG draws them in rust at the buffer stops); `dt site sweep` adds `blocked` (stops by how many) and `clearances` (the mean) per tier and kind. In the world: `dt screenshot --route deepTerritory:2 --site --facility 2 --cam 27985,-34,13 --target 27925,-30,0` (the yard's derelicts at their buffer stops between the shed rows), `--cam 27962,-17,4.5 --target 27935,-25,1.8` (one up close).
    - **Not yet:** the bots never clear a blocked siding, since they don't load from a yard's other tracks at all (I.4). No new art: the derelicts are the cargo car at 0.4 integrity, unlit, and at night their scars barely read; a derelict look of their own (rust, a missing door, a lean) is the art checklist's.
