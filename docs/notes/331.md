331. **The hot box, the first upkeep job while the train runs (queue #71, D1; GDD App. F.3, the director, 7 Oct 2026: "on the train, still relatively boring from point A to point B ... things players do to maintain the train while it runs"; [orchestrator.md](design/orchestrator.md) §5.1 U1).**
    - **What it is.** Every `everyMetres` (2.5 km ± half) of the train's running, more above 18 m/s, a car drawn from those running cool has its rear axle box run dry: `Vehicle.HotBox`, the seconds it's been hot. None in the first 2 km, none in the yard or a fort, never more open than there are crewmates alive (at least one). The engine's own never does. All in the new `content/tuning/upkeep.json`.
    - **The tell.** A dry squeal at the box (`hotbox`, a synth loop: 1.25–1.65 kHz wavering, harsher as it heats), and from about 15 s smoke off the bogie, thick and dark by the end, the box glowing dull red from about 40 s (`Effects.HotBoxSmoke`; `dt screenshot --view hotbox --hotbox 100`).
    - **The fix.** Use held 3 s with the hands within 3 m of the box, on the rear bogie outside the wheel: from the coupling gap behind the car, its end ladder, or the ground beside it (`HotBoxes.Within`; never from the roof or inside). The prompt says GREASE THE HOT BOX. The outline's oil can from the lampman's locker is left out: the grease is at the box, until there's a can to carry.
    - **Left alone.** After 60 s it drags: each box takes 2 m/s off the top speed, held there as the Car Hugger holds its cap (1.1× what the engine can pull, over the cap; a flat 0.15 m/s² was out-pulled). After 120 s it catches: a car fire at the bogie (App. C.5), and the box is done.
    - **Readings.** The outline's "every 4 km of running per car" would be 29 boxes on a 23 km night of five cars; read as one somewhere in the train every 2.5 km, about eight a night. The cap is the outline's "one job open per active player".
    - **Netcode.** The host brings boxes on and sets the fires; the seconds count on every machine (prediction drags as the host does), and greasing is predicted like the hands' other work. The vehicle record carries `HotBox` (protocol 35).
    - **Bots.** A bot in reach of a hot box greases it (`Heed.HotBox`, before a rescue). And a roof walker goes to the nearest one nobody's at: along the roofs to its car, down the end ladder into the gap behind it, and Use held there (`RoofWalkerBot.Grease`; the box is in reach from the ladder's foot). On a 4-bot frontier:7 night with boxes made frequent (every 600 m), 6 of 10 were greased and 3 caught; the same night without the errand, 4 of 9 and 4 caught. The rest are boxes that come on while the walkers are at a stop's work, warming, or after the pack. The harness takes `--upkeep`; without it a harness night has none, so the soak, the balance sweep and the tests are as they were.
    - **Verified.** `HotBoxTests` (7; the walker's errand: one walker greases a box set 20 s in, before it drags, and never does without it): one comes on by the train's running, one at a time with nobody aboard; left alone it holds the train 2 m/s under its top speed; left too long the car's alight; greased from the gap behind and from the ground beside; not from the roof.
