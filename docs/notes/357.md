357. **At a switch stand, Use is the lever's (A1, queue #94; found following up the switch audit, note 289).** `World.CrewAct` works a switch's lever (`SwitchStands.CrewAct`, Use held `junctions.throwSeconds`) before the hands (`Bodies.Handle`), and neither knew of the other: so the press that started a throw also put down what was carried (the lamp you'd carried out to the stand in the dark, dropped at your feet) or, empty-handed, picked up whatever lay by the stand, and the lever still went over. The cab's interactables were already spared the pick-up (`CrewActions.NearestInteractable` blocks it), but a stand isn't one of them, and nothing spared what was carried. The HUD offered the thing lying by the stand before the switch, and with something in hand offered nothing.
    - **Reading:** at a lever, the lever has Use, as at a Holdout's lock or a ruptured boiler with the kit in hand (`keep`): what's carried stays carried and nothing lying by it is picked up. Throw still lets go of what's carried (the same as everywhere). The alternative, "hands first" (put the lamp down to throw the switch), keeps one rule for the whole game, but it makes a lit lamp at a stand a thing you must set down in the dark, and a crate by a stand a thing you must move before you can throw it; the stand was always meant to be worked alone with a lamp (GDD §17).
    - **What changed:** `Bodies.Handle` takes `lever` (World passes `Switches.InReach`: a stand's lever or, with the powered thrower fitted, the cab's): carried, it's kept unless Throw; empty-handed, nothing is picked up. The HUD's switch prompt is one helper (`Hud.SwitchPrompt`, the cab's lever and a stand's) said before what's carried and before what's lying in reach, as the sim decides it. `Bots.Heal` doesn't hold Use for a healing find at a switch's lever, where it would throw the switch (its own rule: "never at a lever or a door").
    - **Verified:** `SwitchTests.AtAStandUseIsTheLeversNotTheHands`: Use held at a stand with a lamp in hand throws it and the lamp's still in hand; empty-handed with a crate by the stand it throws back and the crate stays down; Throw lets go there; a step away, Use picks up as it always did (it failed on main at the lamp). `HudTests.AtAStandThePromptIsTheLeversWhateversInYourHandsOrLyingByIt`: THROW TO with a crate by the stand and with a lamp in hand, and holding Use throws it with the lamp kept.
