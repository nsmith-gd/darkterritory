557. **A weak link adapts (N2, queue #273; the netcode audit's gap 5, note 530).** Down per client sat at §6.2's 64 kbit/s budget with nothing adapting: a client on a thin downlink lost snapshots at random, and a host on a thin upload hurt everyone and was told nothing.
    - **The client says what it's getting (protocol 46).** The input message carries the client's own downlink loss, in percent (`ClientSession.ReportedLossPct`: note 534's counted share, or all of it when nothing has come for a second, since a starved link has no gaps to count). The snapshot carries a flags byte after the queued count; bit 0 says it's one of every other.
    - **A thin link is sent every other snapshot.** Over `link.thinLossPct` (12; note 534's share counts a stale snapshot as lost, and a jittery link reads a few percent) for `thinSeconds` (2), the host sends that client on the ticks of its id's parity only (`HostSession.IsThinned`, `SnapshotsThinned`): half the bytes, whole pictures, within the client's 100 ms interpolation window. The client counts the withheld ticks as heard, so neither end reads 50 % lost. Thinning cures the loss it's for, so it's held `thinHoldSeconds` (20) of quiet before it's let go, or it would come and go every few seconds.
    - **The crew are sent in a different order each tick.** A saturated upload drops the last of each tick's burst, so whoever was sent last starved outright (8 bots on a 250 kbit/s cap: three got everything, two got a sixteenth). Rotated, every one of eight got 1,700–1,800 of 3,600 at 15 Hz, the worst correction 0.11 m, no deaths (`dt harness --host-up-kbps 250`).
    - **The host is told its upload can't carry the crew.** `link.strainCrew` (2) or more reporting a thin downlink at once for `strainSeconds` (5) is `HostSession.UploadStrained`: the one thing they share. The lobby panel and, out on the line, the corner say "YOUR UPLOAD CAN'T CARRY THE CREW: n KBIT/S OUT" (`HostSession.UpKbps`, the snapshots sent over the last second), so the figure can be taken to a router; it clears once the thinning has made them fit. `dt screenshot --hud --upload-strained` (the host's own sends losing a quarter).
    - **The interest radius is left alone:** the tells' 520 m (note 23) is gameplay, and halving the rate halves the bytes without taking a far tell from anyone.
    - **The loopback link has caps:** `LinkConditions.HostUpKbps` (a bucket shared by everything the host sends) and `DownKbps` (each client's); `dt harness --host-up-kbps n --down-kbps n`; the report's `snapshotsThinned` and `uploadStrained`.
