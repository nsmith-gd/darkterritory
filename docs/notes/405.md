405. **The lane ahead, for the forward gun (queue #141, D1; [orchestrator.md](design/orchestrator.md) §5.3 6: "lanes ahead (runners crossing the line in the lamp, for the forward gun)"; GDD App. F.3, the director, 7 Oct 2026: "threats attacking the train tower-defense style, to give the gunners something to do").** Note 328's hound run came only from behind, so the engine's forward gun never had a wave of its own; it had the Track Doll and whatever stood on the line.
   - **What comes.** Of a run's pairs, every `run.aheadEvery`-th (3) from the second on (1: every pair) comes from in front, to a train with a gun laid forward on the engine's rake (`Gun.Facing` < 0; without one the run all comes from behind, as before). A crew of four or more (a run of 4 or 6) meets one pair ahead in each run; a crew of one or two, whose run is one pair or a pair and one, meets none.
   - **How.** Put down `aheadMetres` (330) in front of the engine and `aheadLateral` (12, 16) m out to the pair's flank, they howl where they stand for the hound's `howlSeconds` (the eyes in the headlamp's beam, the howl from in front: the tell). Then they run in at `aheadSpeed` (3 m/s) against the train, easing across the line so they're `aheadCross` (3) m out on its far side as they meet it. They leap aboard the first car behind the engine at its front end as it comes alongside: the engine is hooded, nothing to leap onto (note 338). Aboard, they're the pack fight (App. A.3), staying aboard (note 269), at the front of the train. One the train's passed without boarding goes.
   - **Answered** as the run's are: a ball landing within `scatter` (5) m sends it off, one on it kills it (note 290). At 21 m/s the forward gun has them for about seven seconds, from its range (220 m) in to the stack's mask (about 60 m). That's one shot laid early, or two quick. The pair runs 3 m apart, so one good shot takes both.
   - **Counted.** `Director.AheadPairs`, `AheadRunners(pack)`, and `dt harness`'s `houndRuns[].ahead`. `dt screenshot --run-ahead --view lane`: three pairs ahead in the lamp, from the forward gun's seat.
   - **Tests.** `HoundRunTests`: the run's second pair boards the first car from in front and the first the rear car from behind; the lane on its own (`aheadEvery` 1) is put down ahead to a flank, holds through the howl, crosses the line and boards the first car on the far side; no gun laid forward, no lane; the gunner bot seated at the engine's gun scatters or kills an ahead pair before it boards.
   - **Bot nights.** `dt harness --route frontier:7 --bots 4 --enemies --upkeep --express 21 --seconds 900`, seeds 1-3, before and after:
     - Before: 12.0, 12.0 and 11.9 km; 3, 2 and 2 deaths; 5, 5 and 5 cars lost.

     - No night stood. The shorter runs carried more cars home.
