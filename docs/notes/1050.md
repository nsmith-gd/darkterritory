   **For the director** (one each; the Token Budget Manager puts them in the inbox):
   1. **The Knotter's pull-under.**
      - *The moment:* you're on a car's roof at speed and a line goes taut round your ankle. A second and a half later you're under the train. Your friend was two steps away and never had a chance to grab you.
      - *Background:* §20 says every grab leaves 8–20 s for someone to act, so a friend can save you and the save is the story. `dt social` measures the Knotter at 1.5 s from taut to under. A friend standing right there frees you at once, but nobody can get there in time.
      - *Decision:* should the Knotter hold its line taut long enough for a friend to reach you (8 s or more before it pulls you under)? Or is the taut line itself the warning, so the fair window is the coil's telegraph before it?
      - *Default meanwhile (#750):* an 8 s taut hold built behind a flag, off; the Knotter plays as now.
   2. **The Whistler's carry.**
      - *The moment:* a Whistler takes your friend from a gap at a stop and runs for the dark. You chase. 26 seconds later they're dead in its nest.
      - *Background:* two crewmates prise a carried friend free in 0.6 s, so the carry is fair if you're near. It's 6.7 s past §20's 20 s ceiling, though, so a lone chaser gets the longest wait of any grab.
      - *Decision:* shorten the carry to 20 s, or keep the long chase into the dark and widen §20's band for the Whistler alone?
      - *Default meanwhile (#750):* a 20 s carry built behind a flag, off.
   3. **What counts as the Passenger's and the Choir's grab.**
      - *The moment (the Passenger):* it pulls the pin on the car you're in. Alone, the car is gone in 43 s; with a friend, it's stopped in 1.5 s. It isn't a kill, so the social test's 8–20 s band doesn't fit it.
      - *The moment (the Choir):* it holds you 3 s, alone or with a friend, because its answer is singing together (note 720), not hauling you free.
      - *Decision:* should the Passenger's uncoupling count as a kill with its own 8–20 s count once the pin's out, or should the test judge it by the car being lost? Should the Choir's hold last until two sing, or should the test stage the crew singing?
      - *Default meanwhile (#750):* nothing in the game changes. The test judges the Passenger by the car lost and stages the Choir's singing, and the note says so.
   4. **The tools of upgrades.md §7.3** (approved 14 Oct: "all of §7.3's").
      - *The moment:* at the blacksmith between nights, the TOOLS shelf has the handcar and the grab irons from that list, and none of the other fourteen. There's no communication cord to ring the cab from car 5, no lamp in the cab that goes out when the train parts, no fusees to find the way back.
      - *Background:* each tool answers a trouble that grows with the tiers and doesn't remove it (§2). U1's catalogue (note 678) built ten other tools, and the shelf's whimsy since. These fourteen were never queued.
      - *Decision:* does "all of §7.3's" still stand, including the two majors (the lookout cupola and the hose car)? Any to drop?
      - *Default meanwhile:* #753–#755 build them in that order: the crew's tools, then the engine's, then the fight's and the majors.
   5. **How long a train can get before the flank can't be held** (Part Eleven q3).
      - *The moment:* twelve cars long, the crew can't keep the middle cars clear however they split up, and every night past it is lost at the flank.
      - *Background:* `dt balance` sweeps the train's length but doesn't judge it. #760 measures it first, then asks this with the numbers.
      - *Decision:* is that ceiling the game's real progression cap (§11's car count), or should upgrades push it further?
      - *Default meanwhile:* nothing changes; the measure goes in the note.
   6. **Comet freight** (B.9).
      - *The moment:* a yard has a car of comet material to load: the best pay of any freight, and the whole night more interested in the train for as long as it's aboard.
      - *Background:* B.9 says a stop is the only way it gets aboard, and none does yet. Only a comet fragment found at a stop (a two-handed find, note 718) turns the comet's weights on.
      - *Decision:* should some stops load comet freight (say one night in five, at the deeper tiers), or is the fragment enough?
      - *Default:* the fragment stays the only comet aboard; not queued.
   7. **A seamless hand-over between nights** (level-design T.4; note 692: "if I do want to do seamless loading").
      - *The moment:* the train comes in through the gate at dawn. Instead of the town's card, the crew get down in the same square and the next night leaves from it, with no load between.
      - *Background:* the town a night ends in is already built the same as the next night's departure, to the last person (v1.6). Only the load and the town card sit between them.
      - *Decision:* wanted?
      - *Default:* the town card as now; not queued.
