719. **Big finds (K1, queue #453; the director, 14 Oct 2026, `docs/design/friendslop.md`).**
"Very big value things should take two to carry. That is a good idea." And on 9 Oct (GDD App. F): loot that stays put, so "poor stacking becomes an obstacle ... lethal company style". The audit found the Opportunists (grab what's in front of you, greed against risk) and the Hackers (physics to poke) underserved: hand loot was mostly an abstract stowed sum, nothing broke when dropped, nothing big needed two.

   - **What it was:**
     - Every find was one person's, and none was worth more than a fifth of a car-load but a comet fragment (note 718).
     - Nothing about a find changed with how it was handled.
     - Every open house was alike: open, its door hanging, its finds what its cupboards drew.
     - A find put down in a car was a ball that floor friction stopped dead: the brakes nudged it a little, a bend did nothing, and finds passed through one another (nothing stacked).
   - **GDD §19 first** (the table's Jackpot and Fragile valuable rows, "Big finds"), and App. F.3's line on the village houses.
   - **Now:**
     - **Two-person jackpots** (loot.json `jackpots`; `StopLoot.Jackpots`, `Run.IsJackpot`, `Run.JackpotsAt`):
       - a safe (1.0 car-loads), a church bell (0.85), a ship's figurehead (1.1), each a heavy crate's ball (`items.*.radius`, 0.42 to 0.45 m) with an owner (`LootOwner(stop, 0xE00 + i)`), so it's carried exactly as a strongroom's heavy crate is (spec D.2, T43, `Body.Second`): one end held and it lies; both and it rides between them at the heavy pace; never thrown.
       - It's a find, not freight: lying still in any car it banks and pays at the gate (`StepLoot`), and the cargo loading leaves it alone (`Run.StepLoading`, an autosave's `Loaded`).
       - Where: the jackpot house's at `jackpots.house` (0.4), and one more at a stop's `jackpots.chance` by tier (local 0.08, frontier 0.1, dead lines 0.12, deep 0.15) beside a yard's crate stack or strongroom (1.8 m along from it) or in the middle of an open house's biggest room; never more than `most` (2). On a stream of its own (`StopSeed.Jackpot`). Measured over 30 nights: local 0.57 a night, frontier 1.07, dead lines 0.87, deep 1.0 (about a car-load a night, if the crew go and get them).
       - The HUD names it: "TAKE AN END OF THE SAFE : [E]   IT TAKES TWO", "AN END OF THE SAFE" in hand.
       - Its look (`FindKit.Jackpot`): the safe in green-black iron, its brass dial, handle and hinges and a gilt maker's line; the bell in bronze gone green at the lip, on its oak headstock with its iron straps; the figurehead carved standing and laid on her back, blue skirts, white bodice, arms crossed, gilt hair, the scroll at her feet.
     - **Fragile valuables** (loot.json `fragile`, `worth`; `Body.Knocks`, `Body.Fragile`, `Bodies.Knock`, `LootTuning.Knocked`/`Named`):
       - a crate of glass (`glassware`, 0.09), a cut-glass decanter (0.11), a mantel clock (0.13): in a cellar one time in seven, under the boards one in seven, a cabinet three in 51, and the jackpot house's floor. About six a frontier night.
       - Knocked: a change in its speed of more than `knockSpeed` (5.5 m/s) in a tick beyond what gravity and its car made of it (a fall from over about 1.6 m, a throw, the ballast at line speed), or its slide across a car's floor striking something at more than `slideKnock` (3 m/s); one every `knockSeconds` (0.3) at most. Put down from the hands is a metre's fall, 4.4 m/s: no knock.
       - Each knock leaves `keep` (0.5) of its worth, rounded to fives; a banked one knocked again unbanks and banks at its new worth.
       - Shown only by the thing (`FindKit.Find(..., knocks)`): cracks across the bottles and a smashed crate's stumps and shards; the decanter chipped, then its neck off and lying by it; the clock knocked askew, its dial crazed and its side dented, then its arch split off. And by the clerk: the tally groups finds by kind and damage, "Find: mantel clock, smashed, 30." (`fragile.items` words by knocks). No price anywhere else (note 718).
     - **The jackpot house** (loot.json `jackpotHouse`; `StopLoot.JackpotHouse`, `Run.LockedHouse`/`LockedDoors`/`LockedAt`, `StopWalls.Lock`/`Locked`/`Picking`, `World.DoorAct`):
       - At `chance` by tier (local 0.25, frontier 0.3, dead lines 0.35, deep 0.4) a stop's biggest open house of one door (not a pair, a Holdout or the Gaunt's roost) is locked, on every machine from the route and the seed (`World.EnableRun`, `EnableTown`).
       - Its door's shut and a held Use does nothing; the wrench in hand (the repair kit carried, where it's the repair tool) held there `unlockSeconds` (6, a Holdout lock's `open`) opens it, quietly. How far on the doors' record for the HUD ("PICKING THE LOCK (40%)"; "LOCKED : THE WRENCH OPENS IT" without). Opened, it's a door like any other.
       - Inside: its kept finds drawn `draws` (3) times, the worthiest kept (on `StopSeed.Better`); `extra` (2 to 3) of `items` lying on its floor's corners (`LootOwner(stop, 0xD00 + i)`, `StopSeed.Extra`); a jackpot two times in five. Over 40 frontier nights its things averaged more than three times an ordinary open house's.
       - A house creature's likelier: its share times enemies.json `dwellings.jackpotShare` (2), never past 1, weighted not forced (`Dwelling.Lives(..., jackpot)`).
       - The bots keep out of it while it's locked (`StopCrew.Village.Lived`); their shelter only takes open doors.
       - Its look (`GreyboxScene.HouseDoors`): its door panelled and painted oxblood, a brass knob and knocker; a lit window beside it, its lamp flickering and lighting the ground in front, seen from the line.
     - **Loose loot rides the train** (loot.json `ride`, on; `Bodies.Ride`, `Feel`, `SlideOn`, `Blocks`, `Body.Slide`):
       - Each car's felt acceleration is its frame's velocity's change, the other way (outward on a bend, forward braking, back pulling away), level, capped at `maxFelt` (12 m/s²); a frame moved faster than 3 m/s in a tick (a tool or a test putting the train somewhere) is felt as nothing.
       - A find, jackpot, freight crate, stores crate, toy or powder bag lying in a car's room (not one on a roof, on its mount or in a locker) slides once what it feels passes its `grip` (0.05 g; a jackpot `heavyGrip` 2 times better), against `slide` (0.03 g) of friction, till it stops. The physics' floor friction stops a ball dead, so the slide is a velocity of its own over the physics'. Off its floor (out of a door, over a stack's edge) the slide becomes the physics' motion and it flies.
       - It stops against the car's solids and shut doors and the other things riding there (shoving them a little, waking them); the room's walls stop it as they stopped a throw (note 173), and an open side door lets it out onto the line, where it's lost unless fetched (unbanked: a car's finds pay only for what's in it at the gate).
       - Things are solid to one another in a car (ball on ball): they stack; one resting on another counts the slope it lies on against `stackGrip` (0.3 g), so a find set well off another's middle rolls off and a square stack stands.
       - Cheap: only what's moving is stepped (asleep when still, as before); no more than `liveCap` (12) move at once in a car, the rest held where they lie. Host only, deterministic, in body order; clients draw the bodies as they're sent.
       - Off (`ride.enabled` false), things lie as they did.
