861. **Shared challenges that make laughs on a multiplayer night (the director's playtest of 16 Oct evening, line 8) (Otis, queue #588).**
   - Claimed 2026-10-19 14:20 UTC by the Token Budget Manager (nathaniel3) for Otis.

The director: "This was the first real multiplayer test and socially it was a bit of a dud because we didn't get any of the
challenges to share together that lead to laughs and fun". A shared problem, for this item: a moment two players must solve
together that can go wrong in a funny way (a crewmate grabbed with a friend in reach, two at one job with something coming at
them, a hold broken by a friend).

- **Measured first** (`SharedWatch`, new; the harness report's `shared`, balance.json `shared`). Each tick on the host it
  keeps: `grab` (a crewmate's Held spell), `grabNear` (a living friend within `nearMetres` 26 of it at some point: voice's
  reach, they hear it and can come), `grabAlone`, `rescue` (the attribution log's Rescue by someone else), `pairJob` (two
  crewmates the census reads as committed within `pairMetres` 6 of each other and not each carrying their own thing, or a
  crane's operator and rigger however far apart) and `pairThreatened` (such a pair with an engaged creature within 26 m).
  A **stop** is a stand at a facility, or 5 s still by a village out on the line, until `afterStopSeconds` 30 after it
  moves off; a **problem for two** is `grabNear`, `rescue` or `pairThreatened` (`SharedWatch.Problem`). `dt balance` adds an
  advisory check, "stops shared", against `stopsWithShared` 0.5. A watch only: it changes nothing, so no Sim behaviour moved.
  Gotchas found building it: every crewmate holds their uniform radio (a body), and a winch's hands are let go at the end of
  each host tick (`Run.Step`), so the watch reads busy hands from the census (which counts inside the tick).

- **Why so few:**
  1. **Few stops, and only some ask for two.** A frontier night stands at one to three stops in 18-25 minutes. A wreck yard's
     winch (both handles, spec D.2 "2 mandatory") and a foundry's crane are the only jobs that put two crewmates on one
     thing; a switchyard pick-up is stood at for seconds and nobody gets down.
  2. **Nothing comes at a stop.** On main the director sent nothing while anyone was afoot (`afoot.spawnsAfoot` 0 on every
     night): the facility lull, then "building" and "nothing fits". No grab happened at any stop. That's line 7, Ivy's #587
     (note 860: one waiting creature and one boarder at every stop); the "mine + Ivy" rows above are with her branch at
     badf3eb merged in.
  3. **The run has nothing for two.** Grabs came 0-2 a night, all on the run (Cinder Hounds, the Whistler), most with
     nobody near. In a crew of two the driver is in the cab and the other on the gun, so the gun's loader (note 752) can't
     happen, and nothing else on the run takes two.
  4. **Bots never carry a jackpot** (no bot errand for it), so a bot night can't show a jackpot carried; the counts above are
     the jackpots lying there, the opportunity.

- **Calls for the director** (pitches, nothing built):
  1. **The jackpot's chance and worth.** *(Answered 19 Oct: "a jackpot should be at most 3/10", see the cap above.)* Six stops in ten, each about a car-load of goods if two bring it home: the night's
     takings go up for a crew that carries them. 0.1 on the frontier puts it back as it was.
  2. **A pair at one job counts as free for each other.** The census's collision weight (note 753) needs a *free* crewmate in
     reach, so a crew of two both at the winch, the crane or a jackpot never gets one: the rule against piling on reads it as
     nobody to help. Pitch: at a pair job, either of the pair counts as free for the other (they can let go), so a grab on one
     at the winch is weighed up and the other has to drop the handle to save them.
  3. **Bots carry jackpots in pairs** (A1's area): a bot answers "help me with this" and takes the other end, so a human with
     bot crewmates has a second pair of hands, and bot nights measure the carry.
  4. **More stops a night.** One to three stood stops in a frontier night's 18-25 minutes is few chances for anything (the
     line generator's, B1/B4's).
  5. **Something on the run for two.** Between stops, a crew of two has nothing that takes both; the loader needs a third.
     Pitch: a coupling knocked loose at speed (note 356) is pinned by one in the gap while the other holds the car ahead's
     handbrake on, or it parts.
