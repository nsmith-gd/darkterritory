801. **The cannon's shot heard again with the shot endless (AU1, queue #528; U1's find on #1040).** `GameAudio.CrewGuns` heard a shot as a round gone from the gun (`GunState.Ammo` lower than last seen), not its `LastShotTick`, which is the predicting machine's own tick until the host's arrives and so changes more than once a shot. #960 (note 689, the director: "let them fire and reload as much as they want, infinite ammo") made `Guns.TryFire` take nothing with combat.json's `infiniteAmmo` on, so since 13 Oct no cannon's shot, ignition or recoil has played (the muzzle flash and the creatures' hearing go by `LastShotTick` and were never affected). `CrewAudioTests` faked the round gone, so it held.
- **The fix** (`GameAudio.Shot`): a shot is a round gone from the gun's stock (the night's stock on, note 374), or, with nothing taken, the full reload a shot leaves it needing where it needed none (`ReloadNeeded` up from 0; `TryFire` sets it on every shot while there's a round to load, which with the shot endless is always). A rack filled from empty (`Guns.RackUp`) asks for the reload too, but brings rounds with it, so it's no shot.
- **Tests:** `CrewAudioTests.EveryonesShotIsHeardOnce...` fires as `TryFire` does with the shot endless (and fails on the old trigger); `WithTheNightsStockAShotIsARoundGoneAndARackFilledFromEmptyIsNoShot` holds the finite stock's two shots (the last leaving nothing to reload) and a rack filled from empty, no shot.
