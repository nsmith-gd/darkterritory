822. **The Flooders: houseboat towns on stilts and their flood creed (queue #549, B2; the World Building board's Cultures tab, the director, 12 Oct, "Houseboat Towns": "Towns made entirely of houseboats on stilts. They are inhabited by people known as Flooders, a cult who insist the comet that brought the pestilence that changed the creatures of the world against humanity was an act of God and God will cleanse the Earth with a great flood once again.").**

**Where they live.**
- **Houses.json `byForm`:** the marsh towns (the dykeland, Fenfolk) and the salt flats draw the Flooders' house character as often as their others:
  - a dykeland is half Fenfolk, half Flooders;
  - a salt town is a third each of breakwater, Fenfolk and Flooders.
- **Which nights:** local:1, local:2 and frontier:5 make a Flooder town of their dykeland (`--form dykeland`); frontier:7's is Fenfolk.

**Their houses** (houses.json `flooders`, "a houseboat town").
- **Every shut house is a houseboat** (`HouseCharacter.Hull`, `HouseDesign.Hull`, on its own stream `houses.hull`, so every other house is as it was):
  - a flat-bottomed scow's hull, its ends raked, planked and tarred black, a rubbing strake along its sheer;
  - up on three pairs of braced stilts over the stage;
  - the house on its deck (`MaritimeKit.Hull`; 0.9 m of stilts and 1.3 m of hull, `HullLift` 2.2 m);
  - a ladder leant against the bow up to its door.
- **The houses themselves:** low cabins (mostly one or one and a half storeys), gable and hip roofs, tarred, kelp, bleached and storm paints.
- **Far off and in a street's block:** the same lift and a hull's box.
- **The lamp and the smoke:** a lived house's door lamp and its chimney's smoke are lifted with it.
- **An open house stands on the Sim's floor as ever** (its rooms are the Sim's walls), so it's never a houseboat.

**Their creed** (world/towns.json `creeds`; `HouseCharacter.Creed`).
- **What it is:** what a town's people believe, by its houses' character. It's never drawn as a habit is, so no other town has it.
- **Heard:** its eight lines join the custom's, heard first by the hall's keeper, the folk round the centrepiece and the households. They say:
  - the comet was sent;
  - the creatures were sent to thin us out before the water;
  - every house a boat on its legs;
  - the water marked on the stilts every morning;
  - "Your train runs on the ground. Where will it run when there isn't any ground?";
  - their dead pushed out on the tide in a boat;
  - stores for forty-one days;
  - a grandmother who saw the comet come apart over the bay.
- **Posted:** its first notice, "The Water Book", goes on the board after the custom's order. Its others ("Wanted": pitch, oakum, rope that's never been wet; "To the crews of the train"; "Launching") are dealt with the rest.

**Measured.**
- **`FloodersTests`:**
  - on local:1 at 600 and local:2 at 1500, every shut house is a houseboat and no open one is;
  - its people say the creed, and the Water Book is the board's second notice;
  - frontier:7's dykeland and harbour, and a street town, have neither;
  - it's the same every time.
- **`FloodersArtTests`:**
  - a houseboat's house is the hull's height higher than the same house on the ground;
  - its hull stands out past its walls;
  - far off the same;
  - its chimney's smoke rises from the lifted chimney.
