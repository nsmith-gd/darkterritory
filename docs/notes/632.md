632. **A train taken at dawn ends the campaign (D1.4, queue #362; the director, the inbox, 12 Oct: `D1-eaten-at-dawn`, "c, The campaign ends", roguelike).** Until now a dawn loss (`RunEnd.DawnMissed`, "taken at dawn", note 588) was settled like any other lost night: no pay, the costs off the scrip, the lost cars off the consist, and a fresh board the next morning. Now it's the crew's last night.
    - **The rule** (campaign.json `dawnEndsCampaign`, on; `Campaign.Ends`):
        - `Campaign.Settle` settles a dawn loss like any other, so its night is in the history, then closes the save (`CampaignState.Over`). The company has no more trains for that crew.
        - A closed campaign has no board (`Offers` is empty), and no night can begin on it (`Begin` throws).
        - A derailment, a crew lost and a night stranded are settled as before, and the crew goes on.
        - With the flag off (a mod's), a dawn loss is just a lost night again, as v1's Late Dispatch would want (note 625).
    - **What the crew sees:**
        - **The end card,** on every machine: under "RUN LOST / TAKEN AT DAWN", in red, "THE COMPANY HAS NO MORE TRAINS FOR YOU" (`Hud.CampaignOver`).
            - The setup sent to every joiner says the night is a campaign's (`SessionSetup.CampaignNight`, from `HostedAsApp.Campaign`).
            - Each machine's world is told the rule from it (`World.DawnEndsCampaign`), so followers in co-op read what the host reads. A quick night isn't a campaign's.
        - **The fortress after the night:** "TAKEN AT DAWN. THE COMPANY HAS NO MORE TRAINS FOR YOU." In place of the board, the stores and the purchases, the page has:
            - the heading, with the crew, the route and how many nights they ran;
            - **A NEW CREW**: a fresh campaign in the same slot, three cars on local work and no scrip, from the starting fortress;
            - and back.
        - **The slots list:** "SLOT 1: NIGHTJARS, TAKEN AT DAWN", "N nights. The company has no more trains for them."
    - **The tools:**
        - `dt campaign play --lose-at s` settles with the rule. Its board says `over: true` with no contracts.
        - A second `play` on that slot is refused: "slot 1's campaign is over (taken at dawn): the company has no more trains for Crew 1. dt campaign new --slot 1".
        - `dt campaign nights` stops at a closed campaign and says `Over` each night.
        - `dt screenshot --hud --report-file night.json --campaign` draws the end card as a campaign's (out/shots/over632.png, from `campaign play --lose-at 60` on local:5, slot seed 4).
    - **Verified:**
        - `CampaignTests.ATrainTakenAtDawnEndsTheCampaign`: settled and closed, no board, no night begun. Derailed, crew lost, stranded and delivered go on. The flag off goes on. A new crew is open.
        - `FrontEndTests.ACampaignTakenAtDawnIsOverAndItsSlotStartsANewCrew`: the slot's line, the page without board or purchases, and A NEW CREW's fresh campaign with its board.
        - `CampaignSessionTests.EveryMachineKnowsACampaignsNightEndsItAtDawn`: host and joiner both have the rule on a campaign night; a quick night hasn't.
        - By hand: `dt campaign new --slot 1 --seed 4`, `dt campaign play --slot 1 --bots 4 --lose-at 60` (`dawnMissed`, over, no contracts), then `play` again (refused).
