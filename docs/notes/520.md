520. **The director's inbox (W1, queue #257; the director, 9 Oct 2026: one person giving feedback on everything).** Questions for the director were lines in COORDINATION.md's *Waiting on the director*, fourteen of them on 9 Oct. Some had waited a day, and each was answered, if at all, in that agent's own chat, so no other agent knew the answer. The Director's Inbox (claude.ai/artifact/U72ospwbR1Bpb1jkbSwjxT, its page's source `tools/inbox/inbox.html`) puts them in one place.
    - **What the director sees:** what's waiting, those holding up work first and then the oldest, each as a form with what's asked, its pictures, PR and links. Its answer sits at the foot: Approve / Not this, an option to pick, a question's answer, or Looks right / Needs work, always with a reason box. A strip at the top shows how many are waiting, how many hold up work, the oldest, and how many were decided this week. Behind tabs are the decided ones (with their reasons, and Reopen, which keeps the old answer in `history`) and *Standing orders*: rules the director writes once ("never change a creature's look without a Look Review round") for every agent to read before it submits. There is a filter by agent and a search.
    - **Saved as data:** each answer is written onto its ask in the artifact's store (`decision`: `verdict`, `choice`, `text`, `at`). So an agent reads a decision, and every decision on a subject (`tags`, `queue`), with one `ArtifactData` query. The store's root is writable only at the Editor level and up (`write: "admin"`), so a viewer can read and not answer.
    - **Back to the agent:** an ask names its session (`session`). On an answer, the page sends it to that session as the director (the `Claude Code Remote` connector's `send_message`, asked for once). The ask records `told: sent`, or why not, with "tell them in their chat" shown on the page. The message says to read the ask back from the store before acting on it. The store is the record; a message can be lost or come from anywhere.
    - **Seeded** with the 14 questions in *Waiting on the director* as they stood on 9 Oct (D1, D1.2, D1.3, the orchestrator's §8 and two older ones), their options as their authors wrote them. Orchestrator question 3 (which upkeep job first) was left out: the build order is done but for U6, which has its own ask.
    - **Not yet:**
        - Agents on the director's other accounts (B, D, E, F, …) can open the inbox only once the director shares it with those accounts, as Editor to ask and as Viewer to read. Until then their questions stay lines in COORDINATION.md, and W1 copies them in.
        - The send to a session from the page is refused by the organization's policy: the director's first answer (9 Oct) came back `told: failed:blocked_by_policy`. So agents read their answers themselves, at the start of a session and at each check-in (COORDINATION.md, *The director's inbox*), and W1 copies the answers for agents on other accounts into COORDINATION.md (*Answered in the director's inbox*).
        - Feedback notes from inside the game (note 516) don't show in it yet.
    - **Verified:** the page published (version 3), its store read back at the Viewer level (the 14 asks there, `rules` empty), the asks' fields as the page reads them. The page's answer paths were read through against the store's `update` and `set`. The only write path not exercised is the director's own click.
