699. **An animation pass on G1's creatures: creepier movement, telegraphs that read (G1, queue #431; the director, 13 Oct: "Continue an animation pass in blender of the creatures so we can get more fluid and creepy animations. Focus on making movements creepy and telegraphed intentions clear").**

Twelve creatures' clips were reworked in `tools/blender/<name>.py` and rebuilt through their recipes (bpy 4.5) by G1.13 and G1.14. The Ribbit is E1's (note 558).

   - **The craft, for every one:**
     - **Creepy:** stillness broken by snaps (CONSTANT keys, or 2–3 frame moves); the head leads and the neck, shoulders and hips follow late; idle loops of uneven beats, so they never read as a cycle; twitches out of time, stutters, and limbs that feel the ground before they take it.
     - **Telegraphs:** a big change of silhouette, squared to the target and held still at its peak for the sim's telegraph. The commit goes where the telegraph pointed, then a readable recovery.
   - **Kept:**
     - Every clip's name, and every length the C# or the sim reads.
     - Bones, meshes and budgets.
     - `dt art clearance` is 0 for all twelve.
   - **The telegraphs:**
     - **The Lodger's shriek** is now the sim's 1.5 s (`lodger.shriekSeconds`; it was 1 s and held). The head snaps up and stares; the body unfolds in lurches to its full height, arms flung and jaw unhinged; then it shakes, each arm to its own beat, and ends dead still, tipped at you.
     - **The Householder's rise** (1.5 s): its hands slam flat, it pushes up and holds bent over the table, then it's upright in 3 frames. Its head follows a guest in ticks of about 6° (`CreatureArt.HouseholderTick`), not smoothly. Its hunt is arms out, never swinging, no bob.
     - **The Brakeman's cornered** (2 s): the chain whirls level overhead, faster toward the lash. His head snaps between two crewmates, then locks on one and freezes. It now plays on the lash's beat (`BrakemanClip` reads `phaseSeconds`), so it builds to each lash.
     - **The Moose's squareUp** (2.5 s): head, then neck, then body squared to the target. A hoof is lifted slowly and slammed, then the other, then a grunt. It rocks back onto its haunches aimed down the line, frozen to the end.
     - **Tower Jaw's threat:** reared higher, the tail rising slowly while the claws curl paw by paw, frozen at the top, then the slap in 2 frames. Its tail's rotation was inverted, so main's raise drove the tail into the ground and the slap never showed (`hit` too); both are fixed.
     - **The Gannet:** a held aim before its fold, and wings mantled over a pinned player with the head snapping to new looks.
     - **Hotbox's glow** (the seize tell): the plates lift front to back, hold venting, slam shut together; then the legs ripple and freeze.
     - **The Pickers' bite:** a 3-frame held crouch, the snap held, a recoil.
     - **The Mourners' startle:** a 2-frame flinch and a frozen beat before the arms go up.
     - **The Hanger's grab:** folded to spread in 2 frames. Its legs feel along the beams one at a time while hidden.
     - **The Knotter's clamp:** claws spread, held, then curled shut one after another.
     - **The Freight Beetle's push:** legs stepping in a ripple, all six frozen while the body gathers, then the heave.
   - **Also fixed:** main's Hanger `death` failed clearance (every leg tip through the body); the Lodger's folded arm flipped between frames.
   - **Reads less well (open):**
     - The Beetle's and Hotbox's leg ripples are under their shells: from 10–30 m their tell is the body's.
     - The Moose's hooves still slide on the ram, and Tower Jaw's toes sit about 4 cm under the floor; neither has foot planting, as before.
     - The Lodger's shriek starts from a crouch, not its folded pose, so with no crossfade it pops a little out of `hidden`.
     - The Mourners' `drag` and `scatter`, and the Pickers' `scatter`, `heave` and `descend`, are as they were.
   - **Looked at:** `dt art clip <model> <clip>` contact sheets and each creature's `dt screenshot` views.
   - **Verified:**
     - `art clearance` for each creature.
     - `CreatureArtTests` and `CreatureBodyTests`, including clips at 30 fps that loop closed, budgets, briefs' sizes and deterministic skinning.
