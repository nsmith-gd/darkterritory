"Im soft on the mail. I dont like it so far. Lets cut for now."

   - **What it was:** the mail cranes (note 673) and the catch (note 727): a crane every 350-600 m beside the line from the yard to the terminus's board, each with a board ahead saying which side; someone in an open cargo side door, hook out, caught its bag: mail pay (`sight.json` `mailScrip`, 20-60 scrip a bag, paid at the terminus with the freight), coal for the tender, rounds or spares.
   - **The cut:** one switch, `sight.json` `mailCranes` false (and `SightTuning`'s default, which the line's prop boards, the linegen map and a screenshot's boards read; `LinesideTests` holds the two in step). With it off, `Lineside.Drops` places no crane, so there are no boards, catches, HUD lines (MAIL CRANE, the hook at the door), sounds, bot errands or mail pay downstream. The code stays: `true` brings it all back as it was.
   - **Kept for its return:** the mail's own tests turn the switch on in their setup (`LinesideTests`, `BoardSightTests`, `MailHudTests`, `MailAndCometSoundTests`, `BotGunnerTests`, `CrewOfTwoTests`), as do `Staging.MailCrane` and `dt screenshot --mail`. AU1's mail sounds (#504: the bag snatched or torn off at a crane) stay installed and unused.
   - **GDD first:** §19's mail cranes and App. F.6 say "cut for now"; ROADMAP, friendslop.md and notes 673 and 727 say so too.
   - **What it takes out of the pay** (measured before the cut, on main with the mail on: `dt harness --route <tier>:<seed> --bots 4 --enemies --as-app --seconds 2700`, seeds 1-4; the mail's scrip is in the night's gross and paid only on a delivered night):

     The mail paid about the same each night on every tier (100-200 scrip), so it mattered most early: about a tenth of a Local or Frontier night, a fiftieth of a Dead lines one, under a hundredth of a Deep territory one. (The share is the mail over the rest of the gross, summed over the tier's delivered nights; the missed and lost nights paid no mail.)
   - **The campaign** (`dt campaign sim`, F.4's yardstick): `campaign.json` `standardCrew.runningCosts` 0.05 is "coal, ammunition, repairs and fees less the mail" (note 636's measurement), so the cut raises it by the mail's share. Run with the same runningCosts on every tier: 0.05 (as now) reaches 6 cars in 9 runs, 10 in 21, 15 in 40, 20 in 56; 0.07 (the late tiers' share added) 10, 22, 42, 59; 0.14 (the early tiers') 10, 23, 45, 63. With the early cars on Local and Frontier and the late ones on the Dead lines and Deep territory, 20 cars comes at about 60-62 runs, against F.4's "roughly 55-60".
