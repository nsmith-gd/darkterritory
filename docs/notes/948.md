948. **The two-person heavy carry holds together: the front end walks at the back end's pace (queue #635; n1-B2's findings under #630, note 920; n1-A1, nathaniel1).**
    - **The fault** (note 920, reproduced on main, 21 Oct): a heavy crate or keg up between two from the site's stack, taken to
      a car, fell again and again on the way. The front end walked to the car's steps (`WalkTo`) at a full walk. Only a jackpot
      (note 867) and a heavy from the yard (note 440) were paced to the back end (`HeavyPace`, half a walk). The back end,
      following the crate's trail a pace behind (`Follow`), fell behind, their hands passed `Bodies.HeavySpan` (2.4 m) and
      `CarryHeavy` let it go. Picked up again, it went a metre and fell again.
      - `AHeavyCrateGoesBetweenTwoToWhicheverCarHasRoomAndIsNeverDropped` on main, a heavy for cars 1, 2 and 3 of an 8-car
        train (the spur holds 4): it fell 24, 15 and 5 times, at 172–199 s.
    - **Fixed, in two parts:**
      - **The front end keeps to its back end** (`StopHand.Paced`). A jackpot walks at `HeavyPace` all the way, as before.
        Any other heavy walks at a walk, at `HeavyPace` once the ends are more than `PaceSpan` (0.8) of the span apart,
        and stands past `WaitSpan` (0.92).
        - The gap is read as twice from the front end's hands to the crate, which rides midway between the two pairs.
          `Bodies.HandsAt` is now public. (`World.CrewThisTick` is empty without enemies, so the back end's own state isn't
          there to read.)
        - Pacing every heavy all the way fixed the falls but slowed every carry. That moved the stop's other work and broke
          `TwoHandsCarryTheHeavyCratesInTogether`, `TwoHandsFetchAJackpotLyingInTheYardAndStowIt` and
          `WithTheChoirGatheringAHandLoadingACarShutsItselfInAndOpensUpAfter`, which all pass on main.
      - **The back end closes in** (`StopHand.Follow`). With its hands too far from the crate (more than `PaceSpan` of the
        span, as reckoned above), it heads for the crate itself, not for its point a pace back along the trail. Without
        this, the front end stood waiting while the back end stood at its trail point, and neither moved again till the
        train left (car 2's heavy, from 185 s on).
    - **The turn back through the back end** (note 920's second cause) didn't show for any of the three cars once the front
      end kept to the back end; the back end's trail is otherwise as it was.
    - **Pinned by** `StopCrewTests.AHeavyCrateGoesBetweenTwoToWhicheverCarHasRoomAndIsNeverDropped(target)`, cars 1–5:
      - every cargo car but the target is filled, so the heavy's half fits there alone;
      - it's carried and loaded into that car;
      - it's never let fall on the way (a crate held between two that ends up on the ground counts as a fall).
      - Cars past the spur's capacity are skipped.
