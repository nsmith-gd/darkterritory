   - **Before.** Note 692's town types had their ground in one mud at the yard's level, the harbour's piers bare, nothing on its wharf, the glasshouse town's beds all one crop and the dykeland's hay barn with no hay; the bots stayed at the train in a town ("they work the yard, not the streets"); along the line the Fundy mud was bare and no maple grew in the Maritime woods. The World Building board had no open note or culture on the towns or the shore (25 Oct), so this is the leftovers alone.
   - **Built** (every piece deterministic, from the plan or the night's seed; the Sim's additions on streams of their own, so nothing laid before moves):
     - **Lobster traps along the wharf** (Sim `TownBetween.Lay`'s last pass, `Wharves`; towns.json `between.wharf`): stacks of traps in a broken row along a wharf's or a quay's edge where it runs along the line over the water (the harbour's Hard, the docks' quays of the furnace and the crag), 0.8 m back from the edge, clear of the way along it, anything standing and a pier's foot (2.5 m). They're `TownPiece`s, so solid and drawn as the between-the-houses' traps are (`BetweenKit.Traps`). `TownBetweenTests` keeps them out of the yard but for these (the yard's far side on those three, 40 m out from the line).
     - **Boats at the piers** (Sim `TownForms.Moored`, stream `form.boats`): a dory tied alongside three piers in four, a second the other side half as often, 5 m clear of the pier's foot and its end, on the harbour's piers and the docks'; drawn afloat on the water (`MaritimeKit.Afloat`), as the jetties' dories are.
     - **Boats on the mud at low tide** (Sim `LinesideProps.Mudflats`, stream `lineside-mud`; the lineside piece `doryAground`, `MaritimeKit.Aground`, footprints.json written by `dt art footprints --write`): along a Fundy shore and past a dyke's salt marsh, one 20 m cell in eleven has a dory heeled over on its bilge on the near half of the flat (the half above low water), turned any way, its painter out to a stake. Frontier:44's dyke (5230-5880 m) has two, at 5354 and 5689.
     - **Red maple** (biomes.json `flora.maple`; `NovaKit.RedMaple`, 110 triangles, three variants): the swamp maple in the fall, a short grey trunk forking in three, its bare crown's cards red-brown and its last leaves hung in clumps the deep red the tamarack's gold is to the bog. In the valley farmland 0.4, the forest edge and drumlins 0.35, the bog and the dead fishing town 0.3, the dykelands 0.25, the Atlantic shore 0.15 (none in the black spruce, the barrens, the highland or the coal country). A tree's species is the only thing that changes (every try still draws the same): frontier:7 has 499 maples among its 13,375 living trees, every tree where it stood. `dt linegen lineside --species maple` lists the five nearest the line.
     - **Each town type's own ground** (Game `WorldArt.Underfoot`: `FormBaseTexture`, `FormPavingTexture`, `FormTopTexture`, `FormWayTexture`; the footsteps follow, `Footing.OfTexture`): at the yard's level the harbour's and the salt town's shingle, the dykeland's red Fundy clay, the quarry's and the high line's chippings, the glasshouse town's loam, the furnace's slag, the timber town's sawdust and needles, the crag's bare rock, the drowned borough's mud; the quarters (the wharves, quays, the cranes' and founders' yards, the dyke and the mounds) paved over that, a skin a metre a row merged into quads (the harbour's in granite slabs; the drowned borough's, the furnace's and the crag's in cobbles; the timber yards in planks; the dykeland's turfed); the terraces' tops the furnace's slag, the loam, the heath or the crag's rock where they were mud; the ways the crag's cobbles, the dykeland's clay, the timber town's rutted mud, the furnace's slag.
     - **Crops** (Game `WorldArt.FormGround`'s beds, `BedCrop`): each of a glasshouse town's beds its own crop, by where it lies: kale tall and blue-green, potatoes low and dark on high hills, beet red, or lifted and gone to stubble. **The dykeland's salt hay** (Sim `TownForms.Dykeland`, stream `form.staddles`; fixture `haystack`, `MaritimeKit.Haystack`, 244 triangles): nine stacks on their staddles out in the open marsh between the walks, as the Tantramar's stood (the hay barn's "salt hay off the marsh, cut at low tide"), each 7 m clear of any walk, stage, landmark or the wall; solid, as a landmark is.
     - **The bots in the town's streets between nights** (Sim `StopHand.Town`, `BotCrew.OffDuty`; facilities.json `crew.townStroll` 60, `townStreets` 2, `townLegSeconds` 45, `townLinger` 12): a bot hand or gunner off duty in a town (the night delivered to one; or at the gate while someone playing is down off the train and nobody's sounded the horn) gets down, walks out along one of the two streets nearest the square (by its place in the crew) up to 60 m from its middle, round the houses (`FootPath`), stands looking round 12 s and goes on to the next spot. When it's over (the one playing back aboard, the horn) it walks to the foot of the nearest car's steps and its own part takes it aboard (the driver's all-aboard waits for it, note 995). 0 keeps them aboard. It's intent, as everything a bot does.
