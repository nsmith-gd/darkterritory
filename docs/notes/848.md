848. **The stops' creatures, stop kind by stop kind, and the Grumbler on its perches (B4, queue #576; the director's playtest of 16 Oct evening, `docs/playtest/2026-10-16-evening.md` line 7: "Monsters are positioned and ready at yards and abandoned villages, or use those stops to get onto the train"; level-design H.2).**
   - **The check the line asked for:** `dt afoot census [--routes local:1-6,frontier:1-6,deadLines:1-6] [--crew 4]`. It goes through every stop of every night and stands the train at the stop's point on the main line, braked (note 795's case: that's being at its stop). It puts one crewmate afoot in the middle of the yard's buildings, and again in the village's. For each stop it reports:
     - the creature sites the stop lays (level-design H.2);
     - what its open houses hold by their own dice, and what was put in them tonight;
     - which creatures' spawn rules would let them come to that crewmate there (weight above nothing; tonight's dice aside).

     Tallies are by stop kind: a yard, a yard and its village, a village on its own. It reads the rules; it doesn't change them.
   - **What it found** (18 nights, 47 stops; the crew of four the director plans for):
     - **Yards (27) and yards with villages (18):**
       - Every one lays a warren, the Gaunt's roost, a Soot Child's call and a Whistler's nest.
       - Every one but one also lays the Followers' ground and a Grumbler perch.
       - At every one, the Dragger, Whistler, Ribbits, Moose, Freight Beetle and Soot Children can come.
       - The Gaunt can come wherever its tier gate lets it: Frontier and on, once a night.
       - Followers come only to whoever stands on their ground, the loading faces: 6 of 27 from a yard's middle, none from a village's.
     - **The Grumbler could come at 2 of 45.**
       - A perch is laid under every yard gantry, but its rule asked for the facility's own crane, and only the Foundry has one.
       - **Fixed here (below): 44 of 45.** The one stop left has no crane at all.
     - **The Mouse Moose and Tower Jaw:** they never come to a train stood on the main line. Their rules want it down the spur (`AtFacility`), or approaching a coaling tower or a facility's own gantry. They're their owners' (notes 790, 363); left as they are.
     - **Houses:** a yard and its village averaged 1.5 creatures in its houses (Hangers 17, Householders 10), with the night's `nightChance` deciding which of the dice's picks are about.
     - **Villages on their own (the abandoned villages):** only 2 stops in 18 nights, against 45 yards. There, the Ribbits, Gaunt, Moose, Followers, Soot Children, Whistler and Dragger can all come (`InSettlement`), and their houses roll like any village's. They're rare for two reasons. A village is laid only at a halt or dead town on the main line (`PlanStops`). And the dead settlements come either on an alternate (tiers.json's `settlementLine` trade-off, not the main line, so no stop is laid there) or by the must-place `deadSettlementNearFacility`, which is 0 in every tier. **The director's call** whether there should be more (with lines 6 and 8); not changed here.
   - **The Grumbler on its perch** (level-design H.2's "placed but not yet read"):
     - It comes to any of the stop's cranes with castings stacked, a yard's gantry as much as the facility's own crane. Of those, it takes the crane of the perch nearest the crew on the ground, or the consist's middle with nobody off the train. A line with no perches laid (a hand-laid one) gets the first such crane.
     - It rides that crane's casting: craned aboard, it's in that car eating the cargo. The crane is kept on the host (`Grumbler._crane`), as the Mouse Moose keeps its own.
     - Its weight (×2 with food), its once-at-a-time and its `nightChance` (about one night in ten) are unchanged.
     - The creature gym's call takes the nearest casting stacked by any of the nearest stop's cranes.
   - **Checked:** `GrumblerPerchTests`:
     - At a stop craned only by yard gantries, with a crewmate by the last gantry, the rule lets it come (it didn't before). It's put on that gantry's castings, the perch nearest them, and craned aboard it's in the car.
     - With every casting gone, it doesn't come.
     - The census before and after: Grumbler 2 → 44 of 45 stops; nothing else moved.
