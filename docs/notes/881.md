   - **The contract** (`src/DarkTerritory.Sim/Enemies/Threats.cs`), for #600, #610 and #611 to build on:
     - `enum ThreatState { Dormant, Ambient, Threatening, Engaging, Resolved }`.
     - `Threats.Of(Enemy, World, ThreatTuning)` (and an overload taking the living crew's positions, so a caller reading every
       enemy reads them once: `Threats.LivingAt(world)`). Host-side, deterministic, read from the enemy's own state:
       - **Resolved:** gone, breaking off, or peeled away (a Gannet away, a Freight Beetle driven off, Tower Jaw away or its
         structure down).
       - **Threatening** for a car fire (App. C.5), someone in it or not.
       - **Engaging:** holding someone, or `Director.Confronting` (telegraph, commit, grab, punish).
       - What hunts nobody (`Threats.Hunts`: hazards, the Mourners, the Pickers) is **Ambient**, and never counts.
       - **Threatening** when `Enemy.Working(world)`: doing a train system harm with nobody near. A Hotbox in its truck (not
         prised), a Knotter in a coupling, a Cinder Hound aboard to stay (it feeds and lights the car), a Brakeman climbing,
         walking or winding, a Freight Beetle braced or pushing, Tower Jaw gnawing, a Mouse Moose dragging a casting.
       - **Dormant:** a Brakeman hidden, or a kind that lies in wait (`Threats.LiesInWait`, `Director.Engaged`'s list:
         Dragger, Whistler, Car Hugger, Gaunt, Tippy Toesie, Moose, the four house creatures) still in its dormant phase.
       - **Threatening** in its alert phase, or with a living crewmate within `nearMetres` (20).
       - Else **Ambient**: about and on the move, nobody near (a Climber pacing a car the crew isn't in).
     - `Threats.Counts(state)`: threatening or engaging, what the caps count.
     - Tuning: `enemies.json` `director.threats` `{ on, nearMetres, ambientRoom }`.
