366. **The Freight Beetle: it pushes the freight away from you (G1, queue #103; the director's brief of 8 Oct 2026; design `docs/design/creatures/freight-beetle.md`; GDD §21, A.6, B.6; spec B.18).**
    - **The crate's own physics carries the push:** each tick it sets the crate's pace across the ground (its particle's previous position), so walls stop it, an edge drops it, and one at rest in a car is loaded by the car's rule (`Run`'s settle). Its push turns toward "directly away from the nearest player" at `turnDegrees` a second, so two players steer it. Crates only (cargo, heavy and loot crates): barrels and bales come when the facilities have them.
    - **Verified:** `FreightBeetleTests` (5).
