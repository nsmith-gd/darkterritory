359. **S-bends (queue #96, B1; level-design B.4 and note 278's "Not yet"; the director, 6 Oct 2026: derailing on a bend is the core fear, and always the driver's mistake).** Note 278 gave every night its tier's hard bends, each one turn. Two hard turns close together couldn't be laid: each was its own curve limit and its own demand, and two lethal demands inside half a braking distance fail the lethal spacing check (§7.3), the rule that a driver who brakes for one can brake for the next. An S-bend is the one place that rule's reading is wrong: the driver should brake once and hold it.
    - **The piece** (`LineBuilder.SBend`, setpieces.json `hardBend` `sDeflectionDeg` 30–50 and `sGapM` 20–50): two turns at the hard bend's radius, either way, with a short straight between and its straights either side. Where room is short, shorter straights, then smaller turns; none under its least turn (one turn is laid instead). Realised as a reverse curve (`HShape.Reverse` with `gapM`: the straight between exactly as the piece says, the first turn the way the world band has room for; its heading comes out as it went in).
    - **How many** (tiers.json columns `sBends`): the chance a hard bend is laid as an S. Local 0 (its one or two teach the board, and a Local night lerping toward Frontier stays at none), Frontier 0.2, Dead Lines 0.3, Deep 0.4, Deep max 0.5. Rolled on a stream of its own keyed by where the bend is (`sBend`), so a night without one lays exactly as it did, and an S counts as one of the night's bends.
    - **Held as one** (`CurveLimits`, `HeldTo`): the first turn's limit runs on until the train's tail is round the second (the resume board a train's length past the S's last turn, not its first), so the second turn's limit starts under it at the same figure and is no demand of its own. One board, one demand, one resume; the lethal spacing check sees one lethal check. Form 19's line says "S-bend". The board pass already skips a curve under a lower-or-equal limit that a demand boarded (note 278), and the cab map already inks each curve limit of a board's figure in its reach (note 285, which found an S of ordinary curves on deepTerritory:2), so the second turn is red on the map with its figure.
    - **The land** (`IntentsFor`, `SBendMiddle`): the hill on each turn's inside, the fall on its outside, changing sides halfway along the straight between.
    - **By a branch** (`AwayFromBranches(…, s: true)`): an S comes out on the side it first turns to. Past a dead line's toe it first turns away from the dead line, as a single bend does. In an alternate's window it first turns away from the alternate, so it steps the main line away (a single bend turns toward the alternate's side, which keeps a one-way turn's line on the far side of its chord; an S has no chord to be the far side of).
    - **The metrics** report `sBends`. `hardBends` counts an S's turns as two, as it counts a reverse curve's halves (note 278).
