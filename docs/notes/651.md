651. **The threat's scaling on the Creature Tweaker (D1, queue #381; the director, 12 Oct: "Let's get this all set up as a matrix so we can see when one number change influences another").**
The crew and the train now raise the threat (note 646: "scale by both"), and a long train puts two on a crewmate (note
647). Those numbers lived only in `enemies.json`, so the director couldn't set them or see what they did. They're on the
Tweaker now, beside the dawn setting, under "The whole night":

- **Faster for each crewmate over four** (`director.pressure.crewScale`, 0.12) and **faster for each car over eight**
  (`lengthScale`, 0.04): how much faster the night's pressure builds toward its next creature, below four crew or eight
  cars slower, never under `scaleFloor` (0.5) either way.
- **Two threats on one crewmate from this many cars** (`director.orchestrator.longTrainCars`, 13; 0 never).
- **The crew × cars grid** (crews 1–8, trains of 4–18 cars): in each square how fast the night builds against a crew of
  four on eight cars (where the estimates are measured), how many can be under a threat at once, and the most on one
  crewmate. It recomputes as the director types, tinted orange for more and blue for less, with an amber edge where the
  director's values differ from the game's. The grid is `Director.EngagedCap`, `MaxConcurrent` and `PressureTuning.Scale`
  line for line, from the store's `settings/night` `rules` (the game's fixed numbers: `scaleCrew`, `scaleCars`,
  `scaleFloor`, `engagedPerActive`, `engagedPerCar`, `perPlayer`, `perPlayerLong`, `maxConcurrentSmallCrew`/`LargeCrew`).
  `apply.py --self-test` runs the page's own function under node at 48 squares against the same rule written out in
  Python, and pins ThreatScaleTests' three: 3 at once for four crew on 8 cars, 4 on 12, and 8 on 18 (two each).
- **What the grid doesn't say:** creatures a night. A bigger multiplier doesn't bring that many more, because how many can
  be about at once limits it too (four bots on 18 cars: ×1.40 the pressure, 17 → 26 a night, note 647). The page says so,
  and "Measure my values" plays real nights.

`tools/tweaker/apply.py` writes the three into `enemies.json` as it does the dawn setting, and `--game-out` now writes the
night's `game` and `rules` from the tuning as written (and fills a `want` the page hasn't had yet from the game's, the
director's own kept). Also fixed in it: a row that stays whatever (a house, `staysAlways`) no longer puts its creature in
`wakers.staysAtDawn` (the store's Hollow House row would have).
