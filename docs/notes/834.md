834. **The director's playtest of 16 Oct, lines 19 and 27: the Moose's munching quieter, the walls' guns never on a beat (AU1, queue #562; docs/playtest/2026-10-16.md).**
- **Line 19, the Moose** ("The munching is a bit loud ... We do need to be able to hear it, but we don't want to hear it maybe so loud. We want to allow players to get a little bit closer"). Its grazing sounds (`tell-moose-grazing.chew`, held, and its browse, creak and grunt now and then) were at the tells' default: +8 dB, full by 4 m, rolloff 0.8, out to 250 m, so at 20 m, where its temper starts to rise (`crowdAt`), the chewing was barely 3 dB under its own level, and its tier-1 duck held the train and the crew down 9 dB from 250 m out. Now +2 dB, rolloff 0.9, out to 150 m (`tools/audio/install.py`, the four cues reinstalled, the takes unchanged): about 7 dB quieter from 12 m to the line (-6.6 dB at `closeAt`, -10.6 dB at `crowdAt`, -12 dB from the train going by at 25 m), so it sounds further off than it is and a crew can creep in; it still grows by 4 dB or more as they close in, so how near they are is in the sound. Still tier 1: the grazing is its presence tell (moose.md §4).
- **Line 27, the town walls' guns** ("they seem like they're firing once a second. It should be a bit more random"). Six guns, each on its own steady 4.2 s beat (note 599's `Effects.WallGunBeat`), made a shot about every 0.7 s on a pattern that came round every 4.2 s. Now each gun has its turn every 4.2 s but fires anywhere in the first 60% of it (reloaded and laid, sooner or later: never closer than about 1.7 s), and lets some turns go by, more of them while the town's fire slackens: a swell of two slow waves (23 s and 9.7 s, out of step) over all the guns on one target, from about a quarter of their turns in a lull to nearly all at a burst's height. So the walls fire in ragged bursts with lulls between: each gun about every 7 s on average, all six together a shot every 0.7 s at a burst's height and every 3 s in a lull, never evenly. Still from the clock alone, so the flashes (`Effects.WallGuns`) and the sounds (`GameAudio.Volley`, note 772) keep the one firing; the guards' rifles in the yard (note 608) fire on it too.
- **A ball heard landing is one heard fired:** `Volley` keeps the walls' shots it heard fired, so a ball already in the air as a Waker came to stand isn't heard landing out of nowhere.
- **Tests:** `EffectsTests.TheWallsGunsFireInRaggedBurstsAndLullsNeverOnABeat` (ten minutes of six guns, early on and hours into the app's clock: each gun's gaps uneven and never under a reload, all of them together never a beat, bursts and lulls); `WakerSoundTests.StoodAtTheWallsTheTownsGunsAreHeardFiringOnItAndEachBallLanding` (now a share of the turns fired, every ball heard landing heard fired); `CreatureSoundTests.TheMooseGrazingIsHeardFromTheLineButQuietEnoughToCreepCloser` (the installed sounds at `crowdAt`, `trainPassAt` and closer).
