437. **Burns on a hot run (D1.2 for D1, queue #173; note 380's "left for next").** The burns on D1's 6–8-bot express nights, found and measured. The bots changed; a report counter was added; the creatures' rules didn't change.
    - **Where the fires come from:** boarded hound packs, nearly all of them. A pack aboard keeps setting its car alight while nobody's near (note 269, `CinderHound.Feed`). Over nine nights (frontier:1, 3, 4, 6, 7 and deadLines:2, 3; 6 and 8 bots; `--enemies --upkeep --express 21`, 900 s), packs set 118 fires against 8 hot boxes caught. The harness now reports them as `threats.packFires`, beside `upkeep.caught` (`World.PackFires`).
    - **What killed the walkers** (frontier:7, 8 bots, seed 1: four burned in car 1, replayed with every walker's state logged):
        - **Trapped on a side door's steps.** Four walkers had been at car 1's side door for a mail bag, standing on its steps outside the walls, when the car caught. They made for its extinguishers on a slant, into the door's jamb, and stood against it for 20 s with the car alight round them. Now a walker outside a burning car's walls steps straight in through the doorway first (`RoofWalkerBot.Fight`). The replay to 330 s went from 4 burned to none.
        - **The door against the fire.** On the same steps the Shut step (`WarmUp`) wouldn't hand over to the work, since the doorway is outside the room's box (`PlayerMotor.Indoors`). The open door walked them back out while the fire walked them in. On the car's floor now counts as in: the fire first, the door after.
        - **Into a fire under its pack** (frontier:7's 8-bot night on main, car 10). A walker sent in to fight a fire while the pack that lit it was still on the roof. A fire on a car with a pack aboard is no longer trouble to work from inside; the fit go at the pack (the walkers' own pack fight).
    - **Measured** (the nine nights, main → this branch): Burned 8 → 6; crewmates home 32 → 37 of the nights' crews; all deaths 29 → 25 (Mauled 15 → 13, JumpedAtSpeed 1 → 0). The pack exclusion alone took Burned 8 → 7 and home 32 → 37; the steps fix the rest.
    - **Left for the director (not bot work):** about 13 pack fires a night on an 8-bot hot run. The bots fight what they can, but a pack left aboard keeps lighting its car, and the remaining burns are walkers fighting those fires. Note 269's ignition interval (`igniteEverySeconds`) and how often a pack is left alone are the levers.
    - **Verified:** `CarFireTests.WalkersOnASideDoorsStepsGoInAndPutTheFireOut` (three walkers on car 1's side door steps at its forward jamb, the train at 15 m/s; it fails without the step-in, a walker off the steps dead of the landing) and `PackFireTests` (no work under a pack from beside or on its car, cold or hurt; a fire with no pack is fought).
