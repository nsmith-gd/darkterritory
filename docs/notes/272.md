272. **The damage model: a few big hits, rare healing, an edge flash (the director's decision of 6 Oct 2026, GDD App. F.1).** "Damage model: Lethal Company style. Health exists, but damage comes in a few big hits, never chip damage. Healing items are rare loot. Damage feedback is minimal: an edge flash and a sound. Creatures mostly don't take damage; they're driven off by their rules."
    - **Big hits** (`enemies.json` `damage`: `minHit` 30, `minGapSeconds` 2.5; a hit is 35, a heavy hit 60, as spec B.2's "most attacks 35–60" already said). Before → after: Cinder Hounds 45 every 2.5 s → 35 every 4 s; Grumbler 15 / 1.5 s → 35 / 3 s (`grabBelowHealth` 30 → 35); Climbers inside 25 / 1.5 s → 35 / 3 s; the Gaunt 35 / 2 s → 60 / 4 s; a Choir ghost's hit back 15 for every blow → 35 at most once every `hitBackEvery` 3 s (new, `ChoirGhost.Struck`); the marsh (the Drift) 6 a second → 35 every 2.5 s. Bites still can't kill (App. A.1, kills go through GRAB), so with these each grabbing creature takes two hits to beat you down, then grabs: two or three hits from death, and a clean escape after the first costs one. The Stoker's door (`doorBurn` 60, `doorKill`; note 271) already fits.
