1020. **A crew of two balanced like a crew of three, the Frontier night's end eased, fewer repeats: measured, then proposed on the Creature Tweaker (queue #723; the director's two-player Frontier night of 24 Oct, `docs/playtest/2026-10-24.md` lines 1–4; Alder, nathaniel2).**
   - **The ask.** The director and their wife (her second night, new to the genre) on a two-player Frontier night: "great
     for 3 people", not two; "hard to get into a rhythm and chaotic"; "one too many monsters with some repetition"; "too
     grindy towards the end. The beginning felt good". Measured first; **nothing in `enemies.json` changes** until the
     director answers. The defaults stand.
   - **How it was measured.** `dt harness --as-app --route frontier:7 --enemies --seconds 2700`, the night `measure.sh`
     plays, at 2, 3 and 4 bots, seeds 1–6 each (bot nights run about 24 minutes, faster than a human crew's, so the end
     reads lighter here than it plays). Encounters from the report's `encounters.list`, a head an hour in each third of the
     night by the clock (the gate, the middle, the end). Variants played from a local copy of `content/` with one
     `enemies.json` changed, nothing committed.
   - **What was found: the knobs in the row aren't the ones that act.** With `encounters.drive.pressureByLambda` on (#608,
     note 908, as shipped), `pressure.crewScale` isn't read and the pressure has no crew scale at all; `curve.free`'s
     `atGate`/`atEnd`/`crewFade` aren't read either. With `pressureGate` null the pressure spends as it would alone, and its
     spends pay off λ's debt, so the free channel almost never fires. The pressure made about 11 encounters a night for any
     crew, and a night's load is flat by crew size:

     A crew of two met as many encounters as a crew of three: about 1.45× a three's a head. That's the playtest's line 1.

   - **The proposals** (to the director on the Creature Tweaker, `tools/tweaker/proposals-723.json`, the store's batch):
     1. **A crew of two at 0.6** (`pressure.crewLoad` `{ "2": 0.6 }`): two's load a head about a three's (21.5 / 18.3 /
        13.1 against 19.2 / 18.2 / 15.4), 13.5 encounters a night against 19.5, three and four untouched. Choice: 0.6, 0.67
        (gentler cut), or as is.
     2. **Frontier's end eased** (`pressure.escalationTier` `{ "frontier": 2 }`: ×3 at the terminus, not ×4): the gate the
        same, the night still rising (the director, 16 Oct: "the longer a run goes, the more monsters"). On every crew on
        Frontier: a three's end goes 15.4 → 12.2 a head an hour. Choice: 2, 1.5 (×2.5, a two's end halved), or as is.
        A first-runs-only version needs the campaign's run count in the director (not there today): code, proposed.
     3. **Repetition: the variety knobs don't reach it.** `varietyWindow` 6, `nightRepeat` 0.45 and the Gaunt in
        `curve.kinds` changed one encounter in twelve nights (back-to-back repeats 16 % and 15 % either way). The repeats are
        the pressure on a moving train, where the Cinder Hounds are often the only kind whose rule fits (seed 1: five packs
        in under three minutes, each the only option). The free channel, the six-kind pool the row names, is 0–1 a night.
        With proposal 1 the repeats fell to 3 % for two. What would help (code, not built): the pressure holds instead of
        sending a kind among the last two when it's the only fit (a breather and variety at once), and more kinds allowed
        onto a moving train (#706). Only seven kinds have a free gate in code today (the six, and the Gaunt unweighted).
     4. **Rhythm: a breather after a fight** isn't a knob: `aftermath` (12 s) follows only a death, a lost car, a rescue or
        a Holdout freed. Proposal 1 alone takes a two's median gap 48 → 65 s and the gaps over 90 s 21 → 34 %. A fight won
        (the last threat on the crew driven off or killed) as an aftermath event, with a longer hold for a small crew, is
        code: proposed, not built.
   - **What the Tweaker's own panel shows:** its crew × cars grid works from `crewScale`, which isn't read with
     `pressureByLambda` on; worth a line on the page when it's next updated.
