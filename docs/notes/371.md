371. **Lineside props are solid (queue #108, B1; GDD App. F.1 "the world is solid", note 279's "Not yet": lineside props).** Every tree, boulder and telegraph pole beside a generated line was the art's alone: `PlanArt.PlanDressing` dealt them from a constant seed (7) with `System.Random` and float noise, so the sim knew none of them, and the crew, creatures, balls and bodies passed through the forest. Float noise and seeded `System.Random` aren't promised bit-equal across machines either, so the art's placement couldn't simply be read by the sim.
    - **The sim places them** (`Run.LinesideProps`, one per route, shared by the art and the sim): the same rules the art had, ported. Forest in world-space stands (`Stand`: two value noises on an integer hash, no float trig) as dense as the biome grows it (`Cover`), the odd tree out of them, 50 tries a 12 m slot, 6–9 m to 90 m out (more near than far: u^0.75 from square roots, where the art had u^0.8; DMath has no real powers); the biome's flora by weight (keys in ordinal order, no dictionary order), dead and corrupted by its shares, stunted on the barrens, white pine over the canopy; none on a crag (rise over run over 1.1 on the 2 m further out); boulders by the biome's roughness, bigger and sunk deeper on a slope; a telegraph pole every 50 m, 4.5 m right of the line. A stream per slot from the night's seed (the plan's), every try drawing the same whether it's kept or not, so asking for a stretch or only what's within a reach deals the same things.
    - **Kept off the line's own ground** (`OffTheLine`, `Free`; WorldArt's `OnBranch`, `OnStop`, `InsideWalls` and `PlanClear`, ported): a branch's ground on its side, every stop's buildings, roads, tracks and yard throat (`StopGround`, its 6 m cells; the art's `Clearance` reads it too), an alternate within 9 m, a road's bed and shoulders, water (`TerrainField.WaterAt`, given the height already in hand; a lake's extent and the rivers' and shores' stretches are asked first, since `WaterAt` asks every river), and for trees and boulders each stop's cleared zone and the inside of a fort, its town's square, or a walled town's whole extent behind its wall (`InsideAFort`, `Fort.Bounds`, note 335; the poles run on through a fort). Poles no longer stand on a yard's first siding or in a lake, as the art's did.
    - **Stood as walls** (`LinesideProps.Walls`, added in `World.EnableRun` and again in `EnableTown`): a trunk as the box round its radius (the pieces' own: 0.015 h for the pine, 0.018 h for spruce, fir and tamarack, 0.012 h birch, dead ones 0.014 h, from WorldKit and NovaKit), to its top; a boulder as its lump's box (0.9 by 0.75 of its size; since note 389 the lump's own measured box), turned as it lies, its top where its sinking leaves it, so one sunk under a step (player.json `stepUp`) is walked over as `PushOut` walks anything that low; a pole round its foot. Owner 0, so a facility's module clears any on its work point (note 279). Everything that meets `StopWalls` meets them: the crew (`PlayerMotor`), what's loose (`Solidity`), the Moose's charge and rams, the Whistler's run, balls (`Guns`, a `Structure` hit) and bodies.
    - **Out to a reach** (run.json `walls.linesideReachM` 40): the crew and what hunts them keep within it between the stops, whose ground is cleared further out; past it the woods are seen and not solid, as all of them were. It's the cost: dealing a 24 km night's solids (13,000 on frontier:7, 5,900 on deepTerritory:2) takes about 300 ms on this machine, once per route (cached with the route), where the whole forest out to 90 m (43,000) takes about a second. 0 turns them off.
    - **The art draws from it** (`PlanDressing`, `WorldArt.Lineside`): its trees, boulders and poles where the sim stands them, the kinds, variants and looks as they were (`Tree`, the ghost or dead spruce, the tamarack's gold, the corruption's brass; what's only seen drawn from each prop's own seed). The treeline cards behind (55 m and out) stay the art's, on the sim's stands. Alder, reeds and tufts stay passable and the art's. A hand-laid line's lineside is unchanged.
    - **Same on every machine:** `dt linegen prints` (T116's cross-play checksums, run on Windows and Linux in CI) carries a `lineside` checksum of every prop.
    - **Headless:** `dt linegen lineside --route r [--reach m]` reports the props by kind, biome and species, how many within 20 m, the solids at the reach, and what dealing them costs.
