And let me explore this town after I land. This is a cool town ... I want to see more").**
   - **What it was:**
     - The gatehouse's iron gates were modelled drawn back against its passage walls, always (`StructureKit.Gatehouse`). They were only heard opening and shutting (`GameAudio.PlaceGates`, AU1's place-threshold cues); nothing was ever seen to shut, and the passage was open to walk through all night.
     - A walled town's way out to the next night was stood in the Sim only for the departure (the fort's own gatehouse). The arrival's was a 17 m gap in its wall with no gatehouse at all, so someone on foot could walk out of the far end of the end town.
   - **The gates now:**
     - Each gatehouse has two leaves of heavy timber in an iron frame (`StructureKit.GateLeaf`), hung from iron posts in the passage walls.
     - **When they open:** a gate is open while the train goes through it, from its engine `run.json gates.openAheadM` (40 m) short until its last car is `shutBehindM` (3 m) past. Every other time it's shut: in front of an empty yard, behind the train once it's out of the departure, and behind it on the way into the arrival, well before it's home (`homeInsideGateM`, 15 m). A town's other gate (the departure's way in, the arrival's way on out) stays shut all night, since no train comes to it (`Fortresses.Open`).
     - **How it's kept:** the Sim stands the shut leaves as walls (`Wall.Gate`, keyed by where the gate is along the main line, `Fortresses.GateKey`). `StopWalls.OpenGates` opens and shuts them every tick from the train's front and rear, in `World.Step` on the host and every client alike, so prediction walks into the same leaves. A walled town's wall stands both its gatehouses (`Fortresses.Round`), which closes the arrival's gap.
     - **The wicket:** a door-wide opening (`Fortresses.WicketLo..WicketHi`, the left of the line, 2.1 m high) stands open in one leaf. A crewmate left outside a shut gate can still come in (run.json forts: "A crewmate left behind near the gate can run back inside").
     - **Seen:** the scene swings each gate's leaves toward the walls' state over `swingSeconds` (4 s), inward toward the fortress (`GreyboxScene.SwingGates`, `WorldArt.GateLeaves`). The gate cues go by the same tuning (`GameAudio.PlaceGates`, otherwise unchanged), so what's heard, seen and walked into agree.

     - The arrival town's solids are the world's (`World.EnableTown` adds `Arrival.Walls`), its ground is laid, and it is the same plan as the next night's departure town, solid for solid (`EndTownTests.TheTownPulledIntoIsTheTownTheNextNightLeavesFrom`).

     - Nothing about the run's end holds the crew: the motor runs on after it's delivered, and the result goes after 20 s (note 837).
     - The arrival's way-out gap, the one place it could be walked out of, is now its shut gate.
