212. **The look-out: the sweep's dormant kinds brought on (notes 186 and 204's "not yet"; GDD §34, App. A.4, A.6; WP17).** The combination sweep reported the Gaunt, the Draggers and Ribbits dormant in every combination: placed and never come on, so never judged. Bots keep to their posts. Nobody walked over to the Gaunt asleep 25 m out on the ballast, stood within a metre of a roof's lip, or went off alone where a Ribbit pack outnumbers them. On frontier:7 the crew go in at the start for a bag at a crane, so nobody was even up on the roofs.
    - **Chosen over staging** (the task's option b): no start on the line makes a bot stand at a lip or walk off from the others. Staging only picks where the night starts; this is what a curious player does.
    - **The errand** (`Bots/LookErrand.cs`, `RoofWalkerBot.Errand`, `HarnessOptions.Look`, `balance.json combinations.look`). On an insisted night only (`HarnessOptions.Insist` and `Look` both set; `dt balance --pairs|--triples` and `dt harness --insist` set them), the crew's last walker is the look-out:
      - **On the ballast** (boarding at the start, or down at a stop): it waits up to `waitSeconds` (12) for an insisted Gaunt or Ribbit pack to be sent. Then it walks to within `gauntM` (3) of the Gaunt (it stirs at 9, wakes at 4) or `ribbitM` (5) of the nearest Ribbit, if one's within `seekM` (80). It leaves the others behind, which is what puts it outnumbered. Then it boards as usual.
      - **Up on the roofs** with a Dragger insisted: until it has met one, it stays out there and doesn't go in for a bag (`KeepOut`), so the Dragger is placed under the car it's on. Then it walks along the roofs to that car and stands `edgeM` (0.6) in from the lip on the Dragger's side, beside it along the car (it reaches from `grabRange`, 1.0 m).
      - Each thing that comes on (telegraphs or further, for anyone) is crossed off by id, for good. A Dragger that rearms after is looked at already. Nothing left to look at, and the walker's back to its post.
    - **Fair:** it's only feet and a facing, intent like any player's, worked out from what its own client sees. The enemies' rules aren't touched. What wakes, wakes by its own rule. The bots answer with their normal counters: `Heed.Voice` talks to the Gaunt, a walker steps back from the lip and swings, `Heed.Rescue` hauls a held crewmate free. The errand comes after the repair kit and a car's trouble, and before a stop's part, and not while the walker is getting warm. It's deterministic: the bot's tick, the enemies in id order, a set of ids only asked whether it holds one.
    - **Before / after** (`dt balance --pairs --only Gaunt,Dragger,Ribbit,Climber`, default grid: frontier:7, crew 4, one seed):
      - Before: 6 dormant; never exercised Dragger 3, Ribbit 3, Gaunt 3.
      - After: 3 fair (the three with the Gaunt), 3 trivial (Dragger+Ribbit, Dragger+Climber, Ribbit+Climber); nothing never exercised.
      - `--at-stops`: all three pairs of the three come on (trivial). `--crews 2,8`: they come on at eight; at two there's no walker to send, and they don't.
    - **What it found:** the sweep now judges these kinds, and it fails on them. With one look-out who steps back from the lip and outruns the tongues, a Dragger or a Ribbit pack lands nothing in 90 s. That's the trivial verdict doing its job; it's left for balance, not hidden.
    - **Not yet:** looking more than once at the same thing; the Grumbler's crane out on the line (still unplaced without `--at-stops`). (A look-out in a crew of two: done in note 222, the gunner.)
    - Harness and bots only; protocol and enemy rules unchanged. Test: `AuditTests.TheLookOutGoesAndLooksAtWhatLiesInWait`: a 30 s insisted Gaunt+Dragger night, four bots. Without the look-out, both are placed and neither comes on; with it, both do.
