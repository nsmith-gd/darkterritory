819. **A harbour town's Hope Tower and its Flame of Hope (queue #547, B2; the World Building board's Cultures tab, the director, 12 Oct: "Extremely large lighthouses with bright burning flames above that people of the town call Flames of Hope for the world to find in the darkness", for harbour towns).**

**What it was.**
- A harbour town (note 692's Breakwater Town) stood a lighthouse on its head: `NovaKit.Lighthouse`, a small wooden light of 13 m with a lamp turning.
- From the town it didn't show at all: the head's wall and the chapel's steeple stood over it.
- And every town type's landmarks were drawn twice:
  - once by the form (`WorldArt.Forms`);
  - again by the square (`WorldArt.Square`), as a timber box of the fixture's size. The chapel stood beside a box as big as itself.

**What changed.**
- **The tower** (`HopeKit.Tower`): the harbour's light on its head is its Hope Tower. It's built on the older kind of light, before the lamps: a coal fire in an iron basket (the Isle of May's, Tynemouth's).
  - **The shaft:** near 80 m of tapered dressed stone on a granite plinth, a string course every 12 m.
  - **The windows:** slits round it as the stair climbs, every third one lit (the keeper's lamps on the way up).
  - **The top:** a corbelled gallery railed in iron, and on it the basket on its pedestal, the coals white-hot in it.
  - **The coal:** a davit off the gallery hauls it up on a rope down the tower's side, a tub halfway, to a coal bay at its foot.
  - **Its size:** about the tallest real lighthouses. It stands over the head's wall and the chapel, over the whole town.
  - **Its budget:** a large prop's (the art catalog's `hope-tower`).
- **The Flame of Hope** (`Effects.Beacon`):
  - tongues out of the basket, the tallest three men high, leaning with the wind;
  - a white-hot heart, cinders going up and away;
  - a column of coal smoke lit orange from under;
  - its light over the gallery and thrown wide over the head's roofs.
- **Seen far off:**
  - the tower and its Flame are drawn out to 1.5 km (`WorldArt.Flames`; other landmarks 460 m);
  - far off, the Flame is a point of fire that keeps a least size (1.3% of the way to it), so it stays a point;
  - its glow on the fog (`WorldArt.Glow`) is over the tower, higher and stronger than the town's own: what a crew sees before anything else of the town.
- **In the Sim:**
  - `TownFixtures` "lighthouse" (8.4, 8.4, 84): the plinth and the coal bay, up to the basket's brim. It's solid as drawn, and the form keeps its houses and ways off it.
  - A **keeper of the Flame** ("flamekeeper", byname "{first} the Flame" or "Hope {first}") stands at its door all night, looking out over the town, with five lines of their own.
- **Its words:** "the Hope Tower", in world/towns.json's harbour landmarks, the town's name in them. The breakwater's light at the mole's head stays a small lamp in its cage.
- **Drawn once:** the square leaves a town type's landmarks to the form, so no box stands round the tower or beside the chapel.

**Measured.**
