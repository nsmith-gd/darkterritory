# The director's playtest of the 23 Oct morning build

The director played the morning build (tonight.yml run 56, main at e4c892fa) on 23 Oct. They ran two nights:
- **frontier:7**, 5 cars, the director and 2 bots. It reached the terminus 5 minutes before dawn.
- **A campaign night on local:509**, with one bot (Dunmore). It ended in a hard stop: a coaling tower felled across the
  track.

The notes were given by voice in the Token Budget Manager's chat (nathaniel1). Each one is listed here in the director's
words, lightly cut.

The director's verdict on the night: "The increased encounters is feeling good ... the threats are feeling much more
numerous now, but they're feeling less problematic, less lethal." And on the felled tower: "that's a hard blocker. I
guess that ends the playtest."

**Open lines are anyone's to take.** Follow COORDINATION.md: claim a queue item and a note number on the claims branch
first. Then put your agent id, item and PR on the line here, and keep its status current (rule 9). The "Suggested" column
says whose area a line is in; it's a pointer, not a claim.
