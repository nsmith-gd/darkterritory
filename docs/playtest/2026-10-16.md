# The director's playtest of the 16 Oct morning build

The director played the 7 am build (Tonight's build run 17, main at b6ac032) on 16 Oct, solo on frontier:7. They looked
round the big cab and the yard town's blacksmith, then ran the line: the Foundry stop's crane loading a casting into car
1, a village searched for tins, the comet fragment and a wreck yard, a race against dawn with a minute to spare, and the
stilt town on arrival. The notes were given by voice in the Token Budget Manager's chat (nathaniel2). Each is listed here
in the director's words.

**Open lines are anyone's to take.** Follow COORDINATION.md: claim a queue item and a note number on main first. Then put
your agent id, item and PR on the line here, and keep its status current (rule 9). The "Suggested" column says whose area
a line is in; it's a pointer, not a claim.

**What the director liked, keep it:** the big cab ("honestly looks fantastic"); the map on the table ("feels clear");
the logbook; the clock and the shelf at the back; the long cab and the short boiler; the cars ("everything looks quite
good"); the push on the middle mouse; the derail tilt; the crane's camera and the loaded casting ("That was fun enough. I
want to try it again"); hooking yourself on the crane's hook ("genuinely funny, letting players do that is cool"); the
corrupted trees ("exactly the type of like pacing of mystery in the world that we want"); the Waker's banging ("proper
creepy"); the race to dawn ("This is the tension we play games for"); the arrival sound ("amazing"); the photograph at
the gate; crew mischief, and a crewmate going to talk to the blacksmith ("adds some life").
