# The director's playtest of the 16 Oct evening build: the first real multiplayer night

The director played the 5 pm build (Tonight's build run 19, main at 8c94b18, `DEV 8C94B18` on the title screen) on
16 Oct with a second player: their wife. It was **the first real two-player test** and the first crossplay test. The
director hosted on **Windows**, and she joined from **Linux** (the Linux zip from #1186), over **direct IPv4**, not
Steam. They rode the line together: she took the cannon seat, they stopped at a yard, they fought a fire in the rear car,
and they rolled into the end town exactly at dawn. The notes were given in the Token Budget Manager's chat (nathaniel2).
Each is listed here in the director's words.

**Open lines are anyone's to take.** Follow COORDINATION.md: claim a queue item and a note number on main first. Then put
your agent id, item and PR on the line here, and keep its status current (rule 9). The "Suggested" column says whose area
a line is in; it's a pointer, not a claim. Lines 1-5 are bugs that block a two-player night; lines 6-8 are the
director's call on what a night is for, and they matter most.
