# A two-player night, 24 Oct (the director and their wife)

The director and their wife played a Frontier night together on 24 Oct, the two of them and no bots, on the evening
build (tonight-58 or later). It was her second time playing, and the first time she really met the monsters. The director:
"She doesn't play a ton of friendslop games so she represents a solid demographic for us of new to the genre players."
The notes were given in the Token Budget Manager's chat (nathaniel1), in the director's words.
