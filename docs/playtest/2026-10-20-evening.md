# The director's playtest of the 20 Oct evening build

The director played tonight's build (run 42, main at d9462a2 with #1381 on top) on 20 Oct evening, on a campaign night with
one bot crewmate (Dunmore) driving. The night went out of the yard, through an abandoned village, a yard stop with a
spur, Ashgrove Yard with the crane, and on to the end, where the director let the Waker come and tried driving
backwards between its legs. The notes were given by voice in the Token Budget Manager's chat (nathaniel3). Each one is
listed here in the director's words.

The director's verdict on the night: "So far this has been the run that has felt the best. Like, by a bajillion. This
game is truly finding its balance here." After the notes: "Keep working on those bug fixes, and then when you're done
with those bug fixes, let's work on other outstanding features in the GDD."
