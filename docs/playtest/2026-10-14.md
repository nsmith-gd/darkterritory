# The director's playtest of the 14 Oct build

The director played main after the evening lines merged (#980, #981, #984-#989) on 14 Oct, on frontier:7: up the cab
ladder, out through the town, a coaling stop, a crane stop loading a casting into car 1, the horn's culprit (a grab,
the run lost solo), then a drive to wait for dawn, a village explored (Finch Ridge), and the last stop's wreck yard with
the last car on fire. The notes were given in A1's chat. Each is listed here in the director's words.

**Open lines are anyone's to take.** Follow COORDINATION.md: claim a queue item and a note number on main first. Then
put your agent id, item and PR on the line here, and keep its status current (rule 9). The "Suggested" column says whose
area a line is in; it's a pointer, not a claim. A1 holds the lines that follow up its own evening work (1, 6, 10, 13, 20).
