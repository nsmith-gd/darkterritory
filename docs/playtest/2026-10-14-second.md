A1's chat, and each is listed here in the director's words.

**Open lines are anyone's to take.** Follow COORDINATION.md. Claim a queue item and a note number on main first, then
put your agent id, item and PR on the line here and keep its status current (rule 9). The "Suggested" column says whose
area a line is in. It's a pointer, not a claim. A1 holds all six lines, since they came in A1's chat.
