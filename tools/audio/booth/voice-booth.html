The voice booth

Lines where the game is better with a real voice than a stand-in. Read each one into your phone, add the file to its card, and Claude makes it into the game's voice, so it doesn't sound like you. Then you listen and keep or redo each version.

  1. Record the line in your phone's voice memo app. Do the takes the card asks for in one file, with a beat of silence between them.
  2. Add the file to the line's card: tap Add recording, or drop it on the card.
  3. Tell Claude in the audio chat that takes are waiting. Each comes back as one or more disguised versions.
  4. Listen to each version and mark it Keep or Redo, with a note if you like. Kept versions go into the game.

A quiet room, the phone about 20 cm away, a second of silence before and after. Voice memo (.m4a), .wav, .mp3 or .webm, up to 15 MB a file.
