DARK TERRITORY — a co-op night on the last railway
==================================================

Start DarkTerritory (DarkTerritory.exe on Windows). The title screen has:
  CAMPAIGN      three save slots; the fortress between nights, where you take contracts, buy cars and upgrades
  QUICK NIGHT   any tier and seed, alone or hosted for friends
  JOIN A NIGHT  a friend's address (host:port); Steam friends join from the friends list
  SETTINGS      sound, voice (open mic or push to talk), HUD, VR comfort, mouse speed

On foot
  mouse look, WASD walk, Shift run, Space jump
  E: grab or let go of a ladder; hold E to work things (the firebox shovel, the valve, a door, a coupler, a switch stand)
  E near a crate, a lamp or a body: pick it up or put it down; right mouse: throw it
  left mouse at a gun (the engine's roof, the guard car's roof): fire. Every round brings the Choir.
Driving (from the cab)
  R/F regulator up/down, B brake (hold), X reverser (stopped only)
Voice
  talk nearby with an open mic (or hold V for push to talk); hold T for the radio
  the radio is a thing: E near one puts it on your belt (the train carries two, in the cab and the guard van);
  right mouse with empty hands sets it down for someone else. Without one you're not on it, and it's dead in tunnels and mines
Other
  F1 the HUD, F2 invite Steam friends, Tab chase camera, Esc frees the mouse, Esc again leaves the night
VR
  start with --vr: head to look, left stick to walk (click to run), right stick to turn, grip to use, trigger to fire

Hosting on a LAN or the internet: friends join your address on UDP port 27015 (forward it on your router, or use Steam).
Your saves, settings and any crash reports are in your user folder's app data, under DarkTerritory.
