DARK TERRITORY — a co-op night on the last railway
==================================================

Start DarkTerritory (DarkTerritory.exe on Windows). The title screen has:
  CAMPAIGN      three save slots; the fortress between nights, where you take contracts, buy cars and upgrades
  QUICK NIGHT   any tier and seed, alone or hosted for friends
  JOIN A NIGHT  a friend's address (host:port); Steam friends join from the friends list
  SETTINGS      sound, voice (open mic or push to talk), HUD, VR comfort, mouse speed, controls

Keys (the defaults: SETTINGS > CONTROLS changes any of them)
  W                  Walk forward
  S                  Walk back
  A                  Step left
  D                  Step right
  Left Shift         Run (held)
  Space              Jump
  E                  Use: press to pick up or put down; hold to work things (the shovel, a valve, a door, a coupler, a switch stand, a brake wheel), to carry a crewmate, to struggle free
  Left Mouse         Fire the gun you're at
  T                  Throw what you're holding (the coal off the shovel too)
  R                  In the cab: release the brake and go
  Y                  In the cab: close the regulator (a train with no boiler only)
  B                  In the cab: brake (held)
  X                  In the cab: the reverser (stopped only)
  L                  The engine's headlamp, on or off
  V                  Push to talk (with PUSH TO TALK on in Settings; otherwise the mic is open)
  Right Mouse        The radio, held, with one on your belt: the whole train hears
  Q                  The crew roster (held); with a number, mute that crewmate (Shift and a number: put them off, or vote to)
  C                  The route card, a page a press, then away
  Tab                The chase view; dead, the next of the dead's views
  G                  Swing the tool you carry
  H                  The whistle cord, in the cab
  K                  The lamp in the car you're in
  Z                  Cut a coupling (held, looking down at the coupler)
  F                  Grab the nearest ladder
  P                  Dead: bookmark the view you're following
  Left Alt           Vent the boiler's steam (held, in the cab)
  I                  What's aboard (toggle)
  J                  The emote wheel (held; the mouse picks)
  Left Ctrl          Crouch (held, or a toggle with HOLD KEYS on TOGGLE)
  Middle Mouse       Push the gun along its rail (held, walking)
  5                  Shove the crewmate in front of you
  U                  With a hand lamp: the lamp signals' wheel (held; the mouse picks)
These keys are fixed:
  1-4 · Mouse wheel  The hotbar's slots: pick one · step through them
  1-9 · Enter        Dead, with a ballot: pick a creature · cast it (the same number again casts it too)
  Space              Dead: your live mic to the living, on or off; at the run's end, give the commendation the arrows picked
  F1                 The HUD, off or on
  F2                 Invite Steam friends
  F4                 Hide the first nights' card
  F5                 Reconnect, when the link to the host is gone
  Esc                The night's menu (settings, leave the night); in a menu, back
  Alt+Enter          Windowed or full screen

Working the train
  hold E to work things: the firebox shovel, a valve, a door, a coupler, a switch stand, a brake wheel
  E near a crate, a lamp or a body: pick it up or put it down; T throws it
  the big crates take two: one takes an end with E, then the other; either lets go and it's down
  the winch: one on each crank, both holding E. In VR, turn the crank round with your hand, together
  left mouse at a gun (the engine's roof, the guard car's roof): fire. Every round brings the Choir.
  the radio is a thing: E near one puts it on your belt (the train carries two, in the cab and the guard van);
  hold right mouse to talk on it. Without one you're not on it, and it's dead in tunnels and mines
  driving is from the cab: R releases the brake and goes, B brakes, X the reverser (stopped only), Left Alt vents
VR
  start with --vr: head to look, left stick to walk (click to run), right stick to turn, grip to use, trigger to fire
  both hands on a heavy crate's end to take it

Hosting on a LAN or the internet: friends join your address on UDP port 27015 (forward it on your router, or use Steam).
Your saves, settings and any crash reports are in your user folder's app data, under DarkTerritory.
